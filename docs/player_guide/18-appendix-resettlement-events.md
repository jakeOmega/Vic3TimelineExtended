# Appendix: resettlement events

This appendix lists the events that a Settlement Authority can trigger, behind
[Resettlement events](07-states.md#resettlement-events) in States and
population. Each month that one of your Authorities receives settlers, you have
a 10% chance of one of these events, set in one such Authority's state. After
one fires, none comes for eighteen months, however many Authorities you run.
Money costs are a share of your yearly gross income.

| Event | Fires for | First choice | Second choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Land Rush | Voluntary programs | "Open the land at once": +2,000 settlers a month there for a year, and more pressure on the people already living there | "Survey it first": +10% construction there for two years |
| The Speculators | Voluntary programs | "Send in the inspectors": −50 authority for two years; Rural Folk approve | "The market will sort it out": 500 fewer settlers a month there for two years; Landowners and Industrialists approve |
| A Hard Winter on the Frontier | Any program | "Send relief": 1% of yearly income | "They will have to endure": 1,000 lower-strata deaths and radicals there, and −10 migration pull there for a year |
| Dust Storms | Homesteads, once per state after ten years | "Fund a conservation program": 2% of yearly income, and −5% farm and ranch throughput there for ten years | "Keep plowing": −20% farm and ranch throughput there, fading over five years |
| The Reform Campaign | Coercive programs | "End the coercive programs": every coercive program switches to your best voluntary one; +5% prestige for five years; Intelligentsia approve | "These accounts are exaggerated": −5% prestige for five years; Intelligentsia disapprove |
| Famine in the Settlements | Special Settlements | "Send grain and tools": 2% of yearly income | "Let the settlements fend for themselves": 5,000 farmers die and many turn radical; Rural Folk strongly disapprove |
| A Petition to Return | Coercive programs, once per state after five years | "Let them go home, and end the program here": one in twenty of the lower strata there who are at least second-class citizens return to the state that last sent settlers, which gains loyalists; that Authority switches to your best voluntary program | "The program continues": many radicals among the lower strata there |
| Land Disputes | States where settlers arrive among people below second-class citizens | "Negotiate reserves for them": 1% of yearly income, −25 authority for two years and 300 fewer settlers a month there for ten years; the cultures there below second-class citizens gain loyalists | "Back the settlers' claims": those cultures turn radical |

In the Petition to Return, only pops of at least 2,000 people send anyone home,
so small communities stay. The Reform Campaign's first choice ends coercive
programs in every Authority, while the Petition ends the program only in the
Authority where it fires.
