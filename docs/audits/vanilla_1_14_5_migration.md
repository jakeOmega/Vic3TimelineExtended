The mod's last migrated version was 1.14.4; the install is 1.14.5 "Ice Tea" (`release/1.14.5`, 149 files changed, all dated 2026-09-30). ## War-goal flipping (patch note 6)

Scan of the mod: 1,829 hits, every one bucketed. **No mod JE tests region ownership**, so the vanilla class has no analogue; the only vanilla JE the mod replaces (`je_unite_the_nations`) is unchanged; the eight changed vanilla event files have no mod references. On-actions: **do not add `on_wargoal_enforced_by_timer`** (vanilla's own comments: an un-reversed timer goal fires both, the second at peace); the mod's three `on_wargoal_enforced` handlers only see timer goals later, none regresses. Optional in-game confirmation probe: `debug_log` in a temporary on-action for each of the two.

## Infamy (patch note 2; issue #387)

**Reunify is ported (2026-09-30, owner's call).** `te_reunify_country`'s infamy block is now vanilla `annex_country`'s, block for block: the target's `country_infamy_value` plus the loyal/rebellious-subject multipliers. That value already carries the homeland, shared-language, claim, adjacency, scripted-unification, interest-tier and unrecognized-target terms, so the mod's copy (5 × a population multiplier, interest-tier copy, floor 5, cap 50) is gone, and the nationalism / pan-nationalism / Psychology techs now work on it. The goal's own `possible` gate (claims on 2/3 of the home states) is unchanged. The loc promise ("cheaper than a generic annexation") now reads "costs the same as any other annexation", and the player guide (`08-diplomacy.md`) describes it that way. A side effect worth knowing: the floor of 5 is gone, so a small claimed homeland costs about 3.

Cost before and after (recognized aggressor, interest tier 3–4, not adjacent, world population 1 B / 5 B for the old absolute formula, target GDP share = population share; computed by hand from `common/defines/00_defines.txt` 1113–1175 and `common/script_values/00_infamy_values.txt`, so illustrative):

| Reunify case | before (1 B / 5 B) | now |
|---|---|---|
| small (0.05 % of world), unincorporated, foreign, no claim | 8.8 / 23.8 | 4.4 |
| small, incorporated capital, homeland, claim | 12.5 / 30.0 | 3.3 |
| mid (1 %, 4 states), incorporated, foreign, no claim | 105 / 105 | 154 |
| mid, homeland, claim (with nationalism) | 105 / 105 | 28.9 (26.1) |
| large (5 %, 10 states), incorporated, foreign | 250 / 250 | 523 |
| large, unincorporated, homeland, claim (with nationalism) | 250 / 250 | 31.6 (28.5) |

The mod's three `unification` plays are unaffected (goal `unification` declares 0), so vanilla's scripted-unification discount is moot for them.

Still open:

1. **Subjugate (`te_force_become_subject`, O2)** keeps the 1.13.9 shape: 2.5 / 8.5 for a small target, 25 for mid and large (Sovereign Empire halves), against a vanilla protectorate (0.5 × `country_infamy_value`) of 8.8 / 77 / 262. Options: leave (cap 25), `0.5 × country_infamy_value` (the action becomes unaffordable), or `0.2 × country_infamy_value` (31 / 105 before the 25 cap, so the cap decides anything above small).
2. **`te_un_mandate_restore_state`'s unauthorized fallback** is a stale copy of the 1.13.9 `return_state` formula (reachable only without a live mandate); syncing it to `state_infamy_value` is mechanical. Its "vanilla return_state's figure, verbatim" comments are stale with it.
3. **Game rule `infamy_generation`** (×0.5 / 0.75 / 1.25 / 1.5) is applied by the engine to "calculated infamy". Whether it scales the mod's 47 direct `change_infamy` sites is undocumented; test it in game before deciding whether the aggression-type sites (nuclear taboo, UN war surcharge, covert exposure) should read it.
4. **Engine floor.** `WAR_GOAL_INFAMY_MINIMUM = 1` is documented as also flooring "a war goal's total infamy". If the engine does, the mod's deliberately free goals (authorized UN mandate) cost 1, and the player guide's "at no infamy" (`09-united-nations.md:503`) is off by one. It also puts a floor of 1 under a claimed small reunification. Check in game.
5. **Force nationalization.** Re-express the inert 10× cut by overriding `NDiplomacy|WAR_GOAL_INFAMY_FORCE_NATIONALIZATION_GDP_WEIGHT` (vanilla 0.25; 0.025 would be 10×), or accept vanilla.
6. Smaller: ruler aptitude traits (±0.2/±0.1) are now twice vanilla's Cautious/Reckless (±0.1); `te_force_become_subject`'s AI block lacks vanilla's regional-objective hooks (AI-only behaviour change); `05_transfer_money.txt` (a same-path override of an unchanged vanilla file) lacks `ai_consider_threaten_naval_hostilities` and uses legacy infamy literals.

## Naval (patch note 3)

Nothing mechanical broke. 1.14.5 changed only `ship_vulnerability_add`, `ship_screening_add`, the new `vulnerability_exploitation`, and three `..._hull_damage_mult = 2.0` on torpedo boat, torpedo boat destroyer, destroyer and submarine. Crit, damage, armor, hp, cost and blockade did not move, so the runbook § 7 anchors still hold (except the crit define, below).

1. **Navy prestige (the important one).** Vanilla's 19 ship types each set `ship_power_projection_add` (super dreadnought 465, destroyer 200, submarine 55 …); **no mod ship type or module does**. In 1.14.4 prestige came from "fighting strength", which mod ships had; 1.14.5 reverted it to power projection scaled by max hit points ÷ `PRESTIGE_FROM_NAVY_POWER_PROJECTION_HIT_POINTS_REFERENCE` (1150). So **mod fleets now add about nothing to navy prestige, navy involvement or `country_navy_power_projection`** (unverified in game; check a country holding only mod ships). A vanilla late fleet of 10 super dreadnoughts + 10 light cruisers + 10 destroyers + 6 carriers gives about 250 prestige at multiplier 0. Options: (A) leave 0; (B) give each mod ship a small `ship_power_projection_add` chosen so its prestige matches its vanilla class analogue (pp ∝ 1/hp; for the super-dreadnought's 14.9 prestige per ship: pp = 2.23e6 / hp, e.g. 186 at 12,000 hp, 80 at 28,000); (C) vanilla-like pp (out of band: an antimatter battleship at 465 pp is 87 prestige, 5.8× a super dreadnought). Arithmetic is from the define/script-value comments, not the engine.
2. **Vulnerability and screening scale.** Vanilla rescaled V and S by about ×0.56 (dreadnought 180→99, destroyer S 150→85, aircraft carrier V 100→56); the mod's ships stay on the old scale, so its capitals are ~1.8× heavier than their anchors (arsenal / hypersonic / antimatter V 200 vs 111) and its escorts draw 15–25× the fire of a vanilla light cruiser (V 60–100 vs 4). Options: (A) leave; (B) a ratio-preserving rescale (asw_destroyer / guided_missile_ship V 50→28 S 250→142; capitals V 200→111 S ×0.5; carriers V 100→56; light-cruiser analogues ×0.4; 27 module `ship_vulnerability_add` lines in `extra_ship_modifications.txt`; `unmanned_aerial_vehicles` `ship_screening_add` 50→~28). Illustration: an unescorted mod arsenal fleet takes up to +190 % from a full-exploitation attacker.
3. **`vulnerability_exploitation`.** Unspecified = exploits everything, and every mod ship is unspecified. Vanilla's ladder (65 → 140) only bites because vanilla V rises with era; every mod capital is V 200 whatever its era, so no value separates eras without also blunting a ship against its own contemporaries. Recommendation: add nothing; decide together with 2.
4. Early-hull counter for the mod's torpedo-group ships: recommended no (their hulls already dominate era-3 targets). `ai_weight` on the 19 types is vanilla-dropped and dead (harmless).
5. **Crit armor** is now 0.5 (`NAVAL_BATTLE_CRIT_ARMOR_REDUCTION`), not "ignores all"; runbook § 7 and the war reference were corrected. The mod's crit values are class anchors that did not move.

## Other findings, not from this patch

- **`gui/right_click_menu.gui` is brought up to 1.14.5 (2026-09-30).** It was a full-file override that still carried vanilla 1.13.1's layout: `### Station in State` / `### Deploy to Front` in the two multi-select menus and no `### Rehome` item, where vanilla 1.13.2 replaced them with `### Invasion` and added Rehome (Station and Deploy to Front live on in vanilla's military panel), plus `TransportPlannerPopup.GetSortedFleets` where vanilla now calls `GetFleetsForTransport`, and one `blockoverride = "text"` typo. The file was never in an "at-risk" list because vanilla did not change it in 1.14.x; the stale sections predate that. Rebuilt as vanilla 1.14.5 plus the mod's own hunks only: the treaty-article quantity and generic-company inputs (#542 and its company sibling, 236 added lines and the one divider line they replace) and a placeholder-icon comment. Proven on the changed lines: the merged file differs from vanilla by exactly those hunks and nothing else. The marine-capacity fleet sort the mod added in April is now in vanilla itself. Not tested in game: check the right-click menus on a selected army and on a selected fleet (Invasion, Rehome, transport list), and the treaty designer's quantity field.
- **`gui/military_formation_panel.gui` had the same problem and is merged too (2026-09-30).** Vanilla has 8 `Station` references (the stationing controls, including the UI for 1.14.5's stationing in subjects) and the mod's copy had none: it was vanilla ~1.13.1 (the pre-1.13.2 Invasion / Station / Deploy button layout, the older debug block, `IsLocalPlayer` where vanilla now says `IsPlayer`, trailing-whitespace noise) and **contained nothing mod-specific**: every data function, loc key and texture it used that vanilla 1.14.5 lacks is old vanilla, and none of the commits behind its differing lines is a mod-feature commit (they are the "1.8 compatibility", "Update to 1.9" and "1.12 update start" merges). Rebuilt as vanilla 1.14.5 plus five tweaks kept from the mod's copy because no vanilla version (1.12.4 on) has them and their origin is not certain: mobilization option groups in one column (`datamodel_wrap = 1`), the borrow/lend settings section shown for armies only (two `MilitaryFormation.IsArmy` gates), and the Raise Conscripts / Mobilize icons not disabled by `IsValid(...)` (two `enabled` lines removed). They are listed in the file's header, each is a few lines, and each can be dropped independently if it turns out to be old vanilla too. Not tested in game. Neither merge adds or removes a type, template or widget name, so no other mod GUI file can depend on what was dropped.
- The other same-path overrides were not audited hunk by hunk (`market_panel`, `states_panel`, `construction_panel` are mostly mod content, but the method that found these two would find stale sections in them; runbook § 5).
- AI `wargoal_weights` in `common/ai_strategies/other.txt` are keyed by war-goal **type** (`te_reunify_country`, `te_un_mandate_restore_state`); vanilla keys them by **kind**. If the engine only matches kinds, those two weights are dead. In-game check (start an AI mandate play).
- New AI gate `NAI|START_DIPLO_PLAY_MIN_WARGOAL_SCORE = 20`: check AI holders still open plays with the mod's two war goals. The AI's scarce-resource valuation counts only vanilla's oil, gold and rubber buildings, not the mod's own strategic goods.
- The vanilla mirror `~/src/vic3` is still at 1.13.9 (#392); this migration did not need it.
