# Grand Monuments: audit of the buried sites, gaps and proposals

**Date:** 2026-10-16, asked for by the owner ("audit our current list of 35 buried archaeological sites. Add Pompeii as a
'discoverable' site already being excavated, review the existing list for gaps, and propose additional plausible fictional
sites to add"). Pompeii is built (row 87, rework spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-grand-monument-archaeology-rework-design.md`
§ 5); everything else here is a proposal for the owner to pick from.

Source of the list: issue #939 (14 candidates in its body) and its comment (the Gemini design: 30 historical sites, 8
speculative breakthroughs S1–S8, 6 fantasy easter eggs E1–E6). The MVP built the 30, Olympia, Delphi and Sidon from the body,
and S3 (Villa of the Papyri) and S4 (Tomb of Alexander) as plain sites. The owner skipped the easter eggs and their game
rule (2026-10-14).

Thirty-five sites (thirty-six with Pompeii) across nine regions, but the eastern Mediterranean and Mesopotamia hold twenty of
them, and Empiricism alone opens seven, four of them around the Aegean. There is nothing at all in sub-Saharan Africa, Iran, Central Asia and Russia, Southeast Asia, Japan and
Korea, North America, Mexico beyond Palenque, Iberia or central Europe.

## Checks on the existing rows

- **Placement.** Three sites sit in a stand-in state because the map has none closer: Machu Picchu in Ica (Cusco is the
  real region), Delphi in Attica (Phocis), Sipán in Cajamarca (Lambayeque). Vindolanda is in Yorkshire,
  the map having no Northumberland.
- **Vectors.** Mohenjo-daro is incidental in the table, but it was found by a survey officer (Banerji) following brick
  robbers, which reads as either. Deir el-Bahari is right as incidental (the Abd el-Rassul family). Fine as they are.
- **Technologies.** Göbekli Tepe at E-commerce (1995) and Caral at Digital Telecommunications (1994) are very late for sites
  a survey could identify; they are faithful to the dates, so they fit only long campaigns. No change proposed.
- **Pieces.** Eleven sites have no named part for a trophy: Winged Victory, Antikythera, Oseberg, Nefertiti, Lascaux, Nag
  Hammadi, Dead Sea Scrolls, Ötzi, Caral, Villa of the Papyri, Tomb of Alexander. The statue and stele forms carry off whole,
  so most of these need none; Villa of the Papyri ("the Herculaneum scrolls") and the Tomb of Alexander ("the golden
  coffin") could have one.
- **Pompeii** is the most famous excavation in the world and was being dug in 1836, so it was the clearest omission. It is
  now row 87, seeded as an Antiquarian dig already under way by the owner of Campania.

plainly that it has never been found. The issue's breakthroughs that are not sites (S1, S2, S5, S6, S7) would need the
deferred breakthrough mechanic; S8 can be a site.
