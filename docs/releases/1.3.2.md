- Victoria 3 1.15.1: the mod is updated for the 1.15.1 hotfix. A game saved under 1.3.0 or 1.3.1 carries on, with the new rule settings below on their old behavior.
- State pictures: from the 1930s technologies on, the picture at the top of the state panel shows the state in the decade of its owner's technology. 356 new pictures cover each region's cities by size and by how well their people live, and ports, mines, oil fields, farms and forests, with boomtowns, smog, ruins and nuclear strikes among them. Hover the picture to see which one shows and why.
- Game rules: Covert Warfare, World War, the United Nations and Cultural Hegemony each gain an Early Unlock setting, opening with Central Archives, Trench Works, Multilateral Alliances and the Electric Telegraph, with their later parts locked until the usual technologies. Under it the UN is the League of Nations until some country has Intergovernmental Organizations. Late Unlock, the old behavior, stays the default. A new Small-Economy Construction rule (experimental, off by default) lets a small economy pay as little as a tenth for construction, rising to the full price at a GDP of 10 million.
- Construction: a Construction Site sized before it had hired could deliver ten times its share of the week's purchase at the treasury's cost, the likely cause of the cost spikes reported on the Workshop. Sites can no longer deliver more than you buy: a new site starts working once it has hired its laborer, a week or two after it appears, and the sites that have theirs deliver the whole purchase meanwhile. Construction Cost Scaling moves by at most a tenth of itself a year. With Free Market Construction off, Base Construction is 10 a week (it was 5) and follows the save's own setting, where it could vanish or appear after a load. The base game's Base Construction Capacity rule does nothing with the mod and shows one setting, No Effect.
- Events: the text of every event chain is rewritten to one style, about 3,000 lines. The rewrite found some sixty places where an event did something other than it said (wrong conditions, swapped effects, options that could never be picked), now fixed. Events queued on a day their conditions weren't yet true never appeared; the court events, a sanctions enforcer's Cargo Under Seal, the Nationalization Question, The Budapest Offer and others now arrive. A country is told what another's choice did to it, from a UN verdict to a forced exit from a common currency. Nine options that charged for a program by raising wages charge a weekly expense sized to GDP. 63 event pictures were reframed so the text panel no longer covers their subject, and 19 photographs were replaced.
- Map Room and interface: the Map Room's maps sit in seven collapsible groups and gain six: Aristocrats, Clergymen, Capitalists and Academics (each class's share of a state's people), Investment, and Trade Capacity by state. The journal lists every ending before it happens: the Civil Rights Movement's ten, the Colonial Empire's nine, and a first and a later completion for the Space Race milestones. The Power Grids tab has a Generation panel, the Electricity each kind of plant makes a week.
- Space: the Deep-Space Probe can fly a Grand Tour past Jupiter and the planets beyond. It slows the whole program by a quarter and costs 60 more innovation a week while it runs, and its rewards last twenty years where a single target's last ten. A space colony of fewer than 1,000 people has no politics: it never demands self-government or breaks away.
- Climate: warming differs by latitude. On top of Climate Warming, a state between the tropics carries Tropical Warming and a state 55° or more from the equator High-Latitude Warming. Each degree costs farms 17.5% of their throughput in the tropics and 12.5% in the mid-latitudes and gives them 2% in the far north, and mortality and standard of living differ the same way. Standard of living is a whole number, so the mod's fractional bonuses and penalties did nothing; some two dozen now reach a whole point, Climate Adaptation's +1 among them.
- Decolonization: six era 9 technologies each strengthen a colonial program, so a late empire that pays for all three can hold against Globalization and Knowledge Economy. The Colonial Empire entry waits out a civil war of the country's own, and an AI that isn't a great power plans full decolonization only with a reason. After Order Restored, neither the territory nor the neighbors that rose with it declare again for ten years. The young-state events fire once per independence.
- United Nations and diplomacy: a member that voted against a resolution is offered a refusal only where refusing changes something, and denouncing a decision against another country stands with the accused. Covert operations and Humanitarian Aid build up to 1,000 Involvement, as the base game's pacts do. Subjugate costs a fifth of the annexation's infamy, up to 25; no country starts with 3 infamy from the Rules of War; the mod's extra escalation stops while a play's escalation is paused; and a Grand Monument can't be looted again while a piece of it is abroad.
- Banking: Open-Market Operations pushes prices harder the further inflation sits under target, from 1 point at 2% to 5 at −6%, so a Fiat country at its rate floor can climb out of a deflationary Panic. Two countries in one market, such as an overlord and its subject, can appeal to each other for a banking rescue.
- Economy and navy: the most automated production methods of about twenty buildings, from Automated Harvesters and Planters to Robotic Service Providers, keep a flat crew of 1,000 for the whole building, where they cut a level to 10 or 20 staff. The Advanced Material Fabricator takes more inputs and keeps at least 100 staff a level. The mod's ships move to the base game's current vulnerability and screening scale, keeping their ratios to its ships. The AI founds a Strategic Reserve for a small country inside another's market only above 100 million GDP.
- Civil wars and culture merges: a revolution's winner also keeps the national climate policies, a nuclear crisis freeze, the penalties of a broken peg or a currency reform and UN sanctions. A culture that has switched into its merged culture leaves every country's primary cultures once nothing in the base game depends on it, so a merged Germany shows German alone.
- Late-game speed: the heaviest monthly and yearly passes (construction sites after a border change, the colonial stability bar, the UN's AI proposals, Cultural Hegemony, tourism) read world-wide figures once instead of many times over.
- German and Spanish: both translations cover every line of this version.
- Fixes: under the full Tax Code the Tax Code tab could stay grayed out for good; a nuclear strike sends one notice; election events ask for the technology their text assumes; 75 missing descriptions (power bloc principles, law groups, unit groups) are written; and more from log triage.
