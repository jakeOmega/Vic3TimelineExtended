Released on the Steam Workshop on 18 October 2026, where the launcher showed it as 1.1.8. Tagged afterwards at the last commit on `main` before the upload.

- Global Commons (new game rule, on by default): each Deep-Sea Mine level needs a licence from one of five ocean basins. Licences are shared out every January by coastline, ports and naval power projection. The system adds protected claims, a holding fee, leasing articles, an overlord's claim to a subject's unused claims, blockades that cut mine output, a Seabed tab and a per-state tile. The Deep-Sea Mine runs on half the staff and mines a polymetallic-nodule mix.
- The UN's Law of the Sea gains seabed terms, and there are two new UN measures: an Antarctic Treaty with research-station decisions, and a moratorium on destructive anti-satellite tests.
- Space: colony launches leave in each world's launch window, worked out from real orbits, and progress made while waiting is staged in orbit. Orbital traffic and anti-satellite strikes raise an orbital debris index, which debris-removal methods bring down. Two era-12 logistics methods (Tethered Launch, Fusion Tugs) cut Launch Capacity costs.
- Judiciary: a new law group of six court laws, from Executive Tribunals to Algorithmic Jurisprudence, with a Judicial Independence score. Reviewing courts use the score to slow changes to protected laws, and Forceful Legislation spends it.
- Nation tab: a new first tab in the Timeline Extended window shows the legislative override, Judicial Independence, Wealth Concentration, construction cost and global technology at a glance. It also lists the mod's permanent modifiers by type, with their sources, instead of the country panel. All five tabs now share one tab strip, and the state panel gets a Wealth Concentration tile.
- Grand Monuments: 42 historic landmarks stand in 1836, from the Arc de Triomphe and the Temple of Heaven to the Parthenon and Great Zimbabwe, each with its own name and picture, and ten famous trophies are already abroad. Monuments can be carried off as trophies from occupied states or through a new treaty article, and brought home again. Several monuments can stand in one state, and a monument grows grander with age.
- Archaeology: 36 buried sites, Pompeii among them, dug with methods paid in innovation, with discovery and find events and two treaty articles for digging abroad.
- Commissions become opportunities with a Raise It button. New unique dedications include To the Unknown Soldier and Olympic, and historical opportunities include Nelson's Column and the Washington Monument. Cultural pull is no longer capped, every monument, site and trophy has a picture, and the AI no longer demolishes monuments.
- International actions (UN stances and resolutions, covert pacts, cultural institutes, crisis and aid treaties) now cost influence instead of authority or bureaucracy, and the AI takes them only with influence to spare.
- Covert warfare: you can watch a caught operation instead of exposing it, from a new Known Foreign Operations list. Spying on a friend is no longer blocked, but getting caught at it now costs at home. A 15th operation, Counterintelligence Support, lends your service to a friend.
- Banking: every country holds the Banking entry, dormant until a Stock Exchange and a level-5 Urban Center start its cycle. The overview is a boxed grid in larger type, with a hover on each reading. A contagion scare no longer plays out as a crash, which had inflated crisis waves and could set off the Great Depression.
- UN: the panel states every resolution's, convention's and agency's terms, and conventions apply on ratification. Sanctioned members can petition to lift their sanctions, sanctions lapse when their enforcer is gone, and Extensive Modernization Aid can now be ratified.
- Economy: Construction Cost Scaling now follows labour slack. It applies in full when idle labour is 3% of the workforce or less, and not at all at 15% or more. The Ministries of the Environment and Consumer Protection add +1% construction cost a level (was +5%). Tourism's port and rail bonuses become one excess-infrastructure bonus with diminishing returns, and Highway methods add tourism. The Market panel's Trade tab gets All Goods rows that set one import or export level on every good.
- AI: power plants choose their method by output and no longer subsidise coal and oil plants. AI-managed methods hire a flat crew of 1,000 engineers. Unemployment checks read as a share of the workforce. AI power blocs take capital-city and historical names, and nuclear freeze or disarmament demands come only where they would stop something.
- Laws and events: stall events always slow a law, and every debate event offers a concession. Changing Rules of War now costs or refunds infamy once. The Natalism Initiative decree gives +15% births for 100 authority (was +50% for 250), Pro-Natalist Subsidies becomes Pro-Natalist Policy, and the LGBTQ+ Rights laws no longer cut births. State Eugenics Program and the Ministry of Thought Control unlock earlier, and Privacy Rights moves to the human rights laws. "A Routine Mishap" and the Digital Rights events fire less often.
- Fixes: Environmental Sustainability principles and the Botanical Gardens boon made pollution's health harm worse; they now reduce it. Hovering a taxation law no longer drops a late game to about 4 fps. A monument opportunity could crash the game, and the Rededicate tooltip and the dedication event flooded the error log. Other fixes cover trophies taken by occupation, tech theft aimed at known technologies, civil-war sides and rival events picking real rivals. New icons replace placeholders, and event pictures with garbled lettering are retired.
