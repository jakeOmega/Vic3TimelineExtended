- Power blocs: two new identities. A Creditors' Consortium, available when the Banking System rule is set to Enabled, stands behind its members' debts. When a member defaults, the leader either rescues it on terms (a debt receivership, the leader's economic laws imposed, investment rights with monopolies for its companies, or privatization) or refuses. A Research Commons, which opens with Intergovernmental Organizations, pools research in the leader: members send it 75% of their innovation, every member gets every technology any member knows, and a country that leaves researches at half speed, recovering over ten years. Three new principle groups come with them: Conditionality, Development Lending, and Technology Controls with its Technology Embargo.
- Banking: most banking tools now cost the same small price to switch on, 0.5% of pops radicalized or 0.2% of GDP, and nothing to lift. The exchange tools also work on Fiat Money and Digital Currency, and each tooltip says what the tool does on your currency. Sterilize Capital Inflows is now Sterilization. Leading a common currency now pays: once a member adopts it, the leader earns seigniorage, pays less risk premium and can run more tools at once. An adopter's inflation is pulled toward the leader's. A reserve-currency anchor already at its credit-standing floor gets its cut in risk premium instead.
- Tax Code: under the light setting, the three taxation laws without a dividend tax can levy a low one of up to 5% through the Tax Code entry. Traditionalism rules it out.
- Construction: Small-Economy Construction has three strengths, putting the price with almost no GDP at a half, a fifth or a tenth. Construction Sectors hire a third of the workers they did and Wooden Buildings takes 30 wood, not 40, so a sector turns a profit at the base price.
- Politics: the mod no longer doubles the activism that laws in force give a movement, and no movement opposes a law that no government can replace yet, such as Unrestricted Automation. Both had tilted the base game's Reform or Revolution toward revolution. AI countries start law changes far more often and are more wary of civil war.
- AI: in a market-construction game, AI governments buy only the construction they can pay for, keep a reserve when interest rates are high and may borrow a little when rates are low. When the engine queues nothing for them, they queue administrations, a first power plant, railways, universities, highways and airports themselves. The AI also runs its space program within its budget, answers a restless Environmental Movement with the climate policy it is nearest to adopting, starts no wonder while in default and founds no institution it lacks the bureaucracy for.
- Map Room: a new Map Options section limits every map to one continent, your market, your bloc or your country, and sets the outlier trim, the scale (Linear, Logarithmic or Ranked) and the colors (Colorblind-Safe or Grayscale). There is also a new Population Density map.
- Interface: the journal gathers the mod's systems in a Timeline Extended Systems section and the six social movements in Rights and Society. When you pick a National Park, Public Housing or Carbon Conversion Works to build, the list of states shows each state's pollution, crowding penalty or Electricity price. The state pictures measure poor and affluent streets against the world's average, a boomtown needs 5% growth a year, and a megacity shows its own picture.
- Production: livestock ranches gain meat methods up to era 11, labor-saving Herd Management methods and a Feed Additives group. Plantations gain Mechanical and Autonomous Harvesters, and the Cultured Produce Facility can grow tobacco. The power grids of a bloc holding Market Unification or Internal Trade IV or V join as if under an interconnection agreement, and each grid card shows its generation by source.
- Seabed and space: the ocean basins hold five times the licenses (5,000 in all), the Deep-Sea Mine mines mostly Structural and Conductive Metals, and a Build only on local claims checkbox keeps new levels off long-haul licenses. The Extraplanetary Base's mining methods make about six times the Conductive Metals (95 a level on the Asteroid Mine, from 15) and less Tech-Critical Metals. Moon modules need no shipment slot. The module stock grows with your fabrication and with a new Module Storage group, and a colony takes up to eight modules past its slots.
- Game rules and culture merges: the Covert Warfare and Cultural Hegemony entries come back when their rule is switched back on. Other human players hear when a culture merge begins and completes, and get a popup when one of their cultures switches.
- Translations: Russian joins German and Spanish, machine-translated (corrections welcome). All three cover every line of this version.
- Fixes: the base game's War Wounded entry counts Medevac Helicopters as medics and can complete; a Binding Ceasefire now costs the UN's peace and order each month the aggressor fights on; a banking crisis spreading to a country with no states could crash the game; eight conditions that read activism or turmoil on the wrong scale never passed, among them the movement test for domestic terror attacks and loose-warhead plots (now 50% activism) and the AI's acceptance of Population Transfer; and the Construction Site Crew modifier no longer shows in the state panel.
