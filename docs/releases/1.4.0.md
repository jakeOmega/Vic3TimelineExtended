- Save-game compatible with 1.3.4. Under Full Colonies a game under way is given the Space Forces Bases its fleets and colony modules need, crewed in full for six months. A country with the ICBM or submarine technology keeps that leg, submarines only with a coast. Colony modules need more crew than before, so a colony's modules may run short until settlers arrive. An Extraplanetary Base on the old Resort Colony method switches to another method at the next weekly tick: build a Resort Colony for its tourism. An Indian Leadership play under way is dropped.
- Space colonies: module slots are gone, and a colony's people limit its modules. Each module level past the first, Habitats aside, needs a crew of 10,000 (5,000 with Artificial General Intelligence). Habitats have a cap by the world's size, past which each costs 100 points more than the last. Settlers are now a number, 500 a month doubling with each press, drawn from every state at 3 Launch Capacity each, and a colony's costs are flat Launch Capacity a week rather than a share of throughput. Up to four modules are built a month, Von Neumann Machines doubles fabrication, and an Orbital Yard builds modules for colonies on its own world or its planet's moons with no launch. Space Colonization and Terraforming each add half again to a colony's room, a Research Station raises the innovation cap by 25 instead of research speed, and each discovery's choice doubles one module at its colony.
- Resort Colony and Extraplanetary Bases: the Resort Colony is a building of its own from Space Colonization, and its Luxury Resorts make 1,800 Tourism a level, whatever the state it is built in. Under Simple and Full Colonies it has room and a draw at each world, and Extraplanetary Bases get seven ground types that change what each site mines, with a Bases view that moves levels to the sites that pay best.
- Space forces: under Full Colonies, the new Space Forces Base (Cislunar Infrastructure) employs Spacers, a new pop type, and gives 1,000 Space Forces Personnel a staffed level. A point of fleet strength takes 100 (50 with Artificial General Intelligence), a marine battalion 1,000, and a level of five of the modules, Defenses among them, 1,000 on top of its crew; short of them, each works at its crewed share. Fleets move and fight four times a month, and a stance (Avoid, Hit and Run, Engage, Fight to the Last) sets when one breaks off. Space Command takes orders for a set number of hulls. Seizing a colony takes more than half again its defenders; the Moon's colonies, and Mars's, defend as one; and Training Grounds make marines count up to 25% more. Marines can hold a colony at 100 autonomy, losing battalions each month and approval from the interest groups that oppose Colonial Exploitation.
- Nuclear weapons: the program now builds its ICBMs and missile submarines, and the two technologies only open the projects. From the first device, What Funding Builds picks warheads, ICBMs or submarines, and a leg takes 80 weeks at five funding steps; submarines also need Guided Missiles and a coast. Launch on Warning, Automatic Retaliation and reach to every country need fielded ICBMs. Survivability's target compounds, each part covering a share of what the others leave exposed: 25 with bombers alone, 40 with Radar, 58 with ICBMs, 79 with submarines, 82 with Missile Defense Systems. A city strike destroys 5% to 40% of each urban building's levels by the state's metropolitan share (was 10% to 80%), and a quarter outside Full Demographics (was half).
- Cultures: a new game rule, Assimilation Across Heritages, decides what becomes of a pop whose heritage group no primary culture shares. Unrestricted, the default, lets it join the state's largest primary culture as in the base game, once a year; Hybrid Cultures has it take up the majority's language in one of 584 new cultures, such as Anglophone African; None stops it. Under those two, joining the majority itself takes a Bodily Autonomy law other than Natural Form, from Morphological Freedom (era 12) on, and under every setting Ancestral Citizenship blocks it. Hybrid Cultures needs a new game. Culture Merges is on by default in new games, and the French, Han, Arab and East African merges accept 60% literacy in place of Public Schools.
- Laws and politics: the Bodily Autonomy laws are resized for the late game: Licensed Modification gives 200 authority, Morphological Freedom +75% migration pull for −100% assimilation, Clade Citizenship takes over Self-Directed Evolution's safer work and faster colony growth for −300% assimilation, and Prescribed Form gives 400 authority and +200% assimilation. The Automation Policy laws, and the Displaced Workers penalty under Unrestricted Automation, start at Industrial Robotics (era 8). Under Protected Class women can lead interest groups and take command. Vassalization IV and V let every bloc member peacefully integrate its own subjects. Autocracies and single-party states with Ancestral Citizenship, and some with Cultural Citizenship, now count as fascist toward a world war.
- Economy: Textile Mills, Furniture Manufacturies and Food Industries gain fourteen methods from era 5 to 11, such as Permanent Press, Flat-Pack Furniture and Precision Fermentation. Undersea cables cross open ocean, though never the Southern Ocean. With Global Commons on, only governments build the Deep-Sea Mine. With Naval Convoy Defense (era 6), a supply ship takes a week to build, not four.
- Map Room and Demographics: the Map Room has 90 maps, up from 46, in eight groups by theme: every pop type by headcount or share, Standard of Living, Wealth, Literacy, Unemployment and Pollution among them, and, with Nuclear Weapons on, Nuclear Reach, which colors each state by the leg of your weapons that reaches it. The population pyramid adds the sex balance at all ages, and two concepts explain the uploaded minds.
- Interface: the state view puts the mod's tiles in two columns after the base game's Overview. Small figures read as numbers (0, not 0.00K) and large ones abbreviate (650K, 1.69M). Middle states such as Contested draw yellow, not white. The Power Grids Stations table and the World Development Fund's recipients show ten rows, with Show All, and the monthly history charts open on 5 Years.
- Performance: in a 2032 United States game in Full Screen, the Grand Monuments tab draws 36 frames a second (was 16), Banking with its History open 47 (was 16) and Power Grids 76 (was 48). In a 2024 United States game, the weekly tick grew from 9 seconds to 564 within two weeks of the first Deep-Sea Mines being queued; it takes seconds again.
- Art and events: 18 event pictures are painted again (17 had garbled lettering, one a real politician's likeness), painted icons replace borrowed ones, and the misinformation event is rewritten as "False Stories Online".
- Fixes: Colonies Held counts each world once. Incite Colonial Autonomy and other pushes to 100 autonomy now free a colony, unless its holder's marines hold it. An Antimatter Engine or Antimatter Warhead Plant can't be expanded past its slot cap. A landlocked country no longer gets submarine reach. Swarm Technology follows Autonomous Weapons, so Robotic Soldiers come before Swarm Bots.
- Translations: German, Spanish and Russian cover every line of this version.
