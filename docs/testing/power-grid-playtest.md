# Power grids: play-test checklist

What the next play-test should look at in the power grid: the Grid Transfer Station, the Grid Power good and the
monthly balancing (`docs/systems/mod_systems.md` § Power Grids). In-game checks do not gate a merge (CLAUDE.md,
"In-game checks: self-checks and the play-test list"): a PR lands on the offline gate and adds what only the game can
show here. Each PR adds its own section, newest last, and a fixed item is struck through with the commit that fixed it.

## How to run

- **Deploy** per CLAUDE.md, "Play-testing unmerged work" (or from `main`).
- **Setup.** Research Electrical Capacitors (era 4) and build Power Plants in one state and none in a neighbour, so the
  two have Electricity at very different prices. Build a station in each, the second bordering the first.
- **Where to look.** A station's building panel (its goods, its modifiers Grid Upload / Grid Download, its subsidy),
  the state panel's Electricity price, and the market panel's Grid Power row.
- **Logs.** Read `debug.log`, not `error.log`. Search for `TE_SELF_CHECK` and `grid`.

## Level-scaled lines: a station runs whatever its staffing (2026-10-25)

## Staffing: an attractive job instead of unscaled lines (2026-10-25)

Neither `level_scaled` nor `unscaled` goods lines escape the building's occupancy. In the owner's game, Arkansas's station ran 20.7% of its `level_scaled` setting with 20.8% of its staff and 19.9% with 20.0%, and after the switch to `unscaled` lines, 19.7% with 20.0%. Throughput was −1% and Grid Power was in surplus. The panel's goods tooltip lists no staffing line ("Output percentage: 100.0%"). The factor makes up for staffing down to a fifth staffed (its 0.2 floor), but a station still has to be staffed to move its share. So the station is now a very attractive job: `building_job_attractiveness_mult = 5` on its method. What it can take from other employers is small: 500 engineers and 500 clerks (750 engineers on the HVDC Undersea Interconnector) per state (owner, 2026-10-25). The owner's first check the same day: it works, the stations fill and keep their staff. The lines stay `unscaled`, which at level 1 is the same as `level_scaled`. A year on, the `TE_GRID staffing` line read 479 of 507 stations at 90% staffed or more, 8 under 90%, 3 under half and 17 under a quarter (owner's 1989 game, 2026-10-25): the attractiveness holds.

## Prices, not supply (2026-10-30)

The balancing evened out each state's supply against its demand with its station left out, but the engine prices a state on everything sold and bought there, a sending station's purchase included. A large sender stayed at about base price whatever the grid's surplus, and its receivers were sent that surplus: in the 2013 Byzantium save Attica sold at 29.8 while every other grid state sat at the floor, its Fusion Plant had grown to 2,062 levels on the price, and the stations cost £2.9B a week in subsidy. The balancing now moves Electricity until it sells at one price in the sending states and at that price over their transmission efficiency in the receiving ones. This supersedes the Phase 2 **Equal supply with losses** row.
