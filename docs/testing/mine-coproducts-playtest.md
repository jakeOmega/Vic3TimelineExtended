# Mine co-products: play-test

Ten of the mod's mines make several goods (#1419; `docs/systems/mod_systems/production_methods.md` § The mod's mines and their co-products). Each runs its own copy of its family's equipment groups, generated by `scripts/generators/gen_mine_coproducts.py`. The copies were checked offline (`test_mine_coproducts.py`: same inputs, staff, technologies and output value as their templates); what the game shows and how markets respond were not.

## How to run

Start a new game and play, or console-research, to the late game (Internet of Things for Smart Miners). Build or find one mine of each kind: Bauxite, Manganese, Chromium, Specialty Alloy Metal, Precious and Minor Base Metal, Nickel and Cobalt, Lithium, Rare Earth Metals, Platinum Group Metals and Graphite. For step 5, load a save made before the update that holds at least one of these mines.

## Checks

1. **Names and outputs.** Each mine's production methods panel shows Mining Equipment, Explosives and Automation groups with the usual method names (Diesel Pump, Ammonium Nitrate, Dragline Excavators). A method's tooltip lists every good in the mine's share table, a Bauxite Mine's Diesel Pump 59.5 Structural Metals and 10.5 Conductive Metals a level. No raw key (`pm_diesel_pump_building_bauxite_mine`) shows anywhere, in English or German.
2. **Shared methods unlock.** On a Bauxite Mine running a Condensing Engine Pump or later, maintenance switches to the mechanized method, Tailings Treatment is offered with the Ministry of the Environment, and the transport group offers its infrastructure-4 variant under the Transport principle, as on an Iron Mine.
3. **Carbon capture.** With Clean Energy Technologies, a Graphite Mine on the Atmospheric Engine Pump offers Partial Carbon Capture (10 Coal), and the mine buys 10 Energy and Carbon Minerals a level for it (it used to burn them out of its own output).
4. **The AI's methods.** AI countries move their mod mines up the equipment ladder as they do Iron Mines. The copies cost and earn what their templates did at base prices, so their choices should match.
5. **An old save.** Load the save from before the update. Note which methods each of these mines runs: its equipment groups are new, so the engine may drop it to each group's first method (Picks and Shovels; the Condensing Engine Pump for the four tech metals mines) and retool it. If it does, say so in the release notes.
6. **Tech-Critical Metals supply.** With every deposit worked these mines make about 20% less Tech-Critical Metals than before, mostly from Nickel and Cobalt, and about a fifth more Structural Metals. In a 2000-or-later save, note the Tech-Critical Metals price in the largest markets and whether high-tech buildings stall for it. If it sits well above base price for decades, give Nickel and Cobalt more Tech-Critical Metals in `MINES` and rerun the generators.
