# Ranch and plantation methods: play-test

New production methods for the Livestock Ranch, the plantations and the Cultured Produce Facility (a Workshop request, 2026-11-15; `docs/systems/mod_systems/production_methods.md` § Livestock ranches and plantations). `test_ranch_plantation_methods.py` checked them offline: their technologies, the ladders, every combination's staff and the tobacco swap. What the game shows, and what the AI picks, were not checked.

## How to run

Start a new game and console-research to the late game: Green Revolution (era 7) for Industrial Feedlots, Mechanized Feeding Lines and the Mechanized Farm; Microprocessor (era 8) for Mechanical Harvesters; Biotechnology (era 9) for Recombinant Growth Hormones and the GMO Plantation; Internet of Things (era 10) for Robotic Barns; Genetic Engineering, Autonomous Vehicles and Lab-Grown Food (era 11) for Gene-Edited Livestock, Autonomous Harvesters and the Cultured Produce Facility. Build or find a Livestock Ranch, a Banana, Tobacco and Sugar Plantation, a Vineyard, a Rubber Plantation and a Cultured Produce Facility.

## Checks

1. **Names and order.** A Livestock Ranch's base group lists Open Air Stockyards to Mechanized Slaughterhouses, then Industrial Feedlots, Recombinant Growth Hormones and Gene-Edited Livestock. The group the base game calls Fencing reads **Herd Management**, with Mechanized Feeding Lines and Robotic Barns after Electric Fences. Wool Production lists the base game's three methods only (Sheep Ranch, Sheep Farms, Intensive Wool Gathering), and a **Feed Additives** group follows Refrigeration with Plain Feed and Antibiotic Growth Promoters. Every plantation, the Vineyard and the Rubber Plantation show a Harvesting Process group (Hand Harvesting, Mechanical Harvesters, Autonomous Harvesters). No raw key (`pm_industrial_feedlots`, `pmg_harvesting_process_plantation`) shows anywhere, in English or German, and each new method shows its icon. Industrial Feedlots, Recombinant Growth Hormones, Gene-Edited Livestock, Mechanized Feeding Lines, Robotic Barns and Cultured Tobacco Leaf have painted ones of their own (the meat methods bronze, the two Herd Management methods green, the tobacco purple, as their neighbours are); the rest borrow, as #1828 lists.
2. **The wool and feed methods stay.** On a ranch running Industrial Feedlots or a later tier, Wool Production still offers Intensive Wool Gathering and Feed Additives offers Antibiotic Growth Promoters. Both need one of the listed base methods, and the new ones were added to those lists by `INJECT`, as the mod already does for `pm_precision_tools` and `pm_elastics`. If they are greyed out, the `INJECT` didn't append.
3. **Figures.** A ranch on Recombinant Growth Hormones, Intensive Wool Gathering, Mechanized Feeding Lines, Refrigerated Rail Cars and Antibiotic Growth Promoters buys, among its other inputs, 107.5 grain (32.5 of it for the promoters), 5 Drugs (2 for the promoters) and 10 Chemicals a level, and makes 235 meat, 40 fabric and 5 Chemicals before throughput. It employs 2,000 a level, 500 each of laborers, farmers, machinists and engineers, and 1,000 (machinists and engineers) with Robotic Barns in place of the feeding lines. The tooltips should agree.
4. **Gates.** Autonomous Harvesters is greyed out on a plantation still on its first or second base method, and offered once it runs a Mechanized Farm or a GMO Plantation. With Human Labor Quotas, Robotic Barns and Autonomous Harvesters can't be picked, and a building on one moves to a method still allowed. Both say Robotic or AI-Managed in their tooltip.
5. **The automated share.** In a country with many ranches and plantations, note the Automation Policy share before and after its plantations move to Autonomous Harvesters. The share's base now counts every ranch and plantation level, so it starts lower than before the change and rises when they adopt it.
6. **Carbon capture.** A Sugar Plantation still offers its own carbon-capture group, and the Harvesting Process methods offer none (the farms' harvesters don't either). The Mechanical Harvesters' tooltip lists its oil and a small emissions line.
7. **The AI's methods.** By the 2000s, AI countries run Industrial Feedlots or later on most ranches, and the harvester tiers on plantations with a Mechanized Farm or GMO base. AI ranches take Antibiotic Growth Promoters in Feed Additives about as often as they did in Wool Production (88% of ranch levels in the owner's 2023 save). If the AI leaves the promoters or Recombinant Growth Hormones alone while Drugs are dear, that is expected; if the Drugs price rises sharply after AI ranches adopt them, note the price.
8. **Tobacco.** A Cultured Produce Facility offers Maintain Single Crop (default) and Cultured Tobacco Leaf. With the latter it makes 150 fruit and 120 tobacco a level, and the building's profit rises at base prices.
9. **An old save.** Load a save from before the change that holds these buildings. The new groups start on their first method (Hand Harvesting, Maintain Single Crop, Plain Feed), which change nothing, and the ranch keeps its base method. A ranch that ran Antibiotic Growth Promoters had it in Wool Production, which no longer holds it; read both Wool Production and Feed Additives. The save lists a building's methods by name, not by group, so either (a) the promoters stay on, now under Feed Additives, and Wool Production falls to its first method, Sheep Ranch: 20 wool a level less and no meat lost, and the ranch starts buying the promoters' Drugs at once; or (b) both reset (Sheep Ranch and Plain Feed): 35 wool and 35 meat a level less until Intensive Wool Gathering and the promoters are picked again. Note which. AI ranches should re-pick within a few months. Say so in the release notes either way.
