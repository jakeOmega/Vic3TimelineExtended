# Consumer industry methods: play-test

New production methods for Textile Mills, Furniture Manufactories and Food Industries, and a larger output for Programmable Food Synthesis (a Workshop request, 2026-11-25; `docs/systems/mod_systems/production_methods.md` § Consumer industries). `test_consumer_industry_methods.py` checked them offline: their technologies, the ladders, the gates on the other groups and every combination's staff. What the game shows, and what the AI picks, were not checked.

## How to run

Start a new game and console-research to the late game: Arc Welding (era 5) for Tubular Steel Furniture; Modern Chemical Processes, Stainless Steel Mass Production and Art Deco Architecture (era 6) for Permanent Press, Industrial Fermentation and Modern Design Furniture; Plastic Mass Production (era 7) for Molded Plywood and Plastics; Computer-Aided Design (era 8) for Computerized Cutting and Flat-Pack Furniture; Biotechnology and E-Commerce (era 9) for Enzymatic Processing, Engineered Yeast and Made-to-Order Furniture; Additive Manufacturing (era 10) for Whole-Garment Knitting; Synthetic Biology (era 11) for Bio-Composite Panels, Precision Fermentation and Molecular Spirits; Programmable Matter (era 13) to compare the top tiers. Build or find a Textile Mill, a Furniture Manufactory and a Food Industry.

## Checks

1. **Names and order.** The textile mill's base group lists Electric Sewing Machines, Permanent Press, Computerized Cutting, Whole-Garment Knitting, then Programmable Matter. The furniture manufactory's lists Mechanized Workshops, Tubular Steel Furniture, Molded Plywood and Plastics, Flat-Pack Furniture, Bio-Composite Panels, then Programmable Matter, and its Luxury Production ends in Precision Tools, Modern Design Furniture and Made-to-Order Furniture. The food industry's base group lists Food Additives, Enzymatic Processing, Precision Fermentation, then Programmable Food Synthesis, and its Distilleries end in Patent Stills, Industrial Fermentation, Engineered Yeast and Molecular Spirits. No raw key (`pm_permanent_press`) shows anywhere, and each description reads on hover. Every new method borrows another method's icon for now; note any that reads wrong for its method, for the painting batch.
2. **The other groups stay open.** On a mill running any of the new clothing tiers, Elastics, Textile Finishing Machinery, the AI-Managed Textile Factory, Regional Marketing and Mass Marketing can still be picked once their technologies are in. On a manufactory running a new tier, Precision Tools, Advanced, Robotic and AI-Managed Assembly Lines and both marketing methods can. On a food industry running Enzymatic Processing or Precision Fermentation, Robotic Food Processing can, and the separate copy for Programmable Food Synthesis stays hidden. Elastics and Precision Tools take the new tiers by `INJECT`; if either is greyed out, the `INJECT` didn't append. Modern Design and Made-to-Order Furniture are greyed out on Lathes and offered on Mechanized Workshops and every later base method.
3. **Figures.** A mill on Computerized Cutting alone buys 90 fabric, 30 dye, 10 tools, 2 copper, 10 Chemicals, 20 electricity and 5 electronic components a level and makes 260 clothes before throughput, with 500 shopkeepers, 3,000 laborers, 1,000 machinists and 500 engineers. A food industry on Programmable Food Synthesis makes 500 groceries a level. The tooltips should agree.
4. **Pollution.** Permanent Press, Computerized Cutting and Whole-Garment Knitting add 5 pollution a level, Tubular Steel Furniture and Enzymatic Processing 5, Molded Plywood and Plastics and Flat-Pack Furniture 10. Bio-Composite Panels, Precision Fermentation, the three distilling methods and the two luxury methods add none. None shows a greenhouse-gas line or a carbon-capture group.
5. **The AI's methods.** By the 1960s, AI textile mills and furniture manufactories run Permanent Press and Tubular Steel Furniture or later; by the 2000s, Computerized Cutting, Flat-Pack Furniture and Enzymatic Processing. If AI countries stay on the old methods while the inputs are on sale, note which method and the prices of its new inputs (Chemicals, Glass and Plastics, steel, electronic components).
6. **Markets.** In a late game, note the prices of clothes, furniture, groceries and liquor before and after most of the world moves to the new tiers. Each tier makes more a level, so fewer levels supply the same demand; prices that fall well below base mean the tiers overshoot.
7. **An old save.** Load a save from before the change that holds these buildings. Each building keeps the methods it ran, and the new ones are offered once their technologies are in.
