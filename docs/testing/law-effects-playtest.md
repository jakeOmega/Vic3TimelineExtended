# Taxation law effects behind a hover: play-test list

A taxation law's effects text prices the country's revenue at each of the five tax levels, and vanilla rebuilds it
three times every frame a law tooltip or the change-law panel shows it. Late in a game that held hovering a tax law
near 4 fps. `gui/00_te_law_effects.gui` (generated by `scripts/generators/gen_law_effects_gui.py`, gotcha #42 of
`gui_modding_guide.md`) puts that text behind a hover for taxation laws only. The law tooltip's version was seen in
game on 2026-10-18 from a test copy in the deploy folder: fast until the line is hovered, other laws unchanged. The
rest passed the offline gate only (`test_law_effects_gui.py`, the GUI lint, `gui_reference_audit --strict`). In-game
checks don't gate a merge (CLAUDE.md, "In-game checks"); work through these the next time the game is up.

## How to run

- **Deploy** per CLAUDE.md, "Play-testing unmerged work" (or from `main` once merged).
- **A late-game save**, where the slowdown showed (the 2048 game it was found in).
- **Logs.** Read `debug.log` first, then `error.log`. Search both for `00_te_law_effects.gui` and `te_law_tax_effects`.

| # | Check | What to look for | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The law tooltip | Hover your taxation law in the Laws panel: no slowdown. Under "Effects:" one line, "Tax rates and predicted revenue at each tax level". Lock the tooltip and hover the line: the five tax levels with their predicted revenue. A non-tax law's effects show as before. | The test copy behaved this way, so suspect the generated file first: compare its `FancyTooltip_Law` with the test copy (the same wrapper and line). |
| 2 | The change-law panel | Open a taxation law's full view (click it): no slowdown, and the same line under "Effects:", which opens the same text on hover. The same for a law being enacted. Its layout lines up with the other sections. | Slow: the wrapper didn't hide vanilla's widgets in a `vbox`. Give the `flowcontainer` `layoutpolicy_horizontal = preferred`, or swap it for a `vbox`. Misaligned: the same. |
| 3 | Another country's tax law | In another country's panel (Politics), hover its taxation law: no slowdown, and the line's hover shows that country's tax levels and revenue, not yours (`Law.GetCountry.Self`). | Empty or yours: change `te_law_tax_effects_country_tt` to read the country another way. |
| 4 | The Budget panel | The taxation law shown in the Budget panel uses the same tooltip as check 1. | As check 1. |
| 5 | Load-time errors | No `pdx_data_factory` or GUI parse line naming `00_te_law_effects.gui`. | The line names the binding; fix it in the generator. |
