# Banking Cycle

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Banking Cycle (`je_banking_cycle`)

> See also: `docs/systems/journal_entry_systems.md` for full JE system documentation.

- Uses 4 `scripted_progress_bar` instances and 78 scripted buttons (44 `cb_*` — 22 market tools, each an enable/disable pair — 18 `ce_*`, 16 `cw_*`). `test_banking_tool_roster.py` checks every `cb_*` tool is registered everywhere it has to be (see **Policy tools added 2026-09-23** below).
- **Every country holds the entry; its cycle starts later (2026-10-17, `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-17-banking-always-on-design.md`).** `is_shown_when_inactive` is `te_banking_entry_expected` (the rule on, not `is_country_type = decentralized`, `is_revolutionary = no`), so the engine activates the entry for every country in a game's first fortnight. The **financial cycle** starts once `te_banking_cycle_unlocked` holds (`stock_exchange` and an Urban Center of level 5, the test that used to activate the entry): `banking_cycle_start` seeds 50 / 0 / 0, from `immediate` or from the dormant branch of the monthly pulse, which also posts `banking_cycle_started_notice` to a human player. **The latch is `finance_cycle_value`; ask `te_banking_cycle_running`, never `has_journal_entry`, for "has a banking cycle".** Before the start the entry is dormant: its pulses do nothing but the start check, every tool is greyed (`te_banking_cycle_started_tt`, the first line of all 40 `banking_possible_*`) and invisible to the AI (each enable button's `visible`), and the Monetary Policy section is read-only (owner, 2026-10-17): `banking_mon_control_sgui`'s first condition, and `te_banking_controls_open` (today the same test) in the mandate branches of `te_monetary_update_target`, `te_mon_mandate_runs_bank` and the peg events' tooltip switches, so a dormant central bank runs the Dual Mandate (or its act's) as a country without the entry always did. Every `has_journal_entry = je_banking_cycle` outside the entry reads the entry object or the panel; `test_banking_always_on.py` holds each reader to its row (the spec's § 8). Old saves: the wider gate activates the inactive records; `te_banking_entry_net` (`te_banking_entry_on_actions.txt`, the yearly country pulse) adds the entry where the engine did not, and `te_debug_banking.1` logs `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN banking_entry_everywhere` once a game (`docs/testing/banking-playtest.md`).
- All banking buttons already have detailed AI weights. Each enable button's `ai_chance` is *core* terms (the cycle state the tool answers) plus *flavour* / *resource* terms (law, an independent bank, free points), and the latter only count while the matching `banking_ai_core_cb_*` trigger (`banking_policy_triggers.txt`) is true — as unconditional adds they scored a tool 5–25 in a stable phase and the AI parked prudential tools for 40% of the century. Keep a core term and its gate trigger in step.
- **The AI lifts a tool only once the reason it bought it has gone (2026-10-05).** Every market tool's disable button but moral suasion's ends its `ai_chance` with `multiply = 0` while `banking_ai_hold_cb_<tool>` (`banking_policy_triggers.txt`) is true. A hold covers the enable button's core trigger, less the states its own avoid terms rule out, and reaches past it so a cycle on a phase edge cannot flip a tool: the buffer, reserve requirements and margin requirements hold through stable while momentum is positive; directed credit, asset relief, the bail-in and export credit through stable while it is negative; the deposit guarantee, emergency liquidity and the bank holiday through the whole downturn. Directed credit is still dropped in a panic, which its enable side vetoes (the points go to the lender of last resort). Moral suasion has no hold because switching it costs nothing, so it still gives up its point in a boom or frenzy. Before the gates the lift weights started at the edge of the zone a tool was bought in, and the AI paid the toggle cost to buy back what it had just lifted: 6.7 re-buys a century within a year of a lift in the simulator, 3.2 with them, crashes flat (`banking_cycle_simulation.md` §18, `--tune pre_hold`). The external tools already worked this way (their disables score only when `banking_ai_core_cb_<tool>` is false). `test_banking_tool_roster.py` checks every gated disable carries its gate last, and that the simulator's `ai_holds` covers it. The command economy's planning tools and the cooperative economy's council tools (`banking_alt_economy_buttons.txt`) follow the same rule: 13 `banking_ai_hold_ce_*` / `_cw_*` triggers, each restating the cycle and bubble terms its enable button scores on, since those buttons have no core triggers. The simulator now reads all of these weights and gates directly for its command and cooperative arms (`--economy`; banking-cycle study §23). The coordination protocol, the solidarity campaign and the council directive are exempt for moral suasion's reason (1 point, free to switch, lifted to free the point). Mutual aid grants loyalists when switched on and dividend restraint when switched off, and neither costs anything the other way, so until 2026-10-05 a player could farm loyalists by switching them back and forth. Each grant now comes at most once in five years (`banking_cw_mutual_aid_loyalists_cooldown`, `banking_cw_dividend_loyalists_cooldown`, `days = 1825`), and `test_banking_tool_roster.py` requires every loyalist grant on a toggle to be paid for by radicals on the opposite switch or to sit behind such a cooldown.
- **Emergency liquidity is bounded to its crisis (2026-10-07, study §25).** Like the bank holiday, `banking_possible_cb_emergency_liquidity_program` opens it only in a downturn or panic. Its announcement (+12 cycle value, +10 bubble pressure) lands once per crisis: `banking_effect_cb_emergency_liquidity_program` applies it only without `banking_eliq_announced`, and sets that variable. The closing refund (1% of GDP of the 1.2% it costs) had made an open-close round trip +12 cycle value for 0.2% of GDP, as often as the player clicked. `banking_cycle_eliq_wind_down` (monthly pulse, after the crash check) counts the months the cycle holds at 40 or above in `banking_eliq_recovery_months`, restarting below 40, while the program or the announcement is outstanding. At `banking_eliq_wind_down_months` (12) it closes an open program through `banking_effect_cb_disable_emergency_liquidity_program` (history marker and refund), posts `banking_eliq_wound_down_notice` to a human player and clears both variables. They are variables, not the modifier, so a revolution's winner keeps the crisis's announcement spent. The AI lifts the program from stable anyway, so the AI's crash rate does not move; the wind-down is for the player who forgets it, which in the simulator had taken a 4-point country from 16.0 to 25.8 crashes a century (16.4 now). `banking_eliq_wind_down_tt` and `banking_eliq_announcement_spent_tt` quote the 12, and the row tooltip the +12 and +10; `test_banking_emergency_liquidity.py` pins those, the gate, the once-per-crisis branch, the pulse order and the simulator's port.
- Events: `banking_cycle_events.txt` — 83 event blocks (counted 2026-09-26) covering crises from railway bubbles to derivatives, with command-economy and cooperative-ownership variants (`.1xx` / `.15x`–`.17x`).
- **Events that duplicate a dashboard tool, or narrate monetary state, defer to it (#428).** Their draws in `banking_cycle_random_event_effect` ask triggers at the foot of `banking_policy_triggers.txt`, following events 15 and 58 (PR #416). `.31` *Bank Holiday* is drawn only while the dashboard's Bank Holiday could be pressed (`banking_can_enact_cb_bank_holiday` — the button's `possible` whole, points included, because the tool's own modifier holds them), and its option A enacts that tool. `.39` *Quantitative Easing* never fires beside running open-market operations; under the full system it needs OMO's whole gate (a dial at the rate's floor) and option A enacts OMO — a standing policy, with its activation cost, bubble push and inflation pressure, that runs until it is switched off, which is why B is the default since #448 — and under the simplified rule it keeps its bespoke, decaying stimulus. `.34` *Deposit Insurance* is not drawn while a guarantee is in force (`banking_deposit_guarantee_in_force`), and says "after recent banking failures" only after a crash in the last five years (`banking_recent_crash`, a timed variable set where each crash is decided). `.16` *Central Bank Independence* (market branch only) needs a delegated or independent dial under the full system, a national bank under the simplified rule; its option B moves the bank to the Growth mandate through `te_mon_effect_set_mandate`; option C is a compromise that leaves the mandate alone, and its modifier (`banking_event_dual_mandate`, "Monetary Accord" since #448, the key kept for saves) no longer claims a dual mandate the Mandate row never shows. `.10` / `.110` / `.160` need `scaled_debt > 0.25` and no default, and their option C is a real partial default since #448: `banking_partial_default_write_off` writes off a quarter of the debt (`clear_scaled_debt`), with the old premium and prestige costs and medium radicals among the bondholders. It still does not enter the engine's default state. Since #800 it is the restructuring talks' 25% package (`banking_restructuring_haircut`, below), and `.10`'s option B buys bonds where the bank may monetise. `.55` names a real planned-economy ally (`banking_is_fraternal_aid_donor`). **Wave marks:** `banking_crisis_wave_hit` is every country a contagion wave reached, from the moment it is reached (the cascade dedupe, and the UN docket's loan test through `banking_crisis_wave_reached`); `banking_crisis_wave_crashed` and `banking_crisis_wave_scared` record how its contagion roll came out, seven days later. The Great Depression event reads those two, so a country reached but not yet decided gets the general text and the bystander option. **One contagion event per country at a time (2026-10-17):** `banking_contagion_reach` marks a country `banking_contagion_pending` (120 days) when the spread sends it event `.7`, the event sets the mark again in `immediate` and removes it in `after`, and a reach that finds the mark is turned away: marked reached, with the open event's outcome if it has rolled. `banking_crisis_wave_init` leaves the mark alone, so a second origin crashing in the same pulse no longer sends a second copy (until then two rolls, two popups, and the first copy's options read the second's `crash_severity`). `je_banking_cycle`'s `immediate` drops the mark on a revolution's winner. Self-check `contagion_one_open` (`docs/testing/banking-playtest.md`). The wave's origin is marked `banking_crisis_wave_counted` by `banking_crisis_wave_init`, since the wave's count starts at 1 with it; before 2026-10-17 a contagion event it still held from an earlier wave, if its own roll crashed, counted it a second time. It fires at most once per game: `banking_crisis_wave_check_great_depression` sets the global `great_depression_occurred` when it queues the event. Before that flag, the only guard was that no country still carried a Great Depression modifier, so another crash in the three days before the event fired queued a second one, and a new one could start once the modifiers ran out.
- **Sovereign debt crises as a process (#800).** Three pieces, in `banking_debt_effects.txt` / `_triggers.txt` / `_values.txt` and `events/banking_debt_events.txt`; `test_banking_debt_crisis.py` pins the ladder, the wiring and every figure a tooltip states. (1) **Restructure the Public Debt** (`te_decision_restructure_debt`, `extra_decisions.txt`): shown with the banking entry, not to the rebels of a running revolution, while the debt is priced (`banking_sovereign_debt_is_priced`) or in default; it opens Restructuring Talks (`banking_debt_events.1`) and blocks reopening them for a year (`banking_restructuring_talks`). The talks offer a 10% reprofiling (`banking_restructuring_reprofile`: +1.5pp premium and −5% prestige decaying over five years, −10 relations), the 25% haircut (`banking_restructuring_haircut`, shared with the scare's option C: `banking_event_partial_default`, plus Wary Lenders at −10% `building_cash_reserves_mult` decaying over five years, −25 relations) and, in the engine's default state only (`banking_restructuring_deep_possible`), half (`banking_restructuring_deep_haircut`: +6pp and −25% prestige decaying over ten years, Shut Out of the Markets at −25% cash reserves, −50 relations). Breaking off is the default. A deal sets `banking_debt_restructured` for five years (reprofiling) or ten. **Market exclusion is the cash-reserves cut**: the credit limit is every domestic building's cash reserves plus a GDP-scaled base and no modifier reaches it directly, so lowering what buildings may hold shrinks it until the decaying modifier fades. The investors are every country owning more than 5% of our GDP (`gdp_ownership_ratio`, decolonization_events.15's threshold). The write-off moves the debt-load premium (`te_mon_scaled_debt_premium`, 16/3 pp per unit of `scaled_debt`) by at most 0.53pp at a 10% cut of a debt at the limit, 1.33pp at 25% and 2.13pp at 50% of a debt at 1.2 (the premium is capped at 4pp from 1.0), against tier premiums of 1.5, 4 and 6pp: every tier buys room under the limit at a higher rate for years. The cycle simulator models neither events nor the treasury's credit, so these were sized by that arithmetic, not by `banking_cycle_sim.py`. (2) **The conditional rescue**, `.45` option E: the donor offers option A's sum (`banking_rescue_offer_value`, the same AMOUNT / DAYS / CAP), and the partner decides in `banking_debt_events.2` (refusal the default). Signing (`banking_rescue_accept`) pays a third, adds Rescue Programme (−1pp, −5 legitimacy) and Restored Banking Confidence for two years and starts the programme in variables on the recipient (`banking_rescue_tranches_left`, `_review_months`, `_borrowing_months`, `_tranche`, `_donor`), so a revolution's winner carries it on. The recipient's banking pulse (`banking_rescue_programme_monthly`) counts the months it is `taking_loans`; each yearly review (`banking_rescue_review`) pays the next third only if that count is at most `banking_rescue_borrowing_months_allowed` (6), and otherwise suspends the programme (no more tranches, −20 relations, +2pp decaying over five years). A dead donor lapses it. `.3` and `.4` report each step to recipient and donor; `te_ea_bailout_can_be_asked` keeps a country under a running programme from being asked again. The UN's emergency loan (`un_events.35`) already lends on conditions and is unchanged. (3) **`.10` option B**, under the full system where `te_mon_can_monetise`: one monetisation step for a year (`te_mon_effect_bond_support`, see **Monetisation (§11)** below); the AI's weight on it drops by 25 above the High band edge.
- **Tuned by simulation.** `scripts/analysis/banking_cycle_sim.py` is a Monte-Carlo port of the monthly loop (reads every number from these files at import); `docs/audits/banking_cycle_simulation.md` is the study behind the 2026-09-22 retune — phase bubble adds ~×3, downturn/stagnation recovery ×3 plus a +0.5 climb, crash severity seeded from `banking_crash_severity_scale_value` (0.55) × bubble, a crash branch below cycle 40, the tools' standing momentum ×0.3 and negative bubble ×0.2 (then, the same day, §10's boom-rescue package: the three leaning tools — buffer, margin, moral suasion — back to ×0.6, frenzy's bubble add 20 → 8, the bubble-inertia curve flattened above 50 to reach +0.45 rather than +1.25 at bubble 100, and +0.2 bubble a month on fiat / digital; a maxed player now pulls back about a quarter to a third of booms and a few percent of frenzies — `--rescue`, `--rescue-entry 88`), the fiscal channel annualised and clamped, the stance clamp's loose side −4 → −2, the growth bias −1.0 → −0.25, players delegated by default, and the AI gates above. Re-run it before retuning any of those; the targets it was tuned to are in the study's §2.
- **Cooperative Ownership, measured (2026-10-05, study §21).** The interim cooperative arm posted on #720 found the cooperative cycle too calm on its own (0.6 crashes a century untooled) and two council tools doing all the booming. Shipped: Collective Capital Investment Plan and Credit Union Expansion momentum 0.30 / 0.25 → 0.10 / 0.08, the market tools' scale; cooperative bubble inertia at a fifth of the market's (`bubble_inertia_*_coop`, 0.2 momentum), which puts untooled cooperative crashes at about 2 a century (the owner's target); and the cooperative phases' `country_weekly_investment_pool_mult` read as a share of the pool's weekly gross income (`investment_pool_banking_cycle_income_add`), not of its balance, which compounded. Command economy's planning tools carry the same unretuned momentum scale (Emergency Resource Allocation +0.30, Central Distribution Upgrade +0.25) and are now measured by the command arm (study §23).
- **Simulator extensions (#717, #720).** `--economy market|command|coop` selects phases, inertia, economic-law volatility and tools; command money is administered at 3%, with no inflation or stance. Alternate buttons and hold gates are read from script and checked against an independent interpreter. `--hold-tools cw_capital_plan,cw_credit --points 8` models a player keeping those tools on. `--imported-crash-years 10` adds independent monthly foreign arrivals (off at 0), with crash/scare counts, ceiling coverage and drops reported separately from origin crashes. `--pool` models weekly income and capped private spending, including command auto-balancing and treasury transfers. - **The delegated bank (2026-09-25, study §12).** Three changes answer the delegated bank's overshoot: `te_mon_cycle_lean` reads the phase of `te_mon_cycle_outlook` (value + 4.69 × momentum) rather than today's phase; a mandate-run bank cuts 3× as fast while the cycle is below 40 (`te_mon_drift_step_down`, `te_mon_emergency_cuts`; hikes and the manual dial are unchanged; since #1084 the factor raises only the least step, a cut further than two points off moving a sixth of the distance anyway); and the growth mandate reacts to inflation above 4% at 1.0, not 0.5. The simulator never saw the overshoot because it had no standing inflation pressure: sweep `--wage-pressure` (labour laws grant +0.2 to +1.4pp) whenever you tune a mandate. Standing pressure still costs price stability a high rate and a near-stagnant cycle (§12 F15); that is a balance question, not a bank-logic one. For independent banks, §13 answers it: `law_central_bank_independence`'s per-National-Bank-level bonus is `country_inflation_anchoring_add` (+0.1pp a level), which `te_mon_pressure_anchoring` subtracts from the net positive wage + price pressure, never past zero (a flat negative pressure made the bank run loose and doubled crashes). It replaced −5% crash chance and −2% random momentum a level. The simulator's `--bank-level N` ports the institution; `--tune pre_anchoring` restores the old bonus.
- **Mandates set by law, and what a change costs (2026-10-07, #799, design §6).** Four mandates: **Inflation Targeting** (4, new: the dual mandate's rule with no cycle lean, `te_mon_mandate_inflation_lean_weight` = 0, and +0.1 to the credibility anchor c built over two years, `te_mon_mandate_cred`), the **Dual Mandate** (1, Price Stability renamed; rule unchanged), Growth (2) and Peg Defence (3). Under `law_central_bank_independence` the mandate is an amendment, `amendment_cb_inflation_targeting` or `amendment_cb_dual_mandate` (`common/amendments/extra_amendments.txt`), which step 2 follows after 12 months for AI and player alike (on a convertible gold standard the bank defends the peg instead); `te_mandate.1` (`events/te_mandate_events.txt`) asks for one whenever an independent dial country off gold has none (the law just passed, an old save, an act repealed in the law panel). Without independence the dashboard's buttons (ops 14 / 4 / 5 / 6) stay, but `te_monetary_settle_mandate_change` locks the mandate for 12 months after any change no statute made, and a looser rule (inflation targeting → dual → growth) adds +0.5pp to `te_inflation_expected` and `te_mon_mandate_loosened` (+0.5pp cyclical premium, fading over two years) per step. `banking_cycle_events.16`: B's Growth override is for a delegated bank only; C passes the Dual Mandate Act under independence (or moves a delegated bank), and is the old Monetary Accord where there is nothing to move. Simulation: `docs/audits/banking_cycle_simulation.md` §26.
- **The mandate bank's own asset purchases at the floor (2026-10-01, design §0.12).** A bank that runs its mandate (delegated, AI or independent) under `law_fiat_currency` or `law_digital_currency` (`te_mon_bank_can_buy_assets`) keeps a **virtual rate**, `te_mon_virtual_rate` / `te_mon_virtual_target`: where it would put its rate if the floor were not there. The target comes from the mandate formula with the real target's hysteresis (`te_monetary_update_virtual_target`, step 2c) and may go up to `te_mon_virtual_depth_max` (5; 2.5 until a playtest, design §0.12 "The cap") under the floor; the virtual rate drifts at the real rate's speeds and the real rate is the virtual one held at the floor (`te_monetary_drift_policy_rate`, step 3). `te_mon_pressure_bank_qe` = `min(te_mon_bank_qe_cap, te_mon_bank_qe_per_pp × (floor − virtual rate))` (5 / 1.0) joins `te_mon_pressure_total` beside OMO's flat +1.0, so the purchases grow and shrink a drift step at a time and are gone before the real rate leaves the floor. **The mandate sets the condition** (`te_mon_bank_may_go_under_floor`): price stability may only go under the floor while last month's headline is under the anchor, growth always may. `te_monetary_settle_virtual_rate` (step 1d) resets the virtual pair to the real one every month unless the bank is part-way through buying. It exists because independence forbids monetisation and both mandates are pinned at the floor, so a deflating independent country had only OMO to climb out on. It costs no points and no cash, and a manual dial gets none (it has the monetisation stepper; ruling N1, resolved pending playtests); the Delegation tooltip says so while a manual fiat/digital dial sits on its floor with prices falling (`te_mon_delegation_purchase_hint` custom loc). The simulator ports it (`bank_qe_pressure`, `settle_virtual_rate`, `update_virtual_target`); `--tune pre_bank_qe` switches it off (`docs/audits/banking_cycle_simulation.md` §15). Both purchases are counted outside `country_inflation_pressure_add`, because `te_mon_pressure_anchoring` nets Inflation Anchoring against that channel; an **Asset Purchases** dashboard row (`banking_mon_has_purchases`, under the Anchoring row) prints them instead. `test_monetary_bank_qe.py` pins the script, the effects, the port, the row's wiring and the tooltip figures.

- **Revolutions (2026-09-26, #465).** The entry is `can_revolution_inherit` and cannot deactivate, so its `immediate` runs on the first activation and again on the copy a revolution's winner inherits. That copy arrives after the loser's variables are merged in, with **no modifiers** (`scripting_best_practices.md` § "What a Civil War's Winner Inherits"). So `immediate` seeds `finance_cycle_value` / `finance_cycle_momentum` / `bubble_pressure` (50 / 0 / 0, through `banking_cycle_start` since 2026-10-17, and only for a country that meets the start test) only when they are absent, and the winner carries on the loser's cycle. The rebels hold no entry while they are revolutionary (`te_banking_entry_expected`), so they can't start a fresh cycle whose values would take precedence at the win; before this a win reset a panic to a stable 50 (civil-war audit F6). It zeroes `te_mon_stance_band_applied` unguarded, so next month's swap puts the stance band back (F7), and draws the bars from the variables through `banking_cycle_update_progress_bars`. Every other modifier on the entry is either rebuilt statelessly each month (phase and bubble-inertia modifiers, `financial_cycle_government_fiscal_policy_effect`, `planning_treasury_pool_balance`) or a tool the player switched on, which the winner loses (F8, open). A one-time save migration in `banking_cycle_apply_stance_band` (marker `banking_stance_band_healed`, remove after one release) heals saves in which the band was already lost.

### Policy Dashboard
`gui/journal_entry_widgets/banking_layout_widget.gui` sets one order for the journal entry and the Budget tab (style-guide pass, 2026-09-29, in the UN's shape): the **overview** (the entry's bars, then the readings as icon cells: phase, momentum, bubble pressure, policy stance, price band, budget; icons from PR #586, recorded in `docs/systems/banking_gui_icons.md`; next month's ranges under the first row, below) in `custom_widget_container_1`; the live sections, **Active Policies**, **Monetary Policy** and **Available Interventions**, in `_2`; and the reference sections, the **History** charts and a collapsed **How Banking Works**, in `_3`. The section types live in `banking_dashboard_widget.gui` and `banking_history_widget.gui`.

**Next month's ranges (2026-10-07).** Under each of the overview's first-row cells a third line (`te_banking_ov_change`) gives how far the cycle value, momentum and bubble pressure can move at the next monthly pulse, as a range: `banking_disp_<reading>_change_lo` / `_hi` in `banking_overview_display_values.txt`. They replaced the status text's three monthly modifier totals (`BANKING_CURR_MODIFIERS`), which the Budget tab never showed and which left out the stance, the decay and the nudge; each total, with the engine's breakdown, is now the first line of its range's tooltip (`banking_dash_<reading>_change_tt`). A range is built only from what the panels show, never from the hidden figures: the momentum band's edges (±1, ±3, ±5), the stance band widened by `te_mon_forecast_error_bound` either way (so it leaks nothing of r\*), the three modifier totals, and the nudge, which only pulls towards 50 (`value =` replaces a `random_list` weight, so above 50 it can only push momentum down). The cycle value's range takes next month's momentum, after the decay and pushes, so in a Steady band with nothing else moving it is about −0.9 to +0.9. Overheating (+5 and up) leaves the cycle value's range no top and momentum's no bottom, Freefall (−5 or less) the reverse; `banking_disp_<reading>_change_shape` picks "low to high", "low or more", "high or less" or a single figure. A bank holiday's floor and a full holiday's reopening bounce are counted; crashes and events are not. `test_banking_monthly_change.py` runs `banking_cycle_bank_holiday_reopen` and `banking_cycle_advance_variables` through a small interpreter over sampled hidden states and checks every outcome lands inside the range and every finite end is reached, with the stance thresholds read from `te_monetary_update_stance`, so retuning the decay, a push rate or a band edge fails it until the display values follow.

**Seven momentum bands (2026-10-07).** Freefall (−5 or less), Collapsing (−5 to −3), Falling, Steady, Rising, Surging (+3 to +5), Overheating (+5 and up): the old Collapsing and Surging were open-ended from ±3, so the next-month ranges read "or more" / "or less" whenever momentum passed ±3, which the cycle simulator puts at 9–27% of months (fiat, 8 points or none). Past ±5, the ends of the momentum bar and the deepest crash tier's momentum, it is 0.1–0.5%. The ±3 edges, which the crash-risk badge and the AI's tool triggers act on (`banking_cycle_bubble_risk_rising` / `_risk_falling`), are unchanged, so "Surging" in those texts now reads "Surging or Overheating". The code (`banking_disp_momentum_band_code`, 1–7), the band word, the edge words (`banking_dash_momentum_band_edges`) and the range values all move together; `BandCodeTest` and `test_banking_monthly_change.py` hold them to each other. Freefall and Overheating draw red triple arrows of the mod's own, since vanilla's stop at the double (`banking_gui_icons.md`).

**Single source of truth.** Every button's `possible` and `effect` body lives in a named helper — `banking_possible_<button>` in `common/scripted_triggers/banking_policy_triggers.txt`, `banking_effect_<button>` in `common/scripted_effects/banking_policy_effects.txt` — and both the `scripted_button` and the dashboard's scripted GUI call it. `visible`/`possible` on both halves of every enable/disable pair read the `banking_tool_*_active` family in `market_triggers.txt`. **If you add or retune a policy, edit the helper, never the button or the scripted GUI body.**

Precedent: `common/journal_entries/je_covert_warfare.txt`. The rule above still protects every discrete toggle button, and all of them stay. The AI branch carries no gate the player branch lacks (the orphan-gate trap in `scripting_best_practices.md` § "Scripted Button Gating").

### External & Currency tools (2026-09-27)

External & Currency now contains the existing Outflow Controls, **Export Credit Facility** (moved from Directed Credit), and five new tools. All five need the full monetary system and use the usual shared eligibility/effect, AI button, active/available row, history and law-cleanup registration. The two compulsory exchange controls respect the existing capital-controls law lock (with its wartime exception). Their numeric costs and stock/flow formulas live in `common/script_values/banking_external_values.txt`; `banking_external_effects.txt` settles them once per monetary monthly pulse, after anchor discovery and before the premium. Reserve purchases follow the gold step so vault initialization cannot erase paid reserves.

| Tool | Points | Mechanism and cost |
|---|---:|---|
| Restrict Speculative Inflows | 2 | Requires a dial. Halves positive gold inflows and positive FX carry; never slows exits. Small bureaucracy, momentum and bubble-pressure reductions. |
| Sterilize Capital Inflows | 2 | Requires convertible gold with a dial. Cuts positive gold-flow inflation pressure by 75%, leaves outflow deflation and hot-money liabilities unchanged. Costs 0.01% of annual GDP a month, charged a quarter a week as a government expense (`banking_external_sterilization_charge`, since 2026-10-27; was a monthly `add_treasury`); slightly restrains credit and bubbles. Ends if the treasury cannot cover a month. |
| Limit Foreign-Currency Borrowing | 2 | Requires a national bank. Builds up to 50% protection against the existing FX-debt premium over 24 months; protection unwinds over 24 months after repeal. Adds 0.25pp risk premium and slightly restrains credit while active. |
| Foreign Exchange Surrender Requirement | 2 | Requires convertible gold and exports. Treasury-funded vault purchases, capped by room, available cash and 0.05% of annual GDP, scaled by export-reliance bands. No hot-money liability. Export advantage −10%, bureaucracy −3%. |
| Emergency Import Financing | 3 | Requires a national bank and financial crisis (war alone is insufficient). General import advantage +15%; costs 0.05% of annual GDP a month, rising to 0.10% over 12 funded months, charged a quarter a week as a government expense (`banking_external_import_charge`, since 2026-10-27). Stops when the crisis ends or funding fails. Fatigue falls one month per inactive month; toggling does not reset it. |

**Abstractions and accounting.** The engine exposes general import advantage rather than an essential-goods credit allocation, so import financing supports all imports and does not guarantee goods or overcome a blockade. Surrender uses market export reliance bands (zero / up to 10% / up to 30% / above 30% → 0 / ¼ / ½ / full purchase cap), not a measured exporter FX balance. Cash spending is conservatively reserved across simultaneous tools; purchases debit the exact snapshot credited to the vault, which is why the surrender purchase stayed a monthly `add_treasury` when the other two bills became weekly charges (owner, 2026-10-27): a weekly expense would pay a different sum at a different time, into debt if the cash ran out. The two charges are refreshed monthly at the month's cost ÷ 4, so a year of them is 13 months' worth (`banking_external_charge_*`, whose header has the rules). Sterilization has a funded flag so deferred modifier removal cannot grant unpaid pressure relief. Protection/fatigue stocks are initialized only if absent and are not reset on toggles or inherited journal entries. Regime-ineligible policies are lifted automatically; a reduced intervention budget sheds these tools after the existing crash interventions.

**Validation.** `test_banking_external_tools.py` executes actual script formulas and effects against scenario inputs, including deferred treasury/modifier writes. The cycle simulator ports inflow restriction and sterilization; funding is assumed there, as it has no liquid-treasury model. Its FX index remains at par, so borrowing limits, surrender and import finance are registered but excluded from AI selection in that simulator; their accounting/lifecycle is covered by the script scenarios. In-game balance and presentation still need a play-test.

### Policy tools added 2026-09-23
Seven tools, designed in `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-09-23-banking-tools-expansion-design.md` and sized with the simulator (`docs/audits/banking_cycle_simulation.md` §11). All are ordinary `cb_*` pairs — helper, button, handler pair, rows, loc, simulator entry — so everything in **Policy Dashboard** above applies.

- **Directed credit sectors** — `cb_directed_credit_heavy_industry`, `_agriculture`, `_armaments`, `_electrification` (Electrification, gated on `rural_electrification` by `country_can_use_directed_credit_electrification_bool`). Each shares Infrastructure's activation cost and `country_banking_lock_directed_credit_bool`, but since 2026-09-30 they are five profiles rather than one template (see **Directed-credit sector profiles** below). `bg_high_tech` sits under `bg_heavy_industry`, so Heavy Industry covers high-tech buildings too; Electrification lists only `bg_power` so the two do not double-count.
- **One sector at a time.** `banking_directed_credit_slots_free` (`extra_script_values.txt`) = 1 + `country_directed_credit_sectors_add` − sectors running; every sector's `possible`, Infrastructure's included, requires it above 0 (`banking_dc_sector_cap_tt`). Directed Credit & Development Banks grants `+1`. Step 6b of the monthly pulse sheds the excess after a law change. The sector count is read once into a local variable there, because a `remove_modifier` is invisible to the modifier reads it is built from until the block ends.
- **Directed-credit sector profiles (2026-09-30).** Price, cycle signature and a second effect differ per sector; the table is also the comment block above `banking_directed_credit_infrastructure` in `extra_modifiers.txt`, and the simulator reads points and both monthly lines from those modifiers.

  | Sector | Points | Momentum / bubble per month | Beyond the +10% construction line | Liability |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Infrastructure | 3 | 0.03 / 0.1 | `state_infrastructure_mult` +5%, decree cost −10% | Industrialists −3; the weakest rescue |
  | Heavy Industry | 4 | 0.06 / 0.5 | heavy-industry throughput +5% | `country_greenhouse_gas_emissions_mult` +5% (read by `gw_emission_multiplier_script_value`); Landowners −3 |
  | Agriculture | 2 | 0.03 / 0.4 | `state_food_security_add` +5% | the worst momentum for its bubble |
  | Armaments | 3 | 0.06 / 0.05 | military-industry throughput +5%, `country_military_goods_cost_mult` −10% | Intelligentsia −3 |
  | Electrification | 1 | 0.04 / 0.2 | `goods_output_electricity_mult` +5% | covers power plants only; era-gated (`rural_electrification`) |

  The Directed Credit law's budget is 5 (6 with a national bank), so at a flat 3 its second sector did not fit until the national bank and then used every point. Now Electrification (1) fits beside any sector, Agriculture (2) beside Infrastructure or Armaments, and the sixth point adds Infrastructure + Armaments and Heavy Industry + Agriculture; Heavy Industry never sits beside Infrastructure or Armaments. A static modifier cannot vary with war, so Armaments' liability is a flat Intelligentsia −3, not one that eases in wartime. Point cost is `country_banking_intervention_max_add` on the modifier and the `BANKING_POINTS_n` gate in `banking_possible_cb_directed_credit_*`; keep the two equal, and the dashboard's `banking_dash_cost_cb_*` label and tooltip `Requires:` line in `te_miscellaneous_l_english.yml`.
- **The AI picks a sector by government, from one shared weight.** All five sectors' enable buttons score `banking_dc_ai_weight` (Infrastructure's pre-expansion `ai_chance`), split among the new sectors that have an affinity (`banking_dc_affinity_*`: Industrialists / Landowners or Rural Folk / Armed Forces or at war / Intelligentsia in government) and could be started (`banking_dc_ai_candidate_*`); with none, Infrastructure takes it all. The total weight is therefore the same whoever governs. How often the AI actually presses depends on what it can afford: its button runs the same `banking_effect_cb_*` as a player's, so a sector costs it the same intervention points, and the cheap sectors (Electrification 1 point, Agriculture 2) are affordable at budgets a 3-point sector was not. With the lift weights unchanged the AI pressed them far more often and crashes rose (`banking_cycle_simulation.md` §14, F19; F11 found directed credit to be the AI's riskiest tool). Every sector's disable button therefore adds 40 in Stable (momentum not falling) and 50 in Expansion, where it added 20 and 30; `--tune dc_lift_boost=0` shows the old weights. A lower enable weight is a further lever (untried).
- **Reserve Requirements** (`cb_reserve_requirements`, Prudential) — a leaning tool with no tech: bubble and momentum down, capitalists' pool efficiency −5%, and `country_inflation_pressure_add` −0.5pp (read by the §9.1 pressure sum, so it shows in the dashboard's Price Pressure breakdown). AI: the buffer's weights, +15 while the buffer is still locked, and ×0 while the buffer could be bought instead — the lean before the buffer's tech and a second lean beside it, never a weaker stand-in (simulation study §11).
- **Bank Holiday** (`cb_bank_holiday`, Crisis) — panic or downturn only, no tech. Adds `banking_bank_holiday` to the journal entry **timed, `days = 90`** — the first timed tool modifier in the system; `banking_tool_bank_holiday_active` is `has_modifier`, so the row and the Active Policies entry disappear on expiry, and no "withdrawn" history marker is written then (only on an early Disable). **The run stops (2026-10-05, `banking_cycle_simulation.md` §19):** declaring it sets a negative momentum to 0, and while the modifier is on `banking_cycle_advance_variables` keeps momentum at 0 or above, after every other push and before momentum is applied. **A full term reopens with a bounce:** declaring it also sets `banking_bank_holiday_reopening`, and `banking_cycle_bank_holiday_reopen` (in the monthly pulse, just before `banking_cycle_advance_variables`) adds `banking_bank_holiday_reopen_momentum` (+1) once the modifier has expired and the variable is still there. Every early ending clears the variable first: the Disable action, a change of economic system (`remove_banking_market_modifiers_effect`) and a revolution (the entry's `immediate`, since a winner inherits the variable but not the modifier). `banking_bank_holiday_reopen_tt` and `_forfeit_tt` quote +1 literally, and `test_banking_bank_holiday.py` holds them to the constant. +2 was measured too: under a growth target it roughly doubled the chance of another crash within three years of the holiday (22.6% → 43.5%), and under the gold peg it nearly quadrupled it (7.9% → 30.5%); +1 lands the median slump between 52 and 61 at eighteen months. The old one-shot halving is `--tune pre_holiday` in the simulator. It also sets the five-year cooldown variable `banking_bank_holiday_cooldown` (`days = 1825`) on the country. Declaring it radicalises depositors by strata, middle hardest: `banking_bank_holiday_middle_radicals` (0.05), `_upper_radicals` (0.03) and `_lower_radicals` (0.02) — three `add_radicals` calls, since `strata` takes one value per call (0.02 of the middle strata alone until 2026-09-27). They are the Bank Holiday's own constants, so retuning the shared toggle-cost constants cannot move it. The dashboard row's tooltip states the three figures literally (`banking_bank_holiday_radicals_tt`), so change the two together; event `.31`'s option A adds its own `small_radicals` on the upper strata only in the `else` of its tool check, when the holiday could not be declared, so the tool's radicals are never charged twice. Its crash-chance line shields against contagion too, because `banking_contagion_crash_check` multiplies by the same `banking_crash_chance_multiplier_value`; in practice that is all it does, since the holiday is only declared below cycle 25, where a country's own crash weight is `(bubble − 90) × 0.01` and a slump drains bubble 3–7 a month. Since #715 a contagion crash can only deepen a slump, so the shield matters.
- **Bail-in Regime** (`cb_bail_in_regime`, Crisis) — `globalization`, 3 points, no treasury cost; half Asset Relief's cycle-value push, bubble down, risk premium +0.1pp, upper-strata radicals on enabling. **Mutually exclusive with Asset Relief** — each `possible` requires the other inactive. No law lock: Unregulated Banking cannot afford 3 points.

### Monthly Pulse Architecture
The `on_monthly_pulse` calls these scripted effects in order (all in `common/scripted_effects/banking_cycle_effects.txt` unless noted), only while the cycle runs (`te_banking_cycle_running`). A dormant entry's pulse only checks `te_banking_cycle_unlocked` and, once it holds, calls `banking_cycle_start` and posts `banking_cycle_started_notice` to a human player; its weekly pulse does nothing.
0. **Save migration one-shot** (inline in `je_banking.txt`) — strips a leftover `banking_policy_rate_hike` modifier from `je:je_banking_cycle`. Guarded on `has_modifier`, so it is a no-op on every save but the first migrating month. Removal checklist: **Monetary Policy (phase 1)** in [`monetary_policy.md`](monetary_policy.md).
1. **`banking_cycle_update_fiscal_policy`** — re-applies fiscal policy modifier based on deficit/surplus relative to GDP: `financial_cycle_government_fiscal_policy_effect_size` is 0.1 cycle points a month per 1% of GDP of deficit (so a 10%-of-GDP deficit reaches +1), annualised through `te_mon_deficit_annualise_factor` (weekly flows, yearly `gdp`) and clamped ±1. Until 2026-09-22 it skipped the annualisation and was 1/52 of its comment's claim; the annualised version first shipped at 0.25 (+1 at 4%) and was cut to 0.1 (`banking_cycle_simulation.md` §6 follow-up).
2. **`banking_cycle_advance_variables`** — momentum decay, modifier-driven changes, the monetary-stance push (`te_mon_stance_momentum_add` / `te_mon_stance_bubble_add`, −0.125 and −0.75 per pp of tight stance gap, gap clamped −2…+4 by `te_mon_stance_gap_clamp_loose` / `_tight` — never rendered as a number, per `monetary_policy_design.md` §8), investment pool, random mean-reversion nudge (scaled by `banking_random_nudge_down/up_value`), apply momentum to value, clamp all variables.
3. **`banking_cycle_check_and_execute_crash`** — asymmetric crash probability check based on bubble pressure × phase (a branch per phase band, including one below cycle 40 — without it the weight fell through as raw bubble), scaled by `banking_crash_chance_multiplier_value`; severity is `banking_crash_severity_scale_value` (0.55) × bubble × a 0.5–2.0 roll, so the median crash lands in the 20–40 tier rather than resetting to panic. If crash triggers, fires origin event (.6) and spreads contagion via `banking_cycle_spread_contagion`. **A crash never raises cycle value or momentum (2026-10-05).** The tier figures (5/−5 … 40/−1) are ceilings: `apply_banking_crash_origin_effects` lowers each through `banking_crash_lower_cycle_to` / `_lower_momentum_to` and leaves a figure already under the tier alone — a plain `set_variable` lifted a Downturn country (cycle 12, momentum −3.5) to 40 / −1 when a Market Correction reached it, and contagion reaches any cycle value. Anything that *adds* after the ceilings, or clamps, is bracketed by `banking_crash_note_prior_state` / `banking_crash_hold_to_prior_state` (hidden; they record the figures in `banking_crash_prior_value` / `_momentum` and put either back if the option left it higher): the ten softening responses (`apply_banking_crash_softening_option_effect`), the inline capital-controls option of event .6, and `apply_banking_contagion_effects`, whose scare floors (cycle 5, momentum −5) also pulled low countries up. A new crash option that touches either figure must go through one of these; `test_banking_crash_no_raise.py` runs every option of events .6 and .7 over a grid of states and fails on a raise. Out of scope on purpose: the modifiers a response or the Great Depression leaves behind (`banking_crisis_*`, `banking_crash_intervention_*`) act month by month through the ordinary monthly pulse, and they are the recovery the options buy.
3b. **`te_history_record_banking_samples`** (`common/scripted_effects/te_history_banking_effects.txt`) — writes the month's cycle value, momentum and bubble pressure into the history store (**History Store and Charts**, below). Deliberately placed after the crash check so a crash month's bar shows the post-crash readings beside its crash marker.
4. **`banking_cycle_apply_phase_modifiers`** — removes old phase + bubble-inertia modifiers, applies current ones based on cycle value and economic law type.
4b. **`banking_cycle_apply_stance_band`** — swaps the `banking_stance_band_1..5` JE modifier (the investment-pool side of the monetary stance) when the displayed band changes. Decides from `te_mon_stance_band` vs a stored `te_mon_stance_band_applied`, never from `has_modifier`; band 3 is an empty modifier that is still applied, so the modifier list always names the stance. A stored 0 means "none recorded": the swap then removes all five before adding. `immediate` sets 0 on every activation, including a revolution's inherited copy, which has no modifiers. See **Monetary Policy (phase 1)** in [`monetary_policy.md`](monetary_policy.md), and `docs/systems/monetary_policy_design.md` §8.
5. **`banking_cycle_update_progress_bars`** — updates the 4 JE progress bars (uses `scope:journal_entry`; the policy-stance bar only once the monetary system's `te_mon_stance_bar_pos` exists). Also called from the entry's `immediate`.
6. **`banking_cycle_cleanup_capital_controls`** — removes capital controls modifier if law/war conditions no longer apply.
6b. **`banking_directed_credit_enforce_cap`** — lifts directed-credit sectors, newest first, while more are running than `banking_directed_credit_slots_free` allows (a law change away from Directed Credit & Development Banks). See **Policy tools added 2026-09-23**.
7. **`banking_cycle_update_ce_pool_balance`** — under `law_command_economy`, recalibrates the `planning_treasury_pool_balance` modifier on the JE so investment-pool net monthly income targets zero (state treasury funds all investment directly). Also called from `ce_invest_pool_inject` / `ce_invest_pool_withdraw` button effects.

### The `banking_system_simplified` game rule

`banking_system_rule` has **three** settings, not two (`common/game_rules/extra_game_rules.txt`).
`banking_system_simplified` keeps the cycle this file describes and switches off phases 1–5 of
[Monetary Policy](monetary_policy.md). The player still gets the cycle, its crashes, the prudential /
command / cooperative tools, the financial-regulation laws and the history charts; they get no
policy-rate dial, no inflation, no exchange rate, and no pegs, swap lines, guarantees or shared
currency. Interest is still country-specific — the world reference rate plus the risk premium —
they simply do not steer it.

**One trigger does all of it: `te_mon_full_system`** (`common/scripted_triggers/te_monetary_triggers.txt`,
`has_game_rule = banking_system_enabled`, scope-free). `te_banking_system_on`
(`banking_policy_triggers.txt`) is its counterpart for the *cycle*: true for enabled **and**
simplified, and what the journal entry and the history store ask.

**What the rule reaches:**

| Site | Behaviour when `te_mon_full_system = no` |
|---|---|
| `te_mon_has_dial` | false for every country — one line, and every consumer (steps 2/4, the dashboard block, the OMO gate, the conditions' stance row, the union principles' `ai_weight`) falls to its no-dial side |
| `te_monetary_monthly_update` | steps 0, 0b, 1, 4, 5, 7 and 9 only. 1b, 1c, 5b, 6, 6c, 6d, 8, 8b, 9b and 10 are skipped, so their variables keep step 0's neutral seeds (`te_inflation` 0, `te_fx_index` par, `te_mon_anchor_kind` 0, `te_mon_stance_gap` 0) and every downstream read gets a sane answer rather than a missing variable |
| `te_monetary_set_derived_rate` | the **bankless spread is conditional**: normally every country here is bankless, but with the dial off a country that built a national bank would otherwise be charged 1pp for not having one |
| `te_monetary_refresh_world_rate` | short-circuits to the era base — with no dial anywhere the accumulator would reach its own `else` after two ~200-country scans |
| on-action hooks | the phase-4 world-inflation refresh, the phase-5 anchored-GDP scan, the yearly arrangement scan, `on_become_subject` and the `on_country_default` guarantee call are all skipped |
| `banking_possible_cb_open_market_ops` | drops the regime and rate-floor conditions — OMO reverts to its pre-phase-1 gate (unlock bool, 4 points, law lock). The `on_law_enacted` auto-switch-off is gated to match |
| `cb_capital_controls_outflow` (+ disable) | `ai_chance` falls back to the pre-phase-1 cycle rule; the external-crisis rule reads its neutral inputs and would never fire |
| `te_mon_effect_fx_shock_tt` | the wrapper the ten event options call: no shock **and no tooltip line**, so no option promises a currency move that cannot happen |
| treaty articles 110–112, `principle_monetary_union_1..5` | `visible = no` |
| dashboard / history GUI | the Monetary Policy readout block is hidden; open-market operations fall back to a plain row under the same header, drawn for a market economy only (moral suasion now lives in Prudential Regulation); the inflation and exchange-rate charts are hidden and not sampled |

**`banking_system_disabled` takes the same path through the monetary layer** — `te_mon_full_system`
is false for it too, and it always should have been: with the journal entry gone there was no UI
for the dial, the bands or the hyperinflation chain, but the whole simulation still ran behind it.

**Known roughness.** Five script-only modifier types are granted from `modifier = { }` blocks
that take no trigger, so they still render on law tooltips under the simplified rule with
nothing consuming them: `country_wage_pressure_add`, `country_policy_rate_drift_speed_mult`,
`country_policy_rate_floor_add`, `country_bank_forecast_error_add`,
`country_inflation_pressure_add`. See `monetary_policy_design.md` §0.9.

**What the rule deliberately does *not* reach.** The cancel-`INJECT`s (`base_values`' flat 20%,
the six rank multipliers, laissez-faire, the five finance techs — §16.1) are file-level merges no
game rule can switch off, so the passive rate stack runs under all three settings. Switching it
off as well would leave every country borrowing at 0%.

### Banking Law Modifiers
Two `script_only` modifier types allow laws (and potentially techs, PMs, etc.) to tune the banking cycle:

| Modifier | Effect | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `country_banking_random_momentum_mult` | Scales the random nudge that drives cycle volatility. +50% = 50% larger random swings. | percent, neutral |
| `country_banking_crash_chance_mult` | Scales crash probability when bubble pressure is high. +25% = 25% higher crash chance. | percent, bad |

**Current law values:**

| Law | Volatility | Crash Likelihood | Capitalist Inv Pool |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unregulated Banking | +50% | +25% | — |
| Free & Mutual Banking | — | -10% | — |
| Universal Light Prudence | +10% | — | — |
| Prudential Narrow Banking | -60% | -50% | -10% efficiency |
| Directed Credit Dev Banks | -20% | -20% | — |
| State-Owned Banking | -35% | -35% | -10% contribution |
| Central Bank Independence | -10% | — | — |

Directed Credit & Development Banks also grants `country_directed_credit_sectors_add = 1` — a second directed-credit sector at once (see **Policy tools added 2026-09-23**).

State-Owned Banking (unlocked by `law_command_economy`) also carries `country_government_dividends_efficiency_add` +10%, `country_weekly_innovation_mult` −5% and `country_credit_standing_add` +0.5pp (the least credible arrangement for a lender).

Central Bank Independence has had no crash-likelihood line since 2026-09-25: its per-level `institution_modifier` (on the National Bank) is now **Inflation Anchoring**, `country_inflation_anchoring_add` +0.1pp a level, which replaced −5% crash chance and −2% random momentum a level (the simulator found that pair almost worthless; `docs/audits/banking_cycle_simulation.md` §13). The law's flat −10% volatility stays.

Other laws also carry these two modifiers: the monetary laws (`law_gold_standard` −10% volatility, `law_digital_currency` +50%, `law_decentralized_cryptocurrency` +75% volatility and −15% crash), the economic-system injections in `construction_system_law_injections.txt` (`law_cooperative_ownership` −25%, `law_command_economy` −50% volatility), and `law_command_cooperative_economy` (−25% / −25%). `law_algorithmic_governance` carried −25% volatility until 2026-10-01; it was dropped because it reads as machine rule taking over the central bank's job, which made the monetary laws moot.

(`state_capitalists_investment_pool_efficiency_mult` is a vanilla per-pop modifier; Prudential is the only banking law that uses it. State-Owned Banking cuts capitalists' investment pool *contribution* instead. CBI gets the opposite signal at scale through `institution_national_bank` (+5%/level for capitalists in National Bank states).)

**Script values** (in `extra_script_values.txt`):
- `banking_random_nudge_down_value` — base -1, scaled by `(1 + country_banking_random_momentum_mult)`. Used in the random nudge step.
- `banking_random_nudge_up_value` — base +1, scaled the same way.
- `banking_crash_chance_multiplier_value` — base 1.0, adds `country_banking_crash_chance_mult`, clamped to min 0. Multiplied into crash weight.
- `banking_{crash,contagion}_{chance,severity}_economy_mult` — law-specific damping, shared with the simulator: origin chance 1 / 0.35 / 0.65, severity 1 / 0.6 / 0.8; imported chance 1 / 0.20 / 0.55, severity 1 / 0.45 / 0.75 (market / command / cooperative).

**To add banking volatility/crash modifiers from other sources** (technologies, PMs, etc.), simply add `country_banking_random_momentum_mult = 0.1` or `country_banking_crash_chance_mult = -0.15` to the modifier block.

**Per-tool law gating.** Most `cb_*` buttons are tier-unlocked by `country_banking_intervention_max_add` (a threshold per button — see the `possible` blocks in `timeline_extended_scripted_buttons.txt`). On top of the tier gate, every banking law publishes its tool restrictions via `country_banking_lock_<tool>_bool` modifiers (defined in `banking_cycle_modifier_types.txt`). The lock entries appear as red "Locks: …" lines in the law's modifier-block tooltip, and each `cb_*` button gates on a `custom_tooltip { modifier:country_banking_lock_<tool>_bool = no }` check that surfaces the reason as "Tool not available under your current banking law" when the button is greyed. To add a new law-based restriction: add `country_banking_lock_<tool>_bool = yes` to the law's modifier block — no edits to scripted_buttons needed. Crisis-only tools (`cb_emergency_liquidity_program`) remain available regardless of law as Bagehot-style lender-of-last-resort. `cb_capital_controls_outflow` has a war override (`is_at_war = yes` bypasses the lock).

**Toggle costs.** Each `cb_*` button has friction on toggle to discourage rapid cycling, calibrated to its real-world parallel: monetary tools (OMO, asset relief, directed credit, e-liquidity, export credit) cost treasury via `banking_*_activation_cost` script values scaled to GDP; political tools (countercyclical buffer, raise margin requirements, deposit guarantee) generate radicals/loyalists via `banking_small_radicals_value` (0.02, vanilla `small_radicals`) and `banking_medium_radicals_value` (0.03, between vanilla small and medium), defined in `extra_script_values.txt` to allow tuning banking-button friction independently of global event balance (the Bank Holiday has its own three constants: see its bullet under § Policy tools added 2026-09-23); capital controls cost infamy and -2/-3 relations with great-power trade partners. Moral suasion is the lone exception — speeches have no real-world toggle cost.

### Contagion GDP Scaling
Contagion spread depends on relative GDP sizes (script values in `extra_script_values.txt`):
- **`banking_contagion_min_source_gdp`** — 5% of target country's GDP; source must meet this threshold for contagion to be possible, so a country more than 20 times the crashing economy's size is immune (1% until 2026-09-27, i.e. 100 times). The gate runs at every hop of a cascade (`minor_events_timelineextended.7`'s `after` re-enters `banking_cycle_spread_contagion`), so small-to-large hops drop out of a crisis wave too.
- **GDP chance tiers** — `banking_cycle_spread_contagion`'s roll (base 25) adds +5 when the source is at least the target's size, and a further +10 / +15 / +20 at 2× / 5× / 10× (`banking_contagion_gdp_threshold_2x` / `_5x` / `_10x`): +50 in all for a source ten times the target. An eligible source smaller than the target gets no GDP bonus, only the channel and severity adds. The weight is tiers rather than a ratio because a script value cannot be a `random` modifier's `add`.
- **Scare only for a year (owner, 2026-10-17)** — every crash, an origin's (`banking_cycle_check_and_execute_crash`) or a contagion's (`banking_contagion_crash_check`), sets `banking_crashed_last_year` (365 days, `banking_mark_crashed_last_year`) beside `banking_recent_crash`, and while it is set `banking_contagion_crash_check` skips its roll and sets `contagion_crash = 0`: the event plays its scare text and options and marks the country `banking_crisis_wave_scared`. A crash zeroes the bubble, but the roll's crash weight is bubble pressure plus 5–30 from the origin's severity against 100 for a scare, about 5–23% at zero bubble in a market economy, so without the marker a country whose banks had just failed, an origin a later wave reached among them, could crash again within weeks. The marker goes on where the crash is decided, not in `apply_banking_crash_origin_effects`, which runs in the crash events' options up to three months later. A country with a low bubble that has not crashed keeps the 5–30 floor.
- **A scare is shown once in two years (owner, 2026-10-22: "if a spread happens and it does not trigger a crash, do not fire the relevant event unless it has been at least 2 years since the last time it fired")** — the roll no longer happens in event .7's `immediate`: that is too late to keep a popup from opening. `banking_contagion_reach` sends the hidden event `minor_events_timelineextended.9` six days out, and its `banking_contagion_decide` runs `banking_contagion_crash_check`, then sends .7 a day on (so the popup still lands a week after the reach) for a crash, always, with `contagion_crashed` saved so the title the engine reads at queue time is already right, or for a scare the country has not been shown in two years (`banking_contagion_scare_cooling` is false). Sending a scare sets `banking_contagion_scare_cooldown` (timed, 730 days); a crash neither sets it nor waits for it. A scare inside the cooldown ends in the hidden event: no popup, no cycle drop or radicals, no restart of the clock. The roll still marked the country `banking_crisis_wave_scared`, and `banking_contagion_decide` removes `banking_contagion_pending` and the roll's leftovers (`banking_contagion_clear_stale`) since no .7 will. Event .7's `immediate` rolls only when `contagion_crash` is unset, for a copy an older build sent. The simulator mirrors it (`scare_cooling`, `silenced_scares`).

### Event After Blocks
Banking events (81 of the 83 blocks in `banking_cycle_events.txt` — all but the bailout-appeal pair `.68`/`.69` — plus `.6`/`.7` in `minor_events.txt` and `society_technology_events.30`) have `after = { banking_cycle_post_event_refresh = yes }` blocks that re-apply phase modifiers and update progress bars immediately after the player's choice.

### Other Scripted Effects
- **`banking_cycle_random_event_effect`** — cooldown-gated random event dispatcher. A draw sets `banking_event_cooldown` to 18 months (20, 24 or 30 on five of the draws); nothing is drawn in a crash month.
- **`remove_all_banking_phase_modifiers`** — removes all 24 phase modifiers (6 market + 6 cmd + 6 coop + 3 market and 3 cooperative bubble inertia).
- **`apply_banking_crash_origin_effects`** — sets cycle vars based on crash severity (called from event .6 options).
- **`apply_banking_contagion_effects`** — subtracts from cycle vars based on origin severity (called from event .7 options).
- **`banking_cycle_post_event_refresh`** — combines `banking_cycle_apply_phase_modifiers` + bar update via `je:je_banking_cycle` accessor (for event context where `scope:journal_entry` is unavailable).
