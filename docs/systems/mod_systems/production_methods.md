# Production Methods

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Production Methods (PMs)

- PM group icons are displayed in several GUI panels: `production_methods.gui`, `building_browser_panel.gui`, `building_details_panel.gui`, `goods_state_panel.gui`.
- The mod adds a `pmg_maintenance` PM group that should be **hidden** from all PM icon displays using `visible = "[Not(EqualTo_string(ProductionMethodGroup.GetKey, 'pmg_maintenance'))]"`.
- PM icon displays use `scrollarea` + `flowcontainer` (not `fixedgridbox`) so hidden items collapse properly and scrollbars appear when > 4 PM groups. Max width: `208` pixels (4 × 52px icons).

### Drugs (vanilla `opium`) and Pharmaceutical Industries

Drugs are vanilla's `opium` good under a new name, so anything that adds Drugs supply or demand also touches vanilla's opium content. `building_synthetics_plant_opium` unlocks at vanilla `pharmaceuticals` (era 2) with a six-method ladder in `pmg_synthetic_opium`. Its design rule and wage-breakeven targets are in the comment above `pm_pharma_alkaloid_extraction` (`extra_pms.txt`): the era-2 method loses to a plantation so it can't undercut the British–Qing trade, and later methods beat the opium Mechanized Farm, trimmed to 55. What vanilla keys off the good, and how the mod handles it:

- **The Opium Trade (`je_opium`)** has no timeout and completes when another country's market imports ≥200 of the good, firing `opium_events.1` against that people. Modern Drugs imports would fire it in the 20th century, so `common/journal_entries/te_vanilla_opium.txt` `REPLACE`s it with a 23 January 1912 cutoff (re-diff on every patch).
- **Qing's Opium Crisis ban** (`add_banned_goods = g:opium`) also bans medicine. Pharmaceutical Industries carry the plantation's `is_banning_goods` build check.
- **Cultural obsession** (Han starts with opium). `obsession_demand_*` in a need only override the defines' defaults (`DEFAULT_OBSESSION_DEMAND_MIN/MULT`), and the ±25% shift in what an obsessed pop spends on a need holding the good is global (`OBSESSION_POP_NEED_EXPENSE_MULT`). So leaving the fields out of `popneed_healthcare` should not exempt it: expect Han pops to put about 25% more into Healthcare, taken from their other needs. Not yet checked in game (plan § 4).
- **Healthcare** (`popneed_healthcare`, `common/pop_needs/extra_pop_needs.txt`) is a Drugs-only need from wealth 16 (`healthcare_need` in `pop_needs_curves.py`). The owner's target: Pharmaceutical Industries at 1.5–2% of GDP in a modern rich country, overwhelmingly from healthcare, which is about 3% of consumption. A buy-package value is a cost at base prices, so the anchors (0 at 15, 10 at 20, 30 at 25, 85 at 30, 160 at 35, 290 at 40, 520 at 45, 900 at 50) hold Healthcare at 2.9–3.2% of the package from wealth 29 to 50. Past 50 it rises 76 a level (1,660 at 60, 4,624 at 99, 12,300 at 200): the share falls, but a richer pop still buys more. Wealth 100–200 come from the curve itself (`CURVES_PAST_99`), not the generator's power-law fit. `test_healthcare_need.py` pins the curve, the generated buy packages and the 3% band.
- **Leisure without Drugs.** `REPLACE:popneed_leisure` in `extra_pop_needs.txt` is vanilla's Leisure minus its `opium` entry. Leisure grows without limit (394 at wealth 40, 9,648 at 60), and a need's split follows each good's share of market sell orders, so a market full of Pharmaceutical Industries' output would pull ever more Leisure into Drugs. Intoxicants keeps Drugs at 0.75: the 19th-century British–Qing trade depends on it. Re-diff the replacement on every vanilla patch.
- **Plantation penalties.** Health-system laws (`INJECT:law_*_health_*` in `common/laws/modified_health_system.txt`) carry `building_opium_plantation_throughput_add` −0.02 / −0.03 / −0.05 per level of `institution_health_system` (in game the Ministry of Health; laws Charity Hospitals / Private / Public Health Insurance), and the Ministry of Consumer Protection −0.10 per level. Throughput is the plantation-only lever; `goods_output_opium_mult` applies per good and would cut Pharmaceutical Industries too. Only the enacting country's plantations shrink. `test_opium_plantation_penalties.py` pins the values.
- **Half a Course**, the medicine shortage (`drugs_shortage.1`, `events/drugs_shortage_events.txt`): rolled yearly at 75% from `drugs_shortage_yearly_events` (`common/on_actions/drugs_shortage_on_actions.txt`), cooldown `short_modifier_time`. Its trigger copies vanilla's `market_goods_shortage_alert` for Drugs (`market_goods_shortage_ratio > 0.33`, `country_has_local_shortage`), not banning Drugs, and `drugs_healthcare_population_share >= 3` (percent of people at wealth 25+, `common/script_values/te_drugs_values.txt`). Under the revised Healthcare curve that gate matched "Healthcare is a third or more of the country's Drugs demand" in 57 of 63 of the probe's country-saves, so it stays; the value's comment has the reasoning. Options: buy abroad (`drugs_shortage_spending` × `sv_money_flow_event_medium`), ration (`drugs_shortage_rationing`, +3% mortality), a Pharmaceutical Industries drive (needs one; spending × large plus +25% throughput) or a poppy reprieve (needs a plantation; +30% plantation throughput, Intelligentsia −5 / Devout −3 / Landowners +3 approval). Modifiers in `common/static_modifiers/event_modifiers.txt`.
- **Measuring:** `event te_debug_drugs.1` (console only) logs every country's population share at wealth 10–50, Drugs buildings, price and ban to `debug.log` on `TE_DRUGS_PROBE` lines, plus world supply by method and buy/sell orders.
- **Field Hospitals** use Drugs as in vanilla; First Aid and Medevac Helicopters use liquor.
- **Ranches** buy Drugs on three methods: Antibiotic Growth Promoters (era 8) 2 a level, Recombinant Growth Hormones (era 9) 3 and Gene-Edited Livestock 1 (§ Livestock ranches and plantations, which sizes them at 2% of supply on today's ranches and 6% at most). That demand makes Half a Course a little likelier in a market short of Drugs, and a country still banning Drugs may leave those ranches short of an input. The Opium Trade has ended by then.
- Companies, prestige goods, Britain's plantation JE, the opium mobilization option and Free Trade's ban removal key off plantations or the good existing, not off demand levels.

- **Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs (INCB)**: Assembly business opens after any UN member researches `antibiotic_mass_production`, at authority ≥20. Major powers with `pharmaceuticals` may propose. Country-scoped parties carry `un_narcotics_control_modifier` at ×E: −15% `building_opium_plantation_throughput_add`, +5% `building_synthetics_plant_opium_throughput_add`, +3% prestige. No exporter regime term; see `test_un_convention_registry.py` and the approved narcotics spec.

### Barracks training methods

Five methods `INJECT`ed into vanilla's `pmg_training` (`common/production_methods/barracks_training_pms.txt`). The group has `ai_selection = most_productive`, which on a building with no output means the highest training rate. So `pm_officer_academies` (26/level, `combined_arms`) and `pm_neural_grafting` (120/level, `bioenhanced_soldiers` plus an enhancement-permitting augmentation law; buys `opium` and electronic components, +25% servicemen mortality) extend vanilla's ladder past Mobile Warfare Tactics (24) for the AI. `pm_expeditionary_logistics` (10, `predictive_logistics`, `building_mobilization_cost_mult` −0.1) and `pm_reserve_cadre` (12, `public_works_programs` and National Militia or Mass Conscription) train slower and are player choices. `pm_political_commissars` (20, `mass_media` and Single-Party State or Council Republic, hidden otherwise, 5% officers) trains slower too, but its count type carries `game_data = { ai_value = 5000 }`. The AI appears to score a barracks method by its modifiers' ai_values (`building_training_rate_add` is 100 a point), and with no goods every switch is a downgrade needing ×1.67 (`PRODUCTION_METHOD_STICKINESS_DOWNGRADE` 0.6), so a single-party AI moves to Commissars from Officer Academies on barracks of two levels or more, and to Neural Grafting from Commissars only on large ones.

Building-local effects sit in the PM. A national or state effect is a count in a custom modifier type (`barracks_training_modifier_types.txt`), declared `workforce_scaled` in the PM so staffing counts. A monthly pulse turns each count into a static modifier (`barracks_training_modifiers.txt`) through a never-removed variable. Each modifier has one refresh site, in `barracks_training_effects.txt`. A PM-borne country effect would otherwise stack once per barracks:

| Count (type) | Declared by | Pulse | Becomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `country_academy_trained_battalions_add` | Officer Academies, 1 per staffed level | `te_barracks_doctrine_country_pulse` (monthly country) | `te_officer_academies_standing` × (count ÷ `army_size`, max 1): Armed Forces +20% political strength, +10% attraction at 1 |
| `country_commissar_battalions_add` | Political Commissars, 1 per staffed level | same | `te_political_commissars_indoctrination` × share: +15% in-government attraction at 1 |
| `state_reserve_battalions_add` | Reserve Cadre, 2 per staffed level | `te_reserve_cadre_state_pulse` (monthly state) | `te_reserve_cadre_conscription` (conscription rate = count × 1000 ÷ state population, max 0.25) and `te_reserve_cadre_capacity` (+1 conscription-center max level per reserve battalion) |

Any other source can declare these types (a law, a decree, a building) and the same pulse converts it; the tooltip breaks the count down by source. The reserve conversion assumes the engine sizes conscription from population (the `CONSCRIPTION_CENTER_LEVEL_POPULATION_DIVISOR` define says civilian population; the modifier text says workforce). If a reserve battalion turns out to buy only about half a conscription center in game, the engine uses workforce and the 1000 needs raising.

### Flat crews: no level keeps a token staff (#1684; owner, 2026-11-13)

No combination of a building's methods leaves a level 1 to 99 staff: a level keeps a real crew, or the building hires a flat one. `test_flat_crews.py`'s `TokenCrewTest` checks every building. It exempts the ownership buildings (company headquarters, financial district, manor house), the Manila Cathedral's ruins and the construction market's marker building, and it leaves out the Urban Center's clergy group.

A flat crew is the AI-managed tier's pattern (`docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-16-ai-managed-flat-staff-design.md`). The method cancels its base method's per-level workforce exactly and hires 1,000 in `unscaled`, with `building_job_attractiveness_mult = 5`, so the building's size is no longer limited by the people available to staff it. Where the base methods' crews differ, the method is split by base and hidden when unavailable. Robotic Food Processing has `pm_robotic_food_processing_synthesis` for Programmable Food Synthesis. 3D Printed Buildings has `pm_3d_printed_buildings_nanomaterial` for Nanomaterial and Self-Replicating Construction and their Construction Site twins; the original keeps its per-level cut on the older tiers.

Twenty-one methods that left 10 to 200 a level took a flat crew on 2026-11-13: Automated Harvesters and Planters (and the rice farm's copy), the Textile Mill's and Furniture Manufactory's AI-managed tier, Self-Optimizing Production, Bespoke Alloys, Programmable Plastics, Programmable Paper, Robotic Food Processing's copy, Robotic Service Providers, Autonomous Trains, Vactrain Freight, the Autonomous Highway, Artificial Personalities, Autonomous Networks, Fully Automated Content Creation, AI-Mediated Mind Sharing, AI-Optimized Operations, Automated Deep-Sea Mining (both families) and 3D Printed Buildings' copy. Most crews are engineers. Robotic Service Providers and Autonomous Trains hire clerks, Artificial Personalities shopkeepers, and Automated Deep-Sea Mining one advanced level's 800 engineers and 200 academics. Older base methods keep the staff a cut never covered, such as Modern Farming's 1,500 farmers. `FlatCrewTest` checks that every flat crew cancels some combination exactly and that these are 1,000 strong. In-game checks: `docs/testing/ai-managed-flat-staff-playtest.md` item 8.

### Ownership buildings: owners hired as a partnership (2026-11-28)

Financial Districts and company headquarters hire their owners, capitalists and the aristocrats of aristocrat-owned companies, as a fixed partnership in `unscaled` plus a smaller part a level in `level_scaled` (owner, 2026-11-28; `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-ownership-concentration-scope.md`). Each owner line T a level became 6T once a building and 0.4T a level, so a building breaks even with the old staff at level 10 and keeps 40% of it a level beyond: a Privately Owned Financial District hires 300 + 20 capitalists a level (320 at level 1, 2,300 at level 100 where it hired 5,000), a capitalist-owned headquarters 150 + 10. A larger district or company therefore has fewer owners a level, and each takes a larger share of its dividends, which is what Dynastic Wealth's Scale of Fortunes term reads (`demographics.md` § Dynastic Wealth per state). The sizes are provisional until the owner reads district and headquarters levels on saves (`docs/testing/ownership-concentration-playtest.md`, item 5). The staff (clerks, academics, clergy) and the broad owners of shopkeeper-, bureaucrat- and academic-owned companies stay per level, and so do the government-run and cooperative headquarters and the Manor Houses. The owners' jobs carry no attractiveness bonus: they are wanted jobs, not a crew that must fill, and `test_flat_crews.py` and `test_ai_managed_flat_staff.py` leave the partnerships out. The six vanilla-only headquarters methods (academic-owned and five Divine Economics copies) are `REPLACE`d for it; the Divine Economics copies all take principles II to V and each its own company category (the capitalist copy had lost its category gate).

**The headquarters' automation tiers.** Until 2026-11-28 the headquarters group carried the Financial District's Electronic Brokerage, Digital Trading and Automated Trading, which have no category or law gate: under Command Economy or Cooperative Ownership a headquarters could take one and hire capitalists with their shares, and a tier replaced an aristocrat-, shopkeeper-, academic- or bureaucrat-owned company's owners with capitalists. Each tier now has a headquarters copy per category (`pm_company_headquarter_<tier>[_<category>]`, fifteen), gated by `unlocking_company_categories`, closed by `disallowing_laws` under Command Economy and Cooperative Ownership and hidden when unavailable. A copy keeps its category's owners, partnership, shares and dividends, and automates the back office: its clerks and academics a level fall to 60%, 40% and 20% of the category's base method, the District's tier ratios, for the same goods. The District keeps the three shared tiers. `test_ownership_partnership.py` holds the split, the copies and their gates.

### Deep-Sea Mine and Extraplanetary Base logistics (Global Commons § 1)

The Deep-Sea Mine (`building_ocean_mine`) and the Extraplanetary Base (`building_space_mine`) both mine metal and mineral goods; the base's four mining methods also make Glass and Plastics. Seabed licences cap the first, so each of its levels earns more than an Extraplanetary Base level until era 12's propulsion (spec `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-16-global-commons-design.md` § 1). Per level at base prices, the shared `pmg_maintenance_intensive` left out; a Deep-Sea profit is on Motor Ship Tenders / Magnetic Drive Tenders (support vessels, below):

| Method (era) | Construction | Staff | Profit | With Fusion Tugs | With both upgrades |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deep-Sea Mining (11) | 5,000 | 1,000 | 15,100 (69%) / 16,100 (77%) | | |
| Advanced Deep-Sea Mining (12, Advanced Nanofabrication) | | 1,000 | 48,200 (185%) / 49,200 (197%) | | |
| Automated Deep-Sea Mining (12, Artificial General Intelligence) | | 1,000 flat | 43,400 (141%) / 44,400 (149%) | | |
| Asteroid Mine (11) | 20,000 | 1,000 flat | 7,250 (24%) | 14,450 | 21,650 |
| Mining Colony (12, Space Colonization) | | 1,000 flat | 37,000 (33%) | 65,800 | 94,600 |
| Self-Replicating Miners (13, Von Neumann Machines) | | 500 flat | 104,050 (127%) | 123,250 | 142,450 |

- `pm_ocean_mine` mines the polymetallic-nodule mix and covers every output of the Asteroid Mine but Tech-Critical Metals on lower inputs, with a level's staff the size of the base's whole crew. The era-12 methods produce twice its output on their own inputs. The base method's margin stays under the late land tech-metal mines' (Smart Miners, 172%) on either tender; of the era-12 ones only Advanced Deep-Sea Mining exceeds it since the deposit mix (spec § 11 #3 had both).
- **The deposit mix** (owner, 2026-11-15, with the pools' fivefold: `global_commons.md` § Pools): a level makes 320 Structural, 230 Conductive and 70 Tech-Critical Metals, with the 50 Base Metals, 50 minerals and 50 gold it made before. Until then it was 260 Structural, 260 Tech-Critical and 100 Conductive. The proportions are the seabed's deposits booked the way the land mines book the same metals (the Manganese Mine nine tenths Structural, the Nickel and Cobalt Mine three quarters Structural by units, the Copper Mine all Conductive): about half Structural, three eighths Conductive and a ninth Tech-Critical. The three metals still sum to 620 a level, so 5,000 licences land on the deposits' total; more of them sell at 40 than at 80, so a level's sales fell from 44,700 to 37,100 and its profit by a third. What that moved: a Deep-Sea Mine level earns about twice an Asteroid Mine level (three times before) and eight times as much per point of construction (twelve); the Asteroid Mine now makes more Tech-Critical Metals a level (175 against 70 since the ground types' basket), one of the two goods where the base leads, with Glass and Plastics, which the seabed doesn't make; an Asteroid Mine with both logistics upgrades (21,650, the second needing an era-13 technology) passes an era-11 Deep-Sea Mine, where before it stayed behind; and a Mining Colony with Fusion Tugs (65,800) is a third ahead of an Advanced Deep-Sea Mine, where they were level. `te_seabed_level_output_value` (the royalty's and the holding fee's base) and the AI's price weights restate the method; `DeepSeaMineMix` holds both to it. The rule-off family (`pm_ocean_mine_uncapped` and its twins) is untouched.
- **The mining methods' basket** (the figures: owner, 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-21): a level of the Asteroid Mine makes 250 Structural Metals, 175 Tech-Critical Metals, 95 Conductive Metals, 70 Glass and Plastics, 50 gold, 25 Chemical and Industrial Minerals and 10 Base Metals; the Mining Colony makes four times each, Self-Replicating Miners five times, and Regolith Processing the same shares scaled to its £16,600, to two decimals (111.41 Structural Metals and so on), so every site's ground type scales the four alike (ground types design § 7; `ebase_ground.BASKET`). The 70 Glass and Plastics came out of Tech-Critical Metals at equal value on 2026-11-21 (35 at 80 for 70 at 40), as the 80 Conductive Metals had on 2026-11-15: Conductive Metals were 15 (a 60/40 split of the Conductive and Base Metals figure from before vanilla 1.15's copper, never sized against demand): in a hand-played game's 1958 save, a country fed by 3,699 base levels covered 156% of its Tech-Critical demand and 11% of its Conductive, with Conductive Metals at the price cap (#1834 has the save). those 80 came out of Tech-Critical Metals at equal value (40 at 80 for 80 at 40), so every profit above stands, and the Asteroid Mine stays under the Deep-Sea Mine's Conductive Metals (100 then, 230 since the deposit mix). Base Metals and the minerals have the same shape and were left as they were. `ExtraplanetaryBaseMix` holds the shared mix and Conductive Metals at a tenth of each method's sales; that floor and the four-times rule are the test's own, read off the figures. `test_ebase_ground.Basket` holds the four methods to one basket, and the era-11 Deep-Sea comparison excepts Glass and Plastics with Tech-Critical Metals.
- **Support vessels** (`pmg_support_vessels_building_ocean_mine`, methods after the mine's in `extra_pms.txt`): the mine's ships, for every extraction method of both families, which carry none. Motor Ship Tenders (default, 40 Motor Ships a level, 3,600) and Magnetic Drive Tenders (`modern_material_science`, 20 Magnetic Drive Ships, 2,600). That technology makes Magnetic Drive Ships and gives ports, fishing wharves and whaling stations their magnetic-drive methods, but it is on a separate branch from Abyssal Plain Mining, so the mine starts on Motor Ships. A switch retools the mine (260 construction a level on `pm_maintenance_intensive`'s 0.2 a week), about 5 years of the 1,000 saving at base prices. Both methods only cost, so the group takes `ai_selection = most_productive` and the methods `ai_value` 1000 / 3000, `pmg_emissions_control`'s pattern for moving the AI up and never back (unconfirmed in game: `docs/testing/global-commons-playtest.md`).
- **Logistics groups** (`common/production_methods/te_global_commons_logistics_pms.txt`, groups in `te_global_commons_logistics_pm_groups.txt`): Launch to Orbit (Chemical Launch, default; Tethered Launch, `space_elevator`) and Transport in Space (Chemical Tugs; Fusion Tugs, `compact_fusion_reactors`). Each upgrade is `goods_input_launch_capacity_mult = -0.3` in `building_modifiers.unscaled` (registered in `mod_entity_modifier_types.txt`; vanilla registers that axis for six goods only). The two add to −60%. `space_elevator` requires `compact_fusion_reactors`, so Fusion Tugs always comes first. A method can't be gated on owning a building, so Tethered Launch needs the technology, not an elevator.
- **A flat crew** (owner, 2026-10-31): the Extraplanetary Base's four base methods hire their staff in `building_modifiers.unscaled`, the same at any level (800 on Regolith Processing, 500 on Self-Replicating Miners, 1,000 on the others), since a base needs its colonies' room to pay and its levels are off-world work. Each crew carries `building_job_attractiveness_mult = 5`, as every flat crew (`pm_ai_managed_*`, the Grid Transfer Station). `pm_costs.py` reads only `level_scaled` staff, so their cost comments read Employment 0; `test_deep_sea_rebalance.py` (`ExtraplanetaryBaseFlatCrew`) holds the crew.
- **Self-Replicating Miners** (`pm_self_replicating_miners`, Von Neumann Machines, era 13 spec § 7.10): seed miners that build their copies from the ore they dig. Against the Mining Colony, a level launches two thirds of the Launch Capacity (320) and adds 20 robotics, every output is a quarter larger, and the crew is half (400 engineers, 100 academics). A smaller launch bill makes the logistics upgrades worth less here: it out-earns every Deep-Sea method with no upgrade, and the Mining Colony on every combination (`test_deep_sea_rebalance.py`, `SelfReplicatingMiners`). The base has no automation or AI-managed group, so no other method names its base methods.
- **AI:** the groups keep the default `ai_selection` (most_profitable). An upgrade lowers only the Launch Capacity bill, so it is the more profitable method at every price and can't flip with the market.
- **Known gap:** Space Colonization (society) and Advanced Nanofabrication (production) are both era 12 and independent, so a country with only the first runs a Mining Colony (37,000) beside an era-11 Deep-Sea Mine (15,100). Per point of construction the Deep-Sea Mine still earns 1.6× as much.
- **Rule off keeps the old mine.** These figures belong to the capped mine. With `global_commons_disabled` the mine is uncapped (spec § 2.9, "uncapped as today"), so it keeps its pre-rebalance methods, `pm_ocean_mine_uncapped` and its two era-12 twins (6,200 / 30,400 / 25,600 a level on Motor Ship Tenders, 2,000 staff at the base method), on the same support vessels. The rule's settings switch the two families with `disable_pm_` flags (`extra_game_rules.txt`), every method `is_hidden_when_unavailable`, the pattern of § Locking Production Methods in `scripting_best_practices.md`. Unverified in game, as there: which method an existing building moves to when a mid-game rule change disables its own, and whether a save from before the rule gets either setting's flags.
- **No `required_input_goods`** (2026-11-24). Every method buys Digital Access, but none requires it. When each took `required_input_goods = digital_access`, the Building Registry's switch-all control showed no dropdown while every mine ran the same method, and the era-12 methods could be reached only from a single mine's panel. The Registry checks methods for the whole country, where a local good can't be found, and these methods hide when unavailable (`scripting_best_practices.md` § "A Method That Hides When Unavailable Can't Require a Local Good"). A mine in a state without Digital Access can now take the era-12 methods, which it already could run on the base method, short of the good.
- `test_deep_sea_rebalance.py` holds the orderings from the files (`--table` prints the table above); the cost comments in `extra_pms.txt` are `pm_costs.py`'s.

### Aneutronic Fusion's methods (era 13 spec § 7.8)

Aneutronic Fusion (era 13, military, after Compact Fusion Reactors) adds a base method to two buildings. Per level at base prices, from the `pm_costs.py` comments:

| Method (era) | Group | Input cost | Output | Staff | Profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compact Fusion Power (12) | `pmg_base_building_fusion_power` | 128,000 | 12,000 electricity | 1,100 | 232,000 (181%) |
| Aneutronic Fusion Power (13) | same | 152,500 | 16,000 electricity | 950 | 327,500 (215%) |
| Microgravity Alloying (12) | `pmg_steelmaking_process` | 21,400 (150 iron, 180 electricity, 50 launch capacity) | 600 steel | 3,000 | 8,600 (40%) |
| Fusion Torch Reclamation (13) | same | 20,000 (50 iron, 600 electricity) | 600 steel | 3,000 | 10,000 (50%) |

- **Aneutronic Fusion Power** (`pm_base_building_aneutronic_fusion_power`). Proton–boron fusion gives off charged particles, not neutrons, and its current is drawn from the plasma. Against Compact Fusion Power it takes a quarter of the engines (no turbines) and of the lead (almost no shielding), twice the tech metals, 500 electronic components and 15 advanced materials, and staffs 450 laborers, 300 machinists, 100 engineers and 100 academics a level. The mod has no radioactive-waste mechanic, so "no waste" is the lead cut and the method's description.
- **Its own AI-managed tier.** `pm_ai_managed_power_plant_reactor` cancels 500 laborers, 400 machinists, 100 engineers and 100 academics a level, exact for the three older fusion bases and the Nuclear Plant (#996). The new base staffs fewer, so the reactor method doesn't list it; `pm_ai_managed_power_plant_aneutronic` (hidden when unavailable, the same inputs and 1,000-engineer crew) cancels 450, 300, 100 and 100. A new power base method needs one of the AI-managed power methods to list it, with a cut that matches its staff. The two flat automation tiers (Centralized Control, Integrated Smart Systems) cut at most 420 laborers, 280 machinists and 40 engineers, which the new staff covers.
- **Fusion Torch Reclamation** (`pm_fusion_torch_reclamation`): the 1969 Eastlund–Gough fusion torch, which breaks scrap and waste back into its elements. It trades ore for electricity, with Microgravity Alloying's steel and staff and pollution 10 (Microgravity's 20). Bespoke Alloys lists it (its cut leaves 10 engineers, as on Microgravity), and `gen_carbon_capture_pms.py` puts it on the not-applicable capture method beside Microgravity, since it burns no coal.

### The mod's mines and their co-products (#1419)

Ten of the thirteen mines the mod adds (`common/buildings/03_zz_te_mines.txt`) make several goods, in proportion to their mineral's real end uses: a Bauxite Mine makes Conductive Metals as well as Structural Metals, a Nickel and Cobalt Mine mostly Structural Metals. Each runs its own copy of the three equipment groups that carry a mine's output (`pmg_mining_equipment_`, `pmg_explosives_` and `pmg_steam_automation_building_<mine>`), cloned from the family it used before by `scripts/generators/gen_mine_coproducts.py` into `mine_coproduct_generated_pms.txt` and `mine_coproduct_generated_groups.txt`. Its train automation, tailings and maintenance groups are still the family's.

| Graphite | coal | Energy and Carbon Minerals 70%, Tech-Critical 30% |

Phosphate, Potash and Industrial Mineral and Salt are unchanged: nearly all phosphate and potash go to fertilizer, and salt's other uses (de-icing, food) have no good. Each mine's sources sit with its `MINES` entry in the generator and above its methods in the generated file; shares are rounded to 5%.

- **Value-neutral.** Each copied method keeps its template's inputs, staff, technologies and output value at base prices; only the goods change, so margins and the AI's choice of method don't move. Outputs of one unit or more round to a tenth, smaller ones to a hundredth, and the first good takes the remainder.
- **One ratio per mine**, the same at every tier. A mine that changed its mix by era (graphite's battery share grew from under a fifth to about half between 2019 and 2025) would need a share table per tier; none does yet.
- **Templates stay the source.** Rebalance a pump, an explosive or an excavator in the iron, lead, coal or tech metals family, then rerun the generator and `gen_carbon_capture_pms.py`; `test_mine_coproducts.py` fails while the copies are stale. No building runs the tech metals family's three output groups now, so `gen_carbon_capture_pms.py` gives those templates no emissions lines and no capture groups.
- **Netted fuel becomes an input.** The Coal Mine's pumps and steam donkey burn coal out of their own output (`pm_emissions.NETTED_FUEL`). The Graphite Mine's copies list that coal as an input and split the gross output, so they buy the coal at the same profit and the emissions and capture classes are the template's.
- **Shared methods that unlock on a pump** (`pm_tailings_treatment`, `pm_maintenance_mechanized_mine`, `pm_rail_transport_mine_infrastructure_4`) also name the copies, in a generated tail of their `unlocking_production_methods` after the generator's `# AUTO-GENERATED` marker. The rest of each list is hand-written.
- **Carbon capture**: each copied group gets its own capture group from `gen_carbon_capture_pms.py`, with the template's fuel classes.
- **Supply.** With every deposit worked, these ten mines make about 20% less Tech-Critical Metals by value than before (Nickel and Cobalt holds 35% of the four tech metals mines' deposit levels) and about a fifth more Structural Metals. Late Tech-Critical Metals prices are on the play-test list (`docs/testing/mine-coproducts-playtest.md`); the Nickel and Cobalt line is the lever if they climb too far.

### Livestock ranches and plantations (Workshop request, 2026-11-15)

A Workshop player found the Livestock Ranch's meat ladder stopping at vanilla's Mechanized Slaughterhouses (era 4), no labor saving after Electric Fences (era 3), no labor-saving group on plantations, and no late source of tobacco. Every new method sits on an existing technology; `test_ranch_plantation_methods.py` holds the technologies, the ladders, every combination's staff and the tobacco source (`--table` prints the figures below).

**Ranch meat ladder** (`pmg_base_building_livestock_ranch`, after Mechanized Slaughterhouses), one tier every two eras up to Cultured Meat's:

- **Industrial Feedlots** (`pm_industrial_feedlots`, Green Revolution, era 7): grain-fed pens and artificial insemination, the postwar package that gives farms Modern Farming and plantations the Mechanized Farm on the same technology. Grain 40, tools 5, electricity 5 → meat 100; pollution 20.
- **Recombinant Growth Hormones** (`pm_recombinant_growth_hormones`, Biotechnology, era 9): bacteria-brewed somatotropin, one of the first biotech products, on the technology of the GMO Plantation. Grain 60, tools 5, electricity 10, Drugs 3 → meat 200; pollution 20.
- **Gene-Edited Livestock** (`pm_gene_edited_livestock`, Genetic Engineering, era 11): edited heat-tolerant and disease-resistant breeds, beside Designed Crops (Synthetic Biology). Grain 80, tools 5, electricity 15, Drugs 1 (healthier animals) → meat 320; pollution 15.

Each keeps vanilla's 5,000 staff a level and its floor of 3,500 laborers, and at least 500 farmers, so the labor groups below cut to zero and no further. Vanilla's Intensive Wool Gathering (an `INJECT` of its `unlocking_production_methods`) and Antibiotic Growth Promoters unlock on all three.

**Feed Additives** (`pmg_feed_additives_building_livestock_ranch`, PR #1828). Antibiotic Growth Promoters (`pm_antibiotic_growth_promoters`, Modern Pharmaceuticals, era 8) was the mod's top method of vanilla's Wool Production (`pmg_sheep_ranch`), in place of Intensive Wool Gathering, though it adds meat as well as wool; the owner found that odd and was open to a rename or a move (2026-11-15). It now has a group of its own after Refrigeration: **Plain Feed** (`pm_plain_feed`, the empty default) or the promoters, and Wool Production is vanilla's three methods again. The promoters add what they added over Intensive Wool Gathering, 15 wool and 35 meat for 25 grain and 10 Chemicals, plus 7.5 grain for the 5 Chemicals (manure) Intensive Wool Gathering keeps making beside them. So a ranch on both makes the 40 wool and 35 meat it made on the promoters alone and, before the Drugs below, earns the same. They stack on any wool method (on Sheep Ranch, 20 wool), as feed additives would. The antibiotic itself is Drugs, as the owner asked (2026-11-15); the **2 Drugs** a level are the PR's figure, 9.5% of the method's input cost against the hormone tier's 8.1%; it leaves the promoters 300 a level over Plain Feed. In a running game a ranch that ran the promoters needs Wool Production picked again: the save lists a building's methods by name, so it may keep the promoters (now under Feed Additives) and drop Wool Production to Sheep Ranch, 20 wool a level less, or lose both, 35 wool and 35 meat less (`docs/testing/ranch-plantation-methods-playtest.md`).

**Ranch labor ladder**: vanilla's Fencing group (`pmg_fencing`), renamed **Herd Management** in `localization/english/replace/`, gains **Mechanized Feeding Lines** (`pm_mechanized_feeding_lines`, Green Revolution: feed conveyors, mixer wagons and barn scrapers; 2,000 laborers a level, the farms' Hydraulic Equipment is on the same technology) and **Robotic Barns** (`pm_robotic_barns`, Internet of Things, era 10: feed-pushing and cleaning robots, sensor collars; 2,500 laborers and 500 farmers). Robotic Barns is robotic, so it carries the Automation Policy marker and Human Labor Quotas withdraws it ([automation_policy.md](automation_policy.md)); it keeps a per-level crew (1,000 a level on a new base with Refrigerated Rail Cars), so it hires no flat crew. Autonomous Harvesters likewise keep a per-level crew; the smallest any of these buildings keeps is 250 engineers a level, on a GMO Plantation of bananas, tea or grapes with Extensive Rail Transport, above the floor of 100 in § Flat crews.

**Plantations' Harvesting Process.** The nine plantations, the Vineyard and the Rubber Plantation (`bg_rubber`, the same family of base methods) gain the farms' group of that name: **Hand Harvesting** (default), **Mechanical Harvesters** (`pm_mechanical_harvesters_plantation`, Microprocessor, era 8, the farms' Automated Mechanical Harvesters' technology; engines 1, oil 1; 500 farmers a level) and **Autonomous Harvesters** (`pm_autonomous_harvesters_plantation`, Autonomous Vehicles, era 11, the farms' Automated Harvesters and Planters' technology; engines, electronic components, electricity 2, digital assets; 1,000 farmers and 250 engineers, robotic). A plantation's base methods already carry its labor savings: Mechanized Farm and GMO Plantation cut the field hands, and with Extensive Rail Transport (`pm_rail_transport_mine_infrastructure_4`, the Transport principle's variant) a level keeps no laborers at all on several of them: the GMO Plantation of bananas, tea and grapes, and the Mechanized Farm and GMO Plantation of tobacco with Electric Rollers and of sugar with the centrifugal mill. The Rubber Plantation's Mechanized Farm keeps none with plain Rail Transportation. The machinists left work the dryers, mills and rollers. So the tiers cut farmers (the harvest crews, whose wage weight is twice a laborer's) and engineers only. Autonomous Harvesters needs a Mechanized Farm or GMO Plantation base, the only ones that employ engineers. Ten buildings share `pmg_harvesting_process_plantation`, as the grain farms share Precision Environmental Management; the Rubber Plantation's Mechanized Farm keeps 800 farmers and 100 engineers, so its group (`pmg_harvesting_process_building_rubber_plantation`) has its own autonomous copy cutting those. The harvesters burn oil, so `gen_carbon_capture_pms.py` writes their emissions line, and `pm_carbon_capture.py` leaves both groups out of capture as dispersed machinery, as it does the farms' (the sugar mill keeps its capture group).

**Tobacco.** It was the one plantation good no late building made: coffee, tea and wine come from the Beverage Concentrates Plant, fruit from the Cultured Produce Facility, sugar, dye, silk and fabric, rubber and Drugs from their synthetics plants. Nothing in the history or the docs says leaving it out was a choice. The Cultured Produce Facility (Lab-Grown Food, era 11) gains a secondary group (`pmg_secondary_building_synthetics_plant_fruit`), the farms' pattern: vanilla's Maintain Single Crop, or **Cultured Tobacco Leaf** (`pm_cultured_tobacco_leaf`), which turns 150 of its 300 fruit a level into 120 tobacco, 300 more at base prices. A tobacco cell line is the standard plant cell culture, and the leaf is pressed into sheets, as reconstituted tobacco is. The Beverage Concentrates Plant makes drinks, and the Integrated Biorefinery grain.

### Consumer industries (Workshop request, 2026-11-25)

A Workshop player found Textile Mills, Furniture Manufactories and Food Industries with nothing between their last base method and era 13's Programmable Matter. The automation groups carry on through the eras, but they only cut staff, so what a level made stopped changing at Electric Sewing Machines (era 4), Mechanized Workshops (era 2) and Food Additives (era 6). The glassworks, paper mill and tooling workshop add a tier every two or three eras, profit a worker rising about a third each time. The owner agreed, and asked that the Distilleries (vanilla's Patent Stills, era 2) and the manufactory's Luxury Production (vanilla's Precision Tools, era 2) get later tiers too, and that Programmable Food Synthesis make more so it stays the top grocery method (2026-11-25). Every new method sits on an existing technology, mostly the ones the glassworks and paper ladders step on. `test_consumer_industry_methods.py` holds the technologies, the ladders, the gates and every combination's staff (`--table` prints the figures below).

- **Clothing** (`pmg_base_building_textile_mill`): **Permanent Press** (`pm_permanent_press`, Modern Chemical Processes, era 6: resin-cured wash-and-wear cloth, buys Chemicals, pollution 5), **Computerized Cutting** (`pm_computerized_cutting`, Computer-Aided Design, era 8: more garments from each bolt), **Whole-Garment Knitting** (`pm_whole_garment_knitting`, Additive Manufacturing, era 10: buys Software for its designs).
- **Furniture** (`pmg_base_building_furniture_manufactory`): **Tubular Steel Furniture** (`pm_tubular_steel_furniture`, Arc Welding, era 5), **Molded Plywood and Plastics** (`pm_molded_plywood_and_plastics`, Plastic Mass Production, era 7: Glass and Plastics, Chemicals), **Flat-Pack Furniture** (`pm_flat_pack_furniture`, Computer-Aided Design, era 8: more wood, as particleboard), **Bio-Composite Panels** (`pm_bio_composite_panels`, Synthetic Biology, era 11: grain-based binders in place of Chemicals, no pollution).
- **Luxury furniture** (`pmg_luxury_building_furniture_manufactory`): **Modern Design Furniture** (`pm_modern_design_furniture`, Art Deco Architecture, era 6, beside the glassworks' Modern Design Porcelain), **Made-to-Order Furniture** (`pm_made_to_order_furniture`, E-Commerce, era 9, beside the textile mill's Customized Clothing; buys Transportation and Digital Access). Each takes 25 wood and 70 or 60 furniture off the base method, so it unlocks on Mechanized Workshops and every later base method, not on Lathes.
- **Groceries** (`pmg_base_building_food_industry`): **Enzymatic Processing** (`pm_enzymatic_processing`, Biotechnology, era 9: starch syrups, so more grain and less sugar), **Precision Fermentation** (`pm_precision_fermentation`, Synthetic Biology, era 11). **Programmable Food Synthesis** now makes 500 groceries a level, not 400.
- **Distilleries** (`pmg_distillery`): **Industrial Fermentation** (`pm_industrial_fermentation`, Stainless Steel Mass Production, era 6), **Engineered Yeast** (`pm_engineered_yeast`, Biotechnology, era 9), **Molecular Spirits** (`pm_molecular_spirits`, Synthetic Biology, era 11: Chemicals in place of some sugar). Each takes 25 groceries off the base method, as the stills take 15 and 20.

Each base tier keeps the staff of the method it follows, profession by profession, so the automation methods' fixed cuts (Textile Finishing Machinery, the Robotic and AI-managed methods, Robotic Food Processing) leave what they left before and no combination goes below zero. Every method in another group that names the tier a ladder follows names the new tiers too: Elastics and Precision Tools by `INJECT`, the mod's automation and branding methods, and Robotic Food Processing (whose split with `pm_robotic_food_processing_synthesis` is in `test_flat_crews.py`). The test also fails when a luxury, branding, canning or distilling method can be paired with a new base tier, or a new method with any base, that has less of a good than it takes off. None of the new methods buys coal or oil, so none carries an emissions line or a capture group. Each borrows another method's icon for now (`texture` in `extra_pms.txt`).
