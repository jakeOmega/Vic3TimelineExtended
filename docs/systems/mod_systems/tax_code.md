# Legislated Tax Code

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Legislated Tax Code (`je_tax_code`, `te_tax_code_rule`)

**Experimental, default off.** Under the rule the five vanilla taxation laws cannot be enacted; every country holds the carrier `law_te_tax_code`, whose generated amendments set each rate, and taxes change only through a bill that interest groups must commit to, taking effect on the 1st of its month. The customs option legislates a market owner's tariffs too. AI countries legislate through the same commands (package 6, the schema's "AI legislation"): a staggered monthly step raises taxes on a fiscal-deficit streak, cuts them on a surplus streak, passes emergency bills, bargains with the same offers and keeps or renegotiates its promises; their tariffs stay native until the customs probe (P09b/P09c) decides the mechanism. The UI is the journal entry and the Budget panel's Tax Code tab.

The fourth setting, *Light Tax Code* (`te_tax_code_light`, gated by `te_tax_code_light_on`), keeps the vanilla taxation laws: each law caps every tax it levies at its very-high rate and every levied tax may be cut to nothing, and the player moves each rate a step at a time from the tab, at once, with no bills. The native tax level is the highest step any tax sits on (the tax-level buttons are greyed) and every tax below it carries a relief static modifier, so the country collects the code's rates; the taxed goods and tariffs stay native. Interest groups react to each change (the support model's material and ideology reasons for the step; a pleasing reaction counts half; ±10, fading 30% a month) and the view bands reward relief instead of judging a resembled law, since the engine already gives the enacted law's stance. AI countries keep choosing their tax level and their code follows it.
