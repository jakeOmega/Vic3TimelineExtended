# Private Actors

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

Every company in every country is a **firm**: an actor with a goal derived from its position, three currencies, a yearly turn of its own and, in later phases, holdings and verbs. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-actors-firms-design.md` (#2140); plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-28-private-actors.md`. This file covers the chassis (the plan's Tasks 1 and 2): the firm's record, the turn, the goals, the currencies, the ledger and the rule; the tab (Task 3); the trust and the foundation (Task 4a); and declared moves and the state's actions (Task 4b). Each later chain (the trust, politics at home, the workforce, technology, relief, the chain abroad) adds its own `##` section.

**Files.** Generated, from `scripts/generators/gen_private_actors.py` and its config `private_actors_config.py`: `common/scripted_effects/te_pa_gen_effects.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/te_pa_gen_triggers.txt`, `common/script_values/te_pa_gen_values.txt`, `common/customizable_localization/te_pa_gen_custom_loc.txt`, `common/static_modifiers/te_pa_gen_modifiers.txt`, `common/modifier_type_definitions/te_pa_gen_modifier_types.txt`, `localization/english/te_pa_generated_l_english.yml` (§ The Trust). ## The Firm Record

A firm's record is company variables on the company itself (companies hold variables; `te_company_months_failing` and the prestige goods already use them). `te_pa_make_record` writes it the first monthly pulse a company is seen with the rule on, skipping `te_pa_gen_excluded_type` (the Grand Monument naming placeholder). The plan's Storage table is the full list; the chassis writes:

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `te_pa_init` | the record exists |
| `te_pa_turn_in` | months to the next turn; a new record draws 1 to 12 (`random_list`), so the world's turns spread over the year |
| `te_pa_turns`, `te_pa_age_q` | turns taken; quarters since the record was made (the engine has no company founding date to read) |
| `te_pa_goal`, `te_pa_reason`, `te_pa_reason_val`, `_good`, `_law` | the goal (codes below), its reason code, the reason's figure and the good or law it names, by index (Task 1's indices) |
| `te_pa_p_<g>`, `te_pa_w_<g>`, `te_pa_s_<g>` | each goal's pressure (0-100), weight (0.5-1.5) and score |
| `te_pa_funds`, `te_pa_conn`, `te_pa_stand` | the three currencies (§ Currencies) |
| `te_pa_griev`, `te_pa_restrained_q` | Grievance, and the quarters a Restrained firm has counted toward its next point |
| `te_pa_posture` | 1 Favored, 0 Tolerated, -1 Restrained (§ 4.7); a player's is the player's, an AI's derived each turn |
| `te_pa_levels`, `te_pa_share` | owned levels (a ladder of `company_owned_levels >= N`, each rung a quarter above the last, worldwide), and the share of the sector, `te_pa_levels / max(1, te_pa_g_sector_levels)`, capped at 1 |
| `te_pa_cond`, `te_pa_cycle` | condition: 1 prospering (`company_is_prosperous`), -1 failing (prosperity under 25 and falling, or 12 months of `te_company_months_failing`), 0 steady; the home Banking Cycle: 1 expansion or boom, -1 downturn or panic, 0 otherwise or with the cycle not running |
| `te_pa_bold_t`, `te_pa_force`, `te_pa_patern`, `te_pa_attach` | temperament from the executive, -1 to 1 each, 0 with none |
| `te_pa_exec`, `te_pa_ig_gov`, `te_pa_ig_marginal` | the executive at the last turn (a character), and whether its interest group is in government or marginalized |
| `te_pa_seat`, `te_pa_seat_fallback` | the seat (a state, § 4.5), and 1 while it is the capital for want of a state with the firm's buildings |
| `te_pa_trade` | its stance on protection, -2 to 2, from the home market's trade in its output and inputs (§ 5.4) |
| `te_pa_row`, `te_pa_n_failing`, `te_pa_n_big` | its row container; the two threshold notices' crossings |

Holdings (`te_pa_cartel`, `te_pa_reg`, `te_pa_press`, `te_pa_town`, `te_pa_lab`, `te_pa_found`) are unset until the task that builds each, or while the firm holds none; a reader treats unset as 0 (`te_pa_v_<h>`). The cartel, the regulator and the foundation are § The Trust's. The record is cleared from `on_company_disbanded` (`te_pa_clear_firm`: a ledger line, the row, the marker).

**Temperament** (§ 5.3) is generated: `TRAIT_TEMPERAMENT` and `IDEOLOGY_TEMPERAMENT` in the config give each personality trait and character ideology its weights on four axes (Boldness, Taste for force, Paternalism, Attachment), with a reason for every one that has none; `test_gen_private_actors.py` fails on a trait or ideology in neither table. `te_pa_gen_temper_<axis>` sums the executive's (through `executive ?=`, clamped to -1..1). Attachment adds 0.5 when the executive's culture is a primary culture of the home country and takes 0.5 when it is not.

**The seat** (§ 4.5). Script has no link from a company to its headquarters state (probe 11: the 1.15 event targets give a company only `executive`, `owner`, `type`, `prosperity` and `weekly_prosperity_change`; `any_scope_regional_hqs` reaches its regional headquarters, not its main one). So the seat is the spec's fallback: the incorporated home state with the most levels of the firm's building types (`te_pa_gen_seat_pick`, one branch a type, ordered by `te_pa_gen_seat_levels_<type>`), picked when the firm has none or its seat has left the country, and kept otherwise. With no such state it is the capital, and the pick is tried again each turn. Self-check `firm_hq`.

## The Turn

`on_monthly_pulse_country` runs `te_pa_monthly` (`te_pa_on_actions.txt`). With the rule on: records for new firms, then each firm's `te_pa_turn_in` counts down and at 0 the firm takes its turn and the count starts at 12 again, then the country's `te_pa_q_in` counts down and at 0 the quarter runs (every third month). After the turns and the quarter, the declared move counts down and the month's best candidate declares (§ Declared Moves); a firm with a case under way checks its verdict in the same walk of the firms. The global `on_yearly_pulse` refreshes Task 1's technology cache (`te_pa_gen_tech_cache`); the first monthly pulse with the rule on fills it once.

**The turn** (`te_pa_turn`, company scope, `scope:te_pa_firm` and `scope:te_pa_home` saved): `te_pa_gen_assess` (Task 1's readings `te_pa_g_*`); position (levels, share, condition, cycle); temperament and the executive; the seat; an AI country's posture (§ 12.1: Favored when the executive's group is in government or under Dirigiste Policy, Restrained when it is marginalized or under Antitrust Enforcement with a cartel the state knows of (`te_pa_holds_known_cartel`: a secret one it can't see), Tolerated otherwise; `te_pa_set_posture` logs a change); `te_pa_gen_law_pick` (after the share and posture it reads); the trade stance; the eight scores; the choice; the verb (`te_pa_act`, § The Trust); the exposure rolls (`te_pa_expose_rolls`); the threshold notices; the self-checks' tallies; `te_pa_row_sync`. A type the generator doesn't know (`te_pa_g_known` 0) scores nothing and holds no goal.

**The quarter** (`te_pa_quarter`, country scope) walks the country's firms only, never its states: the cartels' index is rebuilt (`te_pa_cartels_rebuild`), each firm's holdings grow or decay and the cartels' modifiers are refreshed (`te_pa_trust_quarter`, § The Trust), currencies accrue (`te_pa_accrue`), the rows are copied, and rows whose firm no longer resolves are swept (a company removed without `on_company_disbanded`, which `remove_company` may not fire); then an AI government may act against one firm (§ The State's Actions).

## Goals

Eight goals (§ 5.2), codes 1 Cheaper Inputs, 2 A Protected Market, 3 Security of Its Assets Abroad, 4 A Friendly Law, 5 Dominance, 6 Labor Peace, 7 A Technical Lead, 8 Survival (0: none). Each has a pressure `te_pa_p_<g>_value`, 0-100, and a weight `te_pa_w_<g>_value`, 0.5-1.5 (`te_pa_goals_values.txt`); the score is the product, plus `te_pa_goal_held_bonus` (15) for the goal held while its pressure is above 0 (`te_pa_score_one`). The held goal is weighed first and the others must beat it, so a tie keeps it; nothing above 0 means no goal (`te_pa_choose_goal`). `test_private_actors.py` pins the bounds, the formula and the choice.

| Goal | Pressure (first guesses) |
|---|---|
| Cheaper Inputs | an input's price above base, ×120 (the second input at three-quarters): 40% above reads 48 |
| A Protected Market | the output's price below base while the market imports it, ×120 |
| Security abroad | 0 until Task 9 replaces `te_pa_p_abroad_value` |
| A Friendly Law | the largest of: the worst law in force (Task 1's `te_pa_g_law_worst`) or the trade policy's burden, ×25 a point; the law being enacted against it (stance or trade burden), ×35; the law being enacted for it, ×20; the best law it could move to, ×15; and +10 where it wants a tariff on its output (the market imports it) |
| Dominance | share ×125 (40% reads 50); 0 under Command Economy, Cooperative Ownership or the Command or Cooperative Economy antitrust law |
| Labor Peace | the seat's turmoil and radical fraction, their mean ×150 (at most 60), a labor law being enacted against it ×15 a point, `je_strike` +30; ×0.75, 1 or 1.25 by staffing band |
| A Technical Lead | `te_pa_g_tech_gap` ×10 |
| Survival | 0 when prosperous; prosperity below 50 (up to 35), falling while below it (25), the failing months (up to 15 at five years), a slump (10), a law ending private ownership or removing its buildings (20). The parts reach 80 without the fall, so it passes 80 only when prosperity is low and falling |

**Trade** (§ 5.4, § 8.1). `te_pa_trade` is +1 when the market imports the firm's output, -1 when it exports it, -0.5 for each input it imports. A protective trade policy (everything but Free Trade) burdens a firm by its stance against protection, Free Trade by its stance for it (`te_pa_trade_burden_value`, 0-2). Tariff levels are not read yet (they need per-good reads; Task 5's petition reads the level it sets).

**Weights** move from 1 by temperament and condition (§ 5.2a, § 5.4): a slump weighs A Protected Market and Survival up and Dominance down; a boom weighs Dominance, A Technical Lead and Security abroad up; a prosperous firm weighs Dominance and A Technical Lead up, a failing one Survival, A Friendly Law and Cheaper Inputs; Boldness weighs Security abroad, Dominance and A Technical Lead up and Survival down; Attachment weighs A Protected Market up and Security abroad down; Paternalism either way weighs Labor Peace up; an executive whose group is in government weighs A Friendly Law up. Each coefficient is a named value in `te_pa_goals_values.txt`.

**Reasons.** The choice writes the readout that caused the goal (`te_pa_reason_write`; codes in `te_pa_goals_effects.txt`'s header, text `te_pa_reason_text` in `te_pa_custom_loc.txt`): an input's price, a law by name, the share, the seat's unrest, the gap, prosperity. A change of goal is a ledger line carrying the reason.

## Currencies

Accrued each quarter at a quarter of the yearly rate (`te_pa_accrue`; values in `te_pa_core_values.txt`, all first guesses).

- **Funds** (pounds): a tenth a year of the firm's estimated dividends, its share of the country's building levels (`te_pa_levels / building_levels`) times the investment pool's gross income (weekly, ×52), times 0.5 at prosperity 0 to 1.5 at 100; halved in a downturn or panic and halved again for a failing firm; none under Command Economy or Cooperative Ownership. Capped at five years' accrual at today's rate, so a claim shrinks with the pool. A claim, not a transfer: money leaves the pool only when the firm spends, through `te_pa_spend_funds` (§ The Trust). A cartel's rents multiply the accrual by up to 2 (`te_pa_cartel_funds_factor`). A new record starts with one year's accrual.
- **Connections** (0-100): a year's inflow of 0.25 a year of record age (up to 20 years) and 5 while the executive's interest group is in government, ×1.5 Favored and ×0.5 Restrained, less 5% a quarter, so a firm settles at about 27 old and alone, 54 with its group in government, 81 with that and Favored. Starts at 20.
- **Standing** (0-100): drifts a tenth of the way each quarter toward 50, or toward its foundation's strength where that is higher, and stays put between the two (`te_pa_stand_anchor`); a foundation adds 2 a year while Standing is below its strength, and an exposure takes 15 (§ The Trust). Task 6 adds welfare and blood. Starts at 50.
- **Grievance**: a Restrained firm gains one every 20 quarters (§ 4.7).

## The Ledger

The country list `te_pa_ledger`: script containers (tag `te_pa_ledger`, parent the country), at most 20, the oldest evicted as `un_ledger_log_add` does (`any_in_list` count 21, `ordered_in_list` by `te_pa_ledger_age_order`, the negated sequence). One writer, `te_pa_ledger_add` (country scope, `VERB` and `KNOWN`; the caller saves `scope:te_pa_l_actor`, optionally `scope:te_pa_l_target` and `scope:te_pa_l_size`, which it clears when done, and it leaves the entry as `scope:te_pa_l_new`). An entry holds `te_pa_l_seq`, `_verb`, `_actor`, `_target`, `_size`, `_known`, `_month`; a change of goal also the reason under the row's names (`te_pa_r_reason`, `_val`, `_good`, `_law`), so one set of reason lines renders both; `te_pa_l_gone` once its firm is dissolved. After each line `te_pa_ledger_view_rebuild` writes `te_pa_ledger_view`, the same entries newest first, which the tab reads (§ The Tab). Lost to a revolution's winner by design; kept when the rule is switched off.

Verb codes come in blocks: 1-19 the chassis, 20-39 Task 4, 40-59 Task 5, 60-79 Task 6, 80-99 Task 7, 100-119 Task 8, 120-139 Task 9, 140-159 Task 10. The chassis's: 1-8 now seeks goal N, 9 a new executive, 10 is failing, 11 controls 40% of its sector (size: the share), 12 established (a record made after the country's first month), 13 dissolved, 14/15/16 Favored/Tolerated/Restrained (any source, Task 3's control included, through `te_pa_set_posture`), 17 no longer presses for anything. Each has a line in `te_pa_ledger_line` (`te_pa_custom_loc.txt`), loc `te_pa_ledger_verb_<code>`, written as what follows the firm's name: the tab prints the name first, from `te_pa_l_actor` in the `.gui`, because a `.yml` line's reach into a container's scope variable is unverified. `test_private_actors.py` fails on a verb called with no line.

**Rows.** The country list `te_pa_rows` holds a container a firm (tag `te_pa_row`, parent the country) with `te_pa_r_firm` and a copy of every figure the tab shows, written by `te_pa_row_sync` at the end of a turn and of each quarter: goal and reason, condition and cycle, the currencies, Grievance, posture, levels and share, temperament, the eight pressures, weights and scores (`te_pa_r_p_<g>`, `_w_`, `_s_`), the stance on the law being enacted and its index (`te_pa_r_stance_enacting`, `te_pa_r_enacting_law`), the holdings' strengths (0 until built), the seat and the executive (`te_pa_r_seat`, `te_pa_r_exec`), the turns taken and the months to the next, and the Funds bar's fill (`te_pa_r_funds_fill`, from the firm's `te_pa_funds_fill`, which the sync writes). A later task that shows a figure adds its line there; `test_private_actors.py` fails on a `te_pa_r_` variable read anywhere that the sync doesn't write. The list is rebuilt when missing (`te_pa_rows_check`: a revolution's winner inherits no lists, and the teardown destroys the rows), and a row is destroyed with its firm.

**Threshold notices** (§ 4.6, `te_pa_messages.txt`): "is failing" (`te_pa_firm_failing`, condition -1) and "holds 40% of its sector" (`te_pa_firm_big`, share at Task 1's `te_pa_gen_big_share`), once per crossing: a flag on the firm (`te_pa_n_failing`, `te_pa_n_big`) holds while it stays past the line and goes when it falls back. A player's country gets the notice, any country the ledger line. A firm's first turn sets the flags without either, so a game's start reads no crossings.

## The Trust

The domestic chain's first half (plan Task 4a; spec §§ 4.3, 4.6, 5.5, 6.1–6.4, 6.7): the verbs a firm takes at its turn, the one place funds are spent, and three holdings, the cartel, the captured regulator and the foundation. Task 4b's declared moves, petitions, state's actions and alerts are § Declared Moves and § The State's Actions. Files: `te_pa_trust_effects.txt` (its header lists the record and the ledger codes), `te_pa_trust_triggers.txt`, `te_pa_trust_values.txt` (every number), and the generator's modifiers (below). Tests: `test_private_actors_trust.py`.

**The verb.** `te_pa_act` (company scope, the turn, after `te_pa_choose_goal`) dispatches on `te_pa_goal` to `te_pa_act_<g>`, an ordered `if` / `else_if` list of `te_pa_can_<verb>` triggers (legal, affordable, applicable), each with its `te_pa_do_<verb>` effect: one verb a turn, the first that passes. Then, whatever the goal, the foundation (`te_pa_can_endow` / `te_pa_do_endow`), an opportunistic verb, not one in place of the goal's. The lists so far:

| Goal | Verbs, in order |
|---|---|
| Cheaper Inputs (1) | `declare_suit` (§ Declared Moves), `press_cartel`: pay to expose a secret cartel that makes one of its inputs (§ 6.3) |
| A Protected Market (2) | `stall`, `declare_charter`, `form_cartel`, `capture_reg` |
| Dominance (5) | `stall`, `declare_charter`, `form_cartel`, `capture_reg`, `expand` |

The other goals have no list yet. **A later task adds a verb** with one line in a list (`else_if = { limit = { te_pa_can_<verb> = yes } te_pa_do_<verb> = yes }`), one trigger and one effect; a goal with no list gets its `te_pa_act_<g>` and a branch in `te_pa_act`. `test_private_actors_trust.py` fails on a listed verb with no trigger or no effect. The spec orders no trust verb by temperament: each of Dominance's four needs its own position (a cartel or none, the share or below it), so the order tells only for a cartel firm (defend it, then entrench it), and Paternalism's "endows a foundation early" moves the foundation's Standing line (`te_pa_found_stand_line`: 60, ±20 by Paternalism).

**Spending.** `te_pa_spend_funds = { AMOUNT = <a named script value> }` (company scope) is the one place funds are spent: it lowers `te_pa_funds` by the amount and takes the same sum out of the home investment pool, capped at what the pool holds (`te_pa_spend_pool_hit`, `te_mon_union_exit_pool_hit`'s pattern), or spends nothing when the firm can't afford it; `te_pa_spent` (1 or 0) tells the caller. The amount is staged in a company variable and copied to the owner through `scope:te_pa_spend_firm.var:te_pa_spend_amt`, never a saved scope value. Construction the firm starts is not a spend (§ 4.3). `test_private_actors_trust.py` holds every write to `te_pa_funds` to the record's start, the accrual and the spend.

**Expansion** (§ 6.1 stage 1, Dominance's verb below the cartel's share): `start_privately_funded_building_construction` queues private construction of a literal building in a state for the state's owner, which the investment pool pays for as for the engine's own (`scripting_best_practices.md`, "`start_privately_funded_building_construction` Takes a Literal Building…"), so `te_pa_do_expand` queues one level of the firm's main building type at its seat (the generated `te_pa_gen_expand`: the types that make its main output first, most of it first, then its others, flagships last; the first the seat can take a level of, `can_queue_building_levels = 1`, which `te_pa_gen_can_expand` checks), while the pool holds money. No funds are spent and no ledger line is written (the construction queue shows it; a line a year from every Dominance firm would push the rest out of a ledger of 20); `te_pa_expands` counts them. Who owns the new level is the engine's (unread).

**The cartel** (§§ 6.1, 6.2). `te_pa_can_form_cartel`: no cartel, goal Dominance or A Protected Market, share at `te_pa_cartel_threshold_value` (0.5; 0.4 under Strict IP Protection with a laboratory at `te_pa_gen_patent_pool_lab`, a branch waiting for Task 7's laboratory), any law but one that bans cartels (`te_pa_cartel_banned`: private ownership ended, or Command or Cooperative Economy). It forms at strength 5, **open** where the antitrust law allows it (`te_pa_cartel_lawful`: Guilds and Chartered Monopolies, Freedom of Contract, Regulated Utilities outside infrastructure and utilities (`te_pa_g_f_infrastructure`, `_power`), Dirigiste Policy for a Favored firm) and **secret** otherwise (Antitrust Enforcement, Regulated Utilities in its own sectors, Dirigiste Policy for another firm), and joins the country list `te_pa_cartels` (rebuilt each quarter). Each quarter (`te_pa_cartel_quarter`): it ends when a law bans cartels (legislated away) or its share falls below the line less `te_pa_cartel_end_margin` (0.05, so a firm on the line doesn't form and end one at every turn); a secret one the law now allows comes into the open, its lines getting their author (no exposure, no cost); otherwise it grows 10 a year (15 under Freedom of Contract) up to its share × 100, under Regulated Utilities outside its sectors up to 40, raised toward 80 by the captured regulator's strength; the ledger marks 50, 80 and 100. An open cartel the law later forbids stays open and known, illegal: Task 4b's suit reads it.

- **Rents**: one generated country modifier a firm type, applied from the quarter as the dynamic-modifier pattern does (removed, then added at `te_pa_cartel_mult`, strength over 100): `te_pa_cartel_<type>` ("<company> Cartel") while the cartel is open or exposed, `te_pa_cartel_secret_<type>` ("Restricted Supply (author unknown)", which names no firm) while it is secret. Each carries `building_<b>_throughput_add = -0.2` for every building type of the type (`cartel_cut`) and `country_wealth_concentration_economy_add = 5` (`cartel_wealth`: § 6.7, the sum Wealth Concentration's economic laws term reads, `te_demog_wc_econ_term`, clamped to ±15). The multiplier sits in a country variable a type (`te_pa_cm_<type>`), since a multiplier resolves against ROOT (the country, in the monthly pulse) and is read again after it is applied, and two firms' cartels can't share one. An exposure swaps the modifier at the next quarter, its one refresh site. The firm's funds accrue up to twice as fast (`te_pa_cartel_funds_factor` in `te_pa_funds_year`). The Nation tab leaves the family to All Modifiers (`KEEP_IN_LIST`, `te_pa_cartel_*`: 652 names, past a section's group size, each carrying throughput types no section's rows place).
- **Registrations**: a building's throughput type works only where some definition registers it (vanilla does for under half its own buildings), so the generator registers `building_<b>_throughput_add` for every sector building nobody else does (319 of 407 in 1.15.2, nearly all the mod's flagships), with names, in `te_pa_gen_modifier_types.txt`; a type another file takes over drops out at the next run.
- **The defence** (§ 6.4). **Stall the law**: while a law that would outlaw its cartel is being enacted (`te_pa_law_threatens_cartel`: Command or Cooperative Economy, Command Economy, Cooperative Ownership whatever the cartel; while it is lawful, Antitrust Enforcement, Regulated Utilities for its sectors, Dirigiste Policy for a firm not Favored), a cartel firm spends 20 Connections for one `add_enactment_setback`, at most once a year (`te_pa_stall_month`), guarded by `enactment_setback_count < max_setbacks_minus_one` as vanilla's ministry events are; first in both lists, and also from the quarter whatever the firm's goal (`te_pa_trust_quarter`), since a law can start and end between two turns. The line names the law (`te_pa_l_law`, the law table's number from `te_pa_gen_enacting_law_index`, through the generated `te_pa_ledger_law`). Task 5 widens the verb (`te_pa_can_stall` / `te_pa_do_stall`) to its politics. **Capture the regulator**: under a law with a regulator (`te_pa_law_has_regulator`: Antitrust Enforcement, Regulated Utilities, Dirigiste Policy), a cartel firm begins paying; each quarter it pays 2.5 Connections (10 a year) and gains 2 (8 a year, half again under Contracted Administration or the Spoils System, `te_pa_reg_fast`), or decays 1.25 unpaid (no cartel, no regulator in the law, or no Connections), and at 0 is gone. Secret. It cuts the cartel's exposure chance by its strength in percent and raises Regulated Utilities' cap; Task 4b's case reads it too.

**Exposure** (§ 4.6). A yearly roll at the firm's turn, after its verb (`te_pa_expose_rolls`), for each secret holding: `te_pa_expose_chance_value` = 10, the speech law (Outlawed Dissent −6, Censorship −3, Protected Speech +5), 1 a level of the police institution and 2 a level of Home Affairs, the courts ((Judicial Independence − 50) × 0.1, 0 without it), what buyers have paid for (`te_pa_expose_push`), and `te_pa_expose_hook_value` (0: Task 4b's Inquiry and Task 5's press add their terms there), held to 0–50; the cartel's cut by the regulator's strength (`te_pa_expose_cartel_value`), rounded, as `random`'s chance takes a whole percentage. Exposed: the holding is known (`te_pa_cartel_known` / `te_pa_reg_known` 1), its earlier lines get their author (`te_pa_ledger_reveal`, then `te_pa_ledger_view_rebuild`), a line says so, the firm loses 15 Standing and its foundation 20 strength.

**Buyers** (§ 6.3). A firm seeking Cheaper Inputs whose input a secret cartel in its country makes (`te_pa_cartel_sells_to_buyer`: the cartel firm's `te_pa_g_out_good` against the buyer's `te_pa_g_in1_good` / `_in2_good`, found through `te_pa_cartels`) pays a quarter of a year's accrual (at least £500) to raise that cartel's chance by 5 points, up to 20, fading by a quarter each quarter. Its line is its own; the cartel's firm stays unknown. Task 4b adds the petition to bring suit against a known one (§ Declared Moves).

**The foundation** (§ 5.5). `te_pa_can_endow`: no foundation, Standing under `te_pa_found_stand_line`, funds for the founding cost (a year's accrual, at least £1,000) and a year's accrual besides, private ownership standing, and some incorporated home state with no foundation. It goes to a Favored firm's lowest-literacy free state (§ 4.7), another's seat, or, when another firm's foundation is there, the next state by the seat's measure (the generated `te_pa_gen_found_pick`); one foundation a state. Each quarter (`te_pa_found_quarter`) it pays its upkeep through `te_pa_spend_funds` (a fifth of a year's accrual a year, at least £50 a quarter): paid, +5 a year; unpaid, −5; it ends at 0 or when its state leaves the country. Its yield is a **state** modifier, one a firm type (`te_pa_found_<type>`, "<company> Foundation": `state_education_access_add = 0.1` and `state_mortality_mult = -0.05` at strength 100), applied only from the monthly state pulse (`te_pa_state_monthly`, `on_monthly_pulse_state`, ROOT the state, which its multiplier resolves against) from what the firm's quarter writes on the state: `te_pa_st_found_str`, `te_pa_st_found_type` (the firm's `te_pa_g_type`, which the generated `te_pa_gen_assess` writes), `te_pa_st_found_q` (the month); the state stores no company. A foundation no firm has written for six months has lost its firm and decays by itself (§ 5.5: it outlasts its cause); at 0 the state pulse takes the modifier off and clears the state. The global list `te_pa_found_states` indexes the states, so the teardown never walks every state. A changed type number (the generator renumbered the types) takes every type's foundation modifier off that state first.

**Secrecy against the state** (§ 4.6; the player is the state). A secret holding shows nowhere under its firm's name: its lines read "author unknown", the row copies the strength the state knows of (`te_pa_cartel_shown_value`, `te_pa_reg_shown_value`: 0 while secret, so the chip, the detail line and the holdings count all hide it), and a secret cartel's modifier names no firm. The AI's Restrained posture under Antitrust Enforcement reads only a known cartel. A captured regulator applies no modifier.

**Ties** (§ 6.7): National Champion Exemption makes a Favored firm's cartel immune from suit (`te_pa_cartel_immune`, read by the suit); cartel rents add to Wealth Concentration's economic term (above). "Too Large to Break" (`amendment_petition_events.4`) now has a second way in: with the rule on, a country with a cartel it knows of qualifies as hard times do (`te_pa_holds_known_cartels`), and the event names the largest such cartel's firm (`te_pa_save_largest_cartel`, scope `petition_firm`, description `.d_firm`). Its timing is still the on-action's yearly 10% roll: `random_events` weights take no per-country modifier, so "raised by the firm, not at random" is the firm named and its cartel qualifying, not the firm firing it.

**The tab.** The holdings' chips, detail lines and count were Task 3's; their hovers now give the yield (`te_pa_chip_<h>_tt`: the cartel's cut and rents and whether it is lawful, `te_pa_cartel_status`; the regulator's cut and cap; the foundation's state, education access and mortality, from container values `te_pa_row_cartel_cut` and its kin). Rows copy `te_pa_r_cartel_open` and `te_pa_r_found_state`. Ledger codes 20–29 (Task 4b's are 30–39; `te_pa_trust_effects.txt`'s header): 20 forms a cartel in <good> (secret: author unknown), 21 builds it to strength N, 22 no longer holds one, 23 and 24 exposed (cartel, regulator), 25 captures the regulator (secret), 26 stalls <law>, 27 endows a foundation in <state>, 28 no longer keeps one, 29 pays to expose a cartel in <good>; the good and the law through the generated `te_pa_ledger_good` and `te_pa_ledger_law`.

**The rule off and the clear.** `te_pa_trust_teardown` (above, § The Rule). `te_pa_clear_firm` (`on_company_disbanded`) runs `te_pa_trust_clear`: the cartel's modifier goes, the holdings' variables go, the foundation's state keeps decaying by itself.

**Self-check `cartel_rents`** (probe 2): armed just before a firm's cartel modifier is first applied (the sums read there are without it), read at the owner's next monthly pulse: the country's `modifier:building_<main type>_throughput_add` and a seat building's of the type (`te_pa_gen_cartel_tp_country` / `_seat`) against the cut the strength asks (to 0.002). Fallback: a restricted production method switched in on the firm's buildings (`activate_production_method`).

## Declared Moves

The frame every petition uses (plan Task 4b; spec §§ 4.6, 4.7, 12.1), in `te_pa_moves_effects.txt` (its header is the full contract), `te_pa_moves_triggers.txt` and `te_pa_moves_values.txt`. A major move is a firm's petition to its own government, declared in the open, answered six months later in an event. Tasks 5, 8 and 10 add their petitions (a law, relief, protection) to it unchanged.

- **The move** is country variables: `te_pa_move_firm`, `te_pa_move_kind` (a code), `te_pa_move_months` (6 at declaration, `te_pa_move_countdown`), `te_pa_move_last` (the month the last ended), `te_pa_move_target` (what it is against, when anything) and `te_pa_move_hand` (the red alert's read). One at a time per country, none within `te_pa_move_gap` (12) months of the last: `te_pa_move_slot_open`, which every kind's trigger opens with (`te_pa_move_open`). Never secret: every line is `KNOWN 1`.
- **Declaring is the firm's verb**: a `te_pa_do_declare_<kind>` in its goal's list, which calls `te_pa_move_nominate = { KIND = <token> }` (after saving `scope:te_pa_move_nom_target` if the move has one). The firm becomes the month's candidate if its goal score (`te_pa_goal_score_value`) beats the candidate's so far (`te_pa_move_cand`, `_kind`, `_score`, `_target`); once the month's turns and countdown are done, `te_pa_move_settle` declares the winner if the slot is still open, and forgets the rest, which re-score at their next turns (§ 4.6). The declaration (`te_pa_move_declare`) logs line 30, posts `te_pa_move_declared` to a player, and copies the firm's row, which shows the move and its months left (`te_pa_r_move_kind`, `te_pa_r_move_months`, copied monthly while it runs).
- **The end.** `te_pa_move_monthly` counts down (a move whose firm has gone or left the country lapses); at 0, `te_pa_move_end` takes the kind's branch, `te_pa_move_close = { KIND = <token> EVENT = <id> }`, which saves the firm and target (`scope:te_pa_mv_firm`, `scope:te_pa_mv_target`), **clears the move's variables first** (`te_pa_move_clear`, which starts the gap; plan Review Focus 3), and then fires the kind's event for a player (popup) or answers by weight for an AI government. `test_private_actors_moves.py` pins the order, and that every effect in the system that fires an event closes what the event reads first.
- **The answers** (§ 4.7), company scope: `te_pa_move_grant`, `te_pa_move_stand`, `te_pa_move_refuse` (`KIND`), each running the kind's `te_pa_answer_<token>_<shape>` and logging line 31, 32 or 33 with the kind and the reason (`te_pa_move_why` on the firm, `te_pa_l_why` on the entry). Only a refusal is a ruling against the firm: Grievance +1, in the frame. `te_pa_move_default` is the posture's: Favored grants, Tolerated stands aside, Restrained refuses. Each end event has four options, the three shapes and the default, which is the event's `default_option`: a player who lets the event lapse gets the posture's answer, and the option's hover says which.
- **An AI government** (§ 12.1) answers on one axis: the posture's place (Favored 1, Tolerated 0, Restrained −1) plus the kind's lean (`te_pa_ai_lean_<kind>`, positive toward Grant); at 0.5 or more it grants, at −0.5 or less it refuses, between them it stands aside. So the default holds unless the kind's terms add up to half a step against it. The ledger line gives the reason: the posture where the answer is its default, otherwise the kind's largest term (`te_pa_ai_why_<kind>`; codes in `te_pa_moves_values.txt`: 1 the posture, 2 the price of the cartel's good, 3 the government's stance on antitrust, 4 the Standing of the cartel's firm). No hidden switch (§ 12.2 rule 1): every answer is a ledger line with its reason.
- **The kinds so far** (codes in `te_pa_move_code_<token>`; 3 law, 4 relief, 5 protection are reserved for Tasks 5, 8 and 10):

## The Rule

`te_private_actors_rule` (`common/game_rules/extra_game_rules.txt`), default `te_private_actors_disabled`, read only through `te_pa_on` (`te_pa_rule_triggers.txt`), positively, so a save from before the rule plays with it off; `test_private_actors.py` fails on a `has_game_rule` for it anywhere else. Every event the system fires checks `te_pa_on`.

**Switched off mid-game**, each country's next monthly pulse runs `te_pa_teardown` once (`te_pa_q_in` marks a country the system has run in, and the teardown removes it): the rows go, the notices' flags go, and every modifier the system applies goes: the cartels' (`te_pa_gen_cartel_remove_all`, every type's off the country, so it reaches a modifier whose firm has gone) and, once for the world, the foundations' (`te_pa_gen_found_remove_all` over the index `te_pa_found_states`), with `te_pa_cartels` cleared (`te_pa_trust_teardown`); the state pulse applies nothing with the rule off. Task 4b's teardown (`te_pa_actions_teardown`) takes the declared move and its candidates, whose variables are the alerts' conditions, and the interest-group modifiers of the state's actions and the charters. Each later task adds its removal there; `test_private_actors.py` holds every hand-written `add_modifier` in a `te_pa_` file to it, `test_private_actors_trust.py` the generated ones.
