# Inheritance

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Inheritance: Who Inherits (`lawgroup_inheritance`)

Six laws answer one question, who inherits an estate; apart from each law's Wealth Concentration line (below), the group's systemic effects come from script rather than from the laws' modifier blocks. Spec, with the owner's decisions of 2026-10-08: `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-08-inheritance-laws-design.md`. Files: laws in `common/laws/extra_laws.txt` (the INHERITANCE block), amendments at the foot of `common/amendments/extra_amendments.txt`, `te_inheritance_values.txt` (script values), `te_inheritance_effects.txt`, `te_inheritance_triggers.txt`, `te_inheritance_modifiers.txt`, `te_inheritance_on_actions.txt`, `events/inheritance_events.txt`.

| Law (key) | Unlock | Concentration target | Own modifiers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primogeniture & Entail (`law_primogeniture`) | start | 80 | tax capacity −10%, population bureaucracy cost −10% (the gentry sit as unpaid magistrates) |
| Customary Inheritance (`law_partible`) | start; GLOBAL default (`extra_history.txt`) | 50 | population bureaucracy cost −5%, tax capacity −5% (small: 281 countries start on it) |
| Forced Heirship (`law_equal_inheritance`) | Egalitarianism | 25 | Rural Folk +10%, tax capacity +10%, bureaucracy cost +5% |
| Freedom of Testation (`law_free_testation`) | start | 60 | capitalists' investment pool +10%, bureaucracy cost +5% |
| State as Universal Heir (`law_state_universal_heir`) | Command Economy | 0 | +200 authority, dividends efficiency +5%, Bureaucrats +10%, bureaucracy cost +5%, upper strata −20% and middle strata −10% investment-pool efficiency |
| Possession by Use (`law_non_inheritable_usage_rights`) | Cooperative Ownership | 0 | population bureaucracy cost −5%, Rural Folk and Trade Unions +10%, the same investment-pool cuts |

- **Balance (2026-10-09, owner's table).** Each law has an upside and a cost of its own, and none repeats what the score carries: radicals, qualifications and education access come from the state's score (`inh_concentrated_property`), so Primogeniture lost its radicals +10% and qualifications −10% and Forced Heirship its radicals −10% and education access +0.05 (`TestInheritanceBalance` pins it). Primogeniture's bureaucracy saving moved from institutions (−5% investment cost) to local administration (−10% population cost); Customary Inheritance gained a small pair; Freedom of Testation's litigation cost rose from 3% to 5%. State as Universal Heir and Possession by Use are unchanged.
- **Keys are historical.** Each old key kept its meaning (Partible → Customary, Equal Inheritance → Forced Heirship, Usage Rights → Possession by Use), so saves keep their laws. The two new laws come after the old four in file order (a law at the head of an existing group broke pre-change saves, #520); the defensive `te_repair_double_inheritance_law` (`te_old_save_repairs.txt`) clears any country left holding two. A pre-redesign save's Command-Economy holder of Usage Rights moves to State as Universal Heir through the monthly consistency walk, which now finds Possession by Use unlocked by Cooperative Ownership only.
- **Starting laws**: France Forced Heirship, Britain and Japan Primogeniture & Entail, the USA Freedom of Testation, everyone else (Russia included) Customary.
- **Progressiveness** shapes `te_fix_inconsistent_lawgroup_inheritance`: State as Universal Heir 50 and Possession by Use 60 fall to each other when the economic system allows, else to Forced Heirship (25, needs Egalitarianism), else Customary (10). Freedom of Testation is 5 so it is never that fallback.
- **`ideological_opinion_impact = 0.5`** (was 0): groups react to the law in force. France's Landowners now dislike Forced Heirship from 1836; balance item to check in play.
- **Wealth-concentration score** (`te_inh_concentration`, 0–100, concept `concept_inheritance_concentration`). Kept per state since the demographics census (§ Demographics): each state holds a score `te_dg_wc` and a target `te_dg_wc_target`, and `te_inh_concentration` is the national figure, the states' average weighted by where property is held (`te_demog_wc_national`, the one refresh site of the figure and of #822's modifiers). A state's target is the law's (`te_inh_law_amendment_target`: the table above plus Estate Duties −30, Birthright Endowment −20, Perpetual Trusts +30, Undivided Farm Succession +10, clamped 0–100; since 2026-10-21 50 plus each law's and amendment's `country_wealth_concentration_inheritance_add` line, the table's target less 50, so the law's and the amendment's tooltips show it, where only the laws' `on_enact` text had said it) plus land tenure, who owns the state's capital, its income inequality, the country's taxes on wealth, its economic laws and its return on capital against growth (r > g), clamped 0–100, or pinned at the state's score under continuity of title. Each state's yearly pulse (`te_demog_wc_state_yearly`, straight after its walk) closes 3% of the gap (half in ~23 years), takes up to 10 a year off the score at full devastation (10 × the state's 0–1 devastation) and takes the state's ownership shock. The shocks are listed in § Demographics, Wealth Concentration per state. The national refresh (at the yearly country pulse, and after any event shift or shock) reapplies the two country modifiers, which carry the political capture: `inh_great_fortunes` (multiplier (score − 50)/30 above 50, straight, 5/3 at 100: Aristocrats and Capitalists +20% clout and +0.3 bubble pressure a month at full) or `inh_dispersed_wealth` (full at 20, half the slope beyond, 4/3 at 0: Aristocrats and Capitalists −20%, bubble −0.4; the 2026-10-22 effects design, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-demographic-effects-impact-design.md`, sized by `banking_cycle_simulation.md` §27), reading `te_inh_great_fortunes_mult` / `te_inh_dispersed_mult`. The rest acts per state (below, and § Demographics, Wealth Concentration per state). `te_inh_yearly_update` keeps the old country-level drift (`te_inh_concentration_next`, 3% of the gap) only for a country with no scored state, such as a landless one. Game start: the hidden `inheritance_events.1` (`te_inh_game_start`; on_game_started has no ROOT for the multiplier) seeds every state at its own target, then the national figure, and clears the generation clock history set. A save from before the census starts each state from its owner's figure, or from its own target if the owner has none, at its first yearly pulse, so nothing drifts on load. The score and its target are shown in the law group's description, the concept and the Demographics tab's Wealth section.
- **Old Landowner clout, birth rate and migration** moved off the laws: the clout to the score, birth and migration to the rural modifiers.
- **Rural-scaled modifiers** (state, `te_inh_refresh_rural_effects`, the only refresh site, called by `te_demog_state_yearly` on the state's yearly pulse straight after the census walk): multiplier `te_inh_agrarian_share` = Peasants + Farmers over the state's population, read from the walk's `te_dg_agr_share` (the walk replaced the old `te_inh_agrarian_share_value` pop walk, so the mod walks pops once a year per state; London reads 0.174). Forced Heirship `inh_forced_heirship_rural` (birth −15%, migration quota −20% at full share; since phase 3 the birth line sits on the absorbed family too, as Primogeniture's does: outside Full the moved lines' pair applies it as Laws and Institutions, and under Full Family Limitation takes its place, § Phase 3: Family Limitation and Cultural Hegemony's drift); Primogeniture `inh_primogeniture_rural` (+10%, +20%; the birth line is on the absorbed family's script-only type, so outside Full the moved lines' pair applies it as Laws and Institutions, the rural refresh firing `te_demog_events.4` the same day, and under Full Late Marriage takes its place; a once-a-game `TE_SELF_CHECK` in `debug.log` says whether the engine scales the type, `docs/testing/demographics-playtest.md`); Undivided Farm Succession `inh_undivided_farm_rural` (migration +20%) replaces Forced Heirship's. The quota is symmetric, so scaling by rural share is what makes it a rural-exodus lever.
- **Amendments** (parents from other groups; ways in: debate events `inheritance_events.10`–`.13` in `on_law_checkpoint_debate`, petitions `amendment_petition_events.17`–`.20`). Estate Duties (`amendment_estate_duties`): parent Graduated Taxation, on the four private-estate laws, Political Agitation, not beside the Endowment, target −30 and no other modifier (no standing revenue, owner, 2026-10-08): the stronger leveller, since all the duty goes to the treasury. Perpetual Dynastic Trusts (`amendment_perpetual_trusts`): parent Laissez-Faire, Freedom of Testation only, Globalization, capitalists' investment pool +10%. Birthright Endowment (`amendment_birthright_endowment`): parent Old Age Pension, the four laws, Universal Basic Income, target −20 (its duty is set to pay the stakes), qualifications +10%, education access +5%, Laborers', Farmers' and Shopkeepers' investment pool +10%, bureaucracy cost +5% (an account for every young adult); it levies the duty itself, so it replaces Estate Duties. The swap adds the Endowment before removing the duty (`te_inh_swap_duties_for_endowment`), so whether `add_amendment` sees a same-block removal does not matter; `te_inh_tidy_amendments` drops a duty an IG left beside it. Undivided Farm Succession (`amendment_undivided_farm_succession`): parent Peasant Proprietorship, Forced Heirship and Customary, no unlock. The debate events offer to carry an amendment the outgoing law holds over to the incoming one (their `_carry` descriptions).
- **Generation events** (first wave): `.2` The Cadet Sons (Primogeniture), `.3` The Last Division (Forced Heirship, without the farm amendment; one option attaches it), `.4` The Founder's Will (Freedom of Testation; a foundation, −5 and ten years of schooling and innovation, a dynasty, +5 and ten years of capitalist investment, or the heirs go to court, −10, the deepest cut, and five years of dearer institutions), `.5` The Great Estates for Sale (an estate duty held 15 years, `te_inh_duty_years`; the state buys the land for smallholders, −10 and 5% of a year's income, or the market takes it, −2 and +3%, or working farms are exempted, +3). Each rolls 10% a year from its own sub-action, has a 25-year cooldown and waits for `te_inh_law_recent`, set for 20 years by every inheritance law's `on_activate`. Options that move the score call `te_inh_shift_concentration` inside a `custom_tooltip`. The second wave (spec § 11, 2026-10-25): `.14` The Hidden Estates (State as Universal Heir; audit, −3 and 100 authority fading, or look away, +5 and ten years of `inh_estates_overlooked`), `.15` The Empty Holding (Possession by Use; the commune reallocates, −2 and agriculture throughput −3%, or the family keeps it, +5 and +5%), both on the same clock; `.16` The First Cohort (the Birthright Endowment held 5 years, `te_inh_endowment_years`, 25% a year, once per tenure through `te_inh_first_cohort_paid`: schooling, businesses, or spent, +3).
- **Posthumous Title** (`.6`, Mind Backups or Biological Immortality, 20% a year, once per country, `te_inh_posthumous_decided`): recognise continuity of title (`te_inh_title_continuity` pins the target; `inh_continuity_of_title`, −10% innovation and qualifications; `.7` The Heirs Who Never Inherit five years later, with a reversal option) or declare legal death (`inh_legal_death_backlash`, Transhumanist movement +25% for ten years).
- **Wealth Concentration's state modifiers** (demographics spec §4.2, phase 2; `te_inh_refresh_wc_state_effects`, the only refresh site, called by `te_demog_state_yearly` straight after the state's drift, and at game start by the hidden state event `inheritance_events.8`, which `.1` fires for every state once they are seeded; ROOT is the state, so the multiplier reads its own score). Each reads the state's `te_dg_wc`: `inh_concentrated_property` (the high side, (score − 50)/30: Aristocrats' and Capitalists' investment pool efficiency +20%, political strength from wealth +20%, qualifications −20%, education access −0.10 at full), `inh_dispersed_property` (the low side, full at 20, 4/3 at 0: political strength from wealth −10%, qualifications +10%, education access +0.10, radicals −10%; no investment line, since small owners save too), `inh_land_hunger` (the high side × the agrarian share `te_dg_agr_share`: migration quota +30%, Land Reform movement support +25%, radicals +10%; a third modifier because a multiplier is one variable and the rural exodus needs both factors). The sizes are `demographics_effects.SIZES`, pinned by `test_demographics_registry.TestWealthSizes`. Multipliers `te_inh_wc_high_mult`, `te_inh_wc_low_mult`, `te_inh_land_hunger_mult` (state, never removed). A shift or shock made from country scope moves the scores at once and the state modifiers follow at each state's next yearly pulse. All five Wealth Concentration modifiers run under every Demographics rule setting (owner, 2026-10-09). The state's three, with the inheritance law's three rural ones, are listed in the state panel's Wealth Concentration tile and left out of its modifier rows (§ State Panel GUI Enhancements).
- **Revolutions**: the score and Posthumous Title flags are variables; `inh_repair_after_civil_war` (called from `te_civil_war_on_won`) copies the loser's over a revolutionary winner's own, with Wealth Concentration's land-law role and r > g's GDP baseline and growth average (`te_demog_wc_civil_war_inherit`: the winner continues the nation), and reapplies the country modifiers (the state modifiers are the states' and move with them), and `te_inh_apply_concentration_modifiers` restores `inh_continuity_of_title` while its flag holds.
- **Ideology stances** (`ideology_modifications.py`, P C F T S U): traditional SA N D D SD SD, moderate D N N SA SD SD, reform D A A N D D, bonapartist D N SA N D D, progressive (Proletarian) SD N A D A A, communist SD D N SD SA D, anarchist SD D N SD D SA. Hand-written blocks in `extra_ideologies.txt` name all six; Islamic Inheritance D A A D D D; the socialist, imperial-cult and theocratic custom-religion economies approve State as Universal Heir and disapprove Possession by Use. The reform movements (2026-10-22), each strongly against entail: liberal, liberal-republican and national-liberal SD N N A D D; radical SD N A D N D; socialist SD N A D A N; land reformer SD A A N D N. The AI weighs a law change by the movements that want it (`CHANGE_LAW_POLITICAL_MOVEMENT_FACTOR`, 1000 × support × radicalism), and before these only the traditionalist movements, which defend Primogeniture, held a stance; a movement's stance on each law in force also moves its radicalism (`MOVEMENT_RADICALISM_FROM_ACTIVE_LAW_STANCE`, −0.05 times its stance), so these four grow more radical under Primogeniture.
- **Enactment events** `extra_law_events.17`, `.53`, `.77` fire for Forced Heirship, Freedom of Testation, State as Universal Heir and Possession by Use.
