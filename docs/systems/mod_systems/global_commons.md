# Global Commons

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Global Commons (`global_commons_rule`): seabed licences

Design: `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-16-global-commons-design.md` § 2; claims, the holding fee, Sponsor's protection and cost, the policy lock, Relinquish and the panel (layout A): `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-17-seabed-claims-and-panel-design.md` (phase 1; plan `docs/superpowers/archive/plans/2026-10-17-seabed-claims-phase1.md`); leases, unincorporated coast and Administer Seabed Claims: the same spec §§ 4-5 (phase 2; plan `docs/superpowers/archive/plans/2026-10-17-seabed-claims-phase2.md`). Player words: a **licence** is held by a level, built or queued; a **claim** is an unused share (`concept_seabed_claim`); "recorded" stays out of player text (`test_seabed_loc.py`). The UN's side (the Law of the Sea's seabed terms, the Regulated and Scramble regimes, royalties, defection, the Antarctic Treaty) is the UN phase's; it plugs in through the hooks below and is described with the UN.

**What it does.** Every Deep-Sea Mine level (`building_ocean_mine`) needs a licence from one of five ocean basins, each holding a fixed pool shared out each year by claim weight among every coastal country. A country without Abyssal Plain Mining is weighed at `te_seabed_latent_factor` (0.75) and its share above its holdings waits for it (`te_seabed_wait_<basin>`): no claim, so no level, lease or pact uses it, and it turns into claims at the sharing after the country researches the technology (owner, 2026-10-27: a sharing among claimants alone handed the first one every pool, and it could queue them before a rival caught up). Built levels are never taken away; a smaller share only stops new building. Rule off (`global_commons_disabled`), the mine is uncapped as before.

The script follows the Python model `scripts/analysis/seabed_model.py` step for step; each effect names the model function it stands for.

**Basins.** `scripts/generators/gen_seabed_basins.py` (a post-load regenerator) maps each coastal state's `naval_exit_id` to its sea region and the sea region to a basin (`SEA_BASIN`, all 106 sea regions, borderline calls argued in its comment), and writes `te_seabed_set_state_basin`: state variable `te_seabed_basin` = 1 Pacific, 2 Atlantic, 3 Indian, 4 Arctic, 5 Southern. 372 coastal states: Pacific 91, Atlantic 216, Indian 45, Arctic 9, Southern 11. The variable is set on any coastal state without it, by the yearly pass and by any recording that meets one (conquest and splits make new state scopes).

**Room**, read live (`te_seabed_room`): the unbuilt shares, summed, less the levels not yet recorded. One national number; the gate reads no basin.

**A level is recorded as soon as it is queued** (its building's `level_after_queued_constructions`), not when finished. The spec's room already counts queued levels, so this is the same accounting one step earlier, and it closes the leak a sharing that shrinks an entitlement would otherwise open under levels being built. A cancelled construction gives its licence back at the next recording.

**Recording, monthly** (`te_seabed_record_country`, monthly country pulse; also for every participant at the start of the yearly pass): first every coastal state gives back licences above its levels to its owner's shares, distant basins first; then each level without a record takes one from the owner's unbuilt share, the state's own basin first, else the basin with the most; a distant licence swaps for a local one whenever the owner has local share to spare; every coastal state is restamped. A level its own stamped owner can't pay for **waits unrecorded** (pending): it is a level the owner queued past its room through the gate's second branch (below), its room stays below 0, and it takes a licence when a share pays for one. Only § 2.4's three cases are recorded in their own basin **against no share** (counted in `te_seabed_noshare_<basin>`): a state with no stamp (an old save's first recording, a split's new state, the rule switched back on, which clears the stamps so its levels are grandfathered as § 2.9 says), and a holder gone when its state changes hands.

**On a change of owner** (`te_seabed_on_state_owner_change`): unrecorded levels are recorded against the stamped holder (against no share if there is no stamp or the holder holds no state any more; waiting for the new owner's share if the holder exists but can't pay), the records stay on the state for the new owner, and the stamp names the new owner. Unverified: that a state keeps its variables across the change (self-check `seabed_licence_follows_state`).

**The yearly sharing** (`te_seabed_yearly_pass`) runs from the global `on_monthly_pulse` once a year (the first monthly pulse of a new year) and in the month after a new claimant appears (`te_seabed_reshare`), so a country that has just researched Abyssal Plain Mining need not wait for January. Global scope computes every weight before any pool is shared, whatever order the country pulses run in. Order: the year's first sharing is noted (`te_seabed_first_sharing_of_year`, cached as `te_seabed_first_of_year`); state basins (every coastal country's); the regime hook; every participant (claimants, and holders without the technology) records, and the coastal countries with no other part are flagged `te_seabed_wpart` (they only wait); on the year's first sharing the holding fee's snapshots (`te_seabed_take_snapshots`); held, then the leases' and pacts' shift (`te_seabed_lease_shift_held`), and on the year's first sharing Sponsor's floors (`te_seabed_set_floors`); weights; each pool, its floors fitted first (`te_seabed_fit_floors`); stale shares off for countries that no longer take part; the leases settled (`te_seabed_settle_leases`) and subjects' claims passed (`te_seabed_pass_subject_claims`); a non-claimant's policy back to Survey (its lock cleared); Unilateral relations (only on the year's first sharing, `te_seabed_relations_year`); world totals; the rivals' ranks (`te_seabed_rank_basin`); the floor self-check. A civil war's end (`on_civil_war_won`) also asks for a sharing: a winning revolution keeps its own variables, so its shares were the rebel claimant's.

- **Weight** (`te_seabed_compute_weights`, § 2.3; seabed claims spec § 5.1): home basin (coastal states there, an unincorporated one counting `te_seabed_uninc_coast` = 0.5 in both counts) `(2 × states + port levels + navy × states ÷ all coastal states) × policy factor`; elsewhere `0.05 × navy × policy factor`. Policy factor ×0.75 / ×1 / ×1 (Survey's was ×0.25 until 2026-10-27), and `te_seabed_latent_factor` (0.75) in its place without the technology (`te_seabed_claim_factor`), for a holder and a country that only waits alike; navy ×2 under Unilateral Licensing or the Scramble (not stacked). The navy is `te_seabed_navy_effective`, naval power projection ÷ `te_seabed_power_projection_scale` (300, about an era-11 ship's power projection, a first estimate; seabed claims spec § 6.1), read as a value behind the once-a-game `seabed_power_projection` self-check (fallback `navy_size`); the "+1 licence" readout stores the navy needed in those units and shows it × the scale. +5 Southern with `te_antarctic_station`; 0 Southern for `te_seabed_southern_barred` (holdings grandfathered).
- **Sponsor's floors** (`te_seabed_set_floors`, `te_seabed_fit_floors`; seabed claims spec § 2.2): at the year's first sharing a claimant on Sponsor Contractors, outside a Scramble, stores per basin its holdings (`te_seabed_sh_<basin>`) plus `floor(0.75 × own claims)` (`te_seabed_own_claims_<basin>`: leased and subject-passed claims are never protected; Southern: holdings where barred) and that protected part `P` alone (`te_seabed_fprot_<basin>`); every sharing that year freezes it at `max(held, min(floor, held + P))` instead of held (building on claims keeps the floor; a licence lost mid-year adds no protection), a mid-year one honouring the stored floor (void off Sponsor, in a Scramble, for a non-claimant, `te_seabed_floor_void_<basin>`). Floors that don't fit beside the holdings are scaled down by one factor; holdings are never touched. A floor may spill into a Regulated pool's reserved half (the pioneer investors).
- **Water-filling** (`te_seabed_share_basin`): a real `while` until no country is newly frozen; a country holding at least its tentative entitlement is frozen at its holdings, or its floor if more. Whole licences rounded down, leftovers by largest remainder (`ordered_country` on the fractional remainder), so the pool is fully shared; unbuilt = entitlement − `te_seabed_sh_<basin>` for a claimant (without the technology: entitlement = holdings, the rest `te_seabed_wait_<basin>`, unbuilt 0), leased and passed claims re-divided with the rest.
- **Regulated:** the pool is a reserved half (rounded down; claimants passing `te_seabed_reserved_half_eligible`) and an open half (everyone), water-filled together: a country's tentative entitlement is its share of each half it claims in; a frozen country's holdings come off the two halves in proportion to its two tentative shares (all off the open half without a reserved share); a half driven below zero by grandfathered holdings takes the deficit from the other. Licences carry no half. This keeps Σ entitlements = the pool (or the frozen holdings if more) with nothing to store, and a party holding more than its reserved share spills into the open half instead of starving the other parties. A reserved half that no active country is eligible for joins the open half (`te_seabed_share_fold_unclaimed`), so every pool is fully shared; an eligible party without the technology is active, so the half waits with it.

**Leases** (seabed claims spec § 4; model § LEASES). Five directed articles, Lease <Basin> Seabed Claims (`te_seabed_lease_<basin>`, `can_be_enforced`, one template): the source leases N of its own claims in the basin to the target, N being the lease's total (licences built on it plus its unused claims). The hook dispatches: `te_seabed_lease_start` marks the lessee and the hidden `te_seabed.1` a day later reads the article's lessor and quantity from its treaties, then moves up to N of the lessor's own claims (`te_seabed_lease_enter`). A level the lessee records takes its own claims first, then subject-passed ones, then leased ones (`te_seabed_lease_consume`, the end of the recording). At the sharing a lease stands while an in-force article from the recorded lessor names the lessee and both claim (`te_seabed_lease_shift_basin`, `te_seabed_lease_stands`); its licences, at most the lessee's holdings, count as the lessor's. The sharing re-divides leased claims, and `te_seabed_settle_leases` tops a standing lease back up to N less its licences from the lessor's own claims, unless the lessee relinquished the basin. An ended lease's licences go on charging the lessor (`te_seabed_lease_past_<basin>`) while both take part (review C2: else ending and re-signing moves their cost onto third parties), and the record then admits a new lease from that lessor only; it clears when one side stops taking part. An in-force article never taken up (a start event that didn't run, an enforced lease) is adopted at the sharing. AI: offer claims above a year's building pace (`te_seabed_ai_lease_spare_<basin>`), request in a home basin when room is at most 5 and Tech-Critical Metals sell above base (`te_seabed_ai_lease_request_value_<basin>`).

**Administer Seabed Claims** (§ 5.2; model § SUBJECTS): an overlord's pact with a direct subject (Raise Payments' shape without its DLC gate, not on protectorates or personal unions, +0.10 liberty desire, −1 relations a day, overlord only). A lease with no total in the same two counters, kept on the subject: at entry (`accept_effect` → `te_seabed_pact_start`) and after every sharing (`te_seabed_pass_subject_claims`) the subject's own claims pass to the overlord (`te_seabed_pass_basin`), and the licences the overlord builds on them count as the subject's holdings at the sharing (`te_seabed_pact_shift_basin`), so the pair never takes a third party's share. The counters charge the overlord named in `te_seabed_pass_to`, and go on charging it after the pact ends while both take part (review C2), so ending and taking the pact again moves nothing onto a third party; only another overlord's pact starts them afresh. The pact is the record of whether claims pass (`te_seabed_pact_stands`: `has_diplomatic_pact`, `is_direct_subject_of`); when it ends, the passed claims return through the next sharing.

**Long-Haul Seabed Operations** (`te_seabed_long_haul`, `building_throughput_add = -0.25`): a building modifier on the state's Deep-Sea Mine (owner, 2026-10-27; it was a state modifier on `building_ocean_mine_throughput_add`, a type still registered for the blockade fallback), applied from `on_monthly_pulse_state` with multiplier = the state's share of distant licences, kept in the state variable `te_seabed_long_haul_x` and read as `root.var` (a building has no variables), its one refresh site. The refresh also removes a state copy an older save carries. Monthly, not yearly as the spec drafted: the records it reads move at the monthly recording, so a swap lifts the penalty within a month.

**Blockades** (seabed claims spec § 6.2): `building_ocean_mine` is `city_type = port`, the field every blockade-affected vanilla building shares, so a blockade cuts its throughput as it does a fishing wharf's (up to `BLOCKADE_THROUGHPUT_IMPACT_SCALING` = 75%). Unverified: `docs/testing/global-commons-playtest.md` item 28 names the fallback, a state modifier `te_seabed_blockaded` scaled by blockade level.

**The rush, announced** (seabed claims spec § 7): the event `te_seabed.3`, The Seabed Rush Begins, once a game from `te_seabed_global_monthly` to every player, the month the first claimant appears and before its first sharing (an old save past its first sharing sets `te_seabed_rush_announced` silently; the claimant is kept in global `te_seabed_first_claimant`), standing in for `headlines_tech_abyssal_plain_mining` while the rule is on (`common/on_actions/headlines.txt`); then feed notifications (`common/messages/te_global_commons_seabed_messages.txt`, none to decentralized countries): `te_seabed_new_claimant_notice_<basin>` from `te_seabed_claimant_notices` (yearly pass step 7c) to every player with a share there since an earlier sharing and coast on the basin (`te_seabed_coast_<basin>` > 0), when a country's `te_seabed_ent_<basin>` first passes 0 (country flag `te_seabed_claimed_<basin>`; the first sharing to run it only marks, `te_seabed_claimed_seeded`); `te_seabed_basin_full_notice_<basin>` from `te_seabed_world_totals` to every player with a share there or coast on it, when the world's records reach the pool, re-armed below it (global `te_seabed_full_<basin>`). Both are in the message group `te_seabed_notification_group`, so Message Settings can mute them.

**Policy** (`te_seabed_set_policy = { POLICY = n }`, the one change): sets `te_seabed_policy_prev`, then `te_seabed_policy` and `te_seabed_policy_changed` (the month), rebuilds the influence modifier, then calls the UN hook. The cost (seabed claims spec § 2.3) is a unit modifier (`te_seabed_policy_sponsor_cost` / `_unilateral_cost`, `country_influence_cost_add = 1`) applied with multiplier `var:te_seabed_policy_cost_cached`: Sponsor `50 + 0.5 × own unused claims` (`te_seabed_sponsor_cost_value` over `te_seabed_protected_claims`, uncapped; leased and passed claims cost nothing, as nothing protects them), Unilateral `1.5 ×` that, at least 150. The monthly pass re-adds it when the figure moves. **The lock** (§ 2.4): any change holds the policy 60 months (`te_seabed_policy_locked`); Unilateral opens sooner when a country sharing a home basin switched to it after our last change (`te_seabed_unilateral_answer_open`), Survey while influence is in deficit (`te_seabed_influence_deficit`); the buttons and the AI read `te_seabed_can_choose_*`. **Relinquish / Claim Again** (`te_seabed_relinquish`, `te_seabed_claim_again`, per basin, from the panel): claims to 0, floor to 0, weight 0 there until Claim Again; never sets `te_seabed_reshare`. **The holding fee** (§ 2.1): `te_seabed_holding_fee_modifier` (`country_expenses_add`, Budget Breakdown: United Nations), refreshed monthly by `te_seabed_fee_refresh` with multiplier = 2% of a level's base output value a week × the licences on levels already unbuilt at the last two first-sharings of a year (`te_seabed_fee_base`); a Law of the Sea party under the Regulated regime's fee joins the royalties' weekly total. Unilateral claimants that both have incorporated coast in a basin lose 10 relations a year, half from each side, on the year's first sharing only. The weights follow at the next sharing; the influence from the day of the change.

**AI** (`te_seabed_ai_choose_policy`, `on_yearly_pulse_country`, AI claimants only, never a revolution's side): holds the highest score of `te_global_commons_seabed_ai_values.txt` (header table), with a stay bonus. Building needs no AI code: the engine's builder obeys the gate.

**The gate** (`te_seabed_gate`, read from `building_ocean_mine`'s `possible` beside `is_coastal`): with the rule on, the owner's room above 0, or at 0 in a state with a level already under construction (the Solar Power Receiver's form, so construction under way isn't halted by its own queued level); two information lines say which licence the next level takes. The second branch can't tell re-checking a queued level from queueing one more, so at room 0 it lets one extra level in. Recorded against no share, that level would put room back to 0 and the branch would let another in every month (review H1: 124 Arctic records against a pool of 100 in two years); waiting unrecorded, it holds room below 0 and shuts both branches. Every room read sits behind a cheap test (the unbuilt sum, or a mine under construction here), since `te_seabed_room` walks every building of the owner's coastal states.

Fonts in `te_seabed_fontsize_*` (17 / 20 / 16); `test_seabed_layout.py` holds every fixed cell to its longest text. The State Panel's **Seabed Licences** tile (§ State Panel GUI Enhancements).

**The UN's hooks** (bodies in the UN layer, below): `te_seabed_reserved_half_eligible`, `te_seabed_itlos_party`, `te_seabed_southern_barred` (country triggers, `common/scripted_triggers/te_seabed_un_hooks.txt`); `te_seabed_un_update_regime` (no scope, at the start of each sharing) and `te_seabed_un_on_policy_change` (country, after both policy variables are set; reached with ROOT = the country from the buttons and the AI, and with no ROOT from the yearly pass's reset of a non-claimant), in `common/scripted_effects/te_seabed_un_hooks.txt`. ## Global Commons: the UN layer

The UN's part of the Global Commons (`docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-16-global-commons-design.md` §3, §4, §6.5): the Law of the Sea's seabed terms, the Antarctic research station and the Antarctic Treaty, and the Moratorium on Destructive Anti-Satellite Tests. The seabed licences, basins, pools and the seabed policy are the seabed system's (its section beside this one); the strike and the debris index are the space race's. The UN details (docket items, vote sites, the registry) are in `journal_entry_systems.md` § United Nations, "The Global Commons".

### Mechanics

- **Regimes.** Open while the Law of the Sea is not in force, its seabed terms are asleep (nobody has Abyssal Plain Mining, `te_seabed_terms_awake`) or the UN rule is off; Scramble while two or more great powers hold Unilateral Licensing; Regulated otherwise. A Scramble returns to Regulated 24 months after fewer than two remain (`te_seabed_scramble_return_month`, read at the yearly pass, so two passes). Each change of regime is a notification to every country with Abyssal Plain Mining.
- **Party status.** Ratified (the member modifier or its mirror), not on Unilateral Licensing, and not cooling off: a party that switches to Unilateral Licensing while the convention governs the seabed (in force and awake, the live condition `te_seabed_itlos_governs`, not the stored regime, so nobody slips out between a vote and the next pass) pays 10 infamy, 5 standing and 10 defiance once (`te_seabed_defected`); leaving Unilateral Licensing for either lawful policy starts `te_seabed_party_cooldown_end` (60 months). Under the Scramble a defection still costs, since the convention still governs; royalties and the reserved half are suspended.
- **Royalties.** A party under Regulated pays `te_seabed_ocean_mine_levels × te_seabed_royalty_per_level` a week (4% of 44,700, the base method's proposed output value at base prices: 1,788), billed as dues are (`te_seabed_royalty_modifier` × `var:te_seabed_royalty_cached`, refreshed monthly, one site). The monthly global pulse sums them into `te_seabed_royalties_weekly`; `un_dev_fund_pot_weekly_value` adds it while the World Development Fund stands and `un_budget_weekly_value` otherwise. Budget Breakdown: United Nations.
- **The station.** A decision for any country with Space Exploration under the Global Commons rule. +2% research speed, +25 innovation cap, upkeep 0.1% of GDP a year with a floor of 1,000 a week (Budget Breakdown: Space Race). `var:te_antarctic_station` is the truth; `te_antarctic_station_refresh` rebuilds both modifiers monthly.
- **The Antarctic Treaty.** Raised by a country's first station (docket item 15). Parties: +2% prestige (×E), no new Southern licences, +3% research with a station (regime term ×E), +0.25 Policy at ×1 (`te_antarctic_policy_refresh`). Denounced by decision (10 infamy, 5 standing, 10 defiance, `te_antarctic_denounced` until a new treaty); lapses monthly-checked when two great powers outside it hold Southern licences.
- **The Moratorium.** Raised by a destructive anti-satellite strike (docket item 16, `un_dkt_asat_pending`); the striker is not offered it for two years. Parties: +5% prestige (×E); the strike reads `te_asat_moratorium_party`; once a year a party with a removal method books 0.25 × weight to Delivery (reason 40).
- **Rules.** The station and the treaty need the Global Commons rule (`has_game_rule = global_commons_enabled`); the moratorium the space race rule; all UN business the UN rule (the regime is Open without it).
- **Revolutions.** Everything that must survive is a variable or a convention's mirror; the royalty, station, upkeep and Policy modifiers are derived monthly. `je_immediate_reset_audit` has nothing to flag: nothing here runs in an `immediate`.
