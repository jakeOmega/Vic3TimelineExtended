# Grand Monuments

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Grand Monuments (Political Instrument)

A government raises a Grand Monument to what it stands for, and the next government deals with its legacy. Design: `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-09-27-grand-monument-rework-design.md`; plan: `docs/superpowers/archive/plans/2026-09-27-grand-monument-rework.md`. v2: `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-05-grand-monuments-v2-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/archive/plans/2026-10-05-grand-monuments-v2-phases-1-3.md`. Built: phase 1 (1,000 construction a level; vanity and Rededicate a tenth), phase 2 (commissions, names, typed names) and phase 3 (the monument policy; the owner swapped it ahead of the 1836 monuments). Phase 4 is built too: the 23 landmarks standing in 1836 (design `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-12-grand-monuments-landmarks-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/archive/plans/2026-10-11-grand-monuments-landmarks.md`), and since 2026-10-12 nineteen more where Europe's looting took place (the Parthenon, the Obelisks of Karnak, the royal palaces of Benin and Kumasi, the Bukgwan Victory Stele, the Imperial City of Huế, Persepolis, the Red Fort, Great Zimbabwe, the Royal Palaces of Abomey, Klungkung Palace, the Uffizi, the Capitoline Museums, Bodrum Castle, the Temples of Baalbek, Palmyra, Xanthos, the Mesha Stele and the Arch of Ctesiphon) with the ten trophies already abroad in 1836 (design `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-12-grand-monument-loot-and-1836-trophies-design.md`). Commissions became **opportunities** on 2026-10-12 (spec `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-12-grand-monument-opportunities-design.md`): no downsides, every one from a moment, completion gives 5 grandeur (a free rebuild 5 levels taller until the ageing rework), the approval and a ten-year boon. Since 2026-10-16 a monument is **frozen at dedication and grows grander with age**, new levels in its state found a monument beside it, and a monument beside another carries its own dedication (spec `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-16-grand-monument-ageing-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/archive/plans/2026-10-16-grand-monument-ageing.md`; § Ageing below). Phase 5 is built too: trophies (design `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-12-grand-monument-trophies-design.md`). Since 2026-10-16 **unique dedications** (*signatures*: a unique dedication layered on one of the twelve, for landmarks, for the monument that completes certain opportunities, and phase 6 rebuilt as historical opportunities; spec `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-16-grand-monument-signatures-design.md`; § Unique dedications below) are built, with the first six historical opportunities. **Add a dedication:** add its row to `DEDICATIONS` in `test_grand_monument_registry.py`; the failures are the checklist. **Add an opportunity's moment (with its boon), a form, namesake or policy:** its row in `COMMISSION_SOURCES`, `FORMS`, `NAMESAKES` or `POLICIES` in the same file. **Add a form or a style:** its row in `FORMS` or `BUILDING_SKINS` in the same file, then its picture: a new code at the end of its block in `PICTURES` (never renumber) and its entry in the icon pipeline's `gm_picture` category. **Add a landmark:** its row at the end of `LANDMARKS` in the same file (key, state, 1836 owner, dedication, form, stand-in picture, level); the failures list its seeding call, loc, name branches and picture line. Its picture takes the next code from 1,000,000 once drawn; until then it shows the stand-in's. **Add a trophy of 1836:** its row in `TROPHIES_1836` in `test_grand_monument_trophies.py` (landmark, origin and its 1836 owner, site and its holder, levels, share, year); a trophy with no standing monument of its own also needs a `LANDMARKS` row with `placed=False` (no seeding call, no level; its state and owner are its origin's), which it is rebuilt as when it goes home.

- **Grandeur and the curve.** A monument's grandeur is its starting grandeur plus its age grandeur (§ Ageing below); its size is its records (§ Construction sites below), never its building's level, and no script rebuilds a finished monument. Every effect grows in steps. National effects and interest group approval take the **doubling curve**, each step costing twice the grandeur of the last (`gm_curve_steps_f5` / `_f10`, eight terms; `gm_curve_next_*` for the JE's "next step"). Local effects (tourism, each dedication's and each signature's), prestige, cultural pull and Antiquity's pull take the **1/n curve** (owner, 2026-10-19: grandeur priced by world wealth can't be farmed, so they taper less): step n costs n first steps, so each step is worth 1/n of the first a point of grandeur and the effect grows about as √grandeur. Local effects use `gm_curve_steps_lin_f5` (thirty terms, the last ending at 2,325; `gm_curve_lin_steps`), capped per dedication by `gm_state_local_steps_one`'s `CAP` (War Memorial's conscription at `gm_local_cap_war_memorial` = 10 steps of +0.01, Stadium's turmoil at `gm_local_cap_athletic` = 5 steps of −10%; the rest `gm_curve_lin_steps`, after the policy factor); the national ones `gm_set_steps_lin_open`, a `while` with no end that also records the step's width (`gm_w_*`) for the bar. Each static modifier carries **one step** and the multiplier is the step count; `gm_step_*` script values mirror them for loc, pinned equal by the registry test.
- **Construction sites and recorded size (2026-10-18; spec `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-18-grand-monument-sites-and-world-wealth-design.md`, plans `docs/superpowers/archive/plans/2026-10-18-grand-monument-sites-plan-a.md` and `-plan-b.md`, code maps beside them).** A player builds and expands `building_grand_monument_site` (`grand_monument_site.txt`: one per state, `pm_grand_monument_site` does nothing, no upkeep, no effects, `gm_state_can_build_site` its gate, the AI's `ai_value`). `gm_state_count_site_levels` (from `gm_state_monthly` and the dedication) counts its new levels into `gm_site_counted` / `gm_site_grandeur`, each at the world-wealth rate of the month (`gm_world_rate_value`, below), with the grandeur they add on the vanity ledger in hard times; lost levels take their share. **The rate** (Plan B): `gm_world_rate` = `gm_world_gdp_1836` (402,500,000: `global_gdp_script_value` at a new game's first pass, February 1836, read by `gm_world_base` on 2026-10-25; the first figure, 334,000,000 summed from a save of 1836.1.20, read a sixth low) ÷ `global_gdp_script_value`, written each January by `gm_monument_monthly` and by `gm_world_rate_ensure` wherever none is; `gm_world_rate_value` reads it times `gm_level_grandeur_1836` (5, owner 2026-10-19), to the nearest 0.001 and at least 0.001, and `gm_world_wealth_factor` is 1 ÷ the stored ratio; `TE_SELF_CHECK gm_world_base` compares a new game's first pass with the constant. Shown on the site's card, once on the overview's last line, under the policy (`gm_je_world_rate`, `gm_world_wealth_factor` on hover; GM-5 of the 2026-10-27 GUI audit put it above Awaiting Dedication's rows, which hid it until the first site, so it moved on 2026-10-28) and in the site's build tooltip (`gm_site_rate_tt`, an `always = yes` custom tooltip in `possible`). What a monument means counts starting grandeur: opportunities (`gm_com_fit_grandeur`; `gm_com_levels_to_go` = the grandeur missing ÷ the rate, at most 100), vanity, trophy infamy (`gm_tr_infamy` on the grandeur taken), an anniversary's gift (a tenth of the starting grandeur); levels count what it cost (upkeep, Rededicate's 500 a level). The 1836 landmarks, the trophies abroad in 1836 and digs record their grandeur at `gm_level_grandeur_1836` a level, split by `gm_set_historical_grandeur` as if dedicated in the year they were built and aged since at 2% of the start a year for at most a century (owner, 2026-10-19): start = G ÷ (1 + 0.02 × min(years, 100)), age = the rest, so anything a century old in 1836 is a third start and two thirds age and ages no further (the Parthenon 16.7 + 33.3), the Brandenburg Gate (1791) 26.3 + 23.7; the year is each seed call's `BUILT` (negative before Christ; `BUILT` in `test_grand_monument_registry.py`), a dig site's stored as `gm_tr_built` (read by `gm_mon_built`, this year without one; seed round 3 gives it to sites seeded earlier) and split when it is placed or a partage piece is cut; landmarks and trophies already in a running save keep their old split; older saves' records count one a level. Dedicate (`gm_dedicate_sgui`, greyed while a level is under construction) opens the ceremony in mode site (`gm_ceremony_mode`); the answer (`gm_state_finish_site`, under a click guard) demotes a finished monument standing here (`gm_newcomer`: the grander swaps back from `.29` once the newcomer is settled), writes `gm_levels` / `gm_start_grandeur` from the site, removes the site and creates the finished `building_grand_monument` at level 1 with `activate_production_methods` and a `gm_restore_pm` fallback (`gm_site_created`), or switches the one standing. The finished type is `buildable = no`, `expandable = no`, `downsizeable = no`. Records: primary on the state (`gm_levels`, `gm_start_grandeur`, `gm_age`), beside on its container (`gm_tr_levels`, `gm_tr_start_grandeur`, `gm_tr_age`); a missing start reads as the levels (`gm_state_primary_start`, `gm_mon_start`). Takings, returns, digs, Pull Down beside and promotion change records only; the building is made only where none stands (`gm_state_tr_rebuild_from_moving`, `gm_state_raise_orphan`) and removed only when the last monument goes (`gm_state_tear_down`, `gm_state_tr_remove_whole`). A building lost while its records stand is put back at level 1 on `gm_dedication` (`gm_state_lost_building`, `.29`). Upkeep: none since 2026-10-23 (policy spec § 5): `gm_upkeep` on the building, multiplier `root.var:gm_upkeep_mult` = −1 (`gm_state_upkeep_mult`), cancels `pm_maintenance`'s level-scaled input; `pmg_maintenance` stays on the building. Rededicate asks first (`gm_rededicate_asking`, mode rededicate); the answer (`gm_state_apply_rededicate`) pays and halves the records; *Keep it as it is* changes nothing. Older saves: the first monthly pass marks unmigrated finished buildings `gm_premigration` (`gm_sites_marked`); `monument_events.32` (from the refresh, once nothing is under construction or pending) records a dedicated one at its levels and resets its building to 1 (levels finished above its record, `gm_mig_excess`, stand as a site), or makes an undedicated one a site at its level; every building script makes is `gm_migrated`, and until then a building pays its own level-scaled upkeep (`gm_state_upkeep_mult` is 0) and no merge resets it; `TE_SELF_CHECK monument_migrated`. Panels: `te_state_gm_site_card` and `gm_site_row` (Awaiting Dedication), Our Monuments lists finished ones (`gm_state_disp_listed`) but the contested, which Awaiting a Decision lists (GM-3, owner 2026-10-27), and the overview's Sites cell counts sites (`gm_disp_count_sites`, those ready `gm_disp_count_sites_ready`). `WalkedRefreshTests` pins that nothing the refresh calls makes or removes a building or container.

- **Landmarks** (phase 4, `gm_landmark_effects.txt`): placed once from `on_game_started_after_lobby` behind the rule. A landmark is its own skin (`landmark_<key>`) with one form, locked while it stands (`gm_state_is_landmark`): the style offer, the unveiling's default form and `gm_clear_state` (`KEEP_LANDMARK = yes` from Rededicate only) leave its skin, form and picture. The AI preserves a contested landmark at three times the weight. The Grand Opera House, Botanical Gardens and Grand Observatory methods carry no technology, so the engine can't switch a landmark off them; the ceremony checks it, and `gm_state_first_sight` sends a panel pick made without it to the ceremony (`gm_state_lacks_dedication_tech`). - **To History and the heritage landmarks (tourism heritage phases 2–3, 2026-10-25; spec `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-23-tourism-heritage-design.md` §§ 7, 9).** `pm_monument_historic` carries tourism, prestige and cultural pull and nothing else: no `gm_local_historic`, no national sum, no interest group, no anniversary, no signature. Timeless, so it always fits, `gm_state_changed_hands` never contests it, and with nothing to contest Pull Down and Rededicate never reach it; the ceremony doesn't offer it (a player-built monument would get prestige with no politics), and since construction sites no finished building stands undedicated for a panel pick but an older save's before its migration. 51 landmarks of 1836 are seeded on it with the others (`gm_seed_landmarks`, `DEDICATION = historic`, levels 3–15 by the base appeal each takes: 2 steps 3, 3 steps 6, 4 steps 10, 5 steps 15; Petra beside the Mesha Stele), and 14 buried sites come up on it when dug (`gm_arch_seed_site`'s `DEDICATION`). Each record list a dedication passes through names it (`gm_state_record_dedication`, `gm_state_mark_restore_pm`, `gm_state_restore_dedication`, `gm_tr_record_dedication`, `gm_state_stands_on_dedication`). Each has its own picture and trophy picture. A landmark added without one shows a stand-in, and `PLACEHOLDER_PICTURES` in the registry test and the backlog table in `docs/systems/grand_monuments_gui_icons.md` list it. `HistoryDedicationTests` pins the wiring and the policy weighting.
- **PMs carry only staff.** The dedication PMs keep the self-reference ratchet (see the file header) and `unscaled` employment; no `country_modifiers`, `state_modifiers` or goods. A building `create_building` makes starts on the dedication most of its owner's monuments carry, not on `is_default` (2026-10-08), unless `activate_production_methods` names one; the ratchet binds the panel only, `activate_production_method` passes it, and `gm_restore_pm` puts a recorded dedication back a pulse later (`gm_state_restore_dedication`). Since construction sites (2026-10-18) a finished building is always made on its dedication, so the engine's-copy repairs (`gm_state_reset_copied_dedication`, the panel-pick branch of first sight) are gone.
- **Where modifiers live.** Local effects (`gm_local_*`) are state modifiers refreshed by `gm_state_monthly` from `on_monthly_pulse_state`. National effects (`gm_national_*`, `gm_ig_approval_<ig>`) sit on `je:je_grand_monuments`, applied by `gm_country_refresh` with ROOT = the country (monthly, or `monument_events.20` after `on_law_activated`, `on_new_ruler`, `on_state_owner_change` and a finished level, whose ROOTs are not the country). A variable backing a multiplier is zeroed, never removed.
- **JE activation.** `is_shown_when_inactive` auto-activates the entry for new saves the moment a country owns a Grand Monument (gated on `grand_monuments_rule` too). Because a new auto-activating JE never reaches a save that predates it, `gm_country_refresh` also calls `add_journal_entry` explicitly whenever the rule is on, a monument is owned and the entry is missing, so old saves and any save where the entry lapsed pick it back up on the next monthly pulse.
- **Kinds and fit.** Regime (Crown, Republic, Revolution), ruler (Leader), faith (Shrine), timeless (the rest). Fit is read live each month (`gm_state_message_fits`). Records live on the **state** (buildings hold no variables): `gm_raised_by` (a bound monument fits only while that country owns it), `gm_honoree` / `gm_honoree_ig`, `gm_faith`, `gm_skin`. `gm_state_first_sight` writes them for panel picks and old saves; after that a missing record reads as "does not fit".
- **National totals** (`gm_compute_totals`): standing grandeur (every monument not contested: prestige, cultural pull), regime grandeur (fitting regime and ruler monuments: legitimacy), one per dedication with a national effect, and one signed total per IG (approvals minus oppositions, plus its ledger, minus contested monuments whose "new order" it is), each through the curve on its absolute value. `gm_zero_ledgers`: when the last monument is gone and every ledger is idle, the ledgers are zeroed before the pulse stops.
- **Contests** (`gm_check_contests`, skipped while the country has `te_cw_role`): a bound monument that stops fitting records `gm_base_ig` / `gm_supporter_ig` flags and becomes contested; one that fits again is restored. `gm_state_changed_hands` contests a conquered regime or ruler monument and makes a conquered shrine heritage; a monument already contested when its state changes hands is contested afresh for the new owner. The winner of a civil war adopts the loser's monuments (`gm_repair_after_civil_war`, in `te_civil_war_on_won`): while the loser still resolves it rewrites `gm_raised_by` / `gm_tr_raised_by` from the loser to the winner, on every state and on the `gm_monuments`, `gm_trophies` and `gm_shadows` containers, so a monument that reaches the winner later is already its own (the loser is deleted within about two weeks, #1136). A loser still alive, a secession that won, keeps its monuments except those in the winner's states. Choices: `gm_state_tear_down` / `_rededicate` (rebuilt at half level through the shared ladder, which runs to 200) / `_preserve`; each writes decaying ledgers (`gm_teardown_ledger`, `gm_ig_ledger_<ig>`), so nothing stacks. Players get `monument_events.16` (and `.17` per monument); the AI decides monthly. `building_grand_monument` is `downsizeable = no` (AI countries were demolishing monuments as pure upkeep), so these choices, trophies and commission rebuilds, all through `remove_building`, are the only ways one goes or shrinks. A monument that disappears some other way (a save from before the flag) while contested is recorded as torn down by its state pulse (`gm_state_record_teardown`, which also sets `gm_active`), and the JE's `invalid` also waits until no state still carries `gm_seen`, so that teardown is applied before the entry can go.
- **Skins** (`gm_skin`): faith skins by state religion, heritage skins by the language-reform revival partition (`te_heritage_<language>`, also called by `extra_law_events.25`), chosen in `monument_events.11`; `gm_inscription` names a revived language. The choice event is offered to players only; the AI keeps the most specific skin.
- **The dedication ceremony** (`monument_events.2`, a state event) starts through `gm_state_start_ceremony`, which sets what it asks about (`gm_ceremony_mode`): a construction site's dedication (a player's Dedicate button; the AI's from `monument_events.1` on a site's level and from the refresh, `gm_state_offer_missed_ceremony`, once nothing more is queued: `gm_state_ai_dedicates_now`), or Rededicate's (`gm_rededicate_asking`). The trigger reads the mode; an older save's undedicated monument with no mode is asked as before until its migration. *Let it stand as it is, for now* (`gm_state_ceremony_not_yet`) is the site's, weighted 0 for the AI; *Keep it as it is* is Rededicate's. Exactly one default is valid, by complementary triggers. The refresh path exists because five queued levels finishing on one day fired no `on_building_built` (2026-10-07, `scripting_best_practices.md`). `te_debug_monuments.3` asks to rededicate one of our three tallest monuments from the console.
