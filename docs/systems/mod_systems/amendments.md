# Amendments

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Temporary Amendments (Sunset Clauses)

Three amendments can be attached **temporarily** (`add_amendment = { … timeout = <months> }`) as enactment concessions. The engine removes them when the timeout (counted from law activation) elapses and fires `on_amendment_timeout`, which `common/on_actions/amendment_on_actions.txt` routes to a follow-up event offering to make the clause permanent (re-add without `timeout`; `te_legitimacy_drain` + radicals) or let it lapse (Industrialist disapproval + a few capitalist radicals). Engine semantics and authoring rules: `docs/guides/scripting_best_practices.md` § Temporary amendments.

| Amendment | Delivery (DEBATE checkpoint) | cooldown / timeout (months) | Expiry event |
|-----------|------------------------------|-----------------------------|--------------|
| `amendment_env_grandfather_clause` (new; +5% pollution, +5% emissions, +2 Industrialist approval on `law_ministry_of_the_environment`; cancels one institution level) | `ministry_law_events.58` option a | 48 / 120 | `ministry_law_events.59` |
| `amendment_national_champion_exemption` | `extra_law_events.29` option e (option a remains the permanent variant) | 48 / 120 | `extra_law_events.85` |
| `amendment_corporate_data_exemption` | `extra_law_events.31` option e (option a remains the permanent variant) | 12 / 36 | `extra_law_events.86` |

The `has_amendment` guards on events 29/31/58 make the permanent and temporary variants mutually exclusive within one enactment. The expiry events re-derive `sunset_law` from `active_law:<lawgroup>` and `industrialists_ig` in `immediate`, and are not in any checkpoint pool. Deferred from issue #278: a financial-regulation phase-in (the three laws' penalties are structurally different — numeric, none, boolean lock) and a wartime rules-of-war clause (no per-country war-start on-action; would need a timeout on an already-active law).

## Amendment Petitions (`amendment_petition_events`)

Rare events in which an interest group asks for one of the mod's amendments on a law **already in force**, because the country's situation gives it a case. The enactment events (`extra_law_events.28`–`.34`, `.87`–`.90`, `te_fptp_events.2`, `inheritance_events.10`–`.13`, `te_judiciary_events.20`–`.22`, `ministry_law_events.58`) offer amendments only while a law is debated; these reach a law years after it passed. One event per amendment, two options: attach it (option a) or decline (option b, the default).

- **Files**: `events/amendment_petition_events.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/amendment_petition_triggers.txt` (every `te_ap_*` trigger), `common/on_actions/amendment_petition_on_actions.txt`, loc in `te_events_l_english.yml`.
- **Frequency**: each event rolls on its own, from a named sub-action of `on_yearly_pulse_country` (`te_amendment_petition_<amendment>`) whose `random_events` pairs a no-event weight (`N = 0`, the `cultural_hegemony_events_on_actions.txt` idiom) with the event, the two summing to 1000. The event's weight is its yearly chance in tenths of a percent while the country qualifies (the Chance column below), and a country that doesn't qualify sees nothing however the engine treats a failing event. The chance follows how long the situation lasts: lasting ones (a multicultural population, a dominant governing group) roll every year they hold, so they roll low; a war or an election campaign rolls high. Every event's trigger also asks `te_ap_window_open`: each petition sets the country variable `te_ap_recent_petition` for five years in its `immediate`, whatever the answer, so a country sees at most one petition every five years. Each event also has its own 20-year cooldown (`very_long_modifier_time`), so a declined petition doesn't come back soon.
- **Law qualification**: `te_ap_law_ok_<amendment>` holds when a law the amendment allows is in force (the amendment's `allowed_laws`, restated; keep the two in step), the law doesn't carry the amendment yet, and no law of its group is being enacted (`te_ap_enacting_in_<group>` lists every law in the group, variants included; a law added to one of these groups belongs in its list). It is also each event's `cancellation_trigger`. The trigger restates the amendment's `possible` too, because `add_amendment` silently does nothing when `possible` fails.
- **Attachment**: `scope:petition_law` (saved from `active_law:<group>`) gets `add_amendment = { type sponsor = scope:petition_sponsor_ig cooldown = 60 }`: permanent, unrepealable for five years. No timeout: a timeout on an active law is unverified (§ Temporary Amendments).
- **Reactions**: option a gives the sponsor `ig_approval_positive_modifier` and the group that opposes the amendment `ig_approval_negative_modifier` (five years, decaying), plus radicals among the pops it hurts; option b gives the sponsor `ig_approval_negative_modifier` and radicals among its supporters or the pops it would have helped. AI weights favour accepting when the sponsor is in government or powerful, and declining when the opposing group is in government.

## First Past the Post (`amendment_first_past_the_post`)

An amendment on the voting laws that squeezes party politics into two parties. It replaces vanilla's culture-hardcoded American two-party system (`usa_two_party_system`: −10 to five parties for any North American Yankee or Dixie country under Presidential Republic, +3 to the Liberals). Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-first-past-the-post-design.md`.

- **Files**: `common/amendments/te_first_past_the_post.txt`; `common/scripted_effects/te_fptp_effects.txt` (ranking, seed, `te_fptp_var`, self-check); `common/scripted_triggers/te_fptp_triggers.txt`; `common/script_values/te_fptp_values.txt` (the two join-weight sizes); `common/on_actions/te_fptp_on_actions.txt`; `events/te_fptp_events.txt`; the generated `common/parties/zz_te_party_join_weights.txt` (`scripts/generators/gen_party_join_weights.py`) and the mod's three party files; `amendment_petition_events.28`; the USA in `common/history/extra_history.txt`.
- **Amendment**: allowed on Landed, Wealth and Census Voting, Universal Suffrage and Anarchy (not Oligarchy, which grants no voting power). `country_legitimacy_ideological_incoherence_mult = -0.25` (the strongest vanilla law reduction) and `country_legitimacy_base_add = -5`. No parent law; `would_sponsor` is membership of one of the two largest parties (`te_fptp_major_member`) once those parties' groups hold 60%+ of the clout (`te_fptp_major_clout`), as the petition asks. Repealable at `legitimacy_to_repeal_amendment`; the AI never repeals it.
- **Join weights**: every party's `join_weight` gives one of the two largest parties `te_fptp_major_party_join_weight` (+5) and any other party `te_fptp_minor_party_join_weight` (−20), while the amendment is in force and two parties are marked. The bonus sits ahead of vanilla's party-size chain (×2 with at most one prior member for a group that isn't powerful, ×0.5 with two, ×0 with three or more; the mod's three parties have ×0.05 at three), so a party still fills up at three groups and a group's fit still picks its major; the penalty comes after every multiplier. So two major parties hold at most six groups between them, and a group that fits neither strongly stays out of parties. An `INJECT` into `join_weight` replaces vanilla's field, so the generator restates each vanilla party's whole block from the game files (dropping `usa_two_party_system`) — rerun it after a vanilla patch (`--check`). The revolutionary party is never ranked or injected.
- **Ranking**: `te_fptp_refresh_major_parties` on the monthly pulse of every country with a voting franchise (one that loses it drops its marks) orders the active parties by `te_fptp_party_clout` (the members' `ig_clout`) and marks the top two: `te_fptp_major_<party type>` on the country (`te_fptp_mark_major_party`, one branch per type; `te_fptp_party_is_major` reads them), `te_fptp_two_majors`, and `te_fptp_major_member` on each member group. Not inside `join_weight`, because all groups re-pick their parties together at a campaign's start. With fewer than two parties nothing is marked and the terms stay off. A new party type needs a line in the mark, clear and `te_fptp_party_is_major` lists and the two terms in its `join_weight` (`test_first_past_the_post.py` checks).
- **`te_fptp_var`** (country): the country holds first past the post. `te_fptp_track_held` sets it while the amendment is in force and removes it when the country sits on an allowed voting law without it (a repeal, a declined `.1`); on a law without elections it stays.
- **Ways in**: the USA's 1836 history (with the Democrats and Whigs pre-marked as its two majors, for the parties formed at game start); `te_fptp_seed`, once per country on its first pulse, for any country `te_fptp_seed_eligible` (vanilla's culture-term conditions plus an allowed voting law: the Confederacy, Texas, the USA in an older save), never for a revolution's rebels; `te_fptp_events.2` "The Winner Takes All" in `on_law_checkpoint_debate` while an allowed voting law is enacted in a country whose two largest parties hold 60%+ of the clout and no `te_fptp_var`; petition `.28`.
- **Changing the voting law**: amendments leave with their law, so `te_fptp_from_law_scope` (`on_law_activated`) fires `te_fptp_events.1` "Counting the Votes" when an allowed voting law arrives in a country with `te_fptp_var` and no amendment. Option a re-attaches it (default); option b adopts proportional representation and removes `te_fptp_var`. Sponsor: the strongest group in a major party; the strongest group outside them takes the other side's approval change.
- **Civil wars**: a revolution's winner continues the nation. The seed skips revolutionaries (`is_revolutionary`), so the winner inherits the nation's `te_fptp_seed_checked` and can't be handed back a repealed amendment; secessionists are seeded as new nations. `te_fptp_repair_after_civil_war` (in `te_civil_war_on_won`) fires `te_fptp_events.1` when the winner holds `te_fptp_var` (the merge hands it over) on an allowed voting law without the amendment, as a change of voting law does: the rebels hold the law they fought for from the outbreak, so no `on_law_activated` fires at the end.
- **Self-check**: `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN fptp_major_parties`, once a game, the first time a country with three or more parties is ranked (two marked, no unmarked party larger than either marked one, compared pairwise, with a positive control that must be 1 or more so a comparison that didn't read can't pass; the second's clout above 0). Fallback for BROKEN: order by `momentum`. Play-test rows: `docs/testing/first-past-the-post-playtest.md`.
