# War

## Rules of War (`lawgroup_rules_of_war`)

Five laws, one identity each (2026-10-09 redesign from a playtest: the old group was one dial at ×1/×2/×3, and the default was a pure malus). Laws in `common/laws/extra_laws.txt` (the RULES OF WAR header), AI trigger `te_row_serious_war` in `common/scripted_triggers/rules_of_war_triggers.txt`, strike bans in `nuclear_program_modifier_types.txt`, pins in `test_rules_of_war.py`. Every country starts on Traditional (`extra_history.txt`).

| Law (key) | Unlock | For | Signature | Other modifiers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total War (`law_total_war`) | Chemical Warfare (was Bombing Aircraft) | the nation mobilized | mobilization speed +25%, war-support loss from casualties −50% | kill rate +0.10, devastation +100%, battles ±25% war support, Ministry of War impact +25%, Foreign Affairs cap −1, reputation −10, anti-war +20%, escalation +25% both ways, UN −2; unlocks three PMs |
| Traditional (`law_traditional_rules_of_war`) | start | the soldier's code | morale loss −5%, army experience +25% | none |
| War Crimes Forbidden (`law_war_crimes_forbidden`) | Wargaming | the law of occupation | radicals from conquest −25% | devastation −25%, kill rate −0.05, Foreign Affairs impact +10%, reputation +2, escalation −10%, UN +0.5 |
| Humanitarian Regulations (`law_humanitarian_regulations`) | Nuclear Weapons | the trusted partner | recovery rate +10%, reputation +6 (the group's highest) | devastation −50%, kill rate −0.05, strategic strike ban, Foreign Affairs impact +25%, anti-war −10%, escalation −10%, UN +1 |
| Limited War (`law_limited_war`) | ICBMs | proportionality, limited aims | war goal maneuver cost +100% in plays it starts, infamy generation −25%, tactical strike ban, escalation −30% (the group's strongest) | battles −20% war support both ways, anti-war −20%, devastation −75%, kill rate −0.05, strategic strike ban, Foreign Affairs impact +25%, reputation +3, UN +1.5 |

- **Limited War prices aims, it doesn't ban them** (owner, 2026-10-09). Gating the subjugation war goals would need `REPLACE:` copies of vanilla war goal types (an `INJECT` can't add to a `possible` block vanilla declares; read for diplomatic actions, `scripting_best_practices.md` § "A second trigger block is rejected at load"), so the law raises `country_initiator_war_goal_maneuver_cost_mult` instead: war goals in plays it starts cost double, still conquerable but fewer at once. A cost increase can't go below zero; Aggressive Coordination's −0.3 (any tier) softens it. Defensive counter-goals are untouched.
- **No law closes a ministry.** Caps move by at most 1 (Ministry of War's law cap is 3, Foreign Affairs' 2). An impact multiplier scales the whole institution block, wage and goods costs included, so it is a mixed lever; only Total War's Ministry of War +25% and the humane laws' Foreign Affairs boosts use one.
- **Nuclear bans are law modifiers**: `country_strategic_nuclear_strikes_forbidden_bool` (Humanitarian Regulations, Limited War) and `country_tactical_nuclear_strikes_forbidden_bool` (Limited War), `script_only` booleans that show in the law tooltip. `nd_war_law_permits_strategic_strike` / `_tactical_strike` read them; `nd_war_law_exception` (struck, a covered country struck, or `nd_fighting_for_existence`) lifts either.
- **AI** (`ai_enact_weight_modifier` only; vanilla's `ai_will_do` is a hard veto): Total War +200 in a serious war (`te_row_serious_war`: at war with a major or great power, or `nd_fighting_for_existence`), −100 at peace; the other four +150 while a country at peace still holds Total War. Enactment time is the default, so a switch costs months, a law slot and the law-change approval swing.
- **Stances** (`ideology_modifications.py`, TW Trad WCF HR LW): aggressive SA A N D SD; traditional N SA N D D; moderate D N A N D; liberal (liberal, liberal_modern, market_liberal, humanitarian) SD D N SA SA; pacifist (pacifist, anarchist) SD D N A SA. The nuclear posture classes compare Total War with Limited War (`nd_stance_*`), so liberals keep Limited War at strongly approve. The six hand-written custom-religion outlooks in `extra_ideologies.txt` name all five.
- **Kept fixed by the redesign:** the UN Authority values (`test_un_policy_pillar.py`), the UN AI hooks (most reward Humanitarian Regulations), the amendments (No-First-Strike Pledge on Humanitarian Regulations / Limited War, Military Necessity Clause on War Crimes Forbidden / Humanitarian Regulations) and the enactment events `extra_law_events.10`, `.11`, `.32`, `.46`, `.47`, `.70`, `.71` (all a humane bill against military pushback).
- **Infamy on taking effect** (`te_row_charge_infamy`, `common/scripted_effects/rules_of_war_effects.txt`). Each law's `on_activate` passes its standing (Total War +25, Traditional 0, War Crimes Forbidden −10, Humanitarian Regulations −15, Limited War −20), and `change_infamy` moves infamy by the difference from `te_row_charged_standing` (`te_row_infamy_standing`, the standing last charged; 0 when unset; `common/script_values/rules_of_war_values.txt`): the full difference toward a looser law, `te_row_infamy_reduction_share` (0.9) of it, rounded toward zero, toward a stricter one. So a move there and back costs 1 to 5 infamy (owner, 2026-10-16: a loop must be a net loss) and cycling the restrictive laws can't wipe it; leaving Total War takes 22 of its 25 back. Rounding to nearest would make the five-point steps (War Crimes Forbidden ↔ Humanitarian Regulations ↔ Limited War) free to loop, since 0.9 × 5 = 4.5. No pulse resyncs the variable to the law in force: a country from an older save was charged nothing, and a resync would hand it a cut. Edge: a revolution that wins a different Rules of War law can leave the winner on the loser's standing (rebels start with no variables), at most 45 off. It renders in the enactment preview.
- **War-crime scandal** `social_tensions_events.7` fires at war under Total War, Traditional or War Crimes Forbidden; its reform option needs Traditional or War Crimes Forbidden (it had needed a humane law the trigger excluded, so it never appeared).

## War Support Feeds (1.14 hook)

Vanilla 1.14 computes each country's weekly war support change in `common/script_values/war_support_values.txt`; its last term, `war_support_from_journal_entries`, is the only slot that accepts a per-line `desc` key. The mod injects into it from **`common/script_values/zz_te_war_support_injections.txt`** (`INJECT:war_support_from_journal_entries`; the `zz_` prefix was a hedge: directives apply after every bare definition, so an INJECT needn't sort after its target, `scripting_best_practices.md` § Load Order (probed 2026-10-26), fact 4). `root` = country, `scope:war` = the war being evaluated; no randomness; it runs per country per war per beat, so conditions are `has_modifier` / pact / war-participant checks only. Labels live in `te_miscellaneous_l_english.yml` (`WAR_SUPPORT_TE_*`).

| System | Condition (root = country) | Per beat |
|---|---|---|
| World War | `je:je_world_war ?= { has_modifier = ww_home_front_strain_modifier }` (2+ years) — the phase modifiers sit on the **journal entry**, not the country | −0.5 |
| World War | same for `ww_prolonged_war_exhaustion_modifier` (4+ years; stacks with the line above) | −0.5 |
| United Nations | `un_condemned_modifier` on root (`else_if` `un_non_binding_rebuke_modifier`, −0.25) | −0.5 |
| United Nations | an enemy in `scope:war` carries `un_condemned_modifier` ("the world is with us") | +0.25 |
| Covert Warfare | root is `second_country` of a `covert_comms_disruption_action` pact whose attacker fights in `scope:war` (flat — operation phase lives on the attacker's containers) | −0.25 |
| Wars of independence | root holds `var:decol_iw_metropole` (a territory's rebels, a colonial subject or secessionists fighting the metropole) and the metropole fights in `scope:war`: "Fighting for independence"; while a great power arms them (`decol_iw_foreign_arms_modifier`), "Foreign arms" +0.25 | +0.5 |
| Wars of independence | an enemy in `scope:war` names root as its metropole: "A war to keep a colony"; "The world condemns it" −0.25 when the condemners hold a third of great-power prestige, −0.5 at two thirds (`decol_iw_condemner_share`, stored by the monthly settle); "Settlers to defend" +0.25 when that enemy carries `decol_iw_settlers` (our people were a tenth of the territory) | −0.25 |
| Nuclear | root lacks `nuclear_power`, an enemy in `scope:war` has it, and root has no armed guarantor (`nd_has_armed_guarantor_against`: an in-force `nuclear_guarantee` from a country believed armed, or an overlord's nuclear umbrella, removes the "Enemy nuclear arsenal" line — that is what extended deterrence is for). Strikes themselves already drain through devastation and the one-off in `nuclear_industrial_strike` | −0.25 |

Scale: vanilla's per-beat factors run from −5 (fully occupied) to about +2; the rival boost / taking loans are ±0.25. War support is 0–100, drifts toward 50, red band ≤ 25.

**Battle war-support modifiers** (`country_war_support_battles_increase_mult` / `_decrease_mult`, vanilla 1.14; every grant sums into `1 + Σ`, floored at 0, no upper clamp). Mod grants — laws in `common/laws/extra_laws.txt`, techs in `common/technology/technologies/era_7.txt` / `era_9.txt`:

| Entity | increase | decrease |
|---|---|---|
| `law_ministry_of_propaganda` (spin machine; always stacks on `law_outlawed_dissent`) | +0.15 | −0.15 |
| `law_state_controlled_internet` (mirrors `law_censorship`) | −0.10 | −0.20 |
| `law_unregulated_internet` / `law_net_neutrality` | +0.20 / +0.15 | same |
| `law_state_secrets` / `law_freedom_of_information` / `law_open_government` | −0.05 / +0.05 / +0.10 | same |
| `law_total_war` / `law_limited_war` (how much of society is invested) | +0.25 / −0.20 | same |
| `television_broadcasting` / `satellite_communications` / `social_media` (the living-room war) | +0.10 / +0.05 / +0.15 | +0.15 / +0.10 / +0.20 |

The worst-case mod-added negative `decrease` sum is −0.60 (`/modifier-grants/country_war_support_battles_decrease_mult?scope=mod`), so with vanilla `law_outlawed_dissent` (−0.4) it just reaches the floor (Limited War, State-Controlled Internet, State Secrets and the Ministry of Propaganda under Outlawed Dissent); vanilla's `war_propaganda` + `mass_propaganda` techs (−0.2 each) can then floor it, which is intentional — a fully censored state never hears about its defeats. `decrease_mult` is `color=bad`, so a beneficial negative renders red (vanilla's propaganda techs share the quirk). The casualties axis (`country_war_support_casualties_mult`) is deliberately untouched: the mod already saturates it (`law_total_war` −0.50 alone is twice vanilla's largest law value; it was −0.75 before the 2026-10-09 Rules of War redesign).

**Battlefield "losing" reads** — `is_losing_war_against = { ENEMY = scope:x }` in `common/scripted_triggers/nuke_triggers.txt`: most size-weighted battles in the shared war lost (`size_weighted_won_battles_fraction < 0.35` after `num_significant_battles >= 5`) or `enemy_occupation >= 0.25`. Used by the nuke AI `will_propose` (strategic and tactical; the previous war-support proxy also fired for merely unpopular wars) and as the +5 desperation term in the wartime covert ops. The nuke `propose_score` is ×1.5 in a war where `is_at_war_with_rival = ROOT`. `enemy_side_occupation` is the share of the *enemy* we hold (high = winning) and is not a losing read.

**World War stalemate event** `world_war_events.31` (weight 5 in the `je_world_war` monthly pulse): `ww_years_elapsed >= 2` (world-war-wide counter) **and** a war with `war_duration_months >= 24`, `num_significant_battles >= 10`, `has_stalled_wargoal_held_by = ROOT`. Options use one-off `add_war_war_support` (+5 hold the line / −10 armistice talks); `add_war_support_change` is avoided because it accumulates for the war (see `docs/guides/scripting_best_practices.md`).

**Not moved into the hook:** `war_propaganda_on_action` (`extra_on_actions.txt`, monthly per-state `add_war_war_support` driven by the state-scoped `state_war_support_monthly_add`, which does not cascade to a country-scope read) stays a one-off level change; a phase-scaled covert line would need a country variable written by the covert JE pulse.

## Wartime Munitions Demand

**Peacetime armies train with half of vanilla's ammunition. A mobilized battalion burns four times that, which is twice vanilla's peacetime figure.** Ammunition is the good a war uses up rather than wears out, so its demand should swing hard between peace and war. That swing is what makes a munitions industry, imports and the Strategic Reserve matter. In vanilla, mobilization raised ammunition only to 1.5× peacetime, or 2× with extra and luxurious supplies.

- **Peacetime side:** a unit's `upkeep_modifier` is what its barracks buys every week, whether or not it is mobilized. Mod units in `common/combat_unit_types/extra_combat_units.txt` carry half their former ammunition. Vanilla units take an `INJECT` diff of minus half vanilla's value, summed with vanilla's own (squad infantry: 3 − 1.5), so a vanilla patch to any other good flows through untouched. `REPLACE:combat_unit_type_heavy_tank` carries the halved value directly.
- **Wartime side:** `common/mobilization_options/te_wartime_munitions_injections.txt` raises `mobilization_option_basic_supplies`, which no army can switch off, from +50% to +300% ammunition while mobilized. It also cancels the +25% that extra and luxurious supplies each added, so ammunition has a single wartime knob. The per-battalion additions of the mod's own options (Entrenchment +5, Missile Defense +10, Exoskeleton Suits +40) come on top and were not changed.
- **Why ×4:** with `BUY_SELL_DIFF_AT_MAX_FACTOR = 4` and `GOODS_SHORTAGE_PENALTY_THRESHOLD = 0.25`, four times the demand pushes a good whose demand is almost all military to its maximum price, and shortage penalties start only beyond that. A war therefore strains the munitions market without, by itself, starving barracks, whose input shortages cut formation supply and organization.
- **Mobilization is not war.** The spike starts when an army mobilizes, including in a diplomatic play that never becomes a war, and it tapers over the 90-day demobilization.
- **The Strategic Reserve is the buffer.** Its hub is nearly free (`construction_cost_very_low`, 100 points), the AI values it at 2000 in peacetime for a major power and 10 000 more above 10 million GDP, and AI reserves run every good on Stabilize Prices, buying below −20% up to full capacity and releasing above +30% down to empty, on an 8-week average. A war's ammunition spike therefore draws AI stockpiles into the market (`docs/systems/strategic_reserve_system.md` §7).
- **Drift guard:** `test_wartime_munitions.py` checks every diff against `vanilla_parsed/`. It fails when a vanilla unit's ammunition changes, when a new vanilla unit starts using ammunition without a diff, or when a supplies option's vanilla value moves.
- **Not yet read in play:** whether an `INJECT` sums on combat unit types and mobilization options (it does for ranks, techs and static modifiers). The tells are a squad-infantry barracks buying 1.5 ammunition per battalion in peace, and basic supplies' tooltip showing +300% Ammunition next to +50% for the other goods. Two more things to read in the same launch: how formation supply consumption, which 1.14 derives from goods costs, responds; and whether the multiplier touches only the mobilized battalions' own upkeep or the whole barracks.
