# State Panel and Nation Tab

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## State Panel GUI Enhancements

The mod's tiles on the state view's Overview (redesigned in `gui/state-tiles`, 2026-11-24). Each is a `te_state_tile`, one anatomy for all: vanilla's 260-wide tile with its icon in a 60 inset box at the top left (the icon 52), and a 170 text column holding the caption (the concept alone) and then aligned label/value rows; the detail is in the tooltip. They stand in two columns of their own, `te_state_tiles` (`gui/te_state_panel_widgets.gui`), which `states_panel.gui` mounts once after vanilla's whole Overview (its status grid with the state's traits, then the decrees); the full-width cards follow (Grand Monument, construction site, trophies, then tourism).

- **Columns**, each a vertical flow fixed at 260, so no tile waits on the one beside it (the old `wrap_count = 2` grid made each row as tall as its taller tile). Left: Arable Land, Dynastic Wealth, Seabed Licences, Ocean Basin, Solar Collector, Antimatter Facility, UN Mission. Right: Local Climate, Migration Crowding, Power Grid, Homeland Dynamics. Each opens with its always-shown tile; the split keeps the columns within one short tile in common states (heights per state in the PR). `test_state_tiles.py` holds the order, one mount per tile, and the Overview's order.
- **GDP per Capita** is the mod's tile at the head of vanilla's grid, in vanilla's anatomy (70 icon, 160 column): a caption and two rows, Local and National (`te_state_vanilla_tile_row`).

  - `te_state_stat_row`: a fixed-width label column plus a fixed-width value column with its text anchored right, so a stack of rows ends at one x.
  - `te_state_tile_row` sizes it for a tile's 170 text column, one split for every reading (92 + 4 + 74; the longest English label, "Pass-through", is 86 at OpenSans 14, the longest value, "Moderate", 68 bold). `te_state_tile_word_row` (52 + 114) is for a name as the value (the grid's hub, a basin), which wraps rather than elides; `te_state_tile_culture_row` (115 + 51) for a culture's name as the label; `te_state_tile_line` for a line across the column (the UN mission's kind, homeland status lines); `te_state_tile_bar` for a bar under a row. No instance sets widths of its own (`test_state_tiles.py`'s width budget reads the game font's glyph widths). `te_state_wide_row` drops the icon slot for the tourism card's headers and footers.

- **Tourism card** (`te_state_tourism_card`): a 530 px card, the last thing on the Overview, under any monument card (it was between the population block and the status grid, where it pushed every state's tiles down).

These also carry airports (+0.15 per level on `pm_airport`/`pm_spaceport`), highways (+0.05 on the civil, electric civil and autonomous methods, +0.025 on the industrial and electric industrial ones, all in `state_modifiers`), decrees, pollution, grand monuments and so on, and lag the scripted sources by up to a month.
  - Per-source tooltips `te_state_tourism_*_tt` state each tier schedule.
- **Loc keys:** `TE_STATE_*_STATUS` (tile title), `TE_STATE_*_ROW_*` / `TE_STATE_POINTS_ROW_*` (row labels), `TE_STATE_*_TT` (tooltip) and `te_state_tourism_*` in `localization/english/te_miscellaneous_l_english.yml`.

- **Concepts:** Defined in `common/game_concepts/extra_concepts.txt`, with textures for hoverable tooltip links. The tourism rows label with `concept_tourism_*` and reuse those concept textures as row icons.
- **Pattern:** Use `[concept_X]` for hoverable concept links, `[State.GetModifier.GetValueWithBreakdownFor('modifier_key')]` for modifier breakdowns, and `[State.MakeScope.ScriptValue('sv_name')]` for computed values. A value cell that shows one expression inlines it as `raw_text = "#v [...]#!"`.

## Construction List Columns

When the player picks a building to construct, vanilla lists the states it can go in (`map_list_panel.gui`). One column shows each state's productivity impact, except for Government Administration (tax capacity balance) and Trade Center (trade capacity balance). `gui/00_te_build_list_columns.gui` adds the mod's cases (a Workshop request, 2026-11-15):

| Building | Cell | Cell tooltip | Header |
|---|---|---|---|
| National Park | `State.GetStateRegion.GetPollutionImpact\|%R1`, as vanilla's state pollution list shows it | vanilla's `STATE_POLLUTION` (impact, trend, generated pollution) | `state_status_icons/pollution.dds`, `te_build_list_pollution_header_tt` |
| Public Housing | the crowding pull penalty, `migration_crowding_pull_pct\|%0-` (red above 0) | `te_build_list_crowding_tt`: penalty, population, threshold | `generic_icons/population.dds`, `te_build_list_crowding_header_tt` |
| Carbon Conversion Works | the state's Electricity price, `te_build_list_electricity_price` (`extra_script_values.txt`: `sg:electricity` pricier and cheaper over `te_electricity_base_price`, read as the power grid's `te_grid_chk_price_now_value`, whose self-check matched the engine's price in game) | `te_build_list_electricity_tt`: price and its share of base, the state's Electricity production and consumption, and vanilla's productivity impact | `goods_icons/electricity.dds`, `te_build_list_electricity_header_tt` |

- **How:** the file redefines three vanilla types (gotcha #30): the cell `productivity_or_other_information`, which both of the list's layouts instance, and the header rows `construction_headers_condensed` and `construction_headers_full`. It is generated (`scripts/generators/gen_build_list_columns.py`, table `COLUMNS`) from `test_fixtures/vanilla_gui/map_list_panel.gui`, so the rest of vanilla's 5,400-line file stays vanilla's. Productivity's cell and header are hidden for the buildings in `COLUMNS`, and each gets a cell and a header in the same slot.
- **No sorting:** `MapListPanel.Sort` takes a key the engine knows (`tax_capacity_balance`, `trade_capacity_balance`, ...). The data types don't list the keys and script can't add one, so the mod's headers have no `onclick`, and their tooltips say the column can't be sorted.
- **Compatibility:** no Workshop mod installed here (2026-11-15) redefines the three types or ships `map_list_panel.gui`. CMF and the Multi-line Production Methods framework redefine the list's row (`construction_interaction_item_full`) and keep instancing the cell, so the cell's change reaches their row too. A mod that overrides the whole of `map_list_panel.gui` would lose these three types to this file. The three were unchanged from 1.13.0 to 1.15.1.
- **Why Electricity for Carbon Conversion Works:** it unlocks with Carbon Capture and Storage (era 10) on Direct Air Capture, which makes no goods and runs only on subsidies, so its productivity is zero in every state. Synthetic Coal (Genetic Engineering, era 11) sells coal, at one price across a market. Both buy 1,200 Electricity a staffed level (98% and 99% of their inputs at base prices), and Electricity is a local good, so the state's price is what varies a level's running cost between states under either method. The cell can't tell which method a new level would run: the GUI has no lookup of a production method by key, and which methods the engine gives the listed building in a state that has none isn't known offline. Vanilla's productivity figure moved to the cell's tooltip for the coal method. Neither method has a state pollution effect (the automation methods add 2.5 to 5 pollution a level, as on chemical plants), and staffing is already in the list's labor columns. Where the state has no Electricity market at all the price reads as base; its tooltip shows production and consumption of 0.
- **Open:** the Settlement Authority (frontier density), the Grid Transfer Station and the other state-funded buildings; adding one is a `COLUMNS` row.
- **In game:** unverified; `docs/testing/build-list-columns-playtest.md`.

## Nation Tab

Spec: `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-17-national-modifiers-tab-design.md`. The first tab of the Timeline Extended window (`gui/te_systems_window.gui`), always open: no journal entry, no game rule, so the window's launcher has no rule gate either. Everything reads the shown country, `Country`, which each mount supplies: the player's in the window, any country's in National Info, where the tab is public and shows another country all of it, with no control to hide (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-charts-help-national-info-design.md` § 6.4; `test_te_system_tab_bodies.py`, and `test_nation_tab.py`'s `ForeignViewTest`). The modifier types' names the type rows splice keep `GetPlayer`: they render wherever the engine names a type and ask only a game rule (`WORLD_LOC`).

- **What it shows** (`gui/te_nation_tab.gui`): five overview boxes (Override Capacity's strongest Forceful Legislation option, Dynastic Wealth and its target, Construction Cost, Global Technology, Judicial Independence and its ceiling), then five open sections, Governance, Society, Economy, Science and Space, International, each its score rows and then its type rows, then Where These Come From, collapsed, and All Modifiers, collapsed. How the Nation Works is the Help page (`te_nation_help_popup`, 2026-11-02), which the "?" in the tab's header opens (the tab's frame, `te_nation_tab_body`, gave it a header 2026-11-03, when the link went). Rows: Legislative Override Capacity (`GetValueWithBreakdownFor('country_legislative_override_capacity_add')`, a bar from 0 to +30 with lines at 0, 2 and 4 (every value below 0 plays the same, so the bar starts there and the figure carries a negative value; 30 is about the most a country holds at once; `te_nation_override_bar`), and the strongest Forceful Legislation option open, which `te_nation_override_tier` reads from the capacity and the authority produced, as the event's triggers do), Judicial Independence once the score is seeded (`te_jud_display` heading for `te_jud_ceiling`, the ceiling's two sources by breakdown, and the courts' four scaled modifiers under it; the Court Overhaul is timed and stays in the list), the Algorithmic Mandate under Algorithmic Governance, Dynastic Wealth, Construction Cost (`te_cc_scaling_factor` − 1, the scaling's own effect; its tooltip breaks a point's cost into factors, § Construction Cost Scaling), Private Construction while either of its modifiers is in force, Global Technological Development (`var:te_global_tech_spread_applied`, which `tech_spread_on_action` sets beside the modifier, so the GUI never reruns the 54-technology sweep), and in International, once a UN exists, the United Nations lines: the seat (`un_disp_member_code`), International Standing with the score (`un_disp_standing_code`, `un_disp_standing`) and UN Dues with any months in arrears (`un_disp_dues_code`, `un_disp_arrears_months`; the sum owed and Article 19 on hover). They read the UN overview's own codes (`un_overview_display_values.txt`) and its words (`je_un_ov_*`), so the two never disagree; the codes ask the entry's modifiers through `je:` in script, which reads any country. International shows while they do or its type rows do. The rows' values are in `common/script_values/te_nation_values.txt`.
- **The type rows** (#1030): one per modifier type the tab's modifiers carry, "Arable Land: +45%", the type's own name and `GetValueWithBreakdownFor`, so the hover lists every source (technologies, laws, institutions, principles, timed modifiers and the tab's own, a scaled one with its multiplier), and the type's `_desc` on the row's hover. Grouped under subheadings, and sectioned by what a type affects, not which system grants it: one national total can't repeat in four sections, so Prestige is International's even where a space milestone grants it. A row shows while the country holds one of the tab's modifiers that carries it (`te_nation_type_<type>_count`), not whenever the total is non-zero, which would make the tab a copy of the country panel's Modifiers tab. A `state_`, `building_`, `interest_group_` or `character_` row is the country's contribution, and its hover says the scope's own sources are in its panel (the owner, 2026-10-17: the country's modifier holds those fields). `_bool` types are status lines ("In force", `GetFullEntryDescFor` on hover). 35 types get no row: a score row, the Banking tab or their own modifier shows them (`NOT_A_ROW`).
- **The lists** are the country's own timed modifiers (`Country.GetTimedModifiers`), filtered by name (gotcha #40 of `gui_modding_guide.md`): icon, name, the modifier's own tooltip, so the engine renders the effects at their current scale. A score row's own ("what it gives now") sit under it; the sections' lists sit in Where These Come From, collapsed, under each section's name. A section with nothing to show draws nothing. **All Modifiers** (`te_nation_all_panel`, owner, 2026-11-03, #1372) is the same datamodel with no filter: every timed modifier the country has, in vanilla's row (icon, name, the time left, the modifier's tooltip), what the country panel's Information tab listed until then. It is collapsed by default (`GetVariableSystem` flag `te_nation_all_open`), and its rows sit inside the panel that flag shows, so a closed section evaluates none of them. It reads the window's `Country` and is public, so National Info shows another country's too.
- **The registry** (`scripts/generators/gen_nation_tab.py`) places every static modifier script adds without a duration, parameterized names expanded from their callers (700 on 2026-10-17; a duration of `-1` is forever, so `days = -1` counts as permanent): `SECTIONS` (a section's list), `ROWS` (shown under a row), `KEEP_IN_LIST` (overrides in none of the tab's lists, so All Modifiers alone shows them, each with its reason: `nuclear_power` until it moves to its entry, the covert operations' effects on a target, debug modifiers; the name dates from when they were kept in the country panel's list), `NOT_ON_COUNTRY` (lands on an entry, a state, a building, an interest group or a power bloc). A second registry places the types those modifiers carry: `TYPE_ROWS` (section, group, types), `STATUS_ROWS` (bools) and `NOT_A_ROW` (with what shows it). The budget breakdown's sources (`gen_budget_breakdown.SOURCES`) get no list and no row: the Budget panel itemizes them. An entry may name a family with one `*`; an exact name wins. A third, `SOURCE_GROUPS`, puts every name the sections list in one group of one system's modifiers, at most 32 to a group, commonest first; names that read the same in English share a group (`te_tax_ig_view_*`, one view text for every interest group), since a list matches the text and its count the key. `SAME_NAME` pairs the one modifier outside the lists that reads like a listed one (the UN Development Fund's treasury grant, its pool grant's twin), so the counts count it as the list shows it.
- **Generated, never hand-edited:** `gui/te_nation_generated.gui` (the filters `te_nation_list_<section>` and `te_nation_row_<row>`, and the type rows `te_nation_types_<section>`), `common/script_values/te_nation_generated_values.txt` (their `has_modifier` counts, each group's, and each type row's, group's and section's), `common/scripted_effects/te_nation_generated_effects.txt` (`te_nation_refresh_source_flags`), `localization/english/te_nation_generated_l_english.yml` (each row's text and hover, splicing the type's name and `_desc`; organize_loc's `NATION_GENERATED`) and `gui/00_te_country_panel_information.gui`.
- **The country panel** (every country's, the player's and the AI's): `00_te_country_panel_information.gui` redefines vanilla's `country_panel_information_content` (gotcha #30), copied from `test_fixtures/vanilla_gui/country_panel.gui` with one `### MOD: Nation tab` block that replaces the MODIFIERS list with a line, "Modifiers: 45" (`GetDataModelSize(Country.GetTimedModifiers)`, no filter, no script value), and a goto beside it: into the window's Nation tab for the player's own country (2026-10-27 audit, NAT-1), and for another country into National Info on its Nation tab (`te_ni_tab = nation`, then `InformationPanelBar.OpenPopBrowserPanel( Country.Self )`, as its Politics tab opens the window). The line's hover says they are under All Modifiers there and why: the panel redraws everything in it every frame, and sorting the lasting modifiers out of the list row by row (260 names a row, about 11,700 comparisons a frame on New York in 1910) made it lag (owner, 2026-11-03, #1372). The MODIFIERS header keeps vanilla's test, and the line shows on the same one. **Where These Come From's lists are gated by group**: `te_nation_source_flags_on_action`, on `extra_on_actions.txt`'s `on_monthly_pulse_country` (every country, no rule gate), and `te_nation.1` (`events/te_nation_events.txt`), which `te_nation_source_flags_seed_on_action` raises for every country two days after the lobby (not the first day: `te_tax.3`'s migration adds the Tax Code's interest-group views a day later, through `te_tax.4`), run `te_nation_refresh_source_flags`, which sets `te_nation_src_<group>` on the country to how many of the group's modifiers it holds, 0 included, then a sentinel, `te_nation_src_set_<hash>`, named from the group set so a change of groups resets it. A row is compared only with the names of groups whose flag is above 0; the flags are read bare, since each list sits in a container shown on its count, and every count is 0 until the sentinel is set, so a country the pulse hasn't reached (an old save's first 30 days, a new country) draws no list and reads no unset variable. The counts read the same flags, so a list and its count agree; a modifier added mid-month in a group whose flag is off is missing from both until the country's next pass. A revolution's winner inherits the loser's flags and sentinel (variables carry over), so its lists read the loser's groups until its own next pass, the same lag. A score row's list (one to four names) compares live. The saving rests on `And` skipping its second argument when the first is false (gotcha #42 of `gui_modding_guide.md`), unproven beyond one case; the log safety doesn't. If the redefinition loses to vanilla's in game, the fallback is a full-file `country_panel.gui`. **The panel's footer** (owner, 2026-11-21): the same file redefines vanilla's `country_panel` type too, with one `### MOD: National Info` block that puts another country's National Info button (`POP_BROWSER`, `OpenPopBrowserPanel( Country.Self )`) in the fixed footer above Draft a Treaty, on the treaty's own test, so it shows on every tab whatever the scroll. Vanilla's button at the foot of the Politics tab stays (`country_politics_panel.gui`, not in the repository). Play-test: `docs/testing/charts-help-national-info-playtest.md`, "National Info in the country panel's footer".
- **Adding a permanent modifier:** one registry line, and a `SOURCE_GROUPS` line if a section lists it and no family there matches it, then `python3 scripts/generators/gen_nation_tab.py` (`--check` to test), and commit its five outputs. `test_nation_tab.py` fails while a candidate has no line or two, a line places nothing, a listed name has no group or two, two names that read the same sit in different groups, an output is stale, or a listed name shares its display name with one outside the lists (the filter matches names). A field the tab hasn't seen before needs a type registry line too, and fails the same way until it has one.
- **Judgment calls for the owner** (each one line to move): the monetary bands, the tax code's interest-group views and the colonial programmes under Economy; the colonial empire's endings and the Imperial Federation and Mandate System under Economy; the nuclear taboo and the Foreign Cultural Benchmark (`cultural_hegemony_foreign_benchmark`, added to the country by the hegemony entry's pulse) under International; the covert effects and `gm_name_carrier_slot` left to All Modifiers.
- **Play-test list:** `docs/testing/nation-tab-playtest.md`.

## Timeline Extended Window Tabs

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-te-window-wide-mode-design.md` (§§ 9.1, 9.3). The window (`gui/te_systems_window.gui`) keeps its frame, its launcher and its root by hand. Everything else comes from one registry.

- **The registry** (`TABS` in `scripts/generators/gen_te_systems_window.py`): per tab, its key (the stored `te_systems_window_tab` value), its strip, its label and tooltips, its open and on-strip gates, the scripted GUI whose checklist a grayed tab shows, and its body type. Nation is first, and the fallback. A strip of five is vanilla's `tab_buttons`; one of six, the window's own systems' since Power Grids (owner, 2026-11-05) and the second strip since Private Actors joined it (2026-11-28, the first being full), is `te_tab_buttons_six` (`SLOTS_SIX`). `SR_MILESTONES` lists the Space Race's nine entries.
- **Generated, never hand-edited:** `gui/te_systems_window_generated.gui` holds the selection templates, the strips (`te_systems_window_strips_narrow`), the scroll area's column of tab mounts (`te_systems_window_tabs_narrow`) and the Space Race's per-milestone frames (`te_sr_window_milestones`). The generator is manual, not post-load, and `--check` reports a stale file.
- **The tab frame** (`te_system_tab_frame`, `gui/te_system_tab_widgets.gui`) has a block for each part: the header, the gate on a parent of the `GetPlayerJournalEntry` datacontext, the fixed 520 column, the content, the entry's modifier strip and Open Journal Entry. A system's body type fills them: `te_gm_tab_body`, `te_ce_tab_body` and `te_seabed_tab_body`, each in its system's file. Each body declares a layout block (`te_<system>_tab_layout`) for a wide mount to override. The Space Race's `te_sr_tab_body` is a plain column: the milestones, then Space Powers and Orbit, then How the Space Race Works.
- **The entry's modifier strip** (#1383, 2026-11-12; not yet seen in game outside the journal window). A modifier script puts on a journal entry shows only in the journal window's header strip unless a tab draws it, so every body with an entry fills the frame's `tab_entry_modifiers` block with `te_je_modifier_strip = {}`: the two datamodels of vanilla's row, `JournalEntry.GetScalingModifiers` and `GetTimedModifiers`, each icon with its tooltip, under `te_own_country_only`. Since 2026-11-27 (from #2041; a new game's Grand Monuments tab drew eight identical busts) it has the Space Race's grouped form: a panel under a ruled Effects heading, the scaling icons under Ongoing, the timed ones under Timed with their name and time left, each group only with something in it. The block is empty by default, as a body with no entry has no `JournalEntry`. Each Space Race milestone draws it in its own part (`gen_te_systems_window._milestone`), in a grouped form of its own (2026-11-24; not yet seen in game): `te_sr_program_effects` (`space_race_widget.gui`), the same two datamodels in a panel under a ruled Program Effects subheading, the scaling icons under Ongoing, then the timed ones under Timed, one row each with its name and `TimedModifier.GetRemainingDuration`; the panel and each group draw only with something in them. About thirty icons with no heading had read as part of the overview's Program row in full screen. `test_te_entry_modifier_strip.py` scans the `je:je_<x> = { add_modifier ... }` sites and `scaling_modifiers_while_active` and fails on an entry with a tab that draws no strip, or with no tab that isn't in its `NO_TAB` list: on 2026-11-12, `je_world_war`, `je_civil_rights` and `je_world_climate`, whose modifiers only the journal window shows. Another country's National Info shows no strip; a modifier a reader of that panel needs goes through a script value on `Country`, as Cultural Hegemony's bonus does (#1382).
- **The mirrored tabs** (2026-11-02, strips 2 and 3): the ten system tabs that live in vanilla panels, Banking, Tax Code and Budget Breakdown (Budget), Strategic Reserve and Global Warming (Market), Demographics (Population), Cultural Hegemony (Society), United Nations (Diplomacy), Nuclear Weapons and Covert Warfare (Military). Each host tab and the window's mount one body type (`te_banking_tab_body`, `te_tax_tab_body`, `te_st_res_tab_body`, `te_gw_tab_body`, `te_ch_tab_body`, `te_un_tab_body`, `te_nuclear_tab_body`, `te_covert_tab_body`, in each system's layout file), so the two can't drift. The host keeps its outer flowcontainer and anything that reads its panel: the Market panel fills Global Warming's `te_gw_tab_market_view` (its own market's view and another market's) and the Reserve's `te_st_res_tab_elsewhere` and `te_st_res_tab_gate` (the other-market note and the own-market test); the window shows the bodies' defaults, the player's own market. Budget Breakdown mounts `te_budget_breakdown_panel`, always open. Demographics mounts `te_demog_window_body`, the Population tab's sections less the strata incomes (they read `PopsOverviewPanel`), with the player's country as context. The registry reuses each host's gates and, where it fits the window, its tooltip; names are the entries'. Tax Code's draft and bill summaries sit in both frames' `fixed_bottom` (`te_systems_window_tax_footer`), as in Budget's.
- **Two frames:** the side panel (`te_systems_window_panel`, `default_block_window`) and the full screen (`te_systems_window_panel_wide`, on `te_fullscreen_block_window`, generated from vanilla's `fullscreen_block_window`). The client-side key `te_systems_window_wide` picks one, and a text button in each frame's `fixed_top` sets or clears it. The full-screen frame's strips sit side by side at their narrow width (`te_systems_window_strips_wide`), and its column is 1,620 wide (`te_systems_window_tabs_wide`). Each mount there holds the tab's wide layout (the `Tab`'s `wide`: `te_<system>_wide_sections` in its system's file, or for Demographics the Census Data window's `te_demog_browser_sections`), the narrow layout's sections in columns of 520 (`te_ui_columns_three` or `te_ui_columns_two`): it fills the body's layout block, or replaces the body for a tab that declares none (`layout=None`). While the full-screen frame draws, the HUD parts vanilla hides under its own full screens hide too (the outliner, the "Paused" button, the lens toolbar): `gui/00_te_fullscreen_hide.gui` redefines vanilla's three visibility templates with a term that is the window root's `visible` and the frame's, so it follows the window by every route that closes it (`test_te_fullscreen_hud.py`).
- **The full-screen page** (2026-11-24, spec § 7.1; not yet seen in game). Three rules, the same on every tab:
  - **The title spans the frame.** Each tab's title is one bar 1,620 wide (`gen.WIDE_WIDTH`: the three columns and their gutters, the strips' row above it), its map button and "?" at the right end. Its width is a constant, so it never moves when a column opens or closes (style guide rule 8). Each body's header carries `blockoverride "size" { block "te_tab_header_size" { size = { 554 44 } } }` before its "?", vanilla's own 554; only the window's full-screen mount fills it (`gen._wide_content`), so the side panel, the vanilla panels' tabs and National Info draw the 554 header as before. The Space Race's body has no header, so its full-screen mount draws the page's title (the tab's name and the "?") above the composer (`gen._own_titles`); its milestones' headers stay, at their column's 520 (`sr_window_header_size`, filled by `te_sr_wide_sections`).
  - **The columns in use are centred as a group** under the title: `te_ui_columns_*` is centred and every column is a fixed 520, and a column that can be empty closes up on its sections' own gates joined (`column_<n>_visible`; `assert_column_closes_with_its_sections` builds the joined Or for any number of sections). What decides the group's width is those gates, the tab's state, never a row arriving later inside a column. Today: Nation's column 3 (four gates), Banking's 2 and 3 (a market economy, the cycle), Tax Code's 2 and 3 (the Light code), Grand Monuments' 3, Global Warming's 1 and 2 (the rule), the Space Race's 2 (solar colonization), Covert Warfare's 3 (a known operation), Nuclear Weapons' 3 (the player's own, so another country's two columns in National Info sit centred).
  - **Columns balanced by height, each section whole, a bar no wider than its column.** The side panel's order down the columns where it balances; the owner's columns stand (Grand Monuments, Banking, Demographics, Nuclear Weapons, the Space Race). Nation is overview and Governance / Society and the Economy / the rest; Power Grids one section a column; Global Warming the overview and Top Emitters / Mitigation Policies / the Fossil Transition and History (Top Emitters reads before the policies there). Nation's section headings (`te_nation_header`, vanilla's 554) take their column's 520 through `te_nation_header_size`, filled per section by `te_nation_wide_sections`. `test_te_full_screen_layout.py` holds every composer to the rules: 520 columns 30 apart, one row, each side-panel section drawn once, nothing wider than 520 unless the composer narrows it, and every header given a width of its own.
- **Adding a tab:** write its body type and its wide layout, add a `Tab` line and its loc (label, tooltip, locked line), run `python3 scripts/generators/gen_te_systems_window.py`, and commit the generated file. A `Tab` takes no default for `wide`, so the registry can't hold a tab without a wide layout. `test_te_systems_window.py` fails while that file is stale, and evaluates the tab selection for each stored value. `test_te_system_tab_bodies.py` checks the frame and the bodies, and that nothing a tab's body or wide layout reaches, through instances and base types, reads a host panel. The system's layout test holds the wide layout to the narrow one with `gui_test_helpers.assert_wide_matches_narrow` (`docs/guides/python_tools.md`).

## National Info

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-charts-help-national-info-design.md` (§§ 6.2, 7.3); plan PR F1. Vanilla's Census Data window (`gui/pop_browser_panel.gui`, overridden), renamed National Info, shows the country it was opened for: the player's from the Population panel's button, any other's from the foot of its Politics tab.

- **The tabs.** Pops (vanilla's window) first, then each tab of the Timeline Extended window's registry with `public = True`, in `NI_ORDER` (`gen_te_systems_window.py`): Demographics, Nation, Banking, Tax Code, Grand Monuments, the Colonial Empire, the Seabed, Cultural Hegemony, the Space Race, Nuclear Weapons, Power Grids and Private Actors, five a strip side by side, each strip 540 wide but a short last one, which is narrowed to its tabs (`strip_width`) so they are as wide as a full strip's rather than stretched across 540 (2026-11-24; seen in game with every listed tab showing). The width follows the tabs a strip lists, not the tabs it shows: a tab its game rule hides still widens the others on its strip (the first strip's four at about 131 with the Tax Code rule off, the default), and pinning each tab to one width instead drew no first strip in game (#2039). Which are shown is `Tab.public` alone, and the tests derive every expectation from it. The tab shown is the client-side `te_ni_tab`: the last chosen while it is open for that country, otherwise Pops, so switching countries never shows a tab that isn't running there. A grayed tab's tooltip is the player's checklist for the player's own country and one line ("Not running in France") for another.
- **Generated, never hand-edited:** `gui/te_national_info_generated.gui`, the selection templates (`te_ni_<tab>_selected` / `_unselected`, the strip's and the column's gates), the strips (`te_ni_strips`) and the column of mounts (`te_ni_tabs`). The window places them in its `### MOD: National Info` blocks; outside them it is vanilla's file (`test_demographics_census_window.py`).
- **Two mounts a tab.** The player's own country gets the Timeline Extended window's full-screen mount, the body with its gate, its entry and its controls, and below it what the window draws in its frames' `fixed_bottom` (`Tab.footer`: Tax Code's draft and bill summaries, with Introduce, Discard and Review). Another country gets what the body's frame shows, without the frame and under the window's `Country`, with no journal entry: what the system shows the world, without its controls. That is the tab's header (its name and its "?", hidden for an observer, for whom `te_popups` never opens), then its wide composer. A tab that opens without its entry (`Tab.readout`: Banking's monetary readings, the Colonial Empire's stance and colonial powers) shows the readout under the readout's gate and the composer under the entry's, as the frame's `tab_before_gate` and `tab_gate` do, never both, with the readout's own header where the frame swaps it (Banking's Monetary Policy).
- **Charts.** Another country's chart opens in a viewer the window holds at its own level (`te_ni_chart`). A country that keeps no history (neither played nor a major power) shows "History is kept only for played countries and major powers" on its empty charts. An old-save repair sorts each history store once, since another country's charts now show a store scrambled before the sort existed (`history_charts.md`).
- **How a system joins.** Its binding PR:
  1. binds the tab's wide composer, and its readout if it has one, to `Country`, and guards its controls (`test_te_system_tab_bodies.py`'s `BindingTest` and `GuardTest`; `scripts/analysis/te_rebind_country.py <tab>` lists what is left);
  2. sets its `Tab`'s `public = True` and takes it off `UNBOUND` (`national_info_binding.py`);
  3. reruns `python3 scripts/generators/gen_te_systems_window.py`, which writes both generated files (on a refresh after another system joined, take `main`'s copies and rerun; never hand-merge them);
  4. passes `BindingTest`, `GuardTest` and `test_national_info.py`, whose `ForeignViewerTest` walks its charts' large wrappers and keys for player and entry reads.

  Nothing else. A tab whose frame shows something before its gate names it in `Tab.readout`, and a tab whose window draws a footer names it in `Tab.footer`; Banking, the Colonial Empire and Tax Code already do, and `test_national_info.py` holds each to the body and the window. The strip's tooltips are shared keys, every chart already has its foreign viewer, `NI_ORDER` already places every tab, and every test derives what it expects from `public`.
