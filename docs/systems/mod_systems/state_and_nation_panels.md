Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

- **Tourism card** (`te_state_tourism_card`): a 530 px card between the population block and the status grid.

Each capped source has a bar toward its cap (excess infrastructure toward its +100% ceiling, half full at 30,000); the caps are in the `tourism_fill_*_pct` values at the end of `common/script_values/tourism.txt`.

  - Per-source tooltips `te_state_tourism_*_tt` state each tier schedule.

## Nation Tab

Spec: `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-17-national-modifiers-tab-design.md`. The first tab of the Timeline Extended window (`gui/te_systems_window.gui`), always open: no journal entry, no game rule, so the window's launcher has no rule gate either. Everything reads the shown country, `Country`, which each mount supplies: the player's in the window, any country's in National Info, where the tab is public and shows another country all of it, with no control to hide (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-charts-help-national-info-design.md` § 6.4; `test_te_system_tab_bodies.py`, and `test_nation_tab.py`'s `ForeignViewTest`). The modifier types' names the type rows splice keep `GetPlayer`: they render wherever the engine names a type and ask only a game rule (`WORLD_LOC`).

- **What it shows** (`gui/te_nation_tab.gui`): five overview boxes (Override Capacity's strongest Forceful Legislation option, Dynastic Wealth and its target, Construction Cost, Global Technology, Judicial Independence and its ceiling), then five open sections, Governance, Society, Economy, Science and Space, International, each its score rows and then its type rows, then Where These Come From, collapsed, and All Modifiers, collapsed. How the Nation Works is the Help page (`te_nation_help_popup`, 2026-11-02), which the "?" in the tab's header opens (the tab's frame, `te_nation_tab_body`, gave it a header 2026-11-03, when the link went). Rows: Legislative Override Capacity (`GetValueWithBreakdownFor('country_legislative_override_capacity_add')`, a bar from 0 to +30 with lines at 0, 2 and 4 (every value below 0 plays the same, so the bar starts there and the figure carries a negative value; 30 is about the most a country holds at once; `te_nation_override_bar`), and the strongest Forceful Legislation option open, which `te_nation_override_tier` reads from the capacity and the authority produced, as the event's triggers do), Judicial Independence once the score is seeded (`te_jud_display` heading for `te_jud_ceiling`, the ceiling's two sources by breakdown, and the courts' four scaled modifiers under it; the Court Overhaul is timed and stays in the list), the Algorithmic Mandate under Algorithmic Governance, Dynastic Wealth, Construction Cost (`te_cc_scaling_factor` − 1, the scaling's own effect; its tooltip breaks a point's cost into factors, § Construction Cost Scaling), Private Construction while either of its modifiers is in force, Global Technological Development (`var:te_global_tech_spread_applied`, which `tech_spread_on_action` sets beside the modifier, so the GUI never reruns the 54-technology sweep), and in International, once a UN exists, the United Nations lines: the seat (`un_disp_member_code`), International Standing with the score (`un_disp_standing_code`, `un_disp_standing`) and UN Dues with any months in arrears (`un_disp_dues_code`, `un_disp_arrears_months`; the sum owed and Article 19 on hover). They read the UN overview's own codes (`un_overview_display_values.txt`) and its words (`je_un_ov_*`), so the two never disagree; the codes ask the entry's modifiers through `je:` in script, which reads any country. International shows while they do or its type rows do. The rows' values are in `common/script_values/te_nation_values.txt`.
- **The type rows** (#1030): one per modifier type the tab's modifiers carry, "Arable Land: +45%", the type's own name and `GetValueWithBreakdownFor`, so the hover lists every source (technologies, laws, institutions, principles, timed modifiers and the tab's own, a scaled one with its multiplier), and the type's `_desc` on the row's hover. Grouped under subheadings, and sectioned by what a type affects, not which system grants it: one national total can't repeat in four sections, so Prestige is International's even where a space milestone grants it. A row shows while the country holds one of the tab's modifiers that carries it (`te_nation_type_<type>_count`), not whenever the total is non-zero, which would make the tab a copy of the country panel's Modifiers tab. A `state_`, `building_`, `interest_group_` or `character_` row is the country's contribution, and its hover says the scope's own sources are in its panel (the owner, 2026-10-17: the country's modifier holds those fields). `_bool` types are status lines ("In force", `GetFullEntryDescFor` on hover). 35 types get no row: a score row, the Banking tab or their own modifier shows them (`NOT_A_ROW`).
- **The lists** are the country's own timed modifiers (`Country.GetTimedModifiers`), filtered by name (gotcha #40 of `gui_modding_guide.md`): icon, name, the modifier's own tooltip, so the engine renders the effects at their current scale. A score row's own ("what it gives now") sit under it; the sections' lists sit in Where These Come From, collapsed, under each section's name. A section with nothing to show draws nothing. **All Modifiers** (`te_nation_all_panel`, owner, 2026-11-03, #1372) is the same datamodel with no filter: every timed modifier the country has, in vanilla's row (icon, name, the time left, the modifier's tooltip), what the country panel's Information tab listed until then. It is collapsed by default (`GetVariableSystem` flag `te_nation_all_open`), and its rows sit inside the panel that flag shows, so a closed section evaluates none of them. It reads the window's `Country` and is public, so National Info shows another country's too.
- **The registry** (`scripts/generators/gen_nation_tab.py`) places every static modifier script adds without a duration, parameterized names expanded from their callers (700 on 2026-10-17; a duration of `-1` is forever, so `days = -1` counts as permanent): `SECTIONS` (a section's list), `ROWS` (shown under a row), `KEEP_IN_LIST` (overrides in none of the tab's lists, so All Modifiers alone shows them, each with its reason: `nuclear_power` until it moves to its entry, the covert operations' effects on a target, debug modifiers; the name dates from when they were kept in the country panel's list), `NOT_ON_COUNTRY` (lands on an entry, a state, a building, an interest group or a power bloc). A second registry places the types those modifiers carry: `TYPE_ROWS` (section, group, types), `STATUS_ROWS` (bools) and `NOT_A_ROW` (with what shows it). The budget breakdown's sources (`gen_budget_breakdown.SOURCES`) get no list and no row: the Budget panel itemizes them. An entry may name a family with one `*`; an exact name wins. A third, `SOURCE_GROUPS`, puts every name the sections list in one group of one system's modifiers, at most 32 to a group, commonest first; names that read the same in English share a group (`te_tax_ig_view_*`, one view text for every interest group), since a list matches the text and its count the key. `SAME_NAME` pairs the one modifier outside the lists that reads like a listed one (the UN Development Fund's treasury grant, its pool grant's twin), so the counts count it as the list shows it.
- **Generated, never hand-edited:** `gui/te_nation_generated.gui` (the filters `te_nation_list_<section>` and `te_nation_row_<row>`, and the type rows `te_nation_types_<section>`), `common/script_values/te_nation_generated_values.txt` (their `has_modifier` counts, each group's, and each type row's, group's and section's), `common/scripted_effects/te_nation_generated_effects.txt` (`te_nation_refresh_source_flags`), `localization/english/te_nation_generated_l_english.yml` (each row's text and hover, splicing the type's name and `_desc`; organize_loc's `NATION_GENERATED`) and `gui/00_te_country_panel_information.gui`.
- **The country panel** (every country's, the player's and the AI's): `00_te_country_panel_information.gui` redefines vanilla's `country_panel_information_content` (gotcha #30), copied from `test_fixtures/vanilla_gui/country_panel.gui` with one `### MOD: Nation tab` block that replaces the MODIFIERS list with a line, "Modifiers: 45" (`GetDataModelSize(Country.GetTimedModifiers)`, no filter, no script value), and a goto beside it: into the window's Nation tab for the player's own country (2026-10-27 audit, NAT-1), and for another country into National Info on its Nation tab (`te_ni_tab = nation`, then `InformationPanelBar.OpenPopBrowserPanel( Country.Self )`, as its Politics tab opens the window). The line's hover says they are under All Modifiers there and why: the panel redraws everything in it every frame, and sorting the lasting modifiers out of the list row by row (260 names a row, about 11,700 comparisons a frame on New York in 1910) made it lag (owner, 2026-11-03, #1372). The MODIFIERS header keeps vanilla's test, and the line shows on the same one. **Where These Come From's lists are gated by group**: `te_nation_source_flags_on_action`, on `extra_on_actions.txt`'s `on_monthly_pulse_country` (every country, no rule gate), and `te_nation.1` (`events/te_nation_events.txt`), which `te_nation_source_flags_seed_on_action` raises for every country two days after the lobby (not the first day: `te_tax.3`'s migration adds the Tax Code's interest-group views a day later, through `te_tax.4`), run `te_nation_refresh_source_flags`, which sets `te_nation_src_<group>` on the country to how many of the group's modifiers it holds, 0 included, then a sentinel, `te_nation_src_set_<hash>`, named from the group set so a change of groups resets it. A row is compared only with the names of groups whose flag is above 0; the flags are read bare, since each list sits in a container shown on its count, and every count is 0 until the sentinel is set, so a country the pulse hasn't reached (an old save's first 30 days, a new country) draws no list and reads no unset variable. The counts read the same flags, so a list and its count agree; a modifier added mid-month in a group whose flag is off is missing from both until the country's next pass. A revolution's winner inherits the loser's flags and sentinel (variables carry over), so its lists read the loser's groups until its own next pass, the same lag. A score row's list (one to four names) compares live. The saving rests on `And` skipping its second argument when the first is false (gotcha #42 of `gui_modding_guide.md`), unproven beyond one case; the log safety doesn't. If the redefinition loses to vanilla's in game, the fallback is a full-file `country_panel.gui`.
- **Adding a permanent modifier:** one registry line, and a `SOURCE_GROUPS` line if a section lists it and no family there matches it, then `python3 scripts/generators/gen_nation_tab.py` (`--check` to test), and commit its five outputs. `test_nation_tab.py` fails while a candidate has no line or two, a line places nothing, a listed name has no group or two, two names that read the same sit in different groups, an output is stale, or a listed name shares its display name with one outside the lists (the filter matches names). A field the tab hasn't seen before needs a type registry line too, and fails the same way until it has one.
- **Judgment calls for the owner** (each one line to move): the monetary bands, the tax code's interest-group views and the colonial programmes under Economy; the colonial empire's endings and the Imperial Federation and Mandate System under Economy; the nuclear taboo and the Foreign Cultural Benchmark (`cultural_hegemony_foreign_benchmark`, added to the country by the hegemony entry's pulse) under International; the covert effects and `gm_name_carrier_slot` left to All Modifiers.
- **Play-test list:** `docs/testing/nation-tab-playtest.md`.

## Timeline Extended Window Tabs

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-te-window-wide-mode-design.md` (§§ 9.1, 9.3). The window (`gui/te_systems_window.gui`) keeps its frame, its launcher and its root by hand. Everything else comes from one registry.

- **The registry** (`TABS` in `scripts/generators/gen_te_systems_window.py`): per tab, its key (the stored `te_systems_window_tab` value), its strip, its label and tooltips, its open and on-strip gates, the scripted GUI whose checklist a grayed tab shows, and its body type. Nation is first, and the fallback. `SR_MILESTONES` lists the Space Race's nine entries.
- **Generated, never hand-edited:** `gui/te_systems_window_generated.gui` holds the selection templates, the strips (`te_systems_window_strips_narrow`), the scroll area's column of tab mounts (`te_systems_window_tabs_narrow`) and the Space Race's per-milestone frames (`te_sr_window_milestones`). The generator is manual, not post-load, and `--check` reports a stale file.
- **The tab frame** (`te_system_tab_frame`, `gui/te_system_tab_widgets.gui`) has a block for each part: the header, the gate on a parent of the `GetPlayerJournalEntry` datacontext, the fixed 520 column, the content and Open Journal Entry. A system's body type fills them: `te_gm_tab_body`, `te_ce_tab_body` and `te_seabed_tab_body`, each in its system's file. Each body declares a layout block (`te_<system>_tab_layout`) for a wide mount to override. The Space Race's `te_sr_tab_body` is a plain column: the milestones, then Space Powers and Orbit, then How the Space Race Works.
- **The mirrored tabs** (2026-11-02, strips 2 and 3): the ten system tabs that live in vanilla panels, Banking, Tax Code and Budget Breakdown (Budget), Strategic Reserve and Global Warming (Market), Demographics (Population), Cultural Hegemony (Society), United Nations (Diplomacy), Nuclear Weapons and Covert Warfare (Military). Each host tab and the window's mount one body type (`te_banking_tab_body`, `te_tax_tab_body`, `te_st_res_tab_body`, `te_gw_tab_body`, `te_ch_tab_body`, `te_un_tab_body`, `te_nuclear_tab_body`, `te_covert_tab_body`, in each system's layout file), so the two can't drift. The host keeps its outer flowcontainer and anything that reads its panel: the Market panel fills Global Warming's `te_gw_tab_market_view` (its own market's view and another market's) and the Reserve's `te_st_res_tab_elsewhere` and `te_st_res_tab_gate` (the other-market note and the own-market test); the window shows the bodies' defaults, the player's own market. Budget Breakdown mounts `te_budget_breakdown_panel`, always open. Demographics mounts `te_demog_window_body`, the Population tab's sections less the strata incomes (they read `PopsOverviewPanel`), with the player's country as context. The registry reuses each host's gates and, where it fits the window, its tooltip; names are the entries'. Tax Code's draft and bill summaries sit in both frames' `fixed_bottom` (`te_systems_window_tax_footer`), as in Budget's.
- **Two frames:** the side panel (`te_systems_window_panel`, `default_block_window`) and the full screen (`te_systems_window_panel_wide`, on `te_fullscreen_block_window`, generated from vanilla's `fullscreen_block_window`). The client-side key `te_systems_window_wide` picks one, and a text button in each frame's `fixed_top` sets or clears it. The full-screen frame's strips sit side by side at their narrow width (`te_systems_window_strips_wide`), and its column is 1,620 wide (`te_systems_window_tabs_wide`). Each mount there holds the tab's wide layout (the `Tab`'s `wide`: `te_<system>_wide_sections` in its system's file, or for Demographics the Census Data window's `te_demog_browser_sections`), the narrow layout's sections in columns of 520 (`te_ui_columns_three` or `te_ui_columns_two`): it fills the body's layout block, or replaces the body for a tab that declares none (`layout=None`). While the full-screen frame draws, the HUD parts vanilla hides under its own full screens hide too (the outliner, the "Paused" button, the lens toolbar): `gui/00_te_fullscreen_hide.gui` redefines vanilla's three visibility templates with a term that is the window root's `visible` and the frame's, so it follows the window by every route that closes it (`test_te_fullscreen_hud.py`).
- **Adding a tab:** write its body type and its wide layout, add a `Tab` line and its loc (label, tooltip, locked line), run `python3 scripts/generators/gen_te_systems_window.py`, and commit the generated file. `test_te_systems_window.py` fails while that file is stale or a tab has no wide layout, and evaluates the tab selection for each stored value. `test_te_system_tab_bodies.py` checks the frame and the bodies, and that nothing a tab's body or wide layout reaches, through instances and base types, reads a host panel. The system's layout test holds the wide layout to the narrow one with `gui_test_helpers.assert_wide_matches_narrow` (`docs/guides/python_tools.md`).

## National Info

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-charts-help-national-info-design.md` (§§ 6.2, 7.3); plan PR F1. Vanilla's Census Data window (`gui/pop_browser_panel.gui`, overridden), renamed National Info, shows the country it was opened for: the player's from the Population panel's button, any other's from the foot of its Politics tab.

- **The tabs.** Pops (vanilla's window) first, then each tab of the Timeline Extended window's registry with `public = True`, in `NI_ORDER` (`gen_te_systems_window.py`): Demographics, Nation, Banking, Tax Code, Grand Monuments, the Colonial Empire, the Seabed, Cultural Hegemony, the Space Race and Nuclear Weapons, five a strip side by side. Which are shown is `Tab.public` alone, and the tests derive every expectation from it. The tab shown is the client-side `te_ni_tab`: the last chosen while it is open for that country, otherwise Pops, so switching countries never shows a tab that isn't running there. A grayed tab's tooltip is the player's checklist for the player's own country and one line ("Not running in France") for another.
- **Generated, never hand-edited:** `gui/te_national_info_generated.gui`, the selection templates (`te_ni_<tab>_selected` / `_unselected`, the strip's and the column's gates), the strips (`te_ni_strips`) and the column of mounts (`te_ni_tabs`). The window places them in its `### MOD: National Info` blocks; outside them it is vanilla's file (`test_demographics_census_window.py`).
- **Two mounts a tab.** The player's own country gets the Timeline Extended window's full-screen mount, the body with its gate, its entry and its controls, and below it what the window draws in its frames' `fixed_bottom` (`Tab.footer`: Tax Code's draft and bill summaries, with Introduce, Discard and Review). Another country gets what the body's frame shows, without the frame and under the window's `Country`, with no journal entry: what the system shows the world, without its controls. That is the tab's header (its name and its "?", hidden for an observer, for whom `te_popups` never opens), then its wide composer. A tab that opens without its entry (`Tab.readout`: Banking's monetary readings, the Colonial Empire's stance and colonial powers) shows the readout under the readout's gate and the composer under the entry's, as the frame's `tab_before_gate` and `tab_gate` do, never both, with the readout's own header where the frame swaps it (Banking's Monetary Policy).
- **Charts.** Another country's chart opens in a viewer the window holds at its own level (`te_ni_chart`). A country that keeps no history (neither played nor a major power) shows "History is kept only for played countries and major powers" on its empty charts. An old-save repair sorts each history store once, since another country's charts now show a store scrambled before the sort existed (`history_charts.md`).
- **How a system joins.** Its binding PR:
  1. binds the tab's wide composer, and its readout if it has one, to `Country`, and guards its controls (`test_te_system_tab_bodies.py`'s `BindingTest` and `GuardTest`; `scripts/analysis/te_rebind_country.py <tab>` lists what is left);
  2. sets its `Tab`'s `public = True` and takes it off `UNBOUND`;
  3. reruns `python3 scripts/generators/gen_te_systems_window.py`, which writes both generated files (on a refresh after another system joined, take `main`'s copies and rerun; never hand-merge them);
  4. passes `BindingTest`, `GuardTest` and `test_national_info.py`, whose `ForeignViewerTest` walks its charts' large wrappers and keys for player and entry reads.

  Nothing else. A tab whose frame shows something before its gate names it in `Tab.readout`, and a tab whose window draws a footer names it in `Tab.footer`; Banking, the Colonial Empire and Tax Code already do, and `test_national_info.py` holds each to the body and the window. The strip's tooltips are shared keys, every chart already has its foreign viewer, `NI_ORDER` already places every tab, and every test derives what it expects from `public`.
