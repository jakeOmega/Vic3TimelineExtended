# Assimilation Cultures

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-assimilation-cultures-design.md` (the owner's decisions of 2026-11-25); plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-25-assimilation-cultures.md`; play-test list: `docs/testing/assimilation-cultures-playtest.md`. This file and the script describe what was built; where they differ from the spec, the script holds.

## Assimilation Cultures System

- **What:** the engine assimilates a pop only into a culture of its own heritage group (the define below). A pop whose culture shares no heritage group with any primary culture of its country is left to one yearly scripted pass, which under the game rule `assimilation_cultures_rule` moves it into the host culture as the base game would (Unrestricted, the default), has it take up the host's language in a generated hybrid culture that keeps its heritage (Hybrid Cultures), or leaves it until a Bodily Autonomy law opens the way (None). The Bodily Autonomy laws open the heritage step (`country_cross_heritage_assimilation_add`) and Ancestral Citizenship shuts it (`country_cross_heritage_assimilation_blocked_bool`). No journal entry, panel, event, modifier or variable: the pass converts pops and keeps no state.
- **Before 1.12** the mod had the rule always on, standing on `ALLOW_ASSIMILATION_ACROSS_HERITAGES = no`. That define left the game with 1.12, and the mod's 1.12 update (e6538d6f) commented its line out and deleted the old hybrid cultures and their monthly on_action.
- **The define** (`common/defines/extra_defines.txt`, `NPops`): `ALLOW_ASSIMILATION_REQUIRE_HERITAGE_GROUP = yes` (vanilla `no`). The stricter `ALLOW_ASSIMILATION_REQUIRE_HERITAGE_TRAIT` stays at vanilla's `no`: it would stop a Pole assimilating in Germany and multiply the hybrids by 110 heritages. A define is read at load, so it holds in every game on the mod, whatever the rule says and whenever the game began. Vanilla's comment says it filters only the cultures tied for the highest acceptance in the state and that a pop none of them qualifies for does not assimilate (`scripting_best_practices.md` § "Heritage Limits Assimilation Through Two `NPops` Defines, and They Filter Only the Most Accepted Cultures"). What it does in play is unread: self-check `hc_define_blocks` (§ Self-Checks and Tests).
- **Files:** the pass `common/scripted_effects/te_assimilation_culture_effects.txt` (its header has the outcome table and every local variable and saved scope), its triggers `common/scripted_triggers/te_assimilation_culture_triggers.txt`, its numbers `common/script_values/te_assimilation_culture_values.txt`, its hook `common/on_actions/te_assimilation_culture_on_actions.txt`, the two modifier types `common/modifier_type_definitions/te_assimilation_culture_modifier_types.txt`, and the generated files of § The Cultures and the Roster. Hand-written script names are `te_hc_*`, generated ones `te_hcg_*`, the cultures `hc_*`.
- **No culture is named.** The hand-written files test traits and saved scopes only; their one literal is the sentinel `cu:hc_anglophone_african` in `te_hc_mode_hybrid`. Every `cu:hc_*` of the generated ladder stands behind `exists` (`test_assimilation_cultures_pass.py` `NamesNoCultureTest`, `test_gen_assimilation_cultures.py` `test_the_generated_script_names_no_culture_but_hc`).

## The Cultures and the Roster

- **One hybrid per roster language and heritage group,** `hc_<language>_<group>` (`hc_anglophone_african`, `hc_cm_latin_south_asian`): the trait keys less `language_` and `heritage_group_`. Left out is the group that is the only one every culture of the language has, so German has no European hybrid. 56 languages have nine hybrids; the eight whose cultures span two or three groups (Anglophone, Arabic, Dutch, Hindustani, Hispanophone, Lusophone, Oghuz, Persian) have all ten. 584 in all: 539 for the 59 vanilla languages and nine for each of the five merge languages (`assimilation_cultures_config.pairs`). The prefix is there for the reason `cm_` is (`culture_merges.md` § Config and Generator: a duplicate culture key is dropped).
- **The roster** `scripts/generators/assimilation_cultures_config.py`, the single source, a line a language (`Language(key, names_from, word)`), in this order:
  - `COUNTED`: the 30 languages with the most people living under them in 1836 together with the 30 with the most speakers, 39 in all, counted over every country of the 1836 history, decentralized nations included. A count by rulers alone misses colonized peoples' languages (Bengali's is second by speakers, 70th by rulers). `scripts/analysis/assimilation_cultures_roster.py` repeats the count where the game is installed and exits 1 when the list no longer matches it (not in CI).
  - `HAND`: the owner's 20 (2026-11-25): Greek and South Slavic; Philippinic and Burmic; Amharic, Galla and Tigrinya (Ethiopia's three primary cultures), Somali, Lacustrine Bantu, Equatorial Bantu, Nguni, Swahili, Yoruba, Akan, Malagasy, Mande and Manding (Mali's two); and Quechuan, Guarani and Siouan for the Americas. Vanilla splits Africa into about 60 languages, none large enough for either count but Fulani and Hausa, and the mod's formables list many of them (the African Union 49 primary cultures).
  - `merge_languages(plan)`: the five languages the tier-2 culture merges mint (`language_cm_latin`, `_pan_slavic`, `_pan_turkic`, `_pan_malay`, `_east_african`), read from `gen_culture_merges.build_plan`, never typed in.
  - `GROUPS`: the ten heritage groups a vanilla culture has, less Promethean (one culture).
  - `validate` raises on a language or group vanilla lacks, a reference culture of another language or group, a minted heritage vanilla already defines, a key listed twice, a hybrid key that is a vanilla or merge culture, or a word holding a quote mark, a line break or `$`.
- **Traits:** the host language's `language_<key>`, and one diaspora heritage per group, `heritage_hc_<group>`, minted in vanilla's `heritage_group_<group>` (nine, `common/discrimination_traits/te_hybrid_culture_traits.txt`, named "<group> Diaspora"). The African hybrids take vanilla's `heritage_african_diaspora` instead, Afro-American's own trait, so `hc_anglophone_african`, `hc_lusophone_african` and `hc_hispanophone_african` have the traits of Afro-American, Afro-Brazilian and Afro-Caribeño: the reason for the local culture (§ The Yearly Pass).
- **What a hybrid gains.** In 1.15 a culture's acceptance in a country comes from the traits it shares with the country's primary cultures; cultures do not accept each other. A hybrid shares the language trait and no heritage, so it gets the citizenship law's figure for a shared language and keeps the figure for no shared heritage (`scripting_best_practices.md` § "Citizenship Acceptance: Read the Mod's Laws, Not Vanilla's").
- **Names** (`localization/english/te_hybrid_cultures_generated_l_english.yml`, `organize_loc` category `HYBRID_CULTURES_GENERATED`: keys starting `hc_` or `heritage_hc_`): spliced from vanilla's names, `$language_anglophone$ $heritage_group_african$` ("Anglophone African"), so a rename or a translation follows. Where vanilla's word is a linguist's, the row's `word` stands in (owner: "we should try to make them sound good"), for 18 languages: Romanian, Manchu, Bengali, Punjabi, Occitan, Turkic, Nepali, Thai, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Vietnamese, Cantonese, Filipino, Burmese, Oromo (vanilla's "Galla" is a name the Oromo reject), Great Lakes Bantu and Quechua. No group has a word of its own.
- **Every other field is derived** (`gen_assimilation_cultures.hybrid_fields`): religion, `name_format` and every name list from the language's `names_from` (its largest 1836 culture; a merge language's result); graphics and ethnicities from the group's `looks_from`, its largest 1836 culture with one plain ethnicity (Hausa, not the larger Equatorial Bantu, whose ethnicity is `central_african`; North German, not Russian); the color the channel mean of the two (`hybrid_color`); `traditions = { }`, and no obsessions, taboos, seal, homeland or `assimilation_rate`.
- **The nine entries** the engine expects of a culture (`scripting_best_practices.md` § "A New Culture Needs Six Static Modifiers and Three Modifier Types"): `common/static_modifiers/te_hybrid_culture_static_modifiers.txt` and `common/modifier_type_definitions/te_hybrid_culture_modifier_types.txt`, written by the merges' `render_static_modifiers_for` and `render_modifier_types_for`.
- **The ladder** `te_hcg_save_targets` (`common/scripted_effects/te_hybrid_cultures_generated_effects.txt`, state scope), the only generated script: one `if` / `else_if` branch per roster language, in roster order, each on `scope:te_hc_host = { has_discrimination_trait = language_<key> }` and `exists = cu:<its first hybrid>`. The branch saves each of the language's hybrids as the temporary scope `te_hc_t_<group>` and sets the local `te_hc_has_targets` to 1. A host off the roster, or a game begun before the hybrids existed, sets nothing.
- **They exist from 1836 with no pops,** under every setting of the rule: a culture can't be gated at load (`scripting_best_practices.md` § "A Culture Added After a Game Began Has No Object in It").
- **Generator** `scripts/generators/gen_assimilation_cultures.py` (`--check` exits 1 when stale, `--dry-run` writes nothing). It reads the committed `vanilla_parsed/` cultures and traits and the merges' plan, never the live ModState, which holds the generated cultures. In `POST_LOAD_REGENERATORS` after `gen_culture_merges` and before `organize_loc` (`server/post_load.py`); its five script files are in `EXCLUDED_REGISTRY_GLOBS` (`scripts/nightly_audit_select.py`); its six files are in `docs/auto_generated_files.md`. It replaced `scripts/generators/assimilation_cultures.py` (2,341 lines, mostly name lists typed per language, unused since 1.12), deleted with its orphaned rate `pop_culture_conversion_propotion_script_value`.

## The Yearly Pass

- **When:** `te_hc_yearly_on_action` on `on_yearly_pulse_country` runs `te_hc_yearly_country`, for a country with an incorporated state, on the country's own yearly pulse (the engine spreads the countries' pulses over the year). No rule gate: the pass runs under every setting and branches on it, because the define holds in every game. Unincorporated states are skipped: the old pulse skipped them, and vanilla's tooltip `POP_ASSIMILATION` gives "live in an incorporated state" as a condition.
- **The country** (`te_hc_yearly_country`): reads the rule once into the local `te_hc_mode` (1 Hybrid Cultures, 2 Unrestricted, 3 None; § The Rule), the crossing share into `te_hc_cross` and Ancestral Citizenship's bool into `te_hc_blocked`. Where nothing can convert (`te_hc_can` 0: Unrestricted under the block; None under the block or with no crossing share, which is every country before the Bodily Autonomy laws) no state is walked. Otherwise each incorporated state that passes `te_hc_state_has_other_group` (`any_scope_culture` with `NOT = { shares_heritage_trait_group_with_any_primary_culture = scope:te_hc_owner }`: the state's cultures, not its pops) runs `te_hc_state_pass`.
- **The host** (`te_hc_pick_host`): the owner's primary culture with the most people in the state (owner, 2026-11-25). It sets both the language a hybrid takes and the culture a pop joins: Quebec's host is Franco-Canadian, Ontario's Anglo-Canadian. One primary culture living there is the host with no count; several are counted by a pop walk each (`te_hc_weigh_primary`, `total_size` into a local, the largest kept). With none in the state, the capital's host by the same rule, found once per country per pass (`te_hc_find_capital_host`, `te_hc_capital_known`); with none in the capital either, any primary culture. Vanilla's `incorporated_state` static modifier gives every incorporated state `state_allow_assimilation_without_presence_bool`, so the engine too assimilates into a primary culture nobody in the state belongs to.
- **Who is taken** (`te_hc_pop_taken`, in one walk of the state's pops): the pop's culture shares no heritage group with any primary culture of the owner; the state is not one of its homelands, or carries `state_allow_assimilation_in_homeland_bool`; and the owner's laws let its acceptance status assimilate (`te_hc_pop_status_allowed`: `country_allow_assimilation_<status>_bool` by `pop_acceptance` against `acceptance_status_2` to `_5`, vanilla's 20, 40, 60 and 80). So a country with primary cultures of two groups (Paraguay: Platinean and Guarani) leaves both groups' pops to the engine. The switches are on the Minority Rights laws (`common/laws/extra_laws.txt`; the in-game names of two statuses are the mod's, from `localization/english/replace/`):

| Minority Rights law | Statuses that assimilate |
|---|---|
| Violent Hostility | Open Prejudice and above |
| Ghettoization | Second-class Citizen (in game Provisional Acceptance) and Full Acceptance |
| Discrimination, Indifference | Cultural Erasure (in game Latent Hostility) and above |
| Cultural Assimilation, Protection, Affirmative Action | all five |

- **What a taken pop becomes** (`te_hc_convert_pop`; the effects file's header has the same table). "Speaks" is `te_hc_speaks_host` (`shares_language_trait_with_culture = scope:te_hc_host`); "the rate" is the fraction `te_hc_pop_step`, "the rate × the share" `te_hc_pop_cross_step`:

| Setting | A pop that does not speak the host's language | One that does: a hybrid, a local culture, a vanilla culture such as Afro-American under a Yankee host |
|---|---|---|
| Hybrid Cultures | the local culture for its heritage group, else that group's hybrid of the host's language, at the rate (the language step); with neither (a host off the roster), as under None | the host, at the rate × the share (the heritage step) |
| Unrestricted | the host, at the rate | the same |
| None | the host, at the rate × the share | the same |

- **Joining the host never happens** for a slave pop, under the block, or at a share of 0. Under Hybrid Cultures a slave takes the language step and never the heritage step, which is what the engine's exception ("slaves always follow this rule") protects; under the other two settings slaves are left out, as the engine leaves them *(the designer's call, which the owner approved; the spec names Hybrid Cultures and Unrestricted, and None shares the branch)*. A pop that took the language step this year (`te_hc_done`) takes no heritage step in the same pass. Nothing converts in a state with no host (`te_hc_has_host` 0).
- **The local culture** (owner, 2026-11-25; `te_hc_find_locals`, Hybrid Cultures only) takes a pop in place of the hybrid: a culture with people in the country (`every_scope_culture` on the country), of another heritage group than every primary culture, that speaks the host's language and belongs here in one of two ways. Either this state is one of its homelands (`te_hc_here` 2), or the owner holds a state that is a homeland of both it and one of its primary cultures (1). Per group (`te_hc_keep_local`, `has_discrimination_trait_group = heritage_group_<g>`) one whose homeland this state is wins over one that only shares a homeland, and otherwise the first found is kept; it is saved as `te_hc_l_<group>` and flagged in `te_hc_has_local_<group>`. Found by trait, so none is named.
  - The United States: Dixie, a primary culture, shares seven of Afro-American's eight homeland states, so an African-heritage pop becomes Afro-American in New York as in Georgia (the owner: "in New York, they should become Afro-American").
  - Britain after taking Georgia: no British homeland is an Afro-American one, so Africans in London become Anglophone African and those in Georgia Afro-American. The first form of the rule, a homeland anywhere in the country, made London Afro-American (the owner: "that feels much odder").
  - Once Afro-American has switched into American it has no people in the country, so later arrivals become Anglophone African: otherwise the merge would be a way across the wall with no law.
  - A pop that joined a local culture inside that culture's homeland states no longer assimilates, as nobody does in a homeland; outside them it can still take the heritage step.
- **Flags, not `exists`:** a temporary scope outlives the state it was saved for, so the locals `te_hc_has_host`, `te_hc_has_targets` and the ten `te_hc_has_local_<group>` (`te_hc_reset_locals`) are reset in every state's pass before anything reads them, and say whether `scope:te_hc_host`, the hybrids and a local culture are this state's (plan, Review Focus 2).
- **The rate** `te_hc_pop_rate` (pop scope; reads `scope:te_hc_state`), the fraction of the pop that converts that year: `te_hc_monthly_rate` 0.002 (vanilla's `ASSIMILATION_RATE`) × `te_hc_months` 12 × (1 + the state's `state_assimilation_mult`) × (1 + the owner's `country_assimilation_<status>_mult`, `te_hc_status_mult`) × (1 − `pop_radical_fraction`) × (1 − `te_hc_fervor_effect` 0.0075 × the culture's `culture_current_fervor`), each factor no lower than 0, × `te_hc_homeland_factor` 0.75 (vanilla's `ASSIMILATION_IN_HOMELAND_MULT`) in a homeland of the culture, held between 0 and `te_hc_rate_cap` 0.5. The fervor effect is the mod's `CULTURAL_FERVOR_ASSIMILATIOM_EFFECT` (vanilla's typo), and the formula around it is a reading of the define's comment.
  - It reads the engine's own sums, so every law, institution, decree and Cultural Hegemony bonus that speeds assimilation speeds this, with no second list to keep. A state's `modifier:state_*` read includes its country's lines (`scripting_best_practices.md` § "Reading Pops, Buildings and Wars for a Yearly Model"; self-check `hc_rate_inputs`).
  - Each factor is held at 0 on its own, so Clade Citizenship's −300% in a country with under +200% of its own gives 0, never a negative fraction (plan, Review Focus 3).
  - With no modifiers 2.4% a year, half a community in 29 years; at the +690% of the owner's 2039 New York, 19% a year.
- **Small pops** (`te_hc_small_pop` 50): a pop of fewer people converts whole where its fraction is above 0 (`te_hc_pop_step`; `te_hc_pop_cross_step` where the owner's share is above 0 too). At the base rate a year's fraction of such a pop is about one person, the engine's `MIN_ASSIMILATION`, and converting it at once keeps a state from filling with pops of a few people.
- **The crossing share** `te_hc_cross_share` (country): `modifier:country_cross_heritage_assimilation_add`, held between 0 and 1 (§ The Laws).
- **Cost:** one `any_scope_culture` test per incorporated state per year. In a state that passes it, one pop walk, and one more per primary culture living there where the owner has two or more; under Hybrid Cultures also one walk of the country's cultures, and for a culture that qualifies by trait without a homeland here, of the country's states. The old pulse ran about 300 blocks for every state every month. Unprofiled: the play-test list's load-time and monthly-tick row.

## The Rule

- **`assimilation_cultures_rule`** ("Assimilation Across Heritages", `common/game_rules/extra_game_rules.txt`; loc `te_game_rules_l_english.yml`), three settings, each with one flag named after it:
  - `assimilation_cultures_hybrid` ("Hybrid Cultures"): the language step, and the heritage step once a law opens it.
  - `assimilation_cultures_unrestricted` ("Unrestricted"): the imitation of vanilla (§ What Unrestricted Gets Wrong). The default in the release that adds the rule; the spec makes Hybrid Cultures the default once an observer run and a played game have been read.
  - `assimilation_cultures_none` ("None"; the owner's: "a disabled rule, where there is just no assimilation across heritage groups"): Hybrid Cultures without the hybrids. No pop changes heritage group by assimilation until a Bodily Autonomy law opens it, and then it joins the host directly.
- **No setting switches the system off.** The define keeps the engine inside a heritage group whatever the rule says, so the pass runs under all three; the other rules' pause-or-teardown pattern (`game_rules.md`) does not apply.
- **Read through two triggers only** (`te_assimilation_culture_triggers.txt`; `test_assimilation_cultures_rule.py` `test_no_other_file_reads_the_rule`): `te_hc_mode_hybrid` (`has_game_rule = assimilation_cultures_hybrid` and `exists = cu:hc_anglophone_african`) and `te_hc_mode_none`, each a positive test of its own setting; neither true is Unrestricted. Nothing tests the Unrestricted setting, but it stays defined, since a `has_game_rule` on a setting no file defines is true (`scripting_best_practices.md` § "`has_game_rule` on a Setting No File Defines Is True").
- **A game begun before the cultures existed** has no object for them (`scripting_best_practices.md` § "A Culture Added After a Game Began Has No Object in It"), so the sentinel fails and no hybrid is ever named; if it lists no setting for the rule, neither trigger is true either. It goes on assimilating across heritage as before, through the Unrestricted branch (play-test row).
- **Changed mid-game** (from the change-country screen): nothing to tear down, since the system keeps no modifier, variable or journal entry. Hybrid pops stay hybrids. Under Unrestricted they are ordinary pops of another heritage group from the next pass, and drain into the host at the yearly rate; under None they stay as they are until a law opens the wall; back on Hybrid Cultures the language step resumes.

## The Laws

- **`country_cross_heritage_assimilation_add`** ("Assimilation Across Heritages"; `percent = yes`, `color = neutral`, `script_only = yes`, `te_assimilation_culture_modifier_types.txt`; loc `te_modifiers_l_english.yml`): the share of the usual rate at which a pop joins a culture of another heritage group, read capped at 100% (`te_hc_cross_share`). On the Bodily Autonomy laws (`common/laws/te_bodily_autonomy_laws.txt`, header § ASSIMILATION ACROSS HERITAGES): none on Natural Form (which carries nothing), +50% on Licensed Modification (a board approves each change), +100% on Morphological Freedom, Clade Citizenship and Prescribed Form. It acts under Hybrid Cultures and None; under Unrestricted every pop crosses at its full rate, and the description says so.
- **Each law's own assimilation line moves both steps,** since the rate reads the state's sum. Rates a year before a pop's own factors (status, radicals, fervor), in a state whose sum is +100% or +690% before the law's line:

| Law | New line | Its assimilation line | Language step, heritage step: at +100% | at +690% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Natural Form | none | none | 4.8%, closed | 19.0%, closed |
| Licensed Modification | +50% | none | 4.8%, 2.4% | 19.0%, 9.5% |
| Morphological Freedom | +100% | −100% | 2.4%, 2.4% | 16.6%, 16.6% |
| Clade Citizenship | +100% | −300% | none, none | 11.8%, 11.8% |
| Prescribed Form | +100% | +200% | 9.6%, 9.6% | 23.8%, 23.8% |

  So under Licensed Modification a pop crosses heritage at half the speed it would assimilate at if it shared the host's heritage group, in the same state and at the same status; under the other three at the same speed. The language step exists only under Hybrid Cultures; under None the heritage step's column is the whole of it.
- **Ancestral Citizenship** (`law_ethnostate`, in game "Ancestral Citizenship"; `REPLACE:law_ethnostate` in `common/laws/modified.txt`) carries `country_cross_heritage_assimilation_blocked_bool` ("Pops Never Join a Culture of Another Heritage Group", `te_miscellaneous_l_english.yml`; its description in `te_concepts_l_english.yml`), a bool the pass reads, so the law's tooltip says so (owner, 2026-11-25: "Good idea - agreed"). No pop joins a culture of another heritage group, under any setting and whatever the Bodily Autonomy law. Under Hybrid Cultures the language step still runs. Under Unrestricted, where every conversion crosses heritage, nothing is left: such a country assimilates only within its heritage group, which is not vanilla's behavior (the owner kept it: "let's keep, I think it is good"). Within a group the engine assimilates and no law can filter that by heritage trait; it is left to acceptance, where the law already puts a pop sharing only the group 20 points below one sharing the trait (+20 against +40).
- **`test_assimilation_cultures_rule.py`** (`LawTest`, `ModifierTypeTest`) pins the four lines, Natural Form's empty block, that no other law carries either modifier, and both types' names and descriptions. The laws' other lines: `social_movements.md` § Morphological Freedom Debate and Bodily Autonomy.

## With Culture Merges

- **Two grains of one idea.** A merge works on named peoples in their homelands, from a roster; this works on everyone else, by trait. A merge's tier 1b (Afro-American joining American, `culture_merges.md` § Config and Generator) is the heritage step taken early for one named culture, on its own gates.
- **A hybrid is never a merge part.** A merge measures everything in a part's homeland states, and a hybrid has none. The merge roster and its validation are unchanged.
- **When a host's cultures merge.** Most tier-1 and absorbing merges keep the language, so their hybrids are unaffected: the state's host passes from the part to the result at the switch, as the part's people become the result's. Where the merge changes the language, the old hybrids no longer speak the host's language after the switch and take the language step again, at the yearly rate, into the new language's hybrids. That is the five tier-2 merges, which mint a language (why those five are on the roster), and the tier-1 parts with a language of their own: Occitan into French, Yue, Min and Hakka into Han, Ryukyuan into Japanese, Mazanderani into Persian. Four of those languages are on the roster (Occitano-Romance, Yue, Min, Hakka), so their hybrids stop being made once the French and Chinese merges complete.
- **A part of another heritage group than its host** (Afro-American) can be converted by both systems. They don't conflict: a merge converts it into the result, and this into the host, which the merge then converts into the result. A switched part has no people, so it is no country's local culture.
- **`test_culture_merge_mod_refs.py`** asks that a file naming a part also names what the part merges into. This system names no vanilla culture, so it asks nothing; the system's own tests hold it to that, and the generated files must stay free of part names, or the pinned set of generated restatements fails.
- **Shared code:** `gen_culture_merges.py`'s `load_vanilla`, `build_plan`, `render_culture_block`, `render_static_modifiers_for`, `render_modifier_types_for` and `static_modifier_loc_for`, so both families of generated culture keep one field order.
- **Empty cultures:** the Culture Merges play-test row on the 24 mod files that iterate cultures (does any divide by, average over or list the empty ones) covers the 584 hybrids too.

## What Unrestricted Gets Wrong

Compared with the engine doing the same assimilation itself (the owner: "That's fine"):

- **Once a year, not monthly:** a step on each country's yearly pulse, and a slightly different total, since twelve monthly steps compound.
- **Nothing in the interface.** The pop panel's "assimilating into" line and `has_ongoing_assimilation` stay empty for these pops; only the self-check `hc_define_blocks` reads that trigger. The setting's description tells the player.
- **The target.** The host by the rule above, where the engine takes the most accepted culture present, which under generous laws can be one that is not primary.
- **`country_assimilation_delta_threshold_add`** ("Cultural Assimilation Threshold") is not imitated: its base value is in no define, so how much more accepted the target must be is unknown. Affirmative Action carries −100 of it. Play-test row.
- **The fervor factor** is a guess at the formula from the define's comment.
- **The engine's minimum of one person a month** has no counterpart; a pop of under 50 people converts whole.
- **Unincorporated states** are skipped. If the engine does assimilate there, slowly, the pass does not follow it (play-test row).
- **Ancestral Citizenship** assimilates only within the heritage group (§ The Laws).

## Self-Checks and Tests

- **Two self-checks,** TEMPORARY until the play-test, in `common/scripted_effects/te_assimilation_culture_selfcheck_effects.txt` (its header lists what to delete with them). Each logs `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN <name>` once a game with its numbers, behind a latch global (`te_hc_chk_define_blocks_done`, `te_hc_chk_rate_inputs_done`); each figure is copied into a `te_hc_chk_*` global, printed with `GetGlobalVariable` and removed.
  - `hc_define_blocks` (`te_hc_chk_define`, from `te_hc_chk_define_on_action` on the global `on_monthly_pulse`): 30 days after the first monthly pulse of the game, or of the load that brought this build, a walk of every incorporated state's pops with `has_ongoing_assimilation`, counted as of a heritage group a primary culture of their country shares (`same`) or not (`other`), and of `other`, those whose state holds another culture of their own group (`kin`). OK: `same` above 0 and `other` 0. BROKEN: both above 0; `other` equal to `kin` may be only the engine assimilating within the group into a culture that is not primary. `NOT_JUDGED` after three pulses with `same` at 0, so it never walks the world again.
  - `hc_rate_inputs` (`te_hc_chk_rate_inputs`, the first line of `te_hc_convert_pop`): at the first taken pop whose country carries an assimilation line of its own (the country's read of `state_assimilation_mult` not 0), OK when the state's read from pop scope is not 0 either, the rate is between 0 and 0.5, and `country_allow_assimilation_full_acceptance_bool` reads yes. No line after a few years under Hybrid Cultures or Unrestricted means the country-scope read of a `state_` modifier gives 0 everywhere.
  - The fallback for each is in the play-test list's Self-checks table.
