# Automation Policy

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Automation Policy (`lawgroup_automation_policy`)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-era-13-technology-tree-design.md` § 7.1 (the economy law of the trio with Bodily Autonomy and Frontier AI Control). Play-test list: `docs/testing/era-13-playtest.md` § Phase 3: Automation Policy. Who gains when robots and AI do the work people did.

- **Laws** (`common/laws/te_automation_policy_laws.txt`, economy): Unrestricted Automation (**first in the file**, what an unseeded country and an old save get; no gate, so the consistency walk's terminal fallback; history seeds it for every 1836 country in `extra_history.txt`), Robot Tax, Human Labor Quotas and Automation Dividend, each from Humanoid Robotics (era 11). No law needs another law, so `gen_law_consistency.py` generates no cascade for the group (it is in `LAWGROUP_PRIORITY` after Antitrust). Descriptions are flavor with no figures; the rules are each law's `on_enact` `custom_tooltip` lines, since the law's own effects list is empty.
- **The automated share** (`var:te_automation_share`, 0 to 1): the levels of the country's buildings that run a robotic or AI-managed method, over the levels of every building with such a method in one of its groups. Two building-scope triggers in `common/scripted_triggers/te_automation_policy_triggers.txt` define it: `te_ap_runs_automated_method`, which reads the methods' marker (below) and lists no methods, and `te_ap_automatable_building` (the 47 building types the marked methods run in). Counted monthly by `te_ap_monthly_update` (`common/scripted_effects/te_automation_policy_effects.txt`, from `on_monthly_pulse_country` in `common/on_actions/te_automation_policy_on_actions.txt`) through `te_ap_automatable_levels` and `te_ap_automated_levels`, two `every_scope_state` / `every_scope_building` walks, only for a country with Humanoid Robotics or one of the group's laws. Levels, not employment: an Urban Center's level counts like a Tooling Workshop's. `te_ap_share_display` shows it in the law group's description.
- **The marker** (owner, 2026-11-05): each robotic or AI-managed method carries `building_robotic_or_ai_managed_bool = yes`, a script-only boolean building type (`common/modifier_type_definitions/te_automation_policy_modifier_types.txt`), in its `building_modifiers`' `unscaled` block, so it holds at any staffing. `te_ap_runs_automated_method` is `modifier:building_robotic_or_ai_managed_bool = yes`. The line shows in the method's tooltip as Robotic or AI-Managed, so a player sees which methods the laws act on, and the quotas' preview points to it. Only production methods carry it: a building's `modifier:` read also carries its country's lines, so one on a law or a technology would mark every building. A new method takes the marker and the quotas' `disallowing_laws` together, and its building joins `te_ap_automatable_building`. Whether the line reaches the read is unverified in game: `te_ap_marker_selfcheck` logs `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN automation_marker` once a game, comparing three sample methods named directly (`te_ap_marker_sample`) with the marker's read (`docs/testing/era-13-playtest.md` § Phase 3: Automation Policy, Self-checks, which names the fallback).
- **Which methods count** (the trigger file's header has the full list and what is left out): in each group, the first robotic or AI-run tier and every tier above it, so the quotas never move a building to a method that employs fewer people. Factory automation from Robotics' robotic tier up (Tooling Workshop, Furniture Manufactory, Motor Industry, Automotive Industry with Intelligent Manufacturing Systems, Aerospace Industry, Food Industry), AGI's AI-managed tier in the other factories, Smart Grids' AI-managed power plants, Autonomous Logistics and Autonomous Networks, Fully Automated Content Creation and AI-Mediated Mind Sharing, Automated Labs, the automated Deep-Sea Mine (both families), Robotic Service Providers and Drone Delivery Systems in Urban Centers, and Autonomous Vehicles' trains, highway and harvesters. Left out: base methods (Industrial Robotics, Nanobot Assemblers, Robotic Ship Assembly), methods that make robots or chips, AI Assisted Research, mining machinery, government staffing (AI Governance, Automated Casework) and ownership methods.
- **Human Labor Quotas** withdraws the 40 marked methods (37 when built, with the AI-managed Aneutronic Fusion reactor and Vactrain Freight added at merge, since each sits above a withdrawn tier, and the Trade Center's Autonomous Logistics on 2026-11-05): each carries `disallowing_laws = { law_human_labor_quotas }` in `extra_pms.txt`, as vanilla withdraws its slavery and Industry Banned methods. Every affected group (26) keeps an ungated first method. The cost is the lost output. Since the government picks a building's methods, the withdrawal alone would only take options away, so the law's own block carries what the guarantee buys politically (owner, 2026-11-04): Trade Unions +10% political strength and −10% radicals from political movements; it also ends Displaced Workers, and The New Luddites (`post_scarcity_events.4`) can't fire while it holds. Which method the engine moves a building to, and how soon, is unread; `te_ap_quotas_selfcheck` logs `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN automation_quotas` once a game, two monthly pulses after the first country that ran automated levels takes the law.
- **Robot Tax and Automation Dividend** (the dynamic-modifier pattern, shared with Displaced Workers below; [dynamic_modifier_pattern.md](dynamic_modifier_pattern.md)): `te_automation_robot_tax` (+10% tax collection, capitalists' investment-pool contribution −0.05) and `te_automation_dividend` (lower strata +2 and middle strata +1 standard of living, capitalists' contribution −0.10), written at full automation in `common/static_modifiers/te_automation_policy_modifiers.txt`. **One refresh site:** `te_ap_monthly_update` removes both, then adds the one whose law holds, with `multiplier = var:te_automation_share`, or for the dividend `var:te_ap_dividend_scale`: the share, times `TE_AP_DIVIDEND_WELFARE_FACTOR` (1.5) under Universal Basic Income or Post-Scarcity Economy (`te_ap_dividend_welfare_law`), the pairing the spec asks for. A law change takes effect at the next pulse. The variables are never removed, so a civil war's winner keeps them and its next pulse rebuilds the modifier. Both are in the Nation tab's Economy list (`gen_nation_tab.py`); the two strata's standard-of-living types have rows there.
- **Displaced Workers** (`te_automation_displacement`, owner, 2026-11-04): the cost of automating fast, so each law answers the workers the machines displace. Written at a rise of `TE_AP_DISPLACEMENT_FULL_RISE` (0.10) of the automated share in a year: Trade Unions approval −3, radicals from political movements +10%. `te_ap_monthly_update` keeps the year: its first count (a new country, an old save) only sets the baseline `te_ap_share_year_ago`, so what is already automated never counts; every twelfth count after that stores the rise since (`te_ap_disp_rise_value`, 0 when the share fell) in `te_ap_disp_rise`. Every count works out the multiplier `te_ap_displacement_scale` (`te_ap_displacement_scale_value`: the rise over a full one, at most 3, × 1 under Unrestricted Automation, × `TE_AP_DISPLACEMENT_ROBOT_TAX_FACTOR` (0.5) under Robot Tax, 0 under the Dividend and the quotas), so a change of law shows within a month while the rise holds for the year. Same refresh site and pattern as the other two. Each law's `on_enact` says what it does to it (`te_ap_law_displacement_tt`, `_half_tt`, `_none_tt`), the law group's description quotes last year's rise (`te_ap_disp_rise_display`), and it is in the Nation tab's Economy list.
- **Magnitudes** are first estimates, sized low: a tax collection rise below the Power Bloc's fiscal principles (+25%, +50%), a dividend below Full Equality and Protection's +3 for everyone. The spec sizes them from an observer run. No AI enactment weights: the interest groups' stances drive the AI.
- **Stances:** `AUTOMATION_LISTS` (eight named lists, each naming all four laws) and `AUTOMATION_STANCES` (39 vanilla ideologies → list) in `ideology_modifications.py`; `apply_ideologies.py` (offline: `scripts/generators/regen_ideologies_offline.py`) writes them into `common/ideologies/modified.txt`. The Trade Unions' proletarian and populist ideologies strongly approve the quotas; the Industrialists' plutocratic and laissez-faire ones strongly approve Unrestricted Automation and strongly disapprove the quotas. The mod's Corporate and Optimist Transhumanist ideologies carry theirs by hand in `extra_ideologies.txt`.
- **Icons:** each law has its own, painted in icon batch 9 (`gfx/interface/icons/law_icons/<law>.dds`; the `law` prompts are in `scripts/image_pipeline/icon_prompts.py`). They had borrowed other laws' icons until then.
- **Autonomous Logistics** (`pm_trade_center_trade_quantity_advanced_4`, the Trade Center's last trade-quantity tier, Smart Grids; owner, 2026-11-05): cancels International Trading's per-level staff exactly (800 clerks and 200 shopkeepers a level on both base methods, `pm_trade_center` and `pm_trade_center_principle_external_trade_2`; no other group of the building employs anyone) and hires a flat crew of 1,000 clerks (International Trading's own trade; owner, 2026-11-05) with `building_job_attractiveness_mult = 5` in `unscaled`, as the flat-crew AI-managed methods do with engineers (`docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-16-ai-managed-flat-staff-design.md`). Its goods and weekly trades stay workforce-scaled. It carries the marker and the quotas' gate, so the Trade Center is in `te_ap_automatable_building`.
- **Tests:** `test_automation_policy.py` (the default first and ungated, the 1836 seed, the trigger reading the marker alone, the marker's type and that only methods carry it, in `unscaled`, the marked methods against the quotas' gate and every group's fallback, the self-check's samples, Autonomous Logistics' exact cancel, the ladder rule, the share's buildings, the one writer, the preview lines' figures against the modifiers, the quotas' political lines and the neo-Luddite gate, Displaced Workers' yearly count and law factors, the stances).
