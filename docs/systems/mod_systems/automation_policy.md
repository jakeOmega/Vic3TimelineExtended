# Automation Policy

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Automation Policy (`lawgroup_automation_policy`)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-era-13-technology-tree-design.md` § 7.1 (the economy law of the trio with Bodily Autonomy and Frontier AI Control). Play-test list: `docs/testing/era-13-playtest.md` § Phase 3: Automation Policy. Who gains when robots and AI do the work people did.

- **Laws** (`common/laws/te_automation_policy_laws.txt`, economy): Unrestricted Automation (**first in the file**, what an unseeded country and an old save get; no gate, so the consistency walk's terminal fallback; history seeds it for every 1836 country in `extra_history.txt`), Robot Tax, Human Labor Quotas and Automation Dividend, each from Humanoid Robotics (era 11). No law needs another law, so `gen_law_consistency.py` generates no cascade for the group (it is in `LAWGROUP_PRIORITY` after Antitrust). Descriptions are flavor with no figures; the rules are each law's `on_enact` `custom_tooltip` lines, since the law's own effects list is empty.
- **The automated share** (`var:te_automation_share`, 0 to 1): the levels of the country's buildings that run a robotic or AI-managed method, over the levels of every building with such a method in one of its groups. Two building-scope triggers in `common/scripted_triggers/te_automation_policy_triggers.txt` define it: `te_ap_runs_automated_method` (the 37 methods) and `te_ap_automatable_building` (the 46 building types those methods run in). Counted monthly by `te_ap_monthly_update` (`common/scripted_effects/te_automation_policy_effects.txt`, from `on_monthly_pulse_country` in `common/on_actions/te_automation_policy_on_actions.txt`) through `te_ap_automatable_levels` and `te_ap_automated_levels`, two `every_scope_state` / `every_scope_building` walks, only for a country with Humanoid Robotics or one of the group's laws. Levels, not employment: an Urban Center's level counts like a Tooling Workshop's. `te_ap_share_display` shows it in the law group's description.
- **Which methods count** (the trigger file's header has the full list and what is left out): in each group, the first robotic or AI-run tier and every tier above it, so the quotas never move a building to a method that employs fewer people. Factory automation from Robotics' robotic tier up (Tooling Workshop, Furniture Manufactory, Motor Industry, Automotive Industry with Intelligent Manufacturing Systems, Aerospace Industry, Food Industry), AGI's AI-managed tier in the other factories, Smart Grids' AI-managed power plants and Autonomous Networks, Fully Automated Content Creation and AI-Mediated Mind Sharing, Automated Labs, the automated Deep-Sea Mine (both families), Robotic Service Providers and Drone Delivery Systems in Urban Centers, and Autonomous Vehicles' trains, highway and harvesters. Left out: base methods (Industrial Robotics, Nanobot Assemblers, Robotic Ship Assembly), methods that make robots or chips, AI Assisted Research, mining machinery, government staffing (AI Governance, Automated Casework) and ownership methods.
- **Human Labor Quotas** withdraws the 37 methods: each carries `disallowing_laws = { law_human_labor_quotas }` in `extra_pms.txt`, as vanilla withdraws its slavery and Industry Banned methods. Every affected group (25) keeps an ungated first method. No modifier: the cost is the lost output, the gain the jobs. Which method the engine moves a building to, and how soon, is unread; `te_ap_quotas_selfcheck` logs `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN automation_quotas` once a game, two monthly pulses after the first country that ran automated levels takes the law.
- **Robot Tax and Automation Dividend** (the dynamic-modifier pattern, [dynamic_modifier_pattern.md](dynamic_modifier_pattern.md)): `te_automation_robot_tax` (+10% tax collection, capitalists' investment-pool contribution −0.05) and `te_automation_dividend` (lower strata +2 and middle strata +1 standard of living, capitalists' contribution −0.10), written at full automation in `common/static_modifiers/te_automation_policy_modifiers.txt`. **One refresh site:** `te_ap_monthly_update` removes both, then adds the one whose law holds, with `multiplier = var:te_automation_share`, or for the dividend `var:te_ap_dividend_scale`: the share, times `TE_AP_DIVIDEND_WELFARE_FACTOR` (1.5) under Universal Basic Income or Post-Scarcity Economy (`te_ap_dividend_welfare_law`), the pairing the spec asks for. A law change takes effect at the next pulse. The variables are never removed, so a civil war's winner keeps them and its next pulse rebuilds the modifier. Both are in the Nation tab's Economy list (`gen_nation_tab.py`); the two strata's standard-of-living types have rows there.
- **Magnitudes** are first estimates, sized low: a tax collection rise below the Power Bloc's fiscal principles (+25%, +50%), a dividend below Full Equality and Protection's +3 for everyone. The spec sizes them from an observer run. No AI enactment weights: the interest groups' stances drive the AI.
- **Stances:** `AUTOMATION_LISTS` (eight named lists, each naming all four laws) and `AUTOMATION_STANCES` (39 vanilla ideologies → list) in `ideology_modifications.py`; `apply_ideologies.py` (offline: `scripts/generators/regen_ideologies_offline.py`) writes them into `common/ideologies/modified.txt`. The Trade Unions' proletarian and populist ideologies strongly approve the quotas; the Industrialists' plutocratic and laissez-faire ones strongly approve Unrestricted Automation and strongly disapprove the quotas. The mod's Corporate and Optimist Transhumanist ideologies carry theirs by hand in `extra_ideologies.txt`.
- **Icons:** the four laws borrow existing law icons (Automated Bureaucracy, the Legislated Tax Code, Pro-Labor Ministry of Labor, Universal Basic Income), marked `# Placeholder Icon`, with unreviewed `law` prompts in `scripts/image_pipeline/icon_prompts.py`.
- **Tests:** `test_automation_policy.py` (the default first and ungated, the 1836 seed, the withdrawn methods against the share's list and every group's fallback, the ladder rule, the share's buildings, the one writer, the preview lines' figures against the modifiers, the stances).
