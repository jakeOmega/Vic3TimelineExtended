# State Pictures

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-state-pictures-design.md` (the axes, the technology ladders and the whole planned set of about 380 pictures); play-test list: `docs/testing/state-pictures-playtest.md`.

## State Pictures

- **What:** the banner at the top of the state panel. Vanilla shows one of 35 nineteenth-century paintings by the state's terrain and region; the mod adds later pictures that follow the owner's technology and the size of the state's largest city. The same picture shows in the state tooltip, pop details, the invasion and battle panels and the strategic region panel. Cosmetic: nothing reads it.
- **How the engine picks** (read in game, 2026-11-10; `docs/guides/scripting_best_practices.md` § "State pictures"): the state's prevailing terrain (`common/terrain`), then the highest-weight entry of that terrain's `textures` list. The mod doesn't change which terrain prevails. An `INJECT:` adds entries to vanilla's list. Every entry needs an `effect` (the animated overlay): one without it is a load error that leaves its state with the plains picture. The picture can't be logged (`[THIS.GetState.GetBestTexture]` prints empty in `debug_log`).
- **Files:**
  - `scripts/image_pipeline/state_picture_registry.py` — the one source: each picture's terrain lists, technology, rung, region, size, overlay, prompt and picked seed. An entry with `seed: None` is planned art and wires nothing. `DRAFTS` holds the prompts of the waves whose conditions aren't designed yet (wave 3's 58: ports, mines, oil, farms, forestry, power landscapes), each with its technology and a plain-words note of when it should show; a draft moves to `PICTURES` when its wave is built.
  - `scripts/generators/gen_state_pictures.py` → `common/terrain/te_state_pictures_generated.txt` (one `INJECT:` a terrain; `--check` exits 1 when stale).
  - `common/scripted_effects/te_state_picture_effects.txt`, `common/script_values/te_state_picture_values.txt`, `common/on_actions/te_state_picture_on_actions.txt` — the yearly pass.
  - `scripts/image_pipeline/convert_state_picture.py` — a picked render to `gfx/state_pictures/te_<key>.dds` (1020 × 500, BC7 with mips): the entry's `crop` box, then a Kuwahara filter blended four parts to one with the unfiltered picture, which turns FLUX's near-photograph into flat dabs of color beside vanilla's paintings.
  - `test_state_pictures.py`.
- **The weight** is a number in place value, so the most specific picture that applies wins and none tie: the rung on the family's technology ladder × 100,000, the size × 10, a named region its family's digit (1 to 8, so two families that hold the same state can't tie: Japanese outranks East Asian). The spec reserves the places between for prosperity and character, and the place above for conditions. Vanilla's weights are 1 and 2, so a mod picture that applies always wins; before its technology none applies and vanilla's shows.
- **What a weight may read:** the owner's technology (live), vanilla's region triggers (live) and the yearly pass's state variables. The engine may evaluate a weight every frame, so nothing dearer goes in one.
- **The yearly pass** (`te_state_picture_refresh`, on `on_yearly_pulse_state`, ROOT the state) stores `te_sp_size`: 1 town, 2 city (100,000 people in the largest city), 3 metropolis (1,000,000), 4 megacity (10,000,000). The largest city's people are the Demographics walk's urban people (`te_dg_urban`, written every year whatever the Demographics rule) times the metropolitan share (`te_dg_mt` ÷ 100 while the census runs, 0.4 otherwise). A size is left only a tenth below its floor. Before a state's first walk it has no size, and only pictures for any size apply. The floors are provisional until the spec's wave 2 sets them against an observer game. A size picture can lag the city by up to a year.
- **Self-check:** `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN state_picture_size`, once a game, from the first state stored at metropolis or larger, with the people of its largest city and of the state. OK when the city fits inside the state; BROKEN means `te_dg_urban` isn't a head count on `state_population`'s scale and the floors are in the wrong unit. The banner itself is on the play-test list.
- **Pictures wired** (the pilot): European city and European metropolis (Art Deco Architecture, in Europe and in the states of no region family), a port and a mine (Modern Tools, anywhere). Each reuses the vanilla overlay of its kind. The port picture also asks for a Port (`where` in the registry): vanilla's `docks` terrain prevails in every one-province state, port or not, and shows its dry plain where there is none.
- **Adding a picture:** add the registry entry, render and pick, convert, set its `seed`, run the generator, commit the `.dds` with the generated file. A new region or size is a line in the registry's `REGIONS` or `SIZES` (a region family is vanilla's triggers, any one of them, and a digit; `FALLBACK_REGION`, the European family, also takes the states of no family, as vanilla's European picture does); a new axis is a place in the weight and, if it isn't live, a variable in the yearly pass.
