## Power Bloc Summits (`te_pb_summit.1`)

A use for mandates other than principles (#1442), for a bloc whose slots are full. A leader spends one mandate on one initiative the whole bloc carries for ten years. Five initiatives are open to every bloc, and each bloc identity has one more of its own. It replaced the Mandate Reserve (+3 cohesion a year per unspent mandate, up to +12), a 1.14.5 stand-in from when script couldn't spend a mandate; a bonus for holding mandates would pull against spending them. The reserve was deleted outright, with no save cleanup, since 1.15 breaks saves anyway (owner, 2026-11-06).

**Files.** `events/te_power_bloc_summit_events.txt` (the event), `common/static_modifiers/te_power_bloc_summit_modifiers.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/te_power_bloc_summit_effects.txt` (`te_pb_summit_pay`, the three grant helpers, `te_pb_summit_member_relations`, `te_pb_summit_leader_relations`), `common/scripted_triggers/te_power_bloc_summit_triggers.txt` (`te_pb_summit_can_pay`, `te_pb_summit_has_open_borders`, `te_pb_summit_bloc_identity`, `te_pb_summit_named_council`), `common/on_actions/te_power_bloc_summit_on_actions.txt` (the AI), `te_pb_summit_sgui` in `common/scripted_guis/te_power_bloc_sguis.txt` and its button in `gui/power_bloc_panel.gui`; `test_pb_summit.py`. Play-test items: `docs/testing/vanilla-1-15-playtest.md` § "Power bloc mandates".

**The button.** Convene a Summit sits beside the leader's mandate count (vanilla's AVAILABLE MANDATES row, shown only to the leader). `is_valid` is `te_pb_summit_can_pay` under a custom tooltip, so the button greys with its reason while the bloc holds no mandate. It spends nothing: it fires the event with `popup = yes`, and closing the event costs nothing.

**The options.** Each initiative calls `te_pb_summit_pay` (`add_power_bloc_mandate = -1` under the cost line `te_pb_summit_cost_tt`, plus a `TE_PB_SUMMIT <kind> by=ai|player` log line) and lasts `long_modifier_time`. Three helpers grant the modifiers, each removing a running copy first, unseen, since a same-name `add_modifier` stacks: `te_pb_summit_grant_bloc_initiative` (on the bloc), `te_pb_summit_grant_leader_initiative` (the leader) and `te_pb_summit_grant_member_initiative` (every member, the leader included, at the summit: a country that joins later doesn't get it, and one that leaves keeps it until it runs out). The bloc helper prints its own line, `<modifier>_grant_tt`, in place of the engine's: vanilla's `add_modifier` line leaves a power bloc's name blank (`scripting_best_practices.md`).

**The members are told (2026-11-10, #1558).** Only the leader sees `te_pb_summit.1`, so `te_pb_summit_grant_member_initiative` sends every other member `te_pb_summit.2` ("The Summit's Communiqué") a day later. The member carries `<modifier>_told` for a month: it picks the text (one description per member initiative) and the modifier the option lists under `show_as_tooltip`, and the event removes it. A seventh member initiative needs a description key, a branch in the event's trigger, option and `after`; `test_told_of_foreign_effects.py` fails without them.

The religious option's name follows the leader's state religion (`country_has_state_religion`): an ecumenical council (Catholic), a synod (Protestant), a holy synod (Orthodox and Oriental Orthodox), the ulama in council (Sunni, Shia, Ibadi), a rabbinical council (Jewish), a Sangiti (Mahayana, Gelugpa, Theravada), a Dharma Sansad (Hindu), a Sarbat Khalsa (Sikh), and a plain council of the faith for any other (`te_pb_summit_named_council = no`, so it never shows beside a named one). The culture option's per-culture acceptance alternative (`add_culture_acceptance_modifier`, or vanilla's 332 `country_<culture>_cultural_acceptance_add` types) was passed over: the first is unprobed and the second would need a dispatcher over every culture, so it raises acceptance of cultures sharing a heritage or language trait with each member's own.

Every option but Adjourn also needs `te_pb_summit_can_pay`, since the event can wait while the mandate goes on a principle; with none in hand the description says the summit can only adjourn. Magnitudes sit against vanilla's: principles move cohesion by 5 to 20 and the UN's bloc vacuum gives +10; Sovereign Empire's invitation penalty is −30; the UN's dissolution vacuum gives +20% leverage. A spend is worth less than a principle tier (ten years, not permanent), so a bloc with an open slot still buys principles first.

**The AI.** The engine AI spends mandates on principles and never clicks a scripted GUI (`ai_is_valid = { always = no }`). `te_pb_summit_ai_on_action` (on `on_monthly_pulse_country`) gives an AI leader holding two or more mandates a monthly chance to fire the event: 3%, 6%, 10% and 25% at two, three, four and five (`MAX_MANDATES`), about 31%, 52%, 72% and 97% a year (owner, 2026-11-06: from two, rising with the count, since AI leaders fill their slots quickly). At the cap, progress is wasted, so the summit comes within months. The event's `ai_chance` picks the initiative: Common Purpose 10, +30 below 50% cohesion and +30 more below 25%; Recruitment 5, +10 under five members; Leverage 10; Open Borders 5; Mandate at Home 5, +15 below 50 legitimacy; the identity initiative 15 (Joint Exercises +10 at war), above the general five since each is made for its bloc; Adjourn 0.

## Mandates in Script (1.15)

Probed with the power bloc test console's mandate options (`te_debug_pb.1` options g and h, verdict in `te_debug_pb.2`), 2026-11-06: `add_power_bloc_mandate = -1` removes a mandate, and the count drops within the same effect (the `straight_after` reading), so a spend can check and deduct in one click. `power_bloc.num_mandates` reads the count as a value from country scope and matches the `num_mandates` trigger. Not probed: whether a negative value takes the count below 0 (every spend gates on `num_mandates >= 1`, so it doesn't matter yet), and whether `add_power_bloc_mandate` above the cap of 5 is clamped as the docs say.

**Formation mandates (#1458).** Each of the four principle-slot technologies (Intergovernmental Organizations, Containerization, Globalization, Universal Digital Identity) carries `country_power_bloc_formation_mandates_add = 1`, so a bloc founded after them starts with mandates for the slots they open. Not probed: whether the grant lands before `on_power_bloc_formed` runs and whether `MAX_MANDATES` caps it (prep doc C21). The once-a-game `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN formation_mandates` (`common/on_actions/te_pb_formation_selfcheck_on_actions.txt`) reads the new bloc's count against the founder's grant; the fallback and the play-test row are in `docs/testing/vanilla-1-15-playtest.md`. Under the Four slots rule the technologies add no slot, but the formation mandates still apply: a pure bonus there.

## External Trade IV and V (#1458)

Tiers IV and V restate vanilla tier III's flat +25% trade advantage and `country_no_advantage_loss_from_lack_of_interest_bool`, and add 1.15's `country_interest_tier_<tier>_trade_advantage_mult` on top: IV +5%, +10%, +15%, +20% for engaged, influential, pervasive and hegemonic; V doubles them. No vanilla principle uses the tier types (1.15.1 snapshot), so nothing below tier IV needs restating; if a patch adds them to tiers I to III, `principle_tier_audit` will flag IV and V.
