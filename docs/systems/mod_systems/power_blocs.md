# Power Blocs

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

The mod's power bloc content outside this file: principle slots 5 to 8 and their game rule (`common/script_values/te_power_bloc_principle_slot_values.txt`, `docs/systems/mod_systems/game_rules.md`, `test_pb_principle_slots.py`), the identity and principle groups (`common/power_bloc_identities/`, `common/power_bloc_principles/`, `common/power_bloc_principle_groups/`), and the panel override (`gui/power_bloc_panel.gui`, `docs/guides/gui_modding_guide.md`). The player guide's account is `docs/player_guide/09-diplomacy.md` § "Power bloc principles and identities".

Market Unification IV and V and Internal Trade IV and V join the members' power grids as a Grid Interconnection Agreement would (2026-11-14). Their `power_bloc_modifier` blocks carry the script-only marker `power_bloc_grid_interconnection_bool` (`power_bloc_extra_modifier_types.txt`) for the tooltip alone. Script reads the tier through `has_principle` (`te_grid_bloc_links_grids`), as the Monetary Union does, and `TE_SELF_CHECK grid_bloc_link` compares the two reads. A tier VI of either group must be added to that trigger. The mechanism is in `power_grids.md` § Bloc partners.

## Power Bloc Summits (`te_pb_summit.1`)

A use for mandates other than principles (#1442), for a bloc whose slots are full. A leader spends one mandate on one initiative the whole bloc carries for ten years. Five initiatives are open to every bloc, and each bloc identity has one more of its own. It replaced the Mandate Reserve (+3 cohesion a year per unspent mandate, up to +12), a 1.14.5 stand-in from when script couldn't spend a mandate; a bonus for holding mandates would pull against spending them. The reserve was deleted outright, with no save cleanup, since 1.15 breaks saves anyway (owner, 2026-11-06).

**Files.** `events/te_power_bloc_summit_events.txt` (the event), `common/static_modifiers/te_power_bloc_summit_modifiers.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/te_power_bloc_summit_effects.txt` (`te_pb_summit_pay`, the three grant helpers, `te_pb_summit_member_relations`, `te_pb_summit_leader_relations`), `common/scripted_triggers/te_power_bloc_summit_triggers.txt` (`te_pb_summit_can_pay`, `te_pb_summit_has_open_borders`, `te_pb_summit_bloc_identity`, `te_pb_summit_named_council`), `common/on_actions/te_power_bloc_summit_on_actions.txt` (the AI), `te_pb_summit_sgui` in `common/scripted_guis/te_power_bloc_sguis.txt` and its button in `gui/power_bloc_panel.gui`; `test_pb_summit.py`. Play-test items: `docs/testing/vanilla-1-15-playtest.md` § "Power bloc mandates".

**The button.** Convene a Summit sits beside the leader's mandate count (vanilla's AVAILABLE MANDATES row, shown only to the leader). `is_valid` is `te_pb_summit_can_pay` under a custom tooltip, so the button greys with its reason while the bloc holds no mandate. It spends nothing: it fires the event with `popup = yes`, and closing the event costs nothing.

**The options.** Each initiative calls `te_pb_summit_pay` (`add_power_bloc_mandate = -1` under the cost line `te_pb_summit_cost_tt`, plus a `TE_PB_SUMMIT <kind> by=ai|player` log line) and lasts `long_modifier_time`. Three helpers grant the modifiers, each removing a running copy first, unseen, since a same-name `add_modifier` stacks: `te_pb_summit_grant_bloc_initiative` (on the bloc), `te_pb_summit_grant_leader_initiative` (the leader) and `te_pb_summit_grant_member_initiative` (every member, the leader included, at the summit: a country that joins later doesn't get it, and one that leaves keeps it until it runs out). The bloc helper prints its own line, `<modifier>_grant_tt`, in place of the engine's: vanilla's `add_modifier` line leaves a power bloc's name blank (`scripting_best_practices.md`).

**The members are told (2026-11-10, #1558).** Only the leader sees `te_pb_summit.1`, so `te_pb_summit_grant_member_initiative` sends every other member `te_pb_summit.2` ("The Summit's Communiqué") a day later. The member carries `<modifier>_told` for a month: it picks the text (one description per member initiative) and the modifier the option lists under `show_as_tooltip`, and the event removes it. An eighth member initiative needs a description key, a branch in the event's trigger, option and `after`; `test_told_of_foreign_effects.py` fails without them.

The religious option's name follows the leader's state religion (`country_has_state_religion`): an ecumenical council (Catholic), a synod (Protestant), a holy synod (Orthodox and Oriental Orthodox), the ulama in council (Sunni, Shia, Ibadi), a rabbinical council (Jewish), a Sangiti (Mahayana, Gelugpa, Theravada), a Dharma Sansad (Hindu), a Sarbat Khalsa (Sikh), and a plain council of the faith for any other (`te_pb_summit_named_council = no`, so it never shows beside a named one). The culture option's per-culture acceptance alternative (`add_culture_acceptance_modifier`, or vanilla's 332 `country_<culture>_cultural_acceptance_add` types) was passed over: the first is unprobed and the second would need a dispatcher over every culture, so it raises acceptance of cultures sharing a heritage or language trait with each member's own.

Every option but Adjourn also needs `te_pb_summit_can_pay`, since the event can wait while the mandate goes on a principle; with none in hand the description says the summit can only adjourn. Magnitudes sit against vanilla's: principles move cohesion by 5 to 20 and the UN's bloc vacuum gives +10; Sovereign Empire's invitation penalty is −30; the UN's dissolution vacuum gives +20% leverage. A spend is worth less than a principle tier (ten years, not permanent), so a bloc with an open slot still buys principles first.

**The AI.** The engine AI spends mandates on principles and never clicks a scripted GUI (`ai_is_valid = { always = no }`). `te_pb_summit_ai_on_action` (on `on_monthly_pulse_country`) gives an AI leader holding two or more mandates a monthly chance to fire the event: 3%, 6%, 10% and 25% at two, three, four and five (`MAX_MANDATES`), about 31%, 52%, 72% and 97% a year (owner, 2026-11-06: from two, rising with the count, since AI leaders fill their slots quickly). At the cap, progress is wasted, so the summit comes within months. The event's `ai_chance` picks the initiative: Common Purpose 10, +30 below 50% cohesion and +30 more below 25%; Recruitment 5, +10 under five members; Leverage 10; Open Borders 5; Mandate at Home 5, +15 below 50 legitimacy; the identity initiative 15 (Joint Exercises +10 at war), above the general five since each is made for its bloc; Adjourn 0.

## Mandates in Script (1.15)

Probed with the power bloc test console's mandate options (`te_debug_pb.1` options g and h, verdict in `te_debug_pb.2`), 2026-11-06: `add_power_bloc_mandate = -1` removes a mandate, and the count drops within the same effect (the `straight_after` reading), so a spend can check and deduct in one click. `power_bloc.num_mandates` reads the count as a value from country scope and matches the `num_mandates` trigger. Not probed: whether a negative value takes the count below 0 (every spend gates on `num_mandates >= 1`, so it doesn't matter yet), and whether `add_power_bloc_mandate` above the cap of 5 is clamped as the docs say.

**Formation mandates (#1458).** Each of the four principle-slot technologies (Intergovernmental Organizations, Containerization, Globalization, Universal Digital Identity) carries `country_power_bloc_formation_mandates_add = 1`, which vanilla describes as "Additional Mandates to spend on Principles when forming a Power Bloc": a founder after them can spend more mandates on principles at formation, for the slots they open. Not probed: whether the grant works as described and whether `MAX_MANDATES` caps it (prep doc C21). The once-a-game `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN formation_mandates` (`common/on_actions/te_pb_formation_selfcheck_on_actions.txt`) compares the new bloc's unspent mandates plus its principle levels with the founder's grant, since the mandates are spent at formation; the fallback and the play-test row are in `docs/testing/vanilla-1-15-playtest.md`. Under the Four slots rule the technologies add no slot, but the formation mandates still apply: a pure bonus there.

## External Trade IV and V (#1458)

Tiers IV and V restate vanilla tier III's flat +25% trade advantage and `country_no_advantage_loss_from_lack_of_interest_bool`, and add 1.15's `country_interest_tier_<tier>_trade_advantage_mult` on top: IV +5%, +10%, +15%, +20% for engaged, influential, pervasive and hegemonic; V doubles them. No vanilla principle uses the tier types (1.15.1 snapshot), so nothing below tier IV needs restating; if a patch adds them to tiers I to III, `principle_tier_audit` will flag IV and V.

## Creditors' Consortium

One of the two identities added on 2026-11-16, beside the Research Commons: members are bound by credit. The leader stands behind every other member's debts, and a member that defaults is rescued on the leader's terms. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-power-bloc-identities-design.md` § 5. Script: `te_creditors_consortium_*.txt` (scripted triggers, script values, effects, on-actions, laws, static modifiers, principles and principle groups), `events/te_creditors_consortium_events.txt` (`te_cc`), `events/te_debug_cc_events.txt` (`te_debug_cc.1`, the console read-out). The identity itself is in `extra_power_bloc_identities.txt`; `test_creditors_consortium.py` pins the structure; play-test rows and self-checks: `docs/testing/power-bloc-identities-playtest.md`. Script names start `te_cred_` because the construction-cost system already owns `te_cc_` for its variables; the events keep the spec's `te_cc` namespace.

**Requirements.** Visible only under the Banking System rule set to Enabled (`te_mon_full_system`), since its mechanic is the monetary layer's guarantee and receivership; a bloc founded under Full keeps the identity when the rule changes and every piece of the mechanic pauses with it (each asks `te_mon_full_system`). The founder must pass `te_mon_backstop_provider_valid` (a National Bank, no Command Economy, not in default). The leader can't enact Command Economy (an `INJECT` adds the `can_enact` vanilla lacks) or abolish its National Bank (`law_no_national_bank`'s own `can_enact`, `extra_laws.txt`).

**The standing guarantee.** A third branch of `te_mon_has_effective_backstop`, `te_cred_guarantee_is_effective`: a non-leader member, not a command economy, not refusing terms in the last ten years (`te_cred_terms_refused`, a timed variable on the country, so leaving and rejoining restores nothing), with no treaty guarantee or receivership already (`te_mon_has_treaty_debt_relief`), of a leader that is a valid provider and has not broken a guarantee in five years (`te_mon_lolr_suspended_months`, the clock `te_lolr.1`'s Renege starts, so a refusal here and a renege there suspend each other). Its relief is `te_mon_lolr_relief`, added in `te_mon_arr_recipient_value` only where the Monetary Union's tier III backstop is not already paying it (`te_cred_guarantee_pays_relief`); the leader's discovery adds the guarantor's price, relief times the member's GDP over its own, to `te_mon_arr_provider_pts` (`te_cred_discover_leader_costs`). Members and leaders are in the discovery's monthly scan list (`te_mon_arr_scan`), and the leader's monthly pulse asks for a discovery of any member whose `te_mon_arr_scan` is not 1, so a member that joins, or leaves and comes back, is read from its first month rather than the yearly scan.

**The call.** `te_monetary_on_country_default` calls, in order: a treaty guarantor (`te_lolr.1`), then the consortium's leader (`te_cc.1`), then a tier III monetary union's leader (`te_lolr.1`). The call counter and the cooldown are the member's whichever backer answers. The old `else` that sent every non-treaty call to `var:te_mon_anchor` is now an `else_if` on the union backstop, with the anchor checked and no call where a receivership already stands (`te_mon_has_treaty_debt_relief = no`). A member that refused the consortium's terms has lost the union's backstop from the same leader too (`te_mon_union_backstop_is_effective` asks `te_cred_guarantee_forfeit`): no relief, no cost, no call. A member its currency union backs takes its relief from the union and its call from the consortium.

**The rescue (`te_cc.1` to `te_cc.4`).** The leader sees one option for each way to spend its terms (`te_cred_terms_to_pick`: one for any consortium, owner 2026-11-17, plus its Conditionality tier's figure, or every term on offer if fewer), plus Refuse. Every rescue spends all of them. Adjustment and privatization take one term each; concessions take what is left, and each term spent on them buys the tier's monopolies (owner, 2026-11-17: privatization seldom applies, so the higher tiers' terms had nothing to buy). So the options are concessions alone, adjustment or privatization with concessions for the rest, both with concessions for the rest, and the single terms or the pair where they use the terms up: at most four at once (`te_cred_rescue_option_*`, counted by `te_cred_rescue_options` for the AI's split). Concessions can take every term only where a monopoly can be demanded (`te_cred_monopolies_possible`); otherwise one (`te_cred_concession_slots_max`). The offer writes the terms spent on concessions and the monopolies they buy on the member (`te_cred_offer_concessions`, `te_cred_offer_monopolies`), so the count the leader read is the count demanded. A bloc without Conditionality asks concessions without a monopoly (`te_cred_monopolies_allowed` is 0; the `_plain_tt` tooltips), so where the investment rights already stand its concessions term is not offered. The terms on offer (`te_cred_term_*_available`): structural adjustment (needs International Exchange Standards, for the receivership it works through, and a law the leader holds that the member lacks), concessions (Foreign Investment Rights can be made, or already stand and a monopoly could be demanded: the leader has a company whose type has a building in the member, `te_cred_company_can_take_monopoly_generated`, from the same generator as the ladder), privatization (article 105's own gates, through `can_create_treaty`). Refusal: the five-year guarantee clock, `te_mon_lolr_reneged`, −20 cohesion at once (`add_cohesion_percent = -0.2`), −40 relations, no infamy (no treaty broken). A rescue is paid only when the member accepts (`te_cc.2`): the honour cost fixed at the offer (`te_cred_offer_cost`), then each term as its own treaty in force, each behind `can_create_treaty`: `debt_receivership` (member source, leader target), `foreign_investment_rights`, monopolies (`acquire_monopoly_for_company`, one treaty each, for the leader's companies over the member's buildings of their types, from the generated ladder `te_cred_monopoly_ladder_generated`, `scripts/generators/gen_cred_monopoly_ladder.py`; a second monopoly on a building type is refused by the article's own duplicate check, not by `company_has_building_type_monopoly`, which names no country). They go to the member's largest industries first (owner, 2026-11-17): `te_cred_demand_monopolies` scores every pair of a company and one of its building types by the member's levels of that type, makes the best, and repeats over the pairs that come after it by score and then by place in the walk, so no pair is tried twice and the loop ends. Equal scores go to the leader's most prosperous company (`ordered_company` by `prosperity`, the one company figure script reads as a value; self-check `cc_company_order`, with `every_company` as the fallback where the ordered walk misses a company), `enforce_privatization`. Structural adjustment is a variable (`te_cred_adjustment_by`) read by the `can_impose` the 16 economic, trade and tax laws get by `INJECT` (`te_creditors_consortium_law_injections.txt`); the monthly pulse removes it once the receivership has gone, but not in the ten days after the rescue (`te_cred_rescue_pending`), before the discovery has read the new receivership. The member's answer is kept on the member (`te_cc_answer`), so two answers at once can't cross in `te_cc.3`. An AI member accepts unless domineering, belligerent or antagonistic toward the leader; a refusing member stays in default, forfeits the guarantee for ten years and may leave at once.

**The treaty-less receivership.** Where `can_create_treaty` refuses the receivership (114's `can_ratify` wants the leader to outrank or out-earn the member), or `te_cc.9` finds no article in force three days on, the receivership is set directly (`te_cred_direct_receivership`, `te_cred_receiver`). The discovery honors it after the article walk (`te_cred_discover_direct_receivership`) under the article's own conditions (`te_cred_has_direct_receivership`) and exit ramp; an article wins where both exist. Off the Full rule it pauses and resumes, its months out of default kept (`te_mon_leave_backstop_treaties` zeroes the count only where no direct receivership stands); the article is removed by its `requirement_to_maintain`, as before, and `te_cc.9` checks nothing off Full. After five years (`te_cred_direct_binding`, the article's binding period) the member may end it, as it could withdraw from the article: `te_decision_cred_end_receivership` (`common/decisions/te_creditors_consortium_decisions.txt`), which an AI takes only when hostile to its receiver. A revolution's winner keeps it: `te_mon_cw_repoint_to_winner` repoints `te_cred_receiver` and `te_cred_adjustment_by` with the monetary roles.

**Conditionality** (`principle_group_conditionality`, primary and unlocked by the identity, visible under Full). Display fields on the bloc; script reads the tier through `has_principle` (`te_cred_cond_tier_*`). Tier figures, I to V: terms +1, +1, +2, +2, +3 over the one every consortium sets (2, 2, 3, 3 and 4 in all); remittance 0.10, 0.15, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25% of the debtor's GDP a month; monopolies 1, 1, 2, 2, 3 for each term spent on concessions (so up to 2, 2, 6, 6 and 12 a rescue); months to exit 12, 18, 24, 30, 36; bloc leverage +5% a tier. The remittance and exit months reach the monetary layer through `te_mon_receivership_take_share` and `te_mon_receivership_exit_months`, which now add the receiver's figure (`te_cred_take_extra_own`, `te_cred_exit_extra_own`) for any receivership whose receiver leads a consortium. Gates: Central Banking for I to III, Intergovernmental Organizations' `_pb_principles_bool` for IV, Globalization's for V.

**Development Lending** (`principle_group_development_lending`). Each month the leader's pulse (`te_cred_dl_pulse`) stores the bloc's GDP per head, then members above it pay 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5 or 15% of a month of their investment pool's gross income (`te_cred_dl_payment`, never more than the pool holds) and members below it, the leader excepted, share the sum by population, through paired `add_investment_pool`. Nothing is paid while nobody is below the average. From tier II receivers carry `te_cred_dl_receiver_2` to `_5` (+5, +10, +15, +20% `state_construction_mult`), synced by each country's own pulse with no duration (the Nation tab registers them). Runs under any banking rule. Gates: Central Banking for I to III, Keynesian economics' bool for IV, Globalization's for V.

*What the payers get* (review, 2026-11-16): the leader is usually the richest member and pays most, so the group needs a return it can see, and more than a neutral one, since a principle slot could hold a strong group instead. Exploit Members, the transfer the other way, is a positive group: its leader takes authority, influence, legitimacy and income for the cohesion it costs (−5, −10, −20, and the mod's −30, −40). So this group mirrors that cohesion, `power_bloc_cohesion_add` +5, +10, +20, +30, +40 by tier (it moves the cohesion target, so it arrives over weeks), gives the leader a gain of its own, `country_influence_mult` +10%, +15%, +20%, +25%, +30% in `leader_modifier` (the Marshall Plan's standing abroad), and a country below the bloc's GDP per head gives the bloc's invitation and its join treaty `te_cred_dl_invite_per_tier` (5) a tier, `TE_DIPLOMATIC_ACCEPTANCE_CREDITOR_DEVELOPMENT_LENDING` in both AI scores, read off the leader's stored `te_cred_dl_bloc_gdppc`, so it applies only while the bloc holds the group. The principles show it as `power_bloc_te_cred_dl_invite_add`, held to the per-tier value by `test_creditors_consortium.py`. The cohesion is flat, not sized by what moves: a bloc where nothing is paid (the leader alone below the average) gets it too, which is rare enough to leave.

**Existing groups.** Monetary Union, Foreign Investment and Companies become primary for the identity and stay open to all (Companies from the 2026-11-16 review: the concessions term works through the leader's companies, and no other identity has it as primary); Vassalization and Colonial Offices list it among their unlocking identities; Sacred Civics does not.

**Cohesion** (ROOT the leader): base 20; the leader's GDP share × 50 (Trade League's term, its own desc key); −30 per unit of the leader's `scaled_debt`, to −40; worst leader relations; −5 per member under any receivership and −10 per member with Command Economy, each counting a prospective member in and a leaving one out; −50 while the leader is in default. Trade League's mandate progress and −1 per member.

**Leaving.** Vanilla's leverage rule (advantage 30 or less), not while bound to the leader (`te_cred_bound_to_leader`: in its receivership as the discovery reads it, holding its treaty-less receivership, or in the ten days after accepting a rescue, so the money can't be taken and the bloc left before the receivership binds); a member that refused terms in the last ten years may leave whatever the leverage.

**AI.** Founding weight 100, +100 with Industrialists in government, +50 under a quarter of its debt ceiling, ×0.25 at 0.6 and over, ×2 with `ai_strategy_economic_imperialism`. Invitation score (`ai_power_bloc_identity_score`): up to +40 for an invitee in debt (`te_cred_ai_invite_debt_score`), −50 for a command economy, and Development Lending's +5 a tier for an invitee below the bloc's GDP per head. The join treaty's score (`ai_power_bloc_identity_score_treaties`, `scope:target_country` the bloc's side) carries the same three terms (from the 2026-11-16 review; before it the consortium had none there). `te_cc.1`'s odds are `te_lolr.1`'s for a bloc member: each rescue option carries Honour's weight in full (10, +3 in the bloc, −4 at 0.5 and again at 0.9 of the leader's ceiling, −3 per prior call to three) and Refuse carries its weight (2, +3 at war, +2 per prior call), and where two or three rescues show at once each takes a half or a third (`te_cred_rescue_split_2`, `_3`), so the rescues together weigh one Honour. A lean by the leader's government (Trade Unions to adjustment, Industrialists to concessions, laissez-faire to privatization) is a factor of 1.25 on one of them. Laws' `ai_impose_chance`: vanilla's `base_impose_law_weight` × 10 for the adjustment term (Cooperative Ownership keeps vanilla's own).

**The rest.** Summit option p, Debt Conference (`te_pb_summit_debt_conference`, every member, −0.5pp `country_risk_premium_add`, member relations). Statue `pm_power_bloc_statue_creditors_consortium` (prestige 3; +10% `state_capitalists_investment_pool_efficiency_mult` in its own state) and HQ `pm_hq_creditors_consortium` (−0.5pp premium, +10% leverage generation, capitalists' pool efficiency). A statue can go in every state of every member, and a country-wide line is paid once a statue: the −0.25pp premium a statue it first carried reached the cyclical premium's −10pp clamp at forty (review, 2026-11-16). So its identity effect is the state's, as vanilla's statues have theirs, and the premium cut is the headquarters', one to a bloc. Subjugation: `country_subjugation_creditor_strength_add = 0.4` in the identity's own `leader_modifier`, paid against a member in the leader's receivership. Bloc names: four generic ones. Concept `concept_consortium_guarantee`.

**Not built.** The banking dashboard's Backstops rows don't list the consortium's members (the sums are in the premium breakdowns); the principles reuse the Foreign Investment and Construction icons.

## Research Commons

`identity_research_commons` (design `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-power-bloc-identities-design.md` § 6; owner rulings 2026-11-16: the pooled research, 75% and 10%, hard to leave, the leader's share driving cohesion). Unlocked by Intergovernmental Organizations; founding needs the Ministry of Science law. Members research nothing and send the leader 75% of their weekly innovation; the leader's innovation rises by the sum and its cap by a tenth of it; every member is granted every technology any member holds. Play-test list: `docs/testing/power-bloc-identities-playtest.md` § Research Commons; tests: `test_research_commons.py`, `test_gen_research_commons_sync.py`.

**Files.** The identity in `common/power_bloc_identities/extra_power_bloc_identities.txt`; its own script in `te_research_commons_*.txt` under `scripted_effects/`, `scripted_triggers/`, `script_values/`, `on_actions/`, `static_modifiers/`, `power_bloc_principles/` and `diplomatic_actions/` (the Technology Embargo, `te_research_commons_embargo.txt`); the entry `common/journal_entries/je_research_commons.txt`; the generated ladder `common/scripted_effects/te_research_commons_sync_generated.txt` (`scripts/generators/gen_research_commons_sync.py`, a post-load regenerator). Shared files carry one block each: the three marker types (`power_bloc_extra_modifier_types.txt`), the principle groups, names, the statue and headquarters methods, the AI scores at the end of `modified.txt`, the summit option q, the Modernization Aid articles, `law_no_ministry_of_science`, and a guard in five `on_acquired_technology` hooks.

**Script reads the identity, not the marker.** `te_rc_in_commons` and `te_rc_leads_commons` test `has_identity`; `power_bloc_research_pool_bool` only labels the identity's tooltip. The non-leader line `country_tech_research_speed_mult = -10` sits in `non_leader_modifier`, which 1.15 applies to the members other than the leader (`member_modifier` is everyone, the leader included).

**The transfer.** `je_research_commons`, held by the leader and every member, declares three unit modifiers in `scaling_modifiers_while_active`: `te_rc_contribution` (`country_weekly_innovation_add = -1`) at `te_rc_contribution_value` (the member's `var:te_rc_contributed`), `te_rc_pool` (+1) at `te_rc_pool_value` (the members' variables summed live, so a leaver drops out within days) and `te_rc_pool_cap` (`country_weekly_innovation_max_add = 1`) at a tenth of the pool. The entry has no activation triggers: `te_rc_leader_pulse` adds it to the leader and to any member lacking it, which also brings a concluded one back. Its one invalid outcome is `te_rc_in_commons = no`, which runs `te_rc_entry_ended`.

**The fixed point.** A share of the member's present innovation would read its own drain and shrink each month. The leader's monthly pulse instead reads the member's add lines, `modifier:country_weekly_innovation_add`, with last month's contribution added back (`te_rc_member_gross_adds`), and sets the new one to 0.75 × that. The declaration lands 2 to 5 days after the variable changes and the pulse is monthly, so the contribution is exact in one step and doesn't drift: at the fixed point the member's add lines read a third of its contribution, which `TE_SELF_CHECK rc_contribution_lands` checks. The read is in add units, as the declaration is, so the member's innovation multiplier scales both alike. The design's own form, `country_innovation` plus the variable, settles off by the multiplier (C = 0.75(1+m)/(1+0.75m) × the gross), so it was not used and § 11 fact 4 is not relied on. A newcomer pays from the month after its entry appears.

**The ladder.** `te_rc_sync_all` (power bloc scope) has one branch per technology, 369 of the 370 (all three categories; Sericulture, `can_research = no`, left out), in prerequisite order (Kahn's, ties by era then key): if a member holds it and a member lacks it, `te_rc_grant` gives it to each one lacking it. Society is in: the covert generator leaves it out only because no espionage operation steals it. `EXCLUDED_TECHNOLOGIES` in the generator is empty in v1 (design § 6.7). A pass is 738 `any_power_bloc_member` checks, about 369 × N member reads for a bloc of N (each check stops at its first match, but where every member holds a technology the second check reads them all, and where none does the first does). It runs on the leader's monthly pulse (which also finds newcomers, marking each with `te_rc_member` and `te_rc_leader`) and, through `te_rc_on_acquired` on `on_acquired_technology`, whenever a member or the leader acquires a technology other than by a grant. The on-acquired pass runs the whole ladder rather than `add_technology_researched = scope:technology`: that form is unverified (§ 11 fact 7) and couldn't honor the exclusion list. It is skipped for a country holding `te_rc_granted_recent` (three days after a grant): should the hook fire after a grant rather than inside it, each grant would otherwise run a whole pass, and the monthly pass shares a real discovery made in those days.

**A grant is not a discovery.** `te_rc_grant` sets `te_rc_granting` around `add_technology_researched`. `extra_headlines_tech` (`headlines.txt`), `cultural_hegemony_tech_first_on_action` and `agricultural_diffusion_on_action` (`extra_on_actions.txt`) skip a country holding it, and `te_rc_on_acquired` only counts the call. So do the census and arable-land refreshes, `te_demog_lines_country_on_action` (`te_demog_on_actions.txt`) and `add_arable_land_effect_on_action` (`extra_on_actions.txt`): per technology they would send every state a census event (and again a day later) and queue an arable-land event that walks every state, about 9,600 state events for a newcomer 120 technologies behind with 40 states. Both are idempotent, set from the country's state when they run, so the end of a pass runs each once for every member that received a technology (`te_rc_refresh_after_grants`, which `test_research_commons.py` holds to the two hooks' bodies). The law-consistency, wave and AI-probe hooks still run for a grant. **Load order:** the engine runs a merged on_action's lists in file load order, and `te_research_commons_on_actions.txt` loads after `extra_on_actions.txt` and `headlines.txt`, so Cultural Hegemony's world-first test (no other country holds the technology) runs before the leader's discovery is shared; `test_research_commons.py` pins the order. `TE_SELF_CHECK rc_grant_hook` says whether the hook fires inside the grant, after it, or never; `rc_grant_lands` whether grants land.

**Leaving.** `can_leave`: the bloc's leverage advantage over the member is 10 or less (vanilla's six identities take 30). Institutes Left Behind falls only on a country that left a Commons that still stands (coordinator's ruling, 2026-11-16): `te_rc_has_left_commons` asks that the country carry `te_rc_member`, be in no Commons, and that the leader it served (`te_rc_leader`) still exist and still lead one. Such a country gets Institutes Left Behind (`te_rc_institutes_left_behind`, `country_tech_research_speed_mult = -0.5`), refreshed monthly at `te_rc_ilb_months` / 120 so it falls to nothing over ten years. It starts at the entry's invalid outcome, within days of leaving, with the monthly pulse as the backstop; `te_rc_ilb_new` (one day) keeps that day's monthly step from applying it a second time, since a `remove_modifier` doesn't see a copy added in the same effect. A bloc that dissolved, changed identity or lost its leader puts no penalty on its former members; a revolution's winner outside a standing Commons is a leaver (it inherits `te_rc_member` and `te_rc_leader`, not the modifier, and the pulse's backstop finds it). Joining a Commons again ends it at the next leader pulse.

**Cohesion.** A base of 0.2 × the maximum, the leader's share of the bloc's innovation × the maximum (Sovereign Empire's weight for the leader's prestige share), less worst leader relations (vanilla's block), and −1 per member on `power_bloc_modifier`. The share, `te_rc_leader_share`, is stored on the leader by its pulse, and compares production with production: the leader's add lines less the pool plus its own programs' drains (`te_rc_leader_own_adds`), against each member's add lines plus its contribution plus its programs' drains (`te_rc_member_production_adds`, stored as `te_rc_production`), each × its innovation multiplier. `te_rc_program_drains` is the three programs' declared values (the Space Race's seven milestones, the nuclear program, archaeology), read while each entry is active; `test_research_commons.py` holds its terms to the declarations. So a leader that spends the pool on its own programs keeps its share, as § 6.2 says fits the identity (coordinator's ruling, 2026-11-16); the contribution itself is still taken after a member's programs. The block reads it through `scope:power_bloc.power_bloc_leader` (§ 11 fact 8, a play-test hand check). No penalty for members without the Ministry of Science, since most hold Nuclear Weapons only through the Commons.

**Technology Controls** (`principle_group_technology_controls`, primary and unlocked by the identity; Advanced Research and Education also made primary; out of the three explicit-unlock groups). Five tiers, each restating the one below: every tier carries `power_bloc_technology_controls_bool`, which closes Modernization Aid and its upgrade (`science_aid`, `science_aid_2`) to countries outside the bloc through their `possible` (`te_rc_tech_controls_bars_aid`); members' `country_covert_defense_economic_add` and `_military_add` (industrial and military espionage) are 5, 10, 15, 20, 25; III to V add `power_bloc_can_use_technology_embargo_bool` and a display-only `power_bloc_technology_embargo_strength_add` of 50, 75, 100. Gates: I and II Nuclear Weapons' principle unlock (`country_nuclear_weapons_pb_principles_bool`), III Satellite Communications', IV Cellular Networks', V Automated Surveillance'. Knowledge Sharing needs no closing: vanilla's `da_knowledge_sharing` only reaches the actor's subjects or its own bloc's members.

**Technology Embargo** (`te_rc_technology_embargo`). A hostile one-sided pact shaped on vanilla's embargo (cost 100, `forced_duration = 12`, relations −1 a day), one of the leader's own at a time (asked from root with `is_initiator = yes`, since `has_diplomatic_pact` holds for either side), never on a member. Its `monthly_effect` and `accept_effect` run `te_rc_embargo_refresh`: the leader stores `te_rc_embargo_points` in `var:te_rc_embargo_cut` (the bloc's share of the world's innovation add lines × 50, 75 or 100 by tier, at most 50) and the target gets `te_rc_technology_embargoed` (`country_tech_spread_mult = -0.01`) at that multiplier for two months, while the target is outside the leader's bloc (one that joins is cut no more, though the pact runs out its forced duration); the break effects remove it. A bloc with a third of the world's innovation cuts 17%, 25% or 33%; vanilla's Knowledge Sharing gives +25%. The cut is a share of the target's spread, and spread keeps its weight against the target's own research in every era: Global Technological Development (`global_tech_spread_script_value`, `country_tech_spread_add`) adds about 220 a week once great powers reach era 6, 1,800 by era 9 and 4,800 at the end, while technologies raise the innovation cap that bounds directed research from about +250 to +4,300 (review, 2026-11-16), so the embargo needs no era scaling. That assumes the engine applies `country_tech_spread_mult` to the `_add` lines as well as the base, as its add and mult pairs do elsewhere; the play-test row 16 reads the target's spread figure. The tier's factor is read with `has_principle`, and `test_research_commons.py` holds the display field to it.

**Law lock.** `law_no_ministry_of_science` (the mod's own law, no `can_enact` before) gains one: not a bloc leader, or the bloc is no Research Commons. § 11 fact 12 doesn't arise.

**The rest.** Statue method `pm_power_bloc_statue_research_commons` (+3 prestige; +10% `building_university_throughput_add` in its own state), headquarters `pm_hq_research_commons` (Joint Research Institute: `country_te_rc_pool_cap_share_add` 0.025, which `te_rc_pool_cap_value` adds to the identity's tenth, so the cap rises by an eighth of the pool at full staffing; +5% prestige, university throughput, migration pull). Both were flat at first, +5 innovation a statue and +25 cap (review, 2026-11-16), and both shrank against later eras, whose technologies raise the cap from +250 in era 6 to about +4,300 by era 13. A share of the state's universities and a share of the pool grow with the eras. (A line per statue was no fault in the statue's case: universities add innovation without limit too.) Names: Joint Research Council, Academy of Nations, Scientific Union. AI: founding weight 100, +100 Intelligentsia in government, +100 Ministry of Science level 3+, ×2 with the world's highest innovation; invitation and join-treaty scores +2 per technology the leader holds over the country (at most 60), −50 for a great power. No subjugation bonus (as Diplomatic Framework); not exempt from irredentism's leader approval.
