# Judiciary

Part of the [mod systems reference](../mod_systems.md), which indexes every system.

## Judiciary (`lawgroup_judiciary`)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-17-judiciary-design.md`. Phase 1 is the law group and Judicial Independence; phases 2–4 (defying the court, seven amendments, landmark cases) were built on 2026-10-25, before phase 1's play-test, and are recorded in the spec's § 14 with the calls made without the owner (§ 14.4). Play-test list: `docs/testing/judiciary-playtest.md`.

- **Laws** (`common/laws/te_judiciary_laws.txt`, power structure): Executive Tribunals (**first in the file**, what an unseeded country and an old save get; no gate, so the consistency walk's terminal fallback), Religious Courts (`disallowing_laws` Total Separation and State Atheism; the walk drops it to Statutory Courts with Rationalism, else Executive Tribunals), Statutory Courts (Rationalism), Constitutional Review (Human Rights), Judicial Supremacy (Civil Rights Movement, reachable from any court), Algorithmic Jurisprudence (Generative AI). Descriptions are flavour with no figures; **every rule a player plans around is a modifier line on the law** that carries it, a `script_only` type in `common/modifier_type_definitions/judiciary_modifier_types.txt` that script reads (the banking dials' pattern): the ceiling (`country_judicial_independence_ceiling_add`), the paper court (`_ceiling_mult` −50% and `country_court_challenges_ignored_bool`, INJECTed into Autocracy, Bakufu, Neo-Absolutism and Single-Party State by `common/laws/te_judiciary_law_injections.txt` and written inline in Algorithmic Governance), entrenchment (`country_constitutional_law_enactment_success_add`, `country_rights_law_enactment_success_add`), Statutory Courts' two conditional lines and the algorithmic capture.
- **Judicial Independence** is `var:te_judicial_independence` (0–100) on the country and nowhere else, so a revolution's winner inherits it. `te_jud_ceiling` (`common/script_values/te_judiciary_values.txt`) is the ceiling line times one plus the paper court's line, read from the modifier sums, never from `has_law`. Yearly (`te_jud_yearly_update`, `on_yearly_pulse_country`): +4 below the ceiling, −10 above it, clamped to it with `if` + `set_variable`. **One refresh site:** `te_jud_refresh` (`common/scripted_effects/te_judiciary_effects.txt`), called only from `te_jud_monthly` (`on_monthly_pulse_country`, ROOT = the country), is the only writer of `te_judicial_independence` (multiplier score ÷ 100), `te_jud_votes_legitimacy` / `te_jud_unelected_authority` (one unit each times Statutory Courts' line, by `te_jud_elected_government`) and `te_jud_algorithmic_capture` (under Algorithmic Governance). Their multiplier variables are never removed.
- **Seeding.** Modifier sums lag a law change, so nothing reads the ceiling the day a law changes. `on_game_started` only sets `global_var:te_jud_world_seeded`; the first monthly pulse seeds each score at half its ceiling (`te_jud_seed_score`, unless history set it) and sets `te_jud_seeded`. History (`common/history/extra_history.txt`) runs `te_jud_assign_court_by_rule` for every country (Religious Courts for Theocracy or an Islamic state religion under State Religion or Freedom of Conscience, Statutory Courts under a voting law, else Executive Tribunals), then the United States takes Constitutional Review and Britain, France, Prussia, the Netherlands, Sweden and Belgium Statutory Courts, with scores. An old save runs `te_repair_judiciary_seed` (`common/on_actions/te_old_save_repairs.txt`, with its removal marker): courts by rule in the first month, `te_jud_world_seeded` in the second. A new country mid-game is seeded from the law it holds. A revolutionary country is never seeded (`NOT = { is_revolutionary = yes }`): the winner of a civil war keeps its own variables where both sides hold one, so a seeded rebel would replace the nation's score, and unseeded it inherits it. Seeding also waits for `te_jud_ceiling > 0`, since a country's first 30-day pulse can fall before the laws' modifier sums are in place.
- **Entrenchment** (`te_jud_enactment_started`, `on_law_enactment_started`): unless a paper court, a law in the constitutional core (`te_jud_enacting_core_law`: Distribution of Power, Governance Principles, Church and State, Citizenship, Free Speech, Internal Security, State Power, Judiciary) or, under a rights line, a rights group (`te_jud_enacting_rights_law`) gets the enactment modifier its line names: `te_jud_supremacy_challenge` (−15%) at a line of −15% or less, else `te_jud_constitutional_challenge` (−10%). Every line must be one of the two tiers (`test_judiciary.py`). Self-check: `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN judiciary_enacting_law_scope`, once a game, whether `currently_enacting_law` resolves at the hook.
- **Overhaul shock:** `te_jud_law_activated` (`on_law_activated`, ROOT = the law) matches the type of every law that moves the ceiling and fires hidden `te_judiciary_events.1` on `owner` 7 days later, once the new sums are in place. With `te_jud_seeded` and the score 20 or more above the new ceiling, the event stores the gap ÷ 100 in `te_jud_overhaul_mult` and applies `te_jud_court_overhaul` (+2pp risk premium, −10 legitimacy at a gap of 100) for 5 years, decaying; at a gap of 40, middle-strata radicals. A same-name `add_modifier` would stack, so the event removes a running shock first, and fires at all during one only for a larger gap than the stored one.
- **Spending:** `te_jud_spend = { AMOUNT }`, floored at 0. Forceful Legislation (`minor_events_timelineextended.2`) options b, c and e spend 5, 10 and 20 inside a `custom_tooltip`; the tax code's Force Through (`te_tax_force_into`) spends 10.
- **Stances:** `JUDICIARY_LISTS` (eleven named lists, each naming all six laws) and `JUDICIARY_STANCES` (vanilla ideology → list) in `ideology_modifications.py`; `apply_ideologies.py` writes them into `common/ideologies/modified.txt`. The mod's own ideologies carry the same lists by hand in `extra_ideologies.txt`. `gen_law_consistency.py` places the group after State Power in `LAWGROUP_PRIORITY`.
- **Shown by:** `concept_judicial_independence` (the score and its ceiling, live), the scaled modifier in the country's modifier list, and the laws' tooltip lines. The national modifiers tab (`docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-17-national-modifiers-tab-design.md`) takes the score over later.
- **Icons** (icon batch 6, 2026-10-27; `law` entries in `scripts/image_pipeline/icon_prompts.py`), each `law_icons/<law>.dds`: Executive Tribunals a crown on a closed book, Religious Courts a book on a lectern under prayer beads, Statutory Courts a judge's wig on a stack of books, Constitutional Review a magnifying glass on a sealed book, Judicial Supremacy balance scales on a pedestal with a scroll in the lower pan, Algorithmic Jurisprudence balance scales on a computer-chip pillar.

- **Phase 2, defying the court** (spec § 14.1). Constitutional Review and Judicial Supremacy carry `country_court_reviews_forced_laws_bool` ("Courts Review Forced Laws"); `te_jud_court_can_strike_laws` reads it and excludes a paper or packed court. Forceful Legislation's three bypass options call `te_jud_open_challenge = { TIER }` in a `custom_tooltip` (`te_jud_review_<size>_tt`): it stores the forced law and the law it replaces as law types (`te_jud_challenge_law`, `te_jud_replaced_law`) and logs `TE_SELF_CHECK OK|BROKEN judiciary_law_type_variable` once a game. `te_jud_challenge_monthly` (in `te_jud_monthly`) files the case when the enactment is over and `has_law` reads the forced law, one at a time (`te_jud_case_*`, `te_jud_case_filed` for 270 days as a backstop), and fires hidden `te_judiciary_events.2` 180 days on. The court strikes the law down when the score is at least `te_jud_strike_threshold` (90, 60 or 30 by option): `.3` offers comply (`activate_law` of the replaced law, +5 legitimacy decaying and +5 score; −10 legitimacy if none was recorded), defy (−20 score, `te_jud_ruling_defied`: +1pp risk premium and −5 legitimacy decaying) or pack (`te_jud_pack_court`: −15 score, 150 authority fading, ten years of `te_jud_court_packed` with the paper-court line, −30% ceiling and −5 legitimacy, and The Enlarged Bench `.5` a year later: drop to Statutory Courts, keep it, or restore the bench). A weaker court upholds it (`.4`, +3 legitimacy). The tax code's Force Through opens no challenge. The overhaul shock's protests are now `.6` Lawyers in the Streets (let them march: the old middle-strata radicals; clear the streets: 100 authority fading; promise tenure: +10 score).
- **Phase 3, amendments** (`common/amendments/te_judiciary_amendments.txt`, spec § 14.2): Suspension of Habeas Corpus (Statutory, Constitutional; parent Secret Police; wartime, lapses at peace through `te_jud_amendment_upkeep` and its line `country_amendment_lapses_at_peace_bool`), Judicial Elections (Statutory, Constitutional; Universal Suffrage; ceiling −20), Mixed Commercial Courts (Religious, Executive; Free Trade; −0.5pp credit standing, ceiling +10), Jury Trials (Statutory, Constitutional; Guaranteed Liberties; ceiling +10, suppression +10%), Military Tribunals for Civilians (Executive, Statutory; Outlawed Dissent; ceiling −20%), People's Tribunals (Executive under Single-Party State; lifted without it), Human-in-the-Loop Appeals (Algorithmic; Strong Privacy Rights; −2 on the capture line). Ways in: petitions `amendment_petition_events.21`–`.27` (§ Amendment Petitions) and debate events `te_judiciary_events.20`–`.22` (Judicial Elections, Jury Trials, Human-in-the-Loop) in `on_law_checkpoint_debate`.
- **Phase 4, landmark cases** (spec § 14.3; `te_judiciary_events.30`–`.36`, once per country through `te_jud_heard_<case>`): The Free Soil Case, The Ōtsu Case (an AI great power only), Freedom of Contract (whose packing option is phase 2's), The Voided Election (from `on_election_campaign_end`), The Doctrine of Necessity (a fortnight after a paper-court law, from `te_jud_law_activated`), A House of Justice and Courts for a New Code (reform paths out of Religious Courts: `start_enactment` of Statutory Courts, or the commercial courts amendment). Sub-actions `te_jud_landmark_*` in `te_judiciary_on_actions.txt`.
- **Tests:** `test_judiciary.py` (values against the spec, the one refresh site, the seeding order, entrenchment's groups and tiers, the shock's scope hop, spending, stances; phase 2's challenge, filing and ruling; the amendments' hosts, parents and ways in; the landmark cases).
