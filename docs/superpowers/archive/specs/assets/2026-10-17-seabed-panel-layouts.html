▸ How Seabed Licences Work
  • Q1 is the first box. Q2 is the "Free here" column (green = room on that coast, no −25%).
  • Q3 is the pool bar + "+1 licence" (hover: "+3 flotillas, or +1 coastal state here ≈ +2").
  • Q4 opens per basin. Long lists stay short: only claimants in that basin.
  • Tightest on width: five columns at 520 units.
