# ---- The monument policy (v2 §5): on the monument itself (building scope) ------
# Refreshed by gm_state_monthly. The maintenance input, the only goods a Grand
# Monument uses, is level_scaled, which throughput does not touch
# (scripting_best_practices.md § "Production Method Modifier Scaling Blocks"),
# so the policy scales the building's construction-goods input as the engine's
# own pm_retooling does. On the building, not the state, so no other building
# pays it. pm_no_maintenance is off under the default construction market rule,
# so upkeep cannot move by switching methods.
# The finished building's upkeep (sites spec 2026-10-18 §3.4): one modifier,
# its multiplier the levels its monuments record times the policy's factor,
# less the one level the building stands at (gm_state_upkeep_mult). The two
# policy modifiers below are no longer added; they stay for older saves, whose
# pulse removes them.
