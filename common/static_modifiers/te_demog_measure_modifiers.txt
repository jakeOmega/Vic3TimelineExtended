# One country modifier a measure while it is on: its census types (script-only state types,
# demographics_modifier_types.txt, which a state's read takes from its country), its interest groups' approval, its
# authority upkeep (a flat country_authority_add: authority doesn't scale with size) and any other effect. A money
# upkeep sits on a separate _cost modifier, country_expenses_add = 1, applied with a GDP-scaled weekly multiplier
# (te_demog_ms_cost_<measure>, refreshed by the country's yearly census: scripting_best_practices.md, "Expense Scaling
# with GDP"), with the Budget Breakdown's mirror line (its Family Policy row: scripts/generators/gen_budget_breakdown.py).
# A bureaucracy upkeep is an institution's cost multiplier, so it scales with the country.
# Authority upkeep (rebalanced 2026-10-16, owner): a measure applies to every state at once, where a decree costs 100 for
# one, so the measures that need enforcement in every district cost several decrees' worth, scaled by how much
# enforcement they need: Honours 50 (a medal and a ceremony), Later Longer Fewer 150 (persuasion through local cadres),
# Restricted Contraception 200 (a ban the police enforce), Birth Limit 300 at two children and 400 at one (permits,
# fines and quotas in every county), the Crash Campaign 300 (sterilization quotas, five years at most). Authority is
# flat, so the coercive package (one child with the Crash Campaign, 700) takes most of a strong autocracy's authority
# and is out of reach of most democracies (3a's values were 25, 50, 50, 100, 100 and 100).
# Birth Limit has one modifier a setting (two children, one child). The interest groups' lines are
# interest_group_ig_<group>_approval_add on a country modifier, as vanilla's re_media_moderation.
# A release comments the census types' lines out (comment_out_census_inputs.py, rule 1); every modifier keeps a line
# of its own, and nothing applies them there (the buttons and the AI pulse sit under te_demog_effects_run).
# test_demographics_measures.py pins each modifier's lines to the catalogue.
