# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
#  Wealth Concentration (demographics spec §4.2). Unit values, applied
#  with a multiplier of 0-1 read from a variable.
#  Country pair: the national figure, refreshed by te_demog_wc_national
#  (te_inh_apply_concentration_modifiers), multipliers
#  te_inh_great_fortunes_mult and te_inh_dispersed_mult. Political capture
#  is national: a few great fortunes buy influence beyond their own state.
#  State three: the state's own score (te_dg_wc), refreshed on the state's
#  yearly pulse (te_inh_refresh_wc_state_effects), multipliers
#  te_inh_wc_high_mult, te_inh_wc_low_mult and te_inh_land_hunger_mult.
#  Saving, mobility, radicals and the rural exodus happen where the
#  property is.
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
