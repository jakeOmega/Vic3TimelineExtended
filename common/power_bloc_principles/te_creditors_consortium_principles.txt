# ============================================================================
# The Creditors' Consortium's two principle groups (2026-11-16)
# ============================================================================
# Design: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-power-bloc-identities-design.md § 5.4.
# Groups: common/power_bloc_principle_groups/te_creditors_consortium_principle_groups.txt,
# both primary for identity_creditors_consortium and unlocked only by it.
#
# CONDITIONALITY, the Debt Administration: what a rescue may demand
# (te_cc.1). By tier: terms beside the receivership, over the one every
# consortium sets, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3 (te_cred_terms_allowed); the receivership's remittance 0.10, 0.15, 0.15,
# 0.20, 0.25% of the debtor's GDP a month (te_cred_receivership_take_extra);
# monopolies a concessions term demands 1, 1, 2, 2, 3 (te_cred_monopolies_allowed);
# months out of default under a quarter of the ceiling that end the
# receivership 12, 18, 24, 30, 36 (te_cred_receivership_exit_extra). The
# article's own figures (0.1%, 12 months) are tier I's, so a tier only ever
# hardens the terms. At the top a debtor remits 3% of its GDP a year, about
# what the Ottoman Public Debt Administration took. Script pays all of it, so
# the power_bloc_te_cred_* lines are display fields (power_bloc_extra_modifier_types.txt)
# and the scripts read the tier through has_principle (te_cred_cond_tier_*).
# The one engine modifier: the bloc's leverage, +5% a tier, since every rescue
# is a debt the leader holds over a member (Leverage summit: +25% for ten
# years).
#
# DEVELOPMENT LENDING, the Marshall Plan: each month members above the bloc's
# GDP per head pay a share of a month of their investment pool's income to
# members below it, split by population (te_cred_dl_pulse): 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5
# and 15%. From tier II the receivers build faster (te_cred_dl_receiver_2..5,
# te_creditors_consortium_modifiers.txt): +5, +10, +15, +20%, beside vanilla
# Construction II's +10% for every member. Sized against what already moves a
# pool's income: the neocolonial dependency takes 12% of it, the banking
# cycle's cooperative phases -36% to +24% (banking_cycle_effects.txt); the
# Marshall Plan was about 1.2% of American GDP a year, some 8% of its gross
# private investment.
#
# What the payers get (2026-11-16 review): the leader is usually the richest
# member and pays most. Exploit Members, the transfer the other way, is a
# positive group: its leader takes authority, influence, legitimacy and income
# for the cohesion it costs (-5, -10, -20, -30, -40). Mirroring the cohesion
# alone would leave this one net negative, so the leader also gains what the
# Marshall Plan bought the United States, standing abroad: cohesion +5, +10,
# +20, +30 and +40, the leader's influence +10% to +30%, and countries below
# the bloc's GDP per head give its invitation and its join treaty +5 a tier
# (te_cred_dl_invite_per_tier, read by the two AI scores in modified.txt;
# the power_bloc_te_cred_dl_invite_add line is its display field).
#
# GATES. Tiers I to III need Central Banking, as the Monetary Union's do (the
# identity's founder holds a National Bank, which needs it, so it binds only a
# bloc whose leader changed). Tier IV needs Intergovernmental Organizations
# (Conditionality: the IMF's era) or Keynesian economics (Development Lending:
# the Marshall Plan's), tier V Globalization, the mod's usual tier-4/5
# _pb_principles_bool pacing (gen_pb_principle_unlock_descs.py reads them).
# Conditionality is visible only under the Full banking rule, since its terms
# are the monetary layer's receivership; Development Lending moves vanilla's
# investment pools and runs under any rule.
# ============================================================================

			add = 50
		}
	}
