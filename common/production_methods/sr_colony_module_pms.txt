# ============================================================================
# COLONY MODULE FABRICATION — the Space Program's module methods (colony modules design § 5)
# ============================================================================
# The production methods of pmg_space_program_module_fabrication
# (sr_colony_module_pmgs.txt), a secondary group on building_space_program.
# Each gives module fabrication points a month through
# country_sr_module_fabrication_add (workforce-scaled, so a half-staffed
# programme fabricates at half the rate); every queued colony module costs
# sr_module_points (100) of them (sr_mod_month, sr_colony_module_effects.txt).
# The points do something only under the Simple and Full Colonies settings of
# the Space Race rule; a method can't be gated by a game rule
# (scripting_best_practices.md § Locking Production Methods), so its loc says so.
#
#   method               gate                      goods a week                                   points/mo  bill/wk  a point
#   None (default)       -                         -                                              0
#   Orbital Assembly     cislunar_infrastructure   1,300 Launch Capacity, 400 Electronic         5          340,000  68,000
#                                                  Components, 300 Robotics, 300 Radios
#   Orbital Foundries    space_colonization        2,600 Launch Capacity, 15 Advanced            12         732,000  61,000
#                                                  Materials, 800 Electronic Components,
#                                                  600 Robotics, 500 Radios
#   Fusion Shipyards     compact_fusion_reactors   4,800 Launch Capacity, 20 Advanced            25       1,368,000  54,720
#                                                  Materials, 1,600 Electronic Components,
#                                                  1,500 Robotics, 1,000 Radios
#
# The sizing (the owner, 2026-11-20). Orbital Assembly takes no Advanced
# Materials: its technology is era 10 and the Advanced Material Fabricator's,
# modern_material_science, era 11. Each later method costs about 10% less a
# point a month than the one before at base prices, so it costs more a week and
# builds a module only a little cheaper: 29.5M, 26.5M and 23.8M a module at full
# staff. Until then the later methods cost 44% and 54% less a point, and a
# Fusion Shipyards module a quarter of an Orbital Assembly one.
# test_solar_sites.py holds both rules, and holds the two dearer bills to the
# AI's copies of them (sr_mod_fab_bill_base_*, sr_colony_module_values.txt),
# which keep an AI on the dearest method within its budget
# (sr_mod_ai_fab_room_*, sr_mod_activate_best_fabrication).
#
# Orbital Foundries keeps its first name's key, pm_sr_fabrication_lunar_foundries
# (renamed for Mare Imbrium's Mass Driver, lunar colonization design § 15.4).
#
# No method employs anyone: the programme's own mission method staffs the
# building, and these ride on its workforce, as debris removal does
# (pm_employment_audit stays clean).
# ============================================================================
