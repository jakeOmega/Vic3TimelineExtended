# ============================================================================
# SOLAR SYSTEM COLONIZATION — ground-to-orbit batteries' methods (fleets design § 15)
# ============================================================================
# pmg_military_base_orbital_battery, the Military Base's fourth group
# (extra_pm_groups.txt): guns on the ground that fire on any enemy fleet at
# Earth every month, so a fleet that has taken Earth orbit can't simply hold it
# for a whole war. An extension of the anti-satellite strike
# (te_asat_strike_action.txt). Every Space Race setting but Full Colonies
# disables both weapons (extra_game_rules.txt), so there they stay at None.
#
# Each weapon carries its figures as workforce-scaled state modifiers
# (script-only: the engine consumes nothing; they still render in the method's
# tooltip and the state's modifiers), which the script sums over the states the
# country controls (sr_fleet_values.txt; § 5.1: a base in an occupied state
# fires for no one):
#   state_orbital_battery_fire_add   fire on hostile fleets at Earth a month
#                                    (sr_battery_fire, the Earth battle);
#                                    Kinetic Interceptors' adds 0.02 low debris
#                                    a point fired (sr_battery_debris_low)
#   state_orbital_debris_sweep_add   low-orbit debris swept a month, in peace
#                                    only (sr_battery_sweep, the debris step)
# One field each, no building-scoped copy, which the game would list a second
# time (scripting_best_practices.md). Like the base's other groups, they scale
# with its level: five levels of base with a battery are five levels of battery.
#
#   method                gate                       fire/level/mo  sweep/level/mo  inputs a level a week
#   None (default)        -                          -              -               -
#   Kinetic Interceptors  space_militarization       1              -               Launch Capacity 25, Electronic Components 10
#   Ground Lasers         directed_energy_weapons    2              0.01            electricity 200, Electronic Components 15
#
# Every armed level employs 100 officers, 300 engineers and 600 machinists, on
# top of the base's own staff. First guesses (design § 20).
# ============================================================================
