# The mod's own journal entry groups: two sections of the journal. The owner asked for
# Timeline Extended Systems as a section of its own, leaving out the narrative entries
# such as the rights debates; it holds every entry the player works through with its own
# panel, tab, bar or buttons. Rights and Society, the social debates, is the second
# section, from the classification of that rule (PR #1797); the owner put the
# Post-Scarcity Transition there too ("I'd put it in society"). Everything else, and every
# vanilla entry the mod replaces or injects into, keeps its group.
# test_journal_entry_groups.py pins who is in each, so a new entry is placed on purpose.
#
# The fields (vanilla 1.15, <game>/common/journal_entry_groups/00_journal_entries.txt):
# - themed = yes gives the group a section of its own in the journal list: a card with
#   the illustration and the group's name over its entries (gui/journal.gui,
#   journal_entry_group_with_illustration). An entry of a group that isn't themed is
#   listed under "Additional Journal Entries" with no group name: in 1.15, Domestic
#   Affairs and Foreign Affairs are not sections, only a picture on each entry's page.
# - context is the default context of the group's entries: an entry without its own
#   `context` takes the group's (<game>/common/journal_entries/journal_entries.md).
#   Both groups are `country`, as Domestic and Foreign Affairs were, so no entry's
#   context changes. je_world_climate keeps its own `context = none`, as vanilla's
#   contextless je_reform_or_revolution sits in the country group je_group_socialism.
# - illustration (required since 1.15, 1108x200) heads the section and every member
#   entry's page. Until the mod has art of its own, both borrow a vanilla picture from a
#   group that isn't themed, so no other section in the list shows the same one.
# - updates_strategic_region_stances is left out (no), as in Domestic Affairs and the two
#   groups these replace. It is undocumented; vanilla sets it on groups whose entries
#   its AI's region stance scores read with has_journal_entry (the Scramble for Africa,
#   German unification), so an entry there has the AI re-score its stances. No stance
#   score, vanilla's or the mod's, reads a mod entry, so the flag would buy only work.
#   The eleven entries that came from Foreign Affairs (Colonial Empire, Nuclear Weapons,
#   the United Nations, the Space Race) had it.
#
# Order: the journal lists themed sections in the order the groups were defined, and the
# engine reads vanilla's and the mods' files in one ASCII-ordered list
# (scripting_best_practices.md § Load Order). This file's name sorts before vanilla's
# 00_journal_entries.txt, so the two sections come first, Systems on top. Unverified in
# game: docs/testing/vanilla-1-15-playtest.md, "Journal sections".

# Every system: Banking, the Tax Code, the Strategic Reserve, Global Warming and World
# Climate, the Space Race, Nuclear Weapons, the United Nations, Covert Warfare, Cultural
# Hegemony, the Colonial Empire, Monuments, Heir Education and the culture merges
# (gen_culture_merges.py). The picture is vanilla's Development: an engine hall, the
# machinery a country runs.
je_group_te_systems = {
	context = country
	illustration = "gfx/interface/illustrations/journal/je_group_illu_technology.dds"
	themed = yes
}
