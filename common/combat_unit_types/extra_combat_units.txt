### ARMY

### PEACETIME AMMUNITION IS HALF VANILLA'S
### A unit's upkeep_modifier is what its barracks buys every week, mobilized or
### not. While a battalion is mobilized, mobilization_option_basic_supplies
### multiplies its ammunition by 4 (te_wartime_munitions_injections.txt), so a
### battalion at war burns twice vanilla's peacetime ammunition, and four times
### what it trains with in peace. Vanilla units take the cut as an INJECT diff
### summed with vanilla's value (-1.5 against squad infantry's 3), so a vanilla
### patch to any other good flows through untouched; mod units carry the halved
### value directly. test_wartime_munitions.py checks every diff against
### vanilla_parsed/ and fails when a vanilla unit's ammunition changes, or a new
### vanilla unit starts using ammunition, without a matching diff here.
