# ======================================================================
#  AMENDMENT PETITIONS – ON ACTIONS
#
#  Each petition (events/amendment_petition_events.txt) rolls on its own
#  once a year: its pool pairs a no-event weight (`N = 0`, as in
#  cultural_hegemony_events_on_actions.txt) with the event, the two
#  summing to 1000, so the event's weight is its yearly chance in tenths
#  of a percent while the country qualifies. A country that doesn't
#  qualify sees nothing, whether the engine drops a failing event before
#  the pick or after it.
#
#  The chance follows how long the situation tends to last: a lasting one
#  (a multicultural population, a dominant governing group) rolls every
#  year it holds, so it rolls low; a short or rare one (a war, an election
#  campaign the yearly pulse lands in) rolls high.
#    25  = 2.5%   lasting situations
#    50  = 5%     situations that come and go over years
#    100 = 10%    hard times or unrest
#    250 = 25%    a war or an election campaign
#
#  On top of the roll, te_ap_window_open holds back every petition for
#  five years after the last one, and each event has a twenty-year
#  cooldown of its own.
#  Docs: docs/systems/mod_systems.md § Amendment Petitions.
# ======================================================================
