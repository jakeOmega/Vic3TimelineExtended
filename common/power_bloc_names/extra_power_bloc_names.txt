## historical names any bloc can take

# names open to any country, each the name of a standing body, league or union
# of the period. A bloc is its leader and its structure, not its members, so
# none counts or describes them. Names from the founder's capital city ("Berlin
# Pact", "League of Vienna") are in te_capital_power_bloc_names_generated.txt.

##### vanilla's catch-all names: the player's, not the AI's #####
# An AI bloc's name is drawn at random from every name whose trigger passes;
# there is no weight. The draw happens at formation and again whenever the
# leader changes, from the new leader's names. These pass for nearly every
# country, so they took 18 of 22 blocs sampled from saves. The player keeps them
# (the formation panel's randomize button draws from the same list); the AI
# draws instead from the historical names near the top of this file, its
# capital city's names, its identity's names and the country, region, culture
# and ideology ones. Each keeps vanilla's own conditions.
