# Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-demographics-modifier-types-design.md (stage 1).
# Read only by the census (te_demog_mult_*); the engine never applies these types. A law's
# modifier reaches every state the country owns; the Workplace Safety lines and the police lines
# (institution_modifier) are per level of the institution and reach incorporated states only.
# The police laws' lines sit on the Ministry of Public Safety's levels (2026-10-16, owner: a law
# with the Ministry at level 0 or 1 gave the full cut), larger for a national force: Local Police
# -2% a level, Dedicated and Militarized -3% (Private Policing's -2% is in extra_laws.txt). The health laws' access
# lines sit inside their existing blocks in modified_health_system.txt; the women's-rights laws'
# natural fertility (phase 2 step 4) inside their INJECTs in extra_laws.txt, except Women Own
# Property's, which has no other INJECT.
