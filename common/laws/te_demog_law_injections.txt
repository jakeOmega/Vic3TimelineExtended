# Demographics: law terms for the census's causes of death.
# Spec: docs/superpowers/archive/specs/2026-10-09-demographics-modifier-types-design.md (stage 1).
# Read only by the census (te_demog_mult_*); the engine never applies these types. A law's
# modifier reaches every state the country owns; the Workplace Safety lines and the police lines
# (institution_modifier) are per level of the institution and reach incorporated states only.
# The police laws' lines sit on the Ministry of Public Safety's levels (2026-10-16, owner: a law
# with the Ministry at level 0 or 1 gave the full cut), larger for a national force: Local Police
# -2% a level, Dedicated and Militarized -3% (Private Policing's -2% is in extra_laws.txt). The health laws' access
# lines sit inside their existing blocks in modified_health_system.txt; the women's-rights laws'
# natural fertility (phase 2 step 4) inside their INJECTs in extra_laws.txt, except Women Own
# Property's, which is in this file; their women's work share (stage 3) is in this file too. The health laws' longevity care lines (late game §4.2) and the
# church laws' weight on Family Limitation (phase 3) are at the end of this file; Child Labor Allowed and
# Restricted Child Labor carry the 10-14s' share at work (phase 2 step 1) beside their other lines.
# Primogeniture's and Undivided Farm Succession's Late Marriage lines sit in their own definitions
# (extra_laws.txt, extra_amendments.txt). The women's-rights laws' share of women at work (stage 3, 2026-10-19), which
# the migrant profile and Late Marriage read, follows Women Own Property's block (Protected Class's is in
# extra_laws.txt). Wealth Concentration's law lines are not census inputs: te_demog_wealth_law_injections.txt.
