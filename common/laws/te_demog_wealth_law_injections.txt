# The slavery term (2026-10-21, ordered by the owner): property in people, the largest single asset a planter class held.
# What matters is how wide the ownership is, which the supply sets. An open trade keeps slaves cheap, so artisans and
# small farmers own a few and the stock is wide (Slave Trade +5); a closed supply turns the stock into an appreciating
# asset that prices the small owner out (Legacy Slavery +10); debt bondage hands people to creditors (Debt Slavery +10);
# and colonial export plantations, run for absentee owners, were the most concentrated of all (Colonial Slavery +10).
# Slavery Banned none. A flat line, like the tax and economic terms: it is not weighted by a state's slaves, which the
# census walk does not count.
INJECT:law_colonial_slavery = {
	modifier = {
		country_wealth_concentration_slavery_add = 10
	}
}
