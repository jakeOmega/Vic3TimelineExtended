# ============================================================================
# BODILY AUTONOMY (era 13 spec § 7.1; docs/systems/mod_systems/social_movements.md)
# ============================================================================
# Four laws in lawgroup_bodily_autonomy (Human Rights). The group is about how
# a body looks, chosen for self-expression: appearance, sex characteristics,
# fur, tails, and at the end a body plan of one's own. What a body can do is
# Human Augmentation's, a separate group, so the interest groups split
# differently on the two (ideology_modifications.py, BODILY_AUTONOMY_STANCES):
#
#   law_natural_form            the default: no provision for elective change
#   law_licensed_modification   licensed clinics (Morphological Freedom, era 12)
#   law_morphological_freedom   a right to change one's body (the same)
#   law_clade_citizenship       the right to leave the human body plan behind and
#                               remain a citizen (Self-Directed Evolution, era 13)
#
# ORDER MATTERS. law_natural_form comes first in this file: the engine gives a
# country with no law in a group the group's first law in file order, which is
# also what an old save loading this group for the first time gets, and what a
# country created later gets (scripting_best_practices.md, "First Law in a
# Lawgroup File Is the Unseeded Default"). common/history/extra_history.txt
# seeds it too, as it does law_no_augmentation.
#
# NATURAL FORM CARRIES NOTHING. Every country holds it from 1836 until era 12,
# so a modifier on it would apply for two centuries with no law to answer it.
# For the same reason every ideology is neutral on it: the groups that want it
# (the Devout, the Rural Folk) show it by opposing the other laws.
#
# THE ACCEPTANCE HOOK. When appearance is chosen, the heritage trait, the
# nearest thing the game has to how people look, should matter less. The two
# middle laws add to country_acceptance_no_shared_heritage_trait_add on top of
# the citizenship law in force (+5, +10). The mod's citizenship laws
# (common/laws/modified.txt) are not vanilla's: no shared heritage reads -20 to
# 0, and Civil Rights Movement and Social Justice Movements add +10 each by era
# 9, so under four of the six citizenship laws a pop sharing no heritage trait
# already gets +20, against +20 to +25 for one sharing a heritage trait or
# group, and the bonus lifts it above them. Read in game whether the engine
# then gives a pop that shares the trait the higher no-shared value
# (docs/testing/era-13-playtest.md; scripting_best_practices.md, Citizenship
# Acceptance: Read the Mod's Laws, Not Vanilla's).
#
# CLADE CITIZENSHIP TAKES HERITAGE OUT OF ACCEPTANCE (spec § 7.3). A static
# bonus can't do it in this engine. Under the mod's citizenship laws, with Civil
# Rights Movement and Social Justice Movements, a shared heritage trait, a
# shared trait group and none read 20 / 20-25 / 20 under four of the six
# (Sovereign Subjecthood; Cultural, Assimilatory and Universal Citizenship),
# 40 / 30 / 10 under Ancestral and 60 / 60 / 0 under Racialized Citizenship:
# a flat +20 to no shared heritage would rank those pops 20 points above a
# shared trait under the first four, and still leave 30 to 60 points of
# heritage under the other two. So the law carries Morphological Freedom's +10
# (no pop is worse off than under it), and te_clade_parity_refresh
# (common/scripted_effects/te_bodily_autonomy_effects.txt, the monthly country
# pulse) raises each of the three heritage figures to the highest of them,
# with te_clade_parity_trait, _group and _none at multiplier = the gap. Every
# pop then reads the same heritage figure, and none ranks above another by it.
#
# THE DEBATE. common/journal_entries/je_morphological_freedom.txt succeeds on
# law_morphological_freedom or law_clade_citizenship and fails on
# law_natural_form held to its end.
#
# A FIFTH LAW goes last in this file, with a stance in each
# BODILY_AUTONOMY_LISTS row in ideology_modifications.py and in the
# hand-written blocks of common/ideologies/extra_ideologies.txt
# (ideology_lawgroup_audit names any it misses), then
# scripts/generators/regen_ideologies_offline.py.
# ============================================================================
