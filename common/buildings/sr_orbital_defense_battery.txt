# ============================================================================
# SOLAR SYSTEM COLONIZATION — ground-to-orbit batteries (fleets design § 15)
# ============================================================================
# Design: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-solar-colonization-fleets-design.md.
# An extension of the anti-satellite strike (te_asat_strike_action.txt): guns
# on the ground that fire on any enemy fleet at Earth every month, so a fleet
# that has taken Earth orbit can't simply hold it for a whole war. Levels in
# any state, under Full Colonies. Its methods (sr_orbital_defense_battery_pms.txt)
# carry their figures as workforce-scaled state modifiers that the script sums
# over the states the owner controls (§ 5.1: a battery in an occupied state
# fires for no one): sr_battery_fire joins the owner's side of the Earth battle
# (sr_cb_battery_gather, sr_fleet_combat_effects.txt), and Ground Lasers'
# sweep clears the low debris pool in peace (sr_battery_sweep_step,
# sr_earth_orbit_effects.txt). An AI government builds them only at war with
# a country that has fleets (sr_battery_may_build_government).
# ============================================================================

building_orbital_defense_battery = {

	icon = "gfx/interface/icons/building_icons/building_military_base.dds"

		}
	}

	background = "gfx/interface/icons/building_icons/backgrounds/building_panel_bg_military.dds"
}
